### Speculatieve Memorie: Chinese Demographics & Cruelties of Pakubuwana IV (1808)

* Reference: **Van Alphen - Engelhard No. 235**
* **Tollgate Corruption:** Tollgate administration heavily corrupted over the past 20 years; comprehensive reform required. HB II cannot claim *strandgeld* rights since HB I waived claims to Pasisir in 1755.
* **Chinese Demographics in Vorstenlanden:** **2,040 adult males over 14 years old** in Vorstenlanden (Surakarta: 1,282; Yogyakarta: 758).
* **Inter-Court Solidarity:** Engelhard refutes Van Reede and Van Overstraten's view that Solo-Yogya jealousy prevents joint action, noting a gradual rapprochement and secret understanding between courtiers of both kingdoms.

  * In 1795 proposed marrying his sister R.A. Numa to the Yogyakarta Crown Prince (blocked by Van Overstraten).
* **Atrocity Incident at Surakarta Court (March 1795):**
