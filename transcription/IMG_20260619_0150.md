### Archive Entries: December 1808 (Minister Wiese Installation & Solo River Logistics)

* Reference: **ANIP Nationaal No. 22 / Copia Afgaande Brieven 1808 (December 1808)**
* **2 December 1808 (G.W. Wiese Installed as Minister):**
  * **G.W. Wiese assumes office as Dutch Minister to Yogyakarta** (*Daendels Decision 12 Aug. 1808*); fully installed by predecessor Pieter Engelhard at midday, 3 Dec. 1808.
  * Formal ceremony conducted in the Residency garden before political officers, citizens, Crown Prince, and Yogyakarta nobility.
* **5 December 1808:**
  * J.W. Meevis dispatched to Surabaya with government seal on Daendels' orders.
  * Military deserter captured at Cuju sent to the garrison hospital.
* **9 December 1808 (Solo River Transport Logistics):**
  * Wiese confers with Daendels on Solo River ferries loading salt for East Java.
  * Wood, rice, and coal loaded as far downriver as Ragudan (Tuban district). Patih Danureja II orders Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III to coordinate river shipments from Madiun.
  * Acting Resident Van Braam in Surakarta instructs tax farmers that **no river tolls are to be levied on coal, paddy, or rice**.
