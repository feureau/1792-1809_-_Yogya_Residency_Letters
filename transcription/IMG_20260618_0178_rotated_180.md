### [13 December 1805: Engelhard Warning on British Military Build-up at Penang]

* **Date & Correspondents:** `Eng. to Waterloo, 13 Dec. 1805`
* **Intelligence on British Fleet:**
  * `Report of Javanese [corrected from Malays] from Trênggano who had arrived in Semarang of British military build-up in Pulo Pinang.`
  * `30 men of war / 40 Trading vessels (many destined for China).`
* **Directives to Residencies:**
  * `Asks Wat. to encourage HB II / PB IV / Princes to keep watch on N. Coast under pretext of preventing piracy.`
  * `Asks how practicable landing on N. Coast + ability to march to courts.`
  * `How far could Central Javanese rulers be trusted.`

### [12 December 1805: Deposition (*Relaas*) of Sharif Husin from Trengganu]

* **Deponent & Background:**
  * `Relaas of Sarif Husin (Arab) from Trênggano / 12 Dec. 1805 / written in Jav.`
  * `Lives in Buginese kampong in Semarang + Juragan Nadan.`
  * `Goes on chaloup to Trênggano to trade.`
  * `After 3 months in Trênggano returns + Nadan to Semarang; after 28 days at sea arrives back 12 Dec.`
* **Pirate Encounters & Malay Politics:**
  * `At Karimon Java on outward journey meets 32 pirate boats (Ilanong Prajahaps).`
  * `Dispute between Raja Klantan & brother-in-law King of Trênggano; Raja Kelantan gets upper hand.`
* **British Trade & Javanese Sailors:**
  * `2 English Trading ships to Trenggano to get load of pepper.`
  * `Pepper now at 12 R. maaten per pikul.`
  * `Many Javanese from Solo on British ships as sailors.`
* **Slave Trade & British Service:**
  * `Taken as prisoners by Ilanong sea pirates from Banyumas / W. Cirebon.`
  * `Sold at Malakka for 16 Ronde maaten to English captains.`
  * `Treated v. well by " " given upkeep / clothes / with money .: no wish to return to Java.`
* **Marginal Red Ink Note:**
  > `cf Arab priest 16 Dec. 1805 plus Yogya join Brit.`  

* **Riau Trade & British Invasion Rumors:**
  * `Raja of Trênggano = idea of sending ships to Riouw to trade + under Tuan Muda (Yang Dipertuan Muda) take on cargo of ginger & sell it in Semarang.`
  * `Tuan Muda allows him to sail with merchandise.`
  * `But trip cancelled at last moment because news that British about to make an attack on Java.` *(marked with double red vertical bars)*
