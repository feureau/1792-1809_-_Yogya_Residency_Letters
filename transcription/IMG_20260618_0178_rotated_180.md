### Archive Entries: December 1805 (British Penang Build-Up & Trengganu Intelligence)

* **13 December 1805 (Engelhard to Waterloo):**
  * Report from Trengganu Malays arriving in Semarang detailing major British naval build-up at Penang (*Pulo Pinang*): 30 warships, 40 merchantmen (many bound for China).
  * Engelhard instructs Waterloo to urge HB II to maintain coastal watch under pretext of anti-piracy patrols. Inquires into feasibility of British landing on North Coast and dependability of Central Javanese rulers.
* **12 December 1805 (Statement of Narip Husein - Arab Merchant):**
  * Resides in Bugis Kampong in Semarang; trades with Trengganu. Encountered 32 *Elanong* pirate proas off Karimunjawa.
  * Reports conflict between Raja of Kelantan and his brother-in-law the King of Trengganu.
  * 2 British merchant ships loading pepper at Trengganu (@ 12 R. maaten per pikul). Many Surakarta Javanese serving as sailors aboard British ships.
  * **16 December 1805 Note:** **16 Arab/Priyayi subjects from Solo/Yogya join British forces.** Captured by *Elanong* pirates off Banyumas/Cirebon and sold in Malacca to British captains for 16 Ronde maaten. Well treated with pay and clothes; express no desire to return to Java.
  * Raja of Trengganu cancels planned trade voyage to Riau/Semarang due to news of impending British attack on Java.
