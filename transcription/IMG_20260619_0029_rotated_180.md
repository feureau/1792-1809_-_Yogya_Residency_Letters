### Translation of the Gold Farm Charter (*Piagem*) Granted by HB II to R.T. Reksanegara (Page 1)

* **Grant & Scope:**
  * Sultan Hamengkubuwana II issues a sealed charter (*piagem*) conferring upon **Kyai Tumenggung Reksanegara** the tax-farm of all royal domains: tollgates (*bandhars/rangkahs/salarangs*), Kedu tobacco warehouses (*gedhong tembakau*), cattle slaughterhouses, South Coast salt works, and birds' nest cliffs across Yogyakarta and Mancanagara provinces.
  * **Annual Lease Rate:** Fixed at **60,000 silver Ronde Reals per annum** (payable in copper duits @ 1 Real = 28 dubbeltjes of 10 duits each).
  * **Payment Schedule:** Paid in two equal installments of 30,000 R.R. each at the festivals of *Mulud* and *Puasa*.
* **Exceptions:**
  * Capital pasars, Tanjung Tirto, Tegalsari (with tollgate), priestly *pasar perdikan*, Mancanagara river tollgates along the Bengawan Solo, and Mancanagara birds' nest rocks are strictly excluded.
  * Birds' nest harvest valued separately @ 12,000 R.R. per half-year.
  * Sultan retains right to transport 110 *sapi pengluput* draught beasts 3 times yearly tax-free.
* **Annual Ceremonial Tribute (*Pangirings*):**
  * Tribute items: *Pathoolen* textiles (10 pairs *Jelamprang* @ 25 R.R.; 10 pairs *Kanigara* @ 15 R.R.), 10 lbs gold thread (12-thread/18-real quality @ 24 Reals), 20 catties silk (9 green, 9 red, 2 rose @ 8 Reals/catty). Total tribute value: 1,230 R.R. (including 430 R.R. half-yearly for sugar/tea/confituren, 10/1000 *patompos* fee, 30 R.R. for *serat papak*, 31 R.R. for goldsmith accountant).
* **Tollgate Exemptions & Protections:**
  * Subjects carrying non-merchandise loads, royal family, and *priyayis* carrying estate rice/padi pass through tollgates tax-free.
  * Tollgate keepers strictly forbidden from taxing small quantities of cloth or tobacco carried as presents for appanage holders; violators subject to royal punishment.
