### 1793 Loano / Pacitan Report 2 (Van IJsseldijk)

*(Dj. Br. 45: W. van Ysseldijk, 'Eerbiedig Bericht')*

#### Pepper Acreage & Yield Targets:

- **700 cacahs of pepper lands** — to be increased by **100 cacahs** by new agreement.
- + Labourers from Winong (where no pepper planted) to be used on estates.
- V.O.C. could now begin to expect yearly **5,000 pikuls of pepper** promised by HB I.

---

#### Indigo Factory Negotiations (Plaosan & Merapi):

- *Cf. Original Act of Verbintenis of Danureja I (DRI).*
- **Plaosan & Adjacent lands / Desas**:
  - Promise of HB II: V.O.C. to be allowed 100 cacahs for setting up Indigo Factory near Merapi.
  - But Indigo requires huge investment of labour (especially gathering wood to boil/process indigo); doubtful if 100 cacahs sufficient.
  - Very difficult to get *bupatis* to surrender people: all hands needed to meet *pajak* and corvée services of HB II.
  - Proposed site near Merapi turned down due to lack of labour and timber.
- **Winong Alternative**:
  - Area of Winong far more suitable: mountain streams and woods.
  - Crop rotation: Rice in wet season / Indigo in dry season.
  - HB II prepared to surrender people on 100 cacahs in Winong, with permission to tend ricefields during the wet season.

---

#### Production in Pacitan & Labor Organization:

- 700 cacahs (168 jungs) in Loano / Winong etc.
- In Pacitan: not counting 200 cacahs in Pacitan (*+ 200 from Nelo*).
  - Very good appearance; densely bushed.
  - Indigo / pepper could be produced together: Pepper planted in wet monsoon; during harvest fewer hands needed and could work indigo.
- Outside Pacitan: 87 desas (119 jungs) suitable for pepper; **2,245 people** could be employed on pepper estates.
- 2 *Demangs* increased to 4 by Sultan to organize labour force.
- Need to distribute more buffaloes to inhabitants of Loano / Malarang:
  - HB II suggests ½ provided by Resident, ½ by Patih, so that Sultanate retains a semblance of control over the area.

#### Land of Pacitan:

- **Extremely suited for cultivation of pepper.** Currently growing wild — similar to plots in **Banten**.
