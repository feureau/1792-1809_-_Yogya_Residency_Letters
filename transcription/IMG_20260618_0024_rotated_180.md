### 1793 Loano / Pacitan Report 2: Production Targets & Factory Proposals

* **Reference:** P.J. Br. 45 W. Van IJsseldijk *"Eerbiedige Bericht"*
* **Pepper Targets:** 700 cacahs of pepper lands to be increased by 100 cacahs under new agreement. Labourers from Winong (where no pepper planted) to be utilized on estates.
* **Production Expectations:** V.O.C. expects 5,000 pikuls of pepper yearly as promised by HB I (original Act of Verbinteinis of DRI).
* **Indigo Factory Proposals:**
  * HB II promises V.O.C. 100 cacahs for Indigo Factory in Pacitan near Merapi. However, indigo requires massive investment of labour and firewood; 100 cacahs deemed insufficient.
  * Difficult to get *Bupatis* to surrender people because all hands are needed for *pajak* / corvée services of HB II.
  * Proposed Merapi site rejected due to lack of labor and wood.
  * Area of Winong selected as more suitable (mountain streams/woods; rice in wet season, indigo in dry season). MB II agrees to surrender 100 cacahs in Winong.
* **Pacitan Cultivation:** 700 cacahs (168 jungs) in Pacitan (excluding 200 cacahs in Pacitan / 200 from Solo).
* **Potential:** 87 desas, 119 jungs outside Pacitan suitable for pepper; 2,245 people available. Sultan increases Demangs from 2 to 4 to organize labor.
* **Buffaloes & Expense Sharing:** HB II suggests buffaloes be provided 1/2 by Resident, 1/2 by Patih.
* **Land Potential:** **Land of Pacitan** extremely suited for pepper (currently growing wild, similar to Banten).
