| **October 1796** | ### [Header]<br><br>4. MvO Van Overstraten<br><br>### [Transcription]<br><br>#### 1. Succession to the Yogyakarta Grand Vizierate<br><br>- Court rumor: HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] intentionally dismissed **Raden Tumenggung Natayuda** from high administrative offices so that he would **not be in the running to succeed as Patih** upon the death of Danureja I.  <br>- **Despotic nature of HB II's rule:**  <br>  A new Patih would have no alternative but to bow completely to the Sultan's personal dictates, rather than fulfilling the traditional role of acting as an independent intermediary / bridge with the Dutch.<br><br>- **Tumenggung Mandura:** Very obliging and courteous Javanese, but possesses little administrative talent.<br><br>#### 2. Kartasura Conference & Resident Van Nieuwkerken<br><br>- Meeting between Van Overstraten and PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] at **Kartasura**:  <br>  PB IV initially attempted to repudiate his earlier promise to allocate lands (4,000 cacahs) to Prangwedana.<br><br>- **Evaluation of Resident Van Nieuwkerken:**  <br>  Possesses very sound judgment, deep local knowledge, and an exceptional talent for winning the trust and confidence of the Javanese elites.<br><br>#### 3. The Rift between HB II and Resident Van IJsseldijk<br><br>- In 1792, HB II had specifically requested that **Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk** remain at court as first Resident.  <br>- However, Van IJsseldijk subsequently fell into severe disfavour, leading HB II to formally demand his replacement.  <br>- *[Red marginal note:]* **HB II – Van IJ Quarrel.**  <br>  - Van Overstraten corresponded with HB II regarding Van IJsseldijk.  <br>  - HB II replied very abruptly that he insisted on receiving a new Resident by **August 1796**, though he might permit Van IJsseldijk to stay another year if necessary.  <br>  - Van Overstraten replied that Van IJsseldijk must remain for at least another year.  <br>- **Geopolitical constraint:**  <br>  Because of the acute danger of a foreign [British / French] invasion, Van Overstraten dare not adopt too firm or confrontational a stance against HB II over the question of replacing the Resident. |
