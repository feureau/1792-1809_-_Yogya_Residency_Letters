### Yogya Residency Correspondence (June–September 1793)

| Date / Correspondence | Content / Summary |
| :--- | :--- |
| **14 June 1793**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten | Report from Overveen: Indigo & pepper cultivation expected to flourish after initial difficulties surmounted.<br>Resident confers with HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] concerning repairs and strengthening of **Fort at Boyolali**; quarrying stone, burning lime, and felling timber will require substantial time. |
| **27 July [1793]**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Boedelmeester (*Semarang*) | Estate matters regarding the deceased Head Chinese *Bandar* in Kedu, Gan Kangsing (*cf. Tan Jin Sing*). |
| **3 Sept. 1793**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten | Sultan's *mantris* and the bodyguard (*lijfwacht*) commander Willem Verhage travel to Semarang to collect the annual **Strandgeld** (coastal lease annuity) payment. |
| **3 Sept. 1793**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten | **Rise of Mertanegara (Danureja II / DR II)**:<br>Eldest son of DRI, Aria Mandura, replaced as Court (*Dalem*) Regent by **R.T. Mērtanēgara** (grandson of DRI, previously Outer Regent); each allocated 500 cacahs.<br>Younger son of DRI, Citradiwiria (*R.T. Danukusuma*), granted 100 cacahs.<br>**Border Dispute**: Kabupaten Bangil vs Japan (Government territory); Second Resident **Jan Meindert van Bronckhorst** sent to the Oosthoek to mediate during the dry season. |
| **28 Sept. [1793]**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten | **Troop Levies for Batavia** (*War with Revolutionary France*):<br>Demand for reinforcement auxiliary troops for Batavia due to the outbreak of war between France and the Dutch Republic. HB II expresses ready willingness to assist.<br>Second Resident returns from settling the Bangil–Japan frontier; Prangwedana II orders border violations to cease.<br>30 European infantrymen from the Yogya garrison dispatched to reinforce coastal garrisons on the North Coast (departed 23 Sept. 1793). |
