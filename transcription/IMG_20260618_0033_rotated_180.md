## Content

### Archive Entries: June – September 1793 (Boyolali Fort, Patih Succession, Border Disputes)

* **14 June 1793 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten):**
  * Indigo and pepper cultivation progress; operational difficulties surmounted.
  * Discussions with HB II regarding improvements to the Fort at Boyolali (brick baking, lime burning, and timber cutting underway).
* **27 July 1793 (Van IJsseldijk -> Boedelmeester Semarang):**
  * Deceased Head Bandhar in Kedu (Gan Kangorig / father of Tan Tjin Sing).
* **3 September 1793 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten):**
  * Sultan's *Mantris* / Kliwon Willem Wiese sent to Semarang to collect *strandgeld* payments.
* **3 September 1793 (Ibid.):**
  * **Patih Succession:** Oldest son of DRI, Aria Mataram, appointed as *Dalem Regent* for Mertanegara as **Danureja II (DR II)**. R.T. Mangunnegara (grandson of DRI) earlier Outer Regent (500 cacahs each). Younger son of DRI, Citradiwiria, granted 100 cacahs. Red ink: **R.T. Danukusuma.**
  * **Boundary Dispute:** Kabupaten Bangil -> Japan (Gov. Coast). 2nd Resident Jan Meindert van Bronckhorst sent to mediate.
* **28 September 1793 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten):**
  * Requisition for reinforcement troops for Batavia in view of war between France and the Dutch Republic/Holland. HB II agrees to comply.
  * Boundary dispute settled; Prangwedana II ordered to ensure no further disputes occur.
  * 30 infantry from Yogya garrison dispatched to North Coast to reinforce garrisons (depart 23 Sept. 1793).
