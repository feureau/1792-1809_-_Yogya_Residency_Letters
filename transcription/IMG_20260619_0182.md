### [Transcription]

**3.**

*Afternoon (1 Aug.):* CP / HB II to Residency house:  
In Throne Room — Long Talk of Daendels + HB II over "cultures" in Vh.  
Long Talk HB II / CP / & Daendels in Residency house:  
about Duties of HB II  
CP pays close attention —  
*10.00* D Takes Leave of Sultan: after supper.  
Embraces Daendels v. warmly.  
Promises to Remain in Harmony + Dutch Govnmt.

*2 August 1809:*  
*4.30 a.m.* Daendels leaves Yogya.  
DR II / Danukusuma ride out + HB II -> Tollgate: Kanklaka.  
Promise him a good journey.  
-> Bojong then Sêcang.  
.. Returns via Magelang -> Semarang.  
Wiese / [Bracket:]  

- R.T. Natadiningrat (Outer Bupati)  
- R.T. Sindunēgara (Inner Bupati)  
} accompany D -> Sêcang.  
*11.00 a.m.* Remain the Night.  
Wiese takes leave of Daendels.  

+ Natadiningrat, Sindunēgara return to Yogya.
