### Diary of Marshal Daendels' State Visit to Yogyakarta: Part 4 (1–2 August 1809 Departure)

* **1 August 1809 (Afternoon Conferences):**
  * HB II and Crown Prince visit Residency House. Long conference held in Throne Room between Daendels and HB II regarding cash crop cultivation (*cultures*) in the Vorstenlanden.
  * Extended instructions delivered to Crown Prince regarding royal administrative duties.
  * **10:00 p.m. Final Farewell:** Daendels takes leave of Sultan HB II following supper; **HB II embraces Daendels warmly and pledges perpetual harmony with the Dutch government**.
* **2 August 1809 (Departure from Yogyakarta @ 4:30 a.m.):**
  * Daendels departs Yogyakarta @ 4:30 a.m.
  * Danureja II and Danukusuma escort Daendels to Kemlaka tollgate, wishing him a safe journey to Bojong, Secang, Magelang, and Semarang.
  * Minister Wiese, R.T. Natadiningrat (Outer Regent), and R.T. Sindunegara (Inner Regent) accompany Daendels to Secang, remaining overnight before returning to Yogyakarta.
