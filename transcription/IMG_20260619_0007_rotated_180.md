HB II often lays Heavy Fines on wives / children / Favourites

Always difficulties negotiating & HB II over money

**DR II — to most junior Bupati** afraid to broach topics relating to Finance & HB II for fear of Falling into Disgrace & Being Fined heavily

Wat always Deals in Writing & DR II / Nayakas.  
and if DR II / Nayakas blamed by HB II can always take refuge behind Wat.'s letter.

Sometimes DR II = declares himself afraid to take message to HB II.
