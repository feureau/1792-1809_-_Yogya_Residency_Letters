### Tax Farm Charters, Fines, and Ministerial Inaction (1806)

* **Sealed Gold Charter (*Piagem*):** Sealed by Sultan and regarded as holy by subjects. Reksanegara technically in charge, though 1st Resident organizes tax farm. Danureja I regulates disputes, saving Sultan's dignity through gold charter terms.
* **Tax Farm Renewals:**
  * 1780: Raised by 10,000 (see BL Add. 12341 f. 308v-310v).
  * 1796: Raised by 10,000.
  * 1806: Tax maintained on old footing (56,000 R.R.).
* **Fines & Arbitrary Rule:** HB II frequently imposes heavy fines on wives, children, and favorites; if unpaid within short deadlines, fines double or gold/silver ornaments demanded. No courtier dares gainsay him for fear of doubled fines.
* **Ministerial Fear:** Danureja I (most junior Bupati) fears broaching financial topics to HB II lest he fall into disgrace and face heavy fines. Resident Waterloo conducts business in writing with DRI and *Nayakas*, allowing ministers to shelter behind official letters. Last renewal required months of negotiation and a 3,000 R.R. payment from Waterloo to HB II before the new *piagem* was issued.
