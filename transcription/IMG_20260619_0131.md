### Archive Entries: July – August 1808 (Bander Tax Office Raid & Forest Wilderness Investigation)

* Reference: **Pj. Br. 23 (24 July – 11 Aug. 1808)**
* **Investigation of Bander Attack (27 May 1808):**
  * 250 Javanese raiders from Mancanagara attack and burn 7 Chinese houses and the tax office (*Bandar*) at Bander (Oosthoek, Surabaya area), fleeing into the Kantawana wilderness in Yogyakarta territory.
  * Joint investigation commission dispatched under Corporal Pieter Gulin and Royal *Mantris* Aryagaya and Natawikrama.
  * *19 July Report:* Commission convenes a public hearing of 500 residents (including Chinese) at Bander. Local residents refuse to cooperate; 1 Surakarta robber shot dead.
  * **Forest Wilderness Description:** Investigators pursue raiders through uninhabited dense jungle for 2 days amidst tiger-infested terrain split by a major river.
* **Klaten Criminal Accord Proposed:** Engelhard urges HB II to establish a joint inter-court conference at Klaten, leading to the September 1808 Treaty on Criminals.
