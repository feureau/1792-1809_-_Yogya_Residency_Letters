| **1792 to 1809** | ### Text & Marginal Annotations<br><br>[The Faction of Demang Rangga Jajar (Serang / Kadilangu):]<br><br>- The elderly leader had recently died.<br>- **Sons and Grandsons of Demang Rangga Jajar:**<br>  - **Sons:** Eldest = Demang Rangga; Raden Purbakusuma; Raden Anrangkusuma; Raden Sasradipura.<br>  - **Grandsons:** Raden Nastrawijaya; Raden Sumadirana.<br>  - Mostly dwell in the wooden villages (*desas kayu*) around Surakarta.<br>  - **Insurrectionary threat:** Assembling up to 1,000 men to attack Dutch V.O.C. outposts from the landward side if the British launch an attack from the sea.<br>  - Governor Van Overstraten wanted the Radèns captured and imprisoned, following discussions with the Demak Bupati, Patih, and Nayakas at Surakarta.<br>  - However, the authorities succeeded in persuading them to come peacefully to Surakarta of their own free will; Raden Rangga was given an official court position in Surakarta.<br><br>[Regent of Tegal & Bali Exile (f. 160):]<br><br>- **Bp. of Tegal Raden Maji flees to Bali** [underlined in red].<br>  - Later apprehended and sentenced to exile at the Cape of Good Hope, where he died.<br>  - Left an outstanding debt to Gusti of Karangasem (Bali) amounting to **2,000 Sp.D.**<br>  - The wife of the Bupati of Tegal (sister of the Panembahan of Madura) is currently still residing in Bali [red margin bar].<br><br>[Agriculture & Cotton Production (f. 196):]<br><br>- Very poor harvests of *kapas* (raw cotton) in Java.<br>- Cannot withstand foreign competition; best cotton seeds can be obtained from Coromandel and Makassar, where cotton flourishes exceedingly well in Java.<br><br>[Textiles for Company Slaves (f. 197):]<br><br>- Javanese cloth woven and supplied for Company slaves in Batavia.<br>- Contracted and made up by the Kapitan Cina in Yogyakarta: **350 *korgē* (corge = 20 pieces) per annum @ 16.32 Sp.D.**<br>- By 1788, the volume was too large for Yogyakarta alone, so half the quota was transferred to Surakarta. |
