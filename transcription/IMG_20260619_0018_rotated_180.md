### Van den Berg Direct Audiences & Surakarta Court Executions

* **Direct Audiences:** Resident Van den Berg communicates directly with PB IV rather than relying on Regents/Patih (unlike Van Nijvenheim's Monday/Thursday *Dienst dagen* conferences).
* **Execution of Purbanegara & Cakra-winata:**
  * **R.T. Purbanegara** (favored by Palm) and **R.T. Cakra-winata** (favored by Van Reede) both executed by PB IV.
  * **Purbanegara:** Imprisoned without food or water in a **bamboo cage on the Paseban**, then banished to the Ayah forest where he died.
  * **Cakra-winata:** Arrested in broad daylight at *Nrimenganti* after PB IV conversed with him pleasantly until midnight and granted him new lands. Banished toward Ponorogo and **killed on the road by PB IV's executioner (*Martalutut*)**; Patih Danuningrat (then Kliwon) assisted at execution.
* **Surakarta Character:** Surakarta Javanese noted for slyness (*listig van karakter*), but display greater cordiality, refinement, and politeness than Yogyakartans. Highly materialistic (expecting double/triple returns on gifts; rarely returning loans).
* **Political Assessment:** Cakra-winata and Mangkupraja praised as great Javanese politicians who knew too much about V.O.C. internal affairs (telling PB IV that the British were as powerful as the Dutch).
