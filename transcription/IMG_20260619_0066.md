### N. Engelhard MvO (1808): Surakarta Marriage Politics & Historical Retrospect

* Reference: **MVO N. Engelhard to Daendels (14 May 1808) / Solo Br. 127**
* **PB IV Sisters & Pasisir Marriages:**
  * Two sisters of PB IV (legitimate R.Ay. Nekartaji and illegitimate R.Ay. Nusia) request title of Ratu.
  * R.Ay. Nekartaji married Bupati of Kudus. R.Ay. Nusia married Bupati of Pamekasan (husband died 1804); re-married Bupati of Kudus, but second husband also dies.
  * Pasisir Bupatis avoid court marriages (costly, demanding, and princess wives are locked in the *Dalem*).
* **Jan Greuve Memorie (31 Aug. 1791 Retrospect):**
  * In 1791, PB IV had only one son born of Lodwijk (born 1785; future Pakubuwana V), whom he deeply loved.
  * PB IV's brothers P. Mangkubumi and P. Buminata noted for lively dispositions.
  * V.O.C. Forced PB IV to dismiss 5 religious advisors; PB IV arranged Pasisir marriages for them.
  * PB IV's eldest daughter (Nyi Full sister of Pamekasan Bupati) married Bupati of Pamekasan, but died suddenly in 1785.
