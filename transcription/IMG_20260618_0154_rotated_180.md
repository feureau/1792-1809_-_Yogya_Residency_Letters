### Archive Entries: November – December 1803 (Banyumas Plot Summary & Wanacatur Meeting)

* **11 December 1803 (Grobogan Teak & Taxes):** Expiry of Grobogan area tax-farm term; negotiations required with Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III for new lease terms. V.O.C. considers leasing Grobogan teak forests.
* **28 November 1803 (Banyumas Plot Investigation):** Circular letter from Waterloo and Van den Berg confirms Chinese plot in Banyumas is linked to Cirebon/Tegal groups. Conspirators had requested troops from Cirebon and Solo. Confirmed as an isolated *kongsi* brotherhood among Cirebon Chinese without broader political danger.
* **26 December 1803 (Loano Inspection):** Waterloo inspects Loano pepper estates; finds plantations in a **deplorable state**.
* **31 December 1803 (Wanacatur Meeting):**
  * Waterloo meets Sultan HB II at **Wanacatur** to deliver monetary allowance to Crown Prince.
  * HB II attempts to appropriate the funds for himself; Waterloo speaks out boldly and delivers money directly to Crown Prince in person.
  * Discussions held with HB II regarding anti-bandit operations across the *Vorstenlanden*. HB II presents horses to Waterloo.
