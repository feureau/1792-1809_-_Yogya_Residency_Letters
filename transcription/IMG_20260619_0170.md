### Archive Entries: March – April 1809 (Raden Rongga Arrival & Teak Monopoly Reforms)

* **27 March – 2 April 1809:** Wiese refuses to place Parawangan *Bandar* under Chinese Che Hockong; messages sent to Raden Rongga III (Madiun) and Sasrakusuma (Grobogan). Envoys arrive from Eastern Mancanagara.
* **11 April 1809 (Arrival of Raden Rongga):** **Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III arrives in Yogyakarta for Puasa/Mulud**, bringing court labor forces for HB II.
* **14 April 1809:** *Piagem* dispute between Grobogan Regent Sasrakusuma and Chinese tax farmers Tokong and Uing over Grobogan tollgates.
* **17 April 1809 (Dutch Teak Monopoly Overhaul):**
  * **Government takes the teak timber trade out of the hands of local Regents in Blora and Eastern Mancanagara.**
  * Private Chinese and native cutters hired directly by government forestry officers, stripping Regents of timber revenues.
  * **Hardship on Common Peasants:** Severe impact on local populations where lack of sawah forces commoners to depend on timber collection for livelihood.
  * **HB II Protests:** HB II demands an end to unauthorized timber cutting in his teak forests over the past 6 months (*"Die Sultan aan die zeker en het idee verwijd dat wanneer door den lande in zijne bosschen niet meer gekapt word, der zulks verlichting aan zijne onderdanen zal toebrengen"*).
