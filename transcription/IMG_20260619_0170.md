### [Transcription]

*27 March 1809*  
*Wiese to Van Braam*  
Refusal to put Bandhar of Parawangan under orders of Chinese Jye Hockong.  
.. message sent to R. Rongga (Madiun) & Sasrakusuma (Grobogan)  
R. Rongga expected in Yogya.

*2 April 1809*  
*Wiese to Van Braam*  
People arrive from E. MN

*11 April 1809*  
*Wiese to Van Braam*  
R. Rongga arrives from MN for ~~PUASA~~ MULUD festival:  

+ work force for Sultan:

*14 April 1809*  
Question of PIAGEMT for Chinese Tax-Farmers of GUSTI: in GROBOGAN area BETWEEN SASRAKUSUMA & Chinese TO KONG SING.

*17 April 1809*  
*Wiese to Daendels*  

[Red ink: *Teak from E. MN IN BLORA*]

Question of Teak wood trade which Govnmt takes out of the hand of Local Regents  
Chinese / other private persons -> to cut wood for Govnmt  
Severe for Regents but particularly severe for THE COMMON MAN:  
Because no rice cultivation & forced to make their living from collecting wood.  
But HB II wishes to end PINCHING OF WOOD in his Teak Forests: (for last 6 months)  
Regents to be informed that Teak forests would be hired from the Regents by the Govnmt  
But common men — allowed to sell wood in small quantities -> Govnmt  
Important to keep ->  
these DISTRICTS free of evil people — many come to that AREA from PASISIR to cut down wood

[Left margin, in red vertical box]:  
*"Die Sultan kon die zeeken en het idee verwiert dat wanneer door den lander in zynne bosschen niet meer gekapt word, dat zulkene verlichting aan zynne onderdanen zal toebrengen"*
