### 1801 P.J. Brieven: British Blockade, Banditry, and Natakusuma Strategy

* Reference: **P.J. Br. no. 37 (1801)**
* **24 September 1799:** 210 corges of Javanese cloth sent to Semarang by L. Leberfeld (80 corges valued at 2,640 guilders).
* **HB II Aid Offer (21 Nov. 1801):** HB II demonstrates willingness to assist Dutch during British naval blockade of Batavia.
* **Eastern Banditry:** Resident requests HB II issue orders to Rangga Prangwedana to act firmly against bandits in Eastern Mangkunagaran; Kyai Adipati Purwadiningrat of Magetan asked to cooperate.
* **Natakusuma Ambition & Biding Time:**
  * No open discord between HB II and Natakusuma. Though rarely invited to court, Natakusuma attends on his own initiative when letters arrive from Engelhard, and for *slametans*.
  * Shrewd prince who avoids provoking HB II.
  * HB II frequently criticizes V.O.C. and cares little for close alliance. Natakusuma bides his time, hoping the Dutch will select him as a superior ally (drawing parallel to Pangeran Puger being chosen over Amangkurat II in Semarang).
  * Extortions of HB II felt severely by Natakusuma and other *Bupatis*.
* **British Ships:** Van Overstraten falls ill; British ships reappear along Java coast.
