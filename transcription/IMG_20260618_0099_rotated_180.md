### [21 Nov 1801]

* **van den Berg to N. Engelhard, 21 Nov. 1801**
  * HB II shown willing in past year when British strongly blockading Batavia to come to their aid.
  * after watching Tourney on front Pasebaan go into Kraton.
  * HB II gives very abrupt answers, succinct, usually behaves in that way to courtiers as well.
  * Asks HB II to give order to Rangga PWD to act firmly v. Robbers / Bandits in Eastern MN.
  * Kyai Adip. Purwadiningrat of Magetan asked to co-operate.
  * *[Left margin annotation in red ink:]* **Robbers Bandits in E. MN**
  * no further news about Disharmony HB II – Natakusuma.
  * *[Double vertical red line in margin]* although NK rarely invited to court – often goes in on own initiative when letters from Engelhard & for Slametans.
  * If not provoked to extreme this shrewd prince would attempt nothing v. HB II.
  * <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">HB II (v. different from father) is often criticising V.O.C. caring little for close Relations.</ins>
  * **NK:** Biding his time – Hoping to be chosen by V.O.C. as a better ally.
  * *[Asterisk and double vertical red line in margin]* **Just as P. Puger had been chosen by Dutch in Semarang instead of Mangkurat III.**
  * Extortions of HB II felt by NK as well as other Bupatis.
  * Difficulties of Bupatis / courtiers in speaking to HB II.
  * amuses himself in private + wives = Panjis young nobles.
  * van den B only able to carry on contacts in deepest secrecy + various nobles / Bupatis.
  * But would not view any attempts by disgruntled princes to unseat HB II with favour.
  * van Overstraten ill – English appear again along coast of Java. ships.
