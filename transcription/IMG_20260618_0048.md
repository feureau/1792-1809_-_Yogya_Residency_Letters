British Capture of Ceylon & The Amangkurat Prophecy<br><br>- **British capture Ceylon:**  <br>  Javanese exiles residing there include two scions of the Kartasura court:<br><br>- **Pangeran Adipati Amangkurat**  <br>  - **Mas Ambiya**  <br>- **Fear in Sala:**  <br>  An old prophecy in Java foretells that one of the **Amangkurat family** would rule again one day in Java. |
