### Archive Entries: August 1808 (Purwadipura Dismissal & Public Execution Demands)

* Reference: **Pj. Br. 23 (24–29 Aug. 1808)**
* **21 August 1808 (Yogyakarta Hospital Site):** Musterburgh building (1/2 hour from Yogya) converted into a military hospital; empty walled plot owned by Tan Tjin Sing near Residency house converted into a temporary hospital.
* **Timber Requisitions:** Officials from Padangan, Panolan, and Madiun ordered to Yogya concerning Daendels' teak-felling orders in Eastern Mancanagara.
* **Dismissal of Purwadipura:**
  * **HB II dismisses 2nd Inner Bupati R.T. Purwadipura**, stripping him of his official *Bawat* umbrella and staff for failing to promptly deliver Engelhard's request to visit the Kraton with Col. Gerlach.
  * Troops transferred to R.T. Nindunagara and R.T. Prawirawirata.
* **29 August 1808 (Public Execution Demand by Daendels):**
  * Two villagers (*prajurits* of PB IV) who wounded Dutch Jagers sentenced to run the gauntlet of 400 soldiers (*rotting-slagen*) and be executed on the *Paseban* before PB IV.
  * **Daendels demands a far more severe, exemplary public execution.**
  * *Historical Note:* Stockdale notes PB IV's torture methods (e.g. Poisoning half-brothers with *opas* poison); torture and corporal mutilation abolished by Raffles in 1812.
