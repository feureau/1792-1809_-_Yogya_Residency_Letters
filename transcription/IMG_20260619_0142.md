### Reception Protocols for Subsequent Governor-General Visits: Van der Capellen (1819) & Baud (1834)

* **Buyskes Protocol (Item 13):** Engelhard ceremonially presents the fort keys to Buyskes, and Lt.-Col. De Chanteauvieux requests military orders.

#### Van der Capellen State Visit Protocol (Yogyakarta 1819):
* Assistant Resident and Patih welcome G.-G. Van der Capellen at Kalijati (near Kartasura); court princes greet him at Klaten (33-gun salute).
* Pangeran Putra greets G.-G. At Kalasan. HB IV and state coach (*Kyai Jimat*) receive G.-G. At Demangan/Bantulan amidst Hussars and royal *prajurits*.
* **Throne Room Seating (Art. 17):** G.-G. Van der Capellen and Mevrouw van der Capellen seated in the Throne Room with HB IV seated on Mrs. Van der Capellen's left.
* **Srimenganti Seating (Art. 24):** Blood princes sit on the floor on HB IV's left; Dutch officials stand on Van der Capellen's right.
* 33-shot salute fired by royal *prajurits*; *Munggang* gamelan plays at *Lemadhuhur*.

#### Baud State Visit Protocol (1834):
* Assistant Resident, Patih, and Fort Commandant greet Governor-General Baud at Prambanan (P.A. Nataproja, Serang, Numaningrat).
* Pangeran Mangkubumi and guardians of young HB V greet Baud at Nambi Logi with 24 Hussars. HB V greets Baud at Bantulan.
