"Project ceremonieel op de Receptie van den Heere Luitenant Gouverneur Generaal Buyskes aan het Hof van Z.H. den Sultan Hamengkoe Boeono Senopatti Ing Ngalogo Abdul Rachman Sahidin Panoto Gomo Kalifatolah"  
in letter Valck to Alg. Secretaris, 10 Feb. 1837.

1. Day Before arrival of Buyskes Patih to go to Klatèn. + Nayakas. Lt. Col. de Chastelenvieux to go as emissary of Res. Minister. + Cav. Capt. Mollet.
2. Princes of Court to go to Prambanan one Day Before. \*
3. On arrival of Lt.-Gov. Dutch Flag to be flown from 4 Bastions of Fort.
4. at 7 o'clock Crown Prince + Secr. of Res. to go to Jenu.
5. at 6.30 Cav. to go to Kraton to escort HB II to Dêmangan. (Compagnie Cav. te paard) Receptions G.G.
6. at 6.00 <ins>afgaande / aangaande Ministers</ins> to Ride Together. in coach (Wiese + Engelhard / Wiese takes over 4 Dec. 1808) + 6 horses to Reception at Dêmangan. / HB II also to Dêmangan in another " coach / On passing Fort 21 gun salute + 3 salvo's from handgeweeren of Garrison. / Salute Repeated by Field guns at Dêmangan immediately after HB II's arrival. By Sultan's Troops.
7. Ibid Buyskes to be greeted at P + 21 gun salute / 3 Salvos from Rifles. Afterwards Buyskes / HB II to Ride to Kraton in same coach + Engelhard / CP followed by Wiese in separate coaches.  
at Dêmangan pêsanggrahan set up where Buyskes can put on Dress uniform.  
On passing Fort 21 gun salute for Buyskes = Garrison to Be Drawn up in rg. Outside Fort.  
HB II's Troops / pikemen to form a Double Rank along the way.  
<ins>But because of returning MN Regents would not stand / Be drawn up on Road Yogya - Dêmangan</ins> // \* (causing Buyskes anger)  

10. After visiting Kraton / Buyskes to Return to Engelhard to Residency. Dutch musicians / parade of Grenadiers in honour.
11. all civilian and Officers mil. personnel + Princes / Bp. Nayakas of court to Greet Buyskes at Res.
