### Pj. Brieven No. 39: Banten Uprising, Market Lease, & Daendels Visit Ultimatum (1809)

* Reference: **Pj. Brieven No. 39 (1809)**
* **March 1809 (Troop Maneuvers & Van Reede Settlement):** Wiese urges HB II to halt troop exercises and cancel orders for Mangkunagaran Regents to mobilize. **Van Reede's 39,800 R.R. embezzlement ordered returned to Prangwedana.** Wiese requests timber for Klaten Fort.
* **24 April 1809 (Pasar Lease):** Head *Bandhar* sets market lease rate (*Pacht van Pasars*) @ **60,000 Rixdollars per annum**.
* **29 April 1809 (Banten Rebellion):** Wiese reports the Banten rebellion under Ngabehi Adant (5,000–6,000 followers); Banten Prefect Moorrees severely wounded in his carriage and retreats to Fort Speelwijk.
* **31 May 1809 (Forestry Contracts):** Forestry Inspector Von Winkelmann authorized to enter into direct teak supply contracts with Mangkunagaran Regents on HB II's lands.
* **10 June 1809 (Ceremonial Ultimatum):**
  * **DAENDELS WARNS THAT IF HB II REFUSES TO ACCEPT HIS DICTATED CEREMONIAL PROTOCOL, DAENDELS WILL CANCEL HIS VISIT TO YOGYAKARTA AND LIMIT HIS VISIT TO SURAKARTA.**
* **23 June 1809:** Daendels arrives in Semarang; HB II delays accepting the draft ceremonial.
