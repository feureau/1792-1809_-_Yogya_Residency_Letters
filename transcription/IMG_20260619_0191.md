### [Transcription]

[Red ink: **1809–10**] Dj. Brieven no. 39.

*Wiese to DR II, 1 Mar. 1809 (13 Sura 1736):*  
Asks DR II to tell HB II to stop troop manoeuvres. Otherwise would jeopardise good relations before D's visit.

*ID. to ID. 5 Mar. 1809:*  
HB II should cancel order to MN Regents to hold themselves in readiness to march on Yogya.

*Wiese to HB II, (n.d. Mar. 1809):*  
Embezzlement of Van Rheede of 39,800 R.R. on Death of MN I to be paid back to Prangwidana.

*Wiese to DR II, 14 Mar. 1809:*  
[Red arrow & underline:] <u>News of new military manoeuvres.</u>  
Although no Troops except usual detachments of HB II / CP allowed to be exercised.

*ID. to ID. 18 Mar. 1809:*  
MN Regents & work force expected from Regencies.  
Wiese asks DR II for 3rd time to see that necessary timber provided for building of Fort.

*Wiese to HB II, 4 Apr. 1809:*  
Telling of planned visit of Daendels to Yogya.

*ID. to DR II, 24 Apr. 1809:*  
Head bandhar pays 60,000 for Farm of pasar etc.  

[Red ink: *60,000 Farm of Pasar.*]

*Wiese to HB II, 29 Apr. 1809:*  
Relating new Troubles in Banten = Rebellion of Ng. Adant.  
5,-6,000 followers v. Sultan.  
Pref. Moorrees + wife, children attacked = Moorrees severely wounded in carriage. Retiring to Fort Speelwijk.

*Wiese to DR II, 9 May 1809:*  
Roads & Bridges Yogya — Sala in miserable condition.  
Difficult to get by on Horseback.

*ID. to ID. 19 May 1809:*  
Groups of 40–60 soldiers touring vicinity of Chinese kampong by night.

*ID. to HB II, 31 May 1809:*  
Insp. Gen. of Forests, Von Winckelmann to be allowed to enter into separate contracts + MN Regents for supply of Teak from HB II's lands.

*ID. to HB II, 31 May 1809:*  
D wants to inspect some of HB II's Bodyguard in Residency during visit + Dragoons.

*ID. to HB II, 10 Jun. 1809:*  
If HB II refuses to accept ceremonial dictated by Daendels, D would not come to Yogya but limit his visit to Sala.

*Wiese to HB II / HB II to Wiese, 23 Jun. 1809:*  
Arrival of D at Semarang.

*ID. to DR II, 26 Jun. 1809:*  
Continuing lack of decisions of HB II about project ceremonial for arrival of Daendels.

*ID to ID. 1 Jul. 1809:*  
Road between Sala–Yogya still not in a good state.
