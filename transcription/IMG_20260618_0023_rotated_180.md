## Content

### Pepper Cultivation Regulations & Labor (Loano / Pacitan)

1. **Ngt. Gaspar Gilles**: European gardener appointed as *opziener* (overseer) of estates.
2. Should have 30 spans of buffalo at his disposition.
3. *Pragem* to be given by Raden Adipati.
4. Government to provide money to Sultan for rent of land.
5. Pepper to be delivered first to Yogya, then on to Semarang. But very costly in time $\rightarrow$ Resident decides to send yearly a pair of suitable Javanese to sort pepper and have it sent directly to Semarang.
6. 130–132 ponden per pikul of pepper.
7. *Demangs*: 6 jungs of ricefields each.
8. Instead of rented district of Winong / Gèntang, would ask Sultan for lands of Nelotiyang / Pekalongan near Loano + subjects to take part in laying out pepper gardens.
9. Subjects to be protected from arbitrariness of chiefs.

---

### Labor Arrangements & Land Exchanges:

- For every 5 cacahs: 1–2 men to take part in planting.
- 6 jungs of ricefields for each of the 2 Heads of Loano.
- Sultan frees subjects from corvée labour for the Crown so they can devote themselves to ricefields / pepper.
- Work in pepper plantations serves as the obligation for holding sawah.
- HB II delivers over Loano / Pacitan and is willing to facilitate delivery of pepper to Semarang.
- Willing to exchange Winong / Gèntang for Pekalongan / Nelo Tiyang.
  - Lands belong to Crown Prince / **Ratu Bèndara** (former wife of MN I); to be compensated with other fertile lands in *majegan* (domain estates) of Sultan.
  - Local population freed from standard labour services.
  - HB II, despite having issued orders regarding planting of pepper, remains doubtful about beneficial results.

---

### Political Anxieties of Court:

> *Fear of HB II / Nayakas that if pepper plantations were extended, V.O.C. influence would increase in Central Java, and the wong cilik would believe that HB II had resigned authority to the V.O.C. administration, similar to the pasisir (coastal) areas.*
