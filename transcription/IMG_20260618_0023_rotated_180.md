### 1793 Pepper & Indigo Administration Conditions (Loano / Pacitan)

* **European Supervision:** Ngt. Gropar Gilles appointed European gardener as *opziener* of estates. Should have 30 pairs of buffaloes at his disposal.
* **Royal Charter (*Piagem*):** To be given by Raden Adipati. Government to pay rent money to Sultan.
* **Transport & Delivery:** Pepper delivered first to Yogya then to Semarang. To save time, Resident decides to send yearly a pair of suitable Javanese to sort pepper harvest directly to Semarang (130–132 ponden per pikul).
* **Land Demands:** Demangs allocated 6 jungs of ricefields. Instead of rented district of Winong / Gentang, request *Nuwala* for lands of Nilo Tiyang / Pekalongan near Loano.
* **Corvée Relief:** For every 5 cacahs, 1–2 men to take part in planting (6 jungs ricefields for each of 2 heads of Loano). Sultan frees subjects from corvée labour for the crown so they can focus on pepper/sawah.
* **Exchanges & Domains:** HB II willing to exchange Winong / Gentang for Pekalongan / Nilo Tiyang. Lands belong to Crown Prince / Ratu Bindara (ex-wife of MN I) to be compensated with fertile domain lands in Magelang. Local population freed from labour services.
* **Sultan's Hesitation:** HB II doubtful about beneficial results despite orders. HB II and *Nayakas* fear that expanding pepper plantations will increase V.O.C. influence in Central Java, leading *wong cilik* to believe HB II resigned authority like in Pasisir areas.
