### Wat – Eng. | 28 Feb. 1806.

**(11) Yearly Profit of HB II from Rice product:**  
usually included in general Taxation of Sultan organized by **Rêknanêgara.**

* **8 Tollgates** control Trade in Rice in Mataram: Take 9,500 R. Realen p.a.
* **1 Tollgate & subordinate pasars** for Rice in Këdhu: Brings in yearly 2,800
* **Pajang — 3 [Tollgates]** 3,450 p.a.
* **General sum from Rice:** 15,750. p.a. out of Total of 56,000

HB II would stand to loose nearly 30,000 from Rice if declared custom free product in VL [Vorstenlanden] / & to N. Coast.  
Because Bandhars would not dare to Bid v. High for Tax farm if Rice withdrawn — most of profit made from Rice.

HB II would also loose 10,000 from MN [Mancanagara] Tax.  
.: not conceivable that would give up Rice Tax just for sake of the V.O.C.
