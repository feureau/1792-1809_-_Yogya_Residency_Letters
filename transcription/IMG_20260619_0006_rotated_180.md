### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.86: Rice Tax Farm Breakdown & Revenue Breakdown (1806)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.86 (28 Feb. 1806)**
* **Rice Tax Revenue Breakdown (Organized by Reksanegara):**
  * 8 Tollgates controlling Mataram rice trade: 9,500 Ronde Realen p.a.
  * 1 Tollgate + subordinate markets in Kedu: 2,800 R.R. p.a.
  * Pajang Tollgates (3): 3,450 R.R. p.a.
  * **Total General Rice Tax:** 15,750 R.R. p.a. out of 56,000 total tax farm.
* **Financial Risk to HB II:** If rice trade made tax-free, HB II stands to lose 30,000 R.R. because *Bandhars* would not bid high for the tax farm without rice revenues. HB II would also lose 10,000 from Mangkunagaran tax. Impossible for Sultan to yield rice taxes to V.O.C.
* **Total Revenues:**
  * Base Reksanegara silver tax: 56,000 R.R. (if paid in copper @ 70 stuivers/Real, adds 5,250 = f. 3.20–2.50).
  * Homage money (*Pangiring* paid at Puasa & Mulud): 3,356 2/3 R.R.
  * Inception tax fee (3,000 R.R. per 3-year term): 1,000 p.a.
  * **Total Calculated Revenue:** 60,356 1/3 Ronde Realen.
  * With Mangkunagaran land tax, total brings 72,000 R.R.
