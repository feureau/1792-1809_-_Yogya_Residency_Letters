### Archive Entries: February 1796 (Intrigues, Natayuda Dismissal, and Franco-Dutch Treaty)

* Reference: **Van Ov. to Alting (20 Feb. 1796 cont.) - N.O.K. 47**
* **PB IV's Intrigue:** During the swearing-in ceremony, PB IV whispers in Prangwedana's ear hoping he will side with Javanese interests rather than the V.O.C. Prangwedana replies that his loyalty depends on fair treatment by the Javanese.
* **Mangkubumi Plot Allegation:** P.A. Kusumadiningrat (in Semarang) reveals that Pangeran Mangkubumi had repeatedly urged PB IV to launch an attack on Solo/Mangkunagaran upon MN I's death, but Kusumadiningrat dissuaded him. Kusumadiningrat permitted to return to Solo in 2–3 days.
* **Natayuda Dismissal (10 Feb. 1796):** HB II notifies Van Overstraten of Natayuda's dismissal as Kliwon to Danureja I, brought about by Danureja II's hostility. V.O.C. lacks leverage to intervene.
* **European Geopolitics:** Solo and Yogya courts to be notified of Treaty of Friendship between France and Holland, with assurances that Holland will not adopt French Revolutionary principles (which had been portrayed in dark terms to native rulers).
