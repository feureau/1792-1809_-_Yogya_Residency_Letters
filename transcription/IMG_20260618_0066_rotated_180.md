[Red ink heading:]

**Apptmt. of PWD.**

**Van Ov to Alting, 20 Feb. 1796 (cont.):**  

- PB IV whispers in PWD’s ear at the time of taking the oath that he hopes PWD would take the side of the Javanese more than the V.O.C.
- PWD replies that would depend on whether the Javanese treated him well and remained on good terms with the V.O.C. — otherwise he would not do so.

---

[Red marginal bar; red text in right margin: **MB attack on dalem MN II planned.**]

- P. Aria Kusumadiningrat (in exile in Semarang) says that Pangeran Mangkubumi (MB) had approached PB IV numerous times to attack the dalem of Mangkunegaran once MN I died.
- PB IV had often discussed it with Kusumadiningrat, who had always dissuaded PB IV, saying it would lead to trouble for the Surakarta court.
- KD [Kusumadiningrat] to be allowed to go back to Sala court in 2–3 days.

---

[Yogyakarta Internal Politics:]

- HB II rules in such a capricious way that nothing is done; his subjects hate him.
- **10 Feb. 1796:** HB II informs Van Overstraten of the dismissal of Raden Tumenggung Natayuda as *kliwon* of Danureja I (DR I).
- Natayuda (NY) undone by the hatred of Tumenggung Sumadiningrat (SD) towards him.
- Van Overstraten notes that he cannot take up the affair with HB II because the V.O.C. is not powerful enough.

---

[Diplomacy / European Geopolitics:]

- Sala / Yogya to be informed of the Treaty of Friendship between France and Holland [Batavian Republic].
- **\*** *But would have to explain that Holland is not to follow French Revolutionary principles — which had been frequently painted in such black colours to the Javanese rulers.* **\***
