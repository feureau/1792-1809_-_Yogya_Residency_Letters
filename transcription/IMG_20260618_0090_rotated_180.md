### Post-1790 Court Diplomatic Policies & Character Sketches

* **Diplomatic Deadlock:** Post-1790 plan for Solo Patih to pay formal compliments in Yogya fails to materialize.
* **Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat:** Returned Mangkunagaran exile from Ceylon granted 1,000 Rixdollars allowance; V.O.C. policy maintains ban on exiled princes returning to court.
* **Patih Mangkupraja:** Well-disposed toward V.O.C.; intelligent and skillful.
* **Patih Mertanegara (Danureja II) & Natakusuma:**
  * Mertanegara is nephew of Natakusuma (Kedu dynasty). Noble-born (*"Grootsch van hart"*), but slow and indolent; judgment unsharp, but applies himself to business.
  * Strictly follows HB II's orders and avoids seeing Sultan except on Feast Days. State business routinely delayed months as HB II fears loss of prestige.
* **Danureja I & Kliwons:** DRI well-disposed to Europeans. Sumadiningrat remains bitter enemy of Natayuda, but unable to prevent Natayuda's appointment as 1st Kliwon under Danureja II.
* **Dutch Appointment Charter (*Piagem*):** Javanese translation of DRI's appointment charter left blank in archives; Resident instructed to locate old copy. Original kept in Kraton; only copy in Residency.
* **R.T. Yudanegara:** Outer Bupati of Banjarnan appointed Patih over lands granted in fief (*in leen*) to HB I by V.O.C.
