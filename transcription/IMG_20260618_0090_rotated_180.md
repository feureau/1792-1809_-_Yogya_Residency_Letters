### Text & Marginal Annotations

[Surakarta Court & Returning Exiles:]

- **Inter-court Patih visits:** The idea mooted after 1790 of having the Patih of Surakarta pay ceremonial compliments at Yogyakarta (cf. MvO Van Overstraten) came to nothing.
- **Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat:** Senior prince of the Mangkunegaran family returned from exile in Ceylon; granted an annual government allowance of **1,000 Sp.D.**
  - General V.O.C. policy is to keep exiled princes banished away from the royal courts.
- **Raden Adipati Mangkupraja:** Well-disposed towards the V.O.C.; characterized as a shrewd and intelligent Javanese.

---

[Yogyakarta Court & Characterization of Danureja II (red header: **Yogya.**):]

- **Lineage of Danureja II:**
  - **\*** *Mother of Mērtanēgara (Danureja II) = sister of Pangeran Natakusuma (from the Kedu Dynasty).* **\***
- **Character profile of Danureja II:**
  - Soft-natured, but indolent and slow; described as *"grootsch van Hart"* (proud/haughty of heart).
  - Judgement is not particularly sharp, but he applies himself diligently to administrative business.
  - Strictly follows the instructions of HB II, yet never actually sees the Sultan in private audience except on major religious feast days (*Garebegs*).
  - Many state affairs are left hanging for months on end without resolution because HB II constantly fears his absolute royal prestige might be compromised.
  - Danureja II is very well-disposed towards Europeans; his first *kliwon* is **Raden Tumenggung Natayuda**.
- **Sumadiningrat's hostility:**
  - Tumenggung Sumadiningrat is the bitter enemy of Natayuda, but was unable to prevent Natayuda’s reappointment as *kliwon* following Danureja II’s accession.
- **Investiture Piagem of Danureja II:**
  - The Dutch *piagem* (deed of appointment) for Danureja II was transmitted with the space for the Javanese translation left completely blank.
  - The Resident of Yogyakarta was instructed to locate the old archive copy of Danureja I’s appointment; however, the original Javanese text was kept locked inside the Kraton, with only a Dutch draft surviving in the Residency house.
- **Raden Tumenggung Yudanēgara:**
  - (Danureja I's son, formerly Bupati of Banyumas); appointed Patih / administrator of the coastal and overseas lands granted by the V.O.C. *"in leen"* (in fief) to HB I.
