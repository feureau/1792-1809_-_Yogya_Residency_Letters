## Content

### May 1808 Inter-Court Diplomacy & Von Winkelmann Inspection Arrival

* Reference: **AN Pj. Br. 41 (23–28 May 1808)**
* **PB IV Conciliation (24 May 1808):**
  * PB IV sends a 10–11 year old *panakawan* boy (son of Pangeran Pamekasan) to Daendels via Resident Van Braam as a mark of goodwill.
  * PB IV and Pangeran Mangkubumi send gift horses to Daendels in Semarang. PB IV promises to act with complete sincerity (*met opgerichtigheid*) toward Daendels and expresses satisfaction that HB II has agreed to submit to Daendels' authority.
* **Garrison Reorganization (25 May 1808):**
  * Colonel Von Winkelmann arrives in Yogyakarta to oversee the reorganization of Dutch infantry garrisons in Yogyakarta and Klaten.
* **Banyumas Pirates (28 May 1808):**
  * 10–12 large Surakarta-owned praus stationed at Ayah and Cilacap to support pirate operations.
* **Yogyakarta Delegation to Semarang (28 May 1808):**
  * HB II selects **Pangeran Dipakusuma**, **Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III**, and **R.T. Pringgakusuma** (Bupati of Rawa) as Yogyakarta envoys to Semarang (departing 12 June 1808).
  * **Royal Troop Inspection:** HB II schedules a general troop review (*monstering*) under the Crown Prince; Colonel Von Winkelmann authorized to observe the exercises (*ref: BL Add. MS*).
