### Tollgate Tariffs (cont.)

* **Chin. or Jav.** from Kĕbumen to Pĕkalongan or from Kĕbumen to Sĕmarang via Kĕdhu carrying kleedjes: pay 31¼ stuivers more than old tax of 40 ? stuivers.
* From Yogya to Sala carrying rice: 1 stuiver per horse.
* From <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Pedan</ins> (onderd. Beji, <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Klaten</ins>) carrying *kapas*: 1 st. per horse.
* From Yogya to Solo carrying kleedjes either on horseback or per pikul: same tax.  
  *(Annotation: corge = 1,200 avoirdupois lbs.)*

*[Red vertical bracket in left margin]*  

* **Toll of gĕdhong têmbakau** from Yogya to Solo: for each corge 3 ronde reals, thus 6 ronde reals for Solo / Yogya, now 31¼ for Yogya.
* **Tax of gĕdhong of Sibĕbĕk** (? Karangkobar / Banjarnĕgara) and **Batur**: 5 ronde reals per corge passing through Yogya / Solo, now 6 ronde reals.
