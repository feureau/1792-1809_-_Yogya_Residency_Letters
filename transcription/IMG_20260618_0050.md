## Metadata

- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI Archief van Java's Noordoostkust No. 1 / No. 68: Memorie van Overgave van P.G. van Overstraten, Part 2)
- **Date Range:** 1792-08-20 to 1796-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Governor Pieter Gerardus van Overstraten provides high-level political intelligence in his October 1796 *Memorie van Overgave* (handing-over memorandum); analyzes inter-court marriage maneuvers concerning Sunan Pakubuwana IV's illegitimate sister, Raden Ayu Sekartaji, foiling Surakarta's scheme to marry her into the Yogyakarta royal line (to the Crown Prince, later HB III) by orchestrating her marriage to the second son of the Panembahan of Sumenep under V.O.C. pressure; contrasts the despotic and unpopular rule of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II with PB IV's talent for winning popular loyalty; details HB II's family (14 sons and 30 daughters in 1796); documents the solemn recognition of the 27-year-old Crown Prince at Lêmah Dhuwur in the Yogyakarta Kraton on 20 August 1792.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Pieter Gerardus van Overstraten, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Crown Prince / Pangéran Adipati Anom), Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Raden Ayu Sekartaji, Radèn Mas Said (Mangkunagara I), Panembahan / Bupati of Sumenep.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kraton Yogyakarta (Lêmah Dhuwur), Surakarta (Sala), Sumenep (Madura), Oosthoek (Eastern Salient of Java).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Memorie van Overgave (MvO), V.O.C. marriage diplomacy, Kraton factions, Succession recognition.
