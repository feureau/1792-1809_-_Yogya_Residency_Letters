### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.86: Seed Padi, Peasant Fatalism, and Official Exactions (1806)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.86 (28 Feb. 1806)**
* **Seed Padi (*Bibit*) Supply:** During harvest failures, seed padi is often eaten; farmers replenish seed from heads or neighbors. Seed shortages uncommon, as village heads ensure reserves; these villages are the most prosperous. Amount of cultivated land steadily increasing.
* **Peasant Fatalism & Exactions:**
  * Common peasants subject to heavy extortions by *Lurahs* and *Bekels*: *"O Heaven! How is it possible that he can endure it"*.
  * **"Harimau" Fatalistic Attitude:** Javanese peasant accepts oppression through fate: *"Nadah Naya punya untung begitu!"*
  * **Hierarchy of Oppression:** Official hierarchy operates like clockwork—every official from highest to lowest oppresses those below him. If a *Bupati* takes a horse from a *Mantri*, the *Mantri* recoups his loss from the villagers.
* **Migration:** If a farmer finds it impossible to make ends meet, he flees to another area.
