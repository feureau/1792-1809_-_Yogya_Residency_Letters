**(5) If enough Bibit (seed padi) at hand & if sold to Jav. Farmer at Fair price.**

[*In left margin:* red asterisk **\*** and double vertical red rule **//**]

* During Failures of Harvest — seed padi often eaten.
* those Farmers who do eat Bibit get more from Heads / neighbours.
* Price depends on scarcity.
* Lack of seed padi not usual =
* Sometimes local Heads of villages take care that always enough seed padi — But few and far Between.  
  and these villages often most prosperous.  

* although Agriculture follows old pattern  
  amount of land cultivated constantly increasing in size.

---

**(6) If Farmer Discouraged By unfair / Heavy conditions imposed on cultivation e.g. Taxes etc.**

[*In left margin:* red asterisk **\*** and red vertical rule]

* Exactions / Extortions on common man well-known:  
  *"O Heaven! how is it possible that he can endure it"*  

* **"Narima"** attitude of Jav. peasant = Believing in Fate:  
  *"Nadah naya punya untung Begitu!"*  

* Heavy Exactions by Lurahs / Bêkêls.
* **Hierarchy of Officials works like clockwork:**  
  Every Jav. from greatest to smallest oppresses others.  
  If Bup. [Bupati] takes a Horse from a Mantri  
  Mantri recuperates loss from villagers.

---

**(7) If there are [*inserted*] Land which [*inserted*] still remain Fallow because of Lack of activity by Lurah / Bêkêl.**

* Sometimes see land uncultivated
* If Jav. Farmer finds it impossible to make ends meet would flee to another area.
