## Complete Diplomatic Transcription

### [28 February 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
28 Feb.

HB II spends 11 hours in Resident's House  

[Had never done this before to a Resident]

Very Interested in garden – wants Waterloo's help to lay out a new garden especially tree guards.  
Accepts glasses of wine.  
PROMISES Resident Help to Repair House.  
HB II asks for BIMANESE HORSES.

HB II goes out to P. Ngabèhi's Dalem in Carriage;  
everyone else including CROWN PRINCE have to WALK.

---
