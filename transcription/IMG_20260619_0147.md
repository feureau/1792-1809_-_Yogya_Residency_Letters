**TOLL-GATES AMPEL to Chinese JR KENTIEN**  
for 5,400 Sp. Reals p.a.

1. 3 year CONTRACT 1809-11  
2. Tax 2x every year: Mulud, Puasa.  
3. 864 Reals (Sp. Dollars) to be paid Immediately:  
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;11% Tax der Ploegen  
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;5% Tax der Edel Heers Geld (RESIDENT)  
**NO Tax Toll to be levied on STUFF of SULTAN / SUNAN**  
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;also on movement of horses.  
**NO new / increase toll to be levied / or new toll-posts to be put down** = on <ins>Ringgoo</ins>  
Although toll-gate could be widened.
