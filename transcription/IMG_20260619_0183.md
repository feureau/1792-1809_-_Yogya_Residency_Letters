### Archive Entries: August – September 1809 (Loano Pepper Neglect & Grobogan Rice Monopoly)

* Reference: **A.N. Yogya Letters No. 22 (Aug. – Sept. 1809)**
* **19 August 1809:** Minister Wiese inspects pepper estates in Loano and Qulan.
* **26 August 1809 (Loano Pepper Mismanagement):**
  * Wiese reports Loano pepper plantations are severely neglected. European *Opzichter* blocked from useful management by royal *Abdi Dalem*.
  * Teak wood from Jipang/Japan saved from burning for government use.
* **29 August – 3 September 1809 (King Louis Birthday Celebrations):**
  * Daendels' letters delivered to HB II with full ceremonial.
  * HB II, Crown Prince, and royal children attend King Louis Napoleon's birthday party at the Residency house (HB II stays only 1 1/2 hours due to advanced age).
* **12 September 1809 (Grobogan Rice Monopoly by Sasrakusuma):**
  * Prefect Goldbach reports **Grobogan Regent Sasrakusuma seized all rice/padi supplies at Bandar Panawangan** meant for government purchase.
  * Rice stored under poor conditions; Regent pays farmers in kind rather than cash and monopolizes rice purchases in outlying areas (buying 140–150 *coyangs* @ 25 Dutch R.D. per *coyang*).
