### [Transcription]

A.N. Letters Yogya No. 22. **1809 (Aug–Nov) - Yogya -**

*19 Aug. 1809*  
*Wiese - Daendels*  

[Red double margin line:]

BIRTHDAY of King of Holland (Louis Napoleon) to be celebrated 2 Sept.  
Wiese visits Pepper Estates in LOANO of SULTAN.  

[Red ink: *Pepper Estate Loano*]

*26 Aug. 1809*  
*Wiese - Daendels*  

[Red double margin line:]

Pepper Estates very badly RUN.  
European Officers not allowed to do anything useful but SULTAN'S ABDI DALEM.  
Not yet spoken to HB II about Teak wood.  
JIPANG / JAPAN wants to see Jati wood NOT BURNT.

*29 Aug. 1809*  
*Wiese - Daendels*  
Daendels letter to HB II handed over + usual ceremonial.

*3 Sept. 1809*  
*Wiese - Daendels*  
HB II / CP & ALL CHILDREN  
attend Party in Honour of Louis Napoleon's BIRTHDAY in Res house.  
But Sultan only stays 1/2 hour ("because of great age").

*12 Sept. 1809*  
*Wiese - Daendels*  

[Red ink: *Rice in Grobogan.*]

[Red double margin line:]

Letter from Prefect GOLDBACH — that GROBOGAN Regent SASRAKUSUMA makes himself master of all Rice / Padi supplies destined for GOVNMT.  
at BANDHAR PANAWANGAN  

[DELLANUES TO QUESTION BANDHAR: THE]

(1) Rice in GROBOGAN v. badly stored this year  
(2) Regent pays Farmer in KIND earlier in MONEY  
(3) Regent Himself buys Rice in outlying areas & anyone else allowed to buy rice too
