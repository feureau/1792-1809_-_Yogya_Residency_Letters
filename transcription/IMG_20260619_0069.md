## Content

### Archive Entries: 1808–1809 (Daendels Decrees, Prangwedana Valuables, & Ambtgeld Abolition)

* Reference: **Plakaatboek Vol. XIV & XV (1808–1809)**
* **31 January 1809 (Prangwedana Valuables Restitution):**
  * Reference to Daendels' Decree (*Besluit*) of 31 Jan. 1809 regarding the 1806 impoundment by Baron van Reede of **Np.D. 39,800 in Mangkunagara I's family valuables** as surety for Prangwedana II's good faith.
  * Valuables returned by order of Daendels on 31 Jan. 1809.
  * *Decree Citation:* Pangeran Aria Prabu Prangwedana committed *"Openbare spolye, onder den dekmantel, en met misbruik van den naam en autoriteit van het gouvernement gepleegd."* (*"Public spoliation committed under the cloak and abuse of the name and authority of the government."*)
* **1 June 1808:** Appointment of Daendels as Knight Commander (*Comm. Ridder*) of the Royal Order of Holland (*Koninklijke Orde van Holland 1808–1810*).
* **16 August 1808 (Diplomatic Protocol for 2nd Residents):**
  * Reception ceremonial for Ministerial Secretaries / 2nd Residents revised (*28 July 1808*).
  * 2nd Residents no longer required to present wine or sirih; **required to remove their hats in the presence of the Sultan and approach the Sultan bare-headed**.
* **Forced Loans (pp. 304–313):** Forced loans raised by Daendels: 6,000 Rixdollars from Van Braam (Solo); 10,000 Rixdollars from G.W. Wiese (Yogya). Total: 736,000.
* **22 January 1809 (Ambtgeld Abolition & Hospital Construction):**
  * **Ambtgeld official fees abolished in Surakarta and Yogyakarta.**
  * Revenue redirected to **build new military hospitals in Solo and Yogyakarta** and supply wine to hospitalized patients (*cf. Raffles*).
