## Content

[Top margin, in red ink:]

post Java War to Yogya

**Pajang** = rice produced there next to Solo.

**Gunung Kidul** = Beautiful Teak Forests  
[*In red ink, left margin:* **\* //**] Teak used for buildings in V.L. [Vorstenlanden] amongst them new Fort at Klaten.

**Këdhu** = v. fertile = sawahs high on flanks of Sumbing / Sundara. [*underlined in red: sawahs high on flanks of Sumbing / Sundara.*]  
a lot of water — But soil v. clayey.  
mostly Tobacco / Rice

**Bagalèn** = Mostly owned by Sultan.  
Rice / Cotton = Cotton woven by Inhabitants.

---

**Exports to N. Coast considerable:**  
Këdhu Tobacco / Rice from N. Këdhu via market at Jambu to Kendal / Kaliwungu / Jambu  
white / stripped / Batiked cloth from Yogya / Bagalèn.

---

[*Red horizontal dividing line*]

Most of Rice produced in area destined for courts // local consumption  
cost of carrying Rice to N. Coast means little exported

[*In red ink, written vertically/diagonally in left margin:*]

**Little Rice Exported — mainly Grobogan**

Besides sale cost on N. Coast less than in Yogya per pikul.  
cheapest Rice sold at 2 – 2 ¼ silver Rijks dollars per pikul. of 125 ponds.  
Garrison at Yogya supplied at 1 silver Ducatoon per pikul.

---

[*Red horizontal dividing line*]

**E. Mancanagara:**  
In general more land devoted to rice: / Easier transport of products along Solo river.  
**Grobogan (Jipang Wirasari / Magetan) :** [*underlined in red: Grobogan (Jipang Wirasari / Magetan)*]  
can send products down Solo river.  
Receive back salt.  
If Tolls should be free along Solo river would help Trade.

[*In left margin, double vertical red line:* **//**]
