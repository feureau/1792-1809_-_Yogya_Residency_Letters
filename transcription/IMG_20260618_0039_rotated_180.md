### Archive Entries: July – October 1794 (Appointments, Dismissals, and Jipang Appanages)

* Reference: **NvB 1794 (Yogya)**
* **17 July 1794 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Reede):** Joint Solo/Yogya inquiry into attack on a *prahu* on the Solo River belonging to Captain Ji Jok Liang.
* **11 July 1794 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten):** Pepper planting delayed by rainy season; indigo fabric processing ongoing.
* **28 July 1794:** Elevation of Crown Prince's second wife as Raden Ayu (*Raden Ayu Dipati Anom*). Red ink: **R. Ayu = daughter of R.T. Sasradiningrat (Bupati of Rajegwesi)**.
* **15 September 1794:** Arrival of *strandgeld* payments for HB II.
* **10 September 1794:** Affairs concerning Sultan's oldest unofficial daughter, Raden Ayu Aria Sindureja.
* **27 October 1794:** Dismissal of Outer Bupati R.T. Sasradiningrat and appointment of **Raden Rongga Prawiradirja II**. Sasradiningrat (dismissed earlier as Bupati of Wedi in 1790 by MN I) granted 1,500 cacahs in Jipang for upkeep; he is father-in-law of Danureja II and independent of Raden Rongga. Son-in-law of HB II (? Prawirasentika), Bupati of Danuwerna, granted rank of Raden Tumenggung.
