### Report on Pacitan Pepper Cultivation & Local Governance

#### Logistical & Social Assessment:

1. **Distance:** 40 hours' journey E. of Yogya — significant transport difficulties.
2. **Smuggling:** Risk of smuggling which cannot easily be prevented.
3. **Discipline:** Pacitan inhabitants are less disciplined; must be handled much more carefully and gently than those in Loano / Winong.
- *River transport:* Enquiries whether pepper can be exported along rivers.
- Population is already accustomed to growing pepper $\rightarrow$ difficulties in Pacitan do not outweigh benefits.
- Price: **2 – 2 ½ Spanish Dollars (Rijksdaalders)** to be paid per pikul of pepper.

---

### Land Tenure & Hidden Agricultural Expansion:

- **Pacitan**: 200 cacahs / 48 jungs ricefields (very suited to pepper cultivation); 200 cacahs belong to Sunan. Sawahs ringed by mountains.
- Mountain roads frequently impassable.
- Smuggling risk: Chinese tollgate keepers to be alerted and warned against illicit transit.
- Inhabitants possess sufficient sawahs to sustain themselves $\rightarrow$ pepper can be cultivated for the V.O.C.
- **Survey of Ricefields in Pacitan**: Shows that acreage has expanded greatly since the Peace of 1755 (*Giyanti*).
  > *Crucial note:* The increase had been concealed from the royal Courts so that local farmers could quietly appropriate newly opened-up sawahs.  

- With the ending of corvée services, the Regent declares that local inhabitants can make a good living; region is more prosperous now than under Kartasura rule.

---

### Local Administration (The Netrawijaya / Wongsocitro Dynasty):

- Because of past administrative abuse and oppression by headmen and officials, populace must be handled with care to prevent flight.
- **Remoteness from Courts**: Regent **Netrawijaya** (now *Wongsocitro*), succeeded by his son, takes advantage of this distance. However, old Netrawijaya remains in de facto control.
- Resident should closely supervise the Regent's son (who is far less fair-minded than his father) to avert peasant flight.
- To satisfy father and son, their official sawah holding is to be raised from 6 to 12 jungs, while retaining local labour obligations for pepper plantations.

#### Final Land Note:

- **Loano**: 26 jungs could all be cleared and planted with pepper.
