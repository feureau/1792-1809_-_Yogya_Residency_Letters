### Archive Entries: October – November 1801 (Van den Berg Assessment of HB II's Ambition)

* **28 October 1801:** 80 Dragoons stationed as bodyguards for Sunan and Sultan at Solo and Yogya courts.
* **6 November 1801 (Van den Berg to Van Reede):** HB II recovers from illness. Resident notes: *"HB II sees all too clearly to take advantage of our miserable situation to show himself superior to us. Because the yearning for glory plagues him terribly."* HB II demands his toast be drunk above the Council of the Indies.
* **26 October 1801 (Van den Berg to DR II):** Resident holds back strangers in retaliation for HB II's protocol demands. 3rd Inner Regent R.T. Mangundipura suspended by HB II without explanation; reinstated within a week.
* **2 November 1801:** Resident writes: *"Meanwhile I count myself lucky that in my youth I was able to learn Javanese well and that now I know it better than my mother tongue!"* Describes HB II's governance: *"It is unbelievably difficult to have dealings because of his hankering after glory and desire for money everything goes badly."*
* **Complicity in Banditry (*Kècus*):** HB II turns a blind eye to nightly robberies committed against Kyai Adipati Purwadiningrat's subjects in Madiun because HB II receives a financial cut. Danureja II too terrified of HB II to speak frankly.
* **12 November 1801:** HB II's quest for glory reflects declining V.O.C. influence (1st Resident unable to secure interviews on demand; HB II insists on negotiating exclusively through DR II). Resident compares HB II unfavorably to his father HB I, who gave his Resident and Patih a free hand.
