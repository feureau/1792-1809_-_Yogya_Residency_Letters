### [6 Nov 1801]

* **v.d.B to van Reede (Semarang), 6 Nov. 1801** *(note: "17 Feb. 1801" faintly crossed out)*
  * HB II recovered from sickness.
  * *[Double red vertical line in left margin]*
    > "HB II seems all too clearly to take advantage of our miserable situation to show himself superior to us"  
    > "Because the yearning for glory plagues him terribly"  

  * wants Toast to HB II to proceed that to G.G. + Raad van Indië although such a thing had never been allowed before.
  * HB II wants to be toasted at least above <ins>Raad van Indië</ins>.

### [8 Nov 1801]

* **Id. to Id. 8 Nov. 1801**
  * *[Double red vertical line in left margin]*
    > "Meanwhile <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">I count myself lucky that in my youth I was able to learn Javanese well and that now I know it better than my mother tongue!"</ins>  

  * thus in a state to explain situation clearly to HB II.
  * > "It is unbelievably difficult to have dealings Because of his hankering after glory + desire for money everything goes badly."
  * *[Red vertical line in left margin, annotated in red ink: "klews in pay of HB II"]*
  * Turns a blind eye to Nightly Robberies of Kyai Adip. Purwadiningrat's subjects in Madiun because gets [HB II] a cut from them + other similar events occur which create disturbances in Interior.
  * DR II too afraid of HB II to talk frankly to him.
  * vdB thinks should allow events in Europe to settle down then deal + HB II.

### [12 Nov 1801]

* **Id. to Id. 12 Nov. 1801**
  * HB II quest for glory – sign of Decline of Influence of V.O.C. that 1st Resident cannot get an interview + Sultan when requested.
  * HB II wants vdB to always negotiate through DR II.
  * But Kliwons / DR II always dependant on will of HB II.
  * > "HB II's father who was in many respects a great man, gave his 1st Resident + Patih a free hand in many affairs."
