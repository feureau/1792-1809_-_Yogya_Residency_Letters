### Draft Ceremonial Protocol for the Reception of Lt. G.-G. Buyskes at Yogyakarta (19 Nov. 1808)

* Reference: **Project Ceremonieel op de Receptie van den Heere Luitenant Gouverneur Generaal Buyskes (19 Nov. 1808)**
* Document: Protocol governing the state visit of Lieutenant Governor-General Rear-Admiral Buyskes to Sultan Hamengkubuwana II.

#### Reception Protocol Rules (Items 1–10):
* **1–2. Advance Envoys:** Day before arrival, Patih Danureja II and *Nayakas* proceed to Klaten. Lt.-Col. De Chanteauvieux and Cav. Capt. Mollet serve as Resident's emissaries. Court princes proceed to Prambanan.
* **3–5. Arrival Signals:** Dutch flag flown from 4 fort bastions. At 7:00 a.m., Crown Prince and Residency Secretary proceed to Jenu. At 6:30 a.m., cavalry escorts HB II to Demangan.
* **6–7. Demangan Meeting & Salutes:**
  * Outgoing Minister (Engelhard) and incoming Minister (Wiese) ride together in a 6-horse carriage to Demangan (*Wiese assumes office 4 Dec. 1808*).
  * HB II travels to Demangan in a separate coach. 21-gun fort salute and 3 infantry volleys fired as coaches pass the fort (repeated by field guns at Demangan).
  * Buyskes greeted at Demangan *pesanggrahan* with a 21-gun salute and 3 volleys. HB II and Buyskes ride to the Kraton in the same carriage; Engelhard, Wiese, and Crown Prince follow in separate coaches.
* **8–10. Kraton Procession & Residency Banquet:**
  * HB II's troops and pikemen line the procession route in a double rank (*Mangkunagaran Regents exempt from lining the road*).
  * Following the Kraton audience, Buyskes returns to the Residency under a grenadier guard of honor; court princes, Bupatis, and *Nayakas* pay formal respects at the Residency.
