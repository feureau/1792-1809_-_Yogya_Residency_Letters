### [Transcription]

#### 1. Lamongan & Gresik (f. 39)

- **Lamongan:**  
  - Size: **1,000 cacahs**.  
  - In a languishing condition for a long time. Administered by two Bupatis originating from Japara who pay little attention to the district.  
  - Dues: 162 rijksdaalders *cacah-geld*; 250 koyangs rice; 3 pikuls cotton thread.  
- **Gresik:**  
  - Size: **3,200 cacahs**.  
  - Declining from its former prosperity.  
  - *[Red marginal note:]* **Increase in Tollgates along Bengawan Sala:**  
    Decline caused by the rapid proliferation of river tollgates (*bandar*) established by the Central Javanese rulers along the Bengawan Solo river.  

  - Bupatis: **Tumenggung Arjanagara** and **Tumenggung Bratanegara**.  
  - Dues: 487½ rijksdaalders *cacah-geld*; 160 koyangs rice; 6 pikuls cotton thread.  
  - Since 1782, timber felled in the Principalities (*Vorstenlanden*) floated down the river to Gresik; supplemented by 500–600 lengths of timber from Tuban and Rembang.

#### 2. Surabaya — Headquarters of the Oosthoek (f. 40)

- **Administration & Regents:**  
  - **Tumenggung Panji Cakranagara** succeeded R.T. Cakranagara in 1793.  
  - Second Regent: **Mas Tumenggung Jayadirana**.  
  - *Evaluation:* Panji Cakranagara is shrewd, intelligent, cordial, and firmly loyal to the V.O.C. (the colleague is less capable).  
  - Mother of Tumenggung Panji Cakranagara is a daughter of the Panembahan of Madura.  
- **Resources & Contingents:**  
  - District size: **7,000 cacahs**.  
  - Annual contingents: $140\frac{1}{4}$ rijksdaalders *cacah-geld*; **1,000 koyangs of rice**; 24 pikuls cotton thread.
