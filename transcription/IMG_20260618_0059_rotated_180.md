### Coastal Regencies: Lamongan, Gresik, Solo River Tollgates, & Surabaya

* **Lamongan:** 1,000 cacahs. District in long-term decline. Governed by 2 Regents from Japara who pay little attention to local affairs. Delivers 162 Rixdollars cash, 250 *koyangs* rice, and 3 pikuls cotton thread.
* **Gresik (p. 39):** 3,200 cacahs. **Not what it used to be.**
  * **Increase in Tollgates along Bengawan Solo:** Decline caused by proliferation of tollgates established by native rulers along the Solo River under Tumenggungs Arjanagara and Bratanegara.
  * Delivers 487 1/2 Rixdollars cash, 160 *koyangs* rice, and 6 pikuls cotton thread.
  * Since 1782, timber felled in the *Vorstenlanden* transported downriver to Gresik (500–600 timber lengths from Tuban and Rembang).
* **Surabaya (p. 40):** Headquarters of the *Oosthoek*.
  * **Tumenggung Panji Cakranagara:** Succeeded R.T. Cakranagara in 1793 (Mas Tum. Jayadirana). Highly intelligent, cordial, shrewd, and loyal to V.O.C. Mother was daughter of Pangeran of Madura.
  * Jurisdiction: 7,000 cacahs. Annual contingents: 140 1/4 Rixdollars cash, 1,000 *koyangs* rice, and 24 pikuls cotton thread.
