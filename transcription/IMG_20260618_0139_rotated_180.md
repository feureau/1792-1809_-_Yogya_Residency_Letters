## Archival & Historical Notes

- **Court Appointments & Succession (January – July 1803):**
  - Surakarta appoints Raden Ria Mêrtanêgara as Regent of Wirasaba; Mancanagara regents granted leave to return to their districts.
  - Death of Yogyakarta's Mataram Tumenggung Jayawinata: Prince Dipakusuma (son of HB I) succeeds him as Outer Regent (*Bupati Nayaka / Wedana Jawi*); Jayawinata's son is appointed *Bupati Miji* by Sultan Hamengkubuwana II.
  - In Surakarta, PB IV elevates his brothers Pangeran Aria Dipanagara and Pangeran Aria Adiwêgara (Adinegara). The Regent of Kartasura, R.T. Suramadiwiria, is dismissed and replaced by the young R.T. Jayakusuma.
- **Surakarta Bereavement & Elevation (22 August 1803):** Resident Waterloo acknowledges news from Surakarta of the death of PB IV's brother, Pangeran Natakusuma; Raden Mas Aria Gandakusuma (son-in-law of Pangeran Prangwedana) is elevated as Pangeran Aria Adiwijaya.
- **Panolan Security (25 September 1803):** Reports of bandit gangs operating in the Panolan district under Bupati Raden Tumenggung Natawijaya.
- **Illness of Ratu Agêng Tegalreja (27 September 1803):**
  - Sultan Hamengkubuwana II elevates his younger brother Raden Mas Sudarma as Pangeran Santakusuma.
  - Ratu Agêng (consort of HB I, mother of HB II, and guardian of Prince Dipanegara) is reported critically ill. The illness began after a fall the elderly queen suffered alongside a small garden pond (likely the reflecting pools at her estate in Tegalreja), precipitating a severe fever from which she would not recover (passing away on 18 October 1803).
- **Elevation of Tan Jin Sing (September 1803):**
  - Tan Jin Sing (the future Raden Tumenggung Secadiningrat), previously serving as Head *Bandhar* (toll-farmer) in Kêdhu, is formally promoted to **Capitan Cina of Yogyakarta**. This marks a pivotal milestone in his career, positioning him as the principal intermediary between the Yogyakarta Kraton and the European authorities.
