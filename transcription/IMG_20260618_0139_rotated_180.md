### AvJ Archive Entries: 1803 (Court Promotions, Death of P. Natakusuma, & Tan Tjin Sing)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1803**
* **7 January 1803:** R. Aria Mertanegara appointed Regent of Wirasaba by Surakarta; Mangkunagaran Regents permitted home.
* **Mataram Regency Death:** Mataram Tumenggung Jayawinala dies; **Pangeran Dipakusuma** takes his place as Outer Regent; son R.T. Jayawinala appointed *Bupati Miji* by HB II.
* **5 May 1803:** Mangkunagaran Regents granted permission to return home.
* **5 July 1803:** PB IV appoints brothers Pangeran Aria Dipasana and Pangeran Aria Adiwijaya. Kertasara Regent R.T. Suradiwiria dismissed; replaced by young R.T. Jayakusuma (died 1795).
* **22 August 1803:** **Death of Pangeran Natakusuma** (brother of PB IV); Prangwedana's son-in-law R.M. Aria Gandakusuma raised as **Pangeran Aria Adiwijaya**.
* **25 September 1803:** Robber band under R.T. Narawijaya active in Panolan area.
* **27 September 1803:** HB II's younger brother R.M. Sundarna raised as **Pangeran Santakusuma**.
* **Ratu Ageng Illness:** Ratu Ageng Tegalreja (mother of HB II) falls ill with fever following a fall near a small pond at Tegalreja.
* **Capitan Cina Elevation:** Newly promoted Kedu Head Bandhar **Tan Tjin Sing raised to Capitan Cina in Yogyakarta**.
