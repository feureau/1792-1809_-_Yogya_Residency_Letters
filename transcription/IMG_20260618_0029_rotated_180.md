### Aftermath of Hamengkubuwana I's Death (March–April 1792)

#### Succession Agreement (25 March 1792 Cont.):

- According to the express wish of HB I, the Crown Prince is to be recognized as Sultan immediately, having already ruled de facto beside his father.
- **Condition:** He should **not** be officially installed/crowned until the arrival of the Governor of Java's Northeast Coast and the formal renewal of treaties.
- All nobles present affirm their solemn obligation to observe this procedure.
- Crown Prince promises to await the arrival of the Governor before formal coronation.
- Direction of state affairs placed firmly in the hands of **Resident Van IJsseldijk** and **Patih Danureja I (DRI)**.
- No princes display inclination to rebel; precautionary measures prepared by V.O.C. are not required.

---

#### Official Notifications to Surakarta:

- **26 March 1792**: IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede (Solo):
  - Official letter from Crown Prince (HB II) to PB IV announcing the death of HB I.
  - Envoys (*gandèk*): Jiwanegat sent to PB IV; Ng. Kartayuda and Trunamēnggala sent to Crown Prince of Solo.

---

#### Investiture of Royal Princes:

- **3 April [1792]** (IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten) / **13 April** ($\rightarrow$ Reede tot de Parkeler):
  - Investiture of 5 sons of HB I as Princes (*Pangeran*) this morning:
    1. Raden Aria Paninggah
    2. Raden Aria Mērtasana
    3. Raden Aria Wiramēnggala
    4. Raden Aria Pamot
    5. Raden Aria Moendiman

---

#### Defiance of Pangeran Ngabehi:

- **Pangeran Ngabèhi** declares himself entirely exempt from taking orders from HB II:
  > *Vrij verklaart van het droegen der lasten* (declares himself free from bearing burdens).
