### Archive Entries: March – April 1792 (Succession & Royal Elevations)

* **Succession Terms:** In accordance with wish of late HB I, Crown Prince proclaimed Sultan over father's realm. Formal installation deferred pending arrival of Governor of Java's N.E. Coast and renewal of treaties.
* **Oaths:** All nobles take oath to observe terms. Crown Prince promises to await Governor's arrival before formal coronation. State affairs directed by Van IJsseldijk & DRI (Danureja I).
* **Court Stability:** No princely opposition or revolt observed.
* **26 March 1792:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Reede: HB II sends letter to PB IV notifying death of HB I. Envoys sent to Solo: Ng. Kantayuda, Trunamenggala.
* **9 April 1792:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten: HB II elevates 5 of his sons as Princes this morning: R. Aria Paringah, Martasana, Wiramenggala, Parrot, Moondiman.
* **13 April 1792:** -> Resident of P.: Ngabehi declared exempt from court burdens by HB II (*vrij verklaard van het dragen der lasten*). Intrigues noted. Red ink: **Pangeran Amardika**.
