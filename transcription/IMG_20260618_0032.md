### Yogya Residency Correspondence (January–May 1793)

*(Port. 3 No. 1 / NvB. 1793 Yogya)*

| Date / Correspondence | Content / Summary |
| :--- | :--- |
| **19 Jan. [1793]**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten | HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]'s envoy (*gandèk*) Jiwaraga dispatched to Semarang to convey formal notice of an impending marriage in the Sultan's family. |
| **Ibid.**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede (*Solo*) | HB II's *gandèk* Prawirasari sent to Surakarta to announce the royal wedding taking place the following week. |
| **22 Jan. [1793]**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ D. Barkey (*Gezaghebber Oosthoek*) | HB II's sergeant-coachman B. Lamarens and Assistant Wenceslas Neves dispatched to escort a shipment of ordnance/ammunition from Surabaya overland to Semarang. |
| **22 Jan. 1793**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Resident of Gresik | *(Red ink note: **Cannon / ammunition from Grèsik**)*<br>The aforementioned detachment sent to take delivery of cannons and powder at Gresik and transport them up the Solo River to Central Java. |
| **4 Feb. [1793]**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten | 50 pistols sent by Governor Van Overstraten to Yogyakarta; purchase price to be defrayed by HB II. |
| **13 Feb. [1793]**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten | **Pepper Cultivation & Indigo Factory**: Lands formally ceded by HB II for Company exploitation. |
| **14 Feb. [1793]**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede | Sunan's *gandèk* Prawiramēnggala and Cakradirana arrive to announce a marriage within PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV]'s family. |
| **15 April [1793]**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede | Investigation into the murder of travellers near **Prambanan**: perpetrated by the Chinese tollgate keeper (*bandar*) at Brenkong Santran; his nephew found guilty. |
| **4 May 1793**<br>IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Boedelmeester (*Semarang*) | **Death of Kapitan Cina of Kedu**, Gank Kaksing.<br>Declaration taken from the Head Chinese *Bandar* in Yogyakarta, **Qué Tjin Sing** (*father of Tan Jin Sing*), concerning the estate of the Indian Muslim / Moor (*Koja*) named Koja Libé. |
