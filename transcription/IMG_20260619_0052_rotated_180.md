### N. Engelhard "Speculatieve Memorie" (14 May 1808): Annexation Plans & Border Adjustments

* Reference: **Van Alphen - Engelhard No. 235 (14 May 1808 - Speculatieve Memorie)**
* **Giyanti War Cost:** Giyanti War cost the V.O.C. f. 4,286,000.
* **Danureja II Appointment:** HB II refused to allow Patih Danureja II to accept his official hat and appointment from Semarang in 1799 due to feudal terminology (*Leen / Leenheer*), only permitting it in 1802 during Engelhard's visit.
* **Land Expansion & Clearances:**
  * HB II refuses to permit creation of new land appointments at official investitures.
  * Extensive new sawahs opened up since 1774 (including drainage of Trawas marshes and clearances along Grobogan, Lamongan, and Juwana borders).
* **Proposed Annexations & Border Redefinitions:**
  * Engelhard proposes drawing new boundaries between the Pasisir and Vorstenlanden upon the accession of new rulers to secure rich agricultural lands for the government (*precedent for Daendels' 1810 Treaty*).
  * **Chap. IV Annexation Recommendations:** Recommends annexing **Pacitan, Tanjong, Loano, and Malang** (which PB IV claims).
* **Fortifications & Garrison Labor:** Rulers should surrender full legal title to lands occupied by forts, Residencies, and European/Chinese kampongs. Native court coolies for fort maintenance to be increased to 250–300 men (40 permanent coolies assigned to fort upkeep).
