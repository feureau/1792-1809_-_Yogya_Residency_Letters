### Reorganization of Jayeng-Sekan Regiments (1808–1809)

* Reference: **Jayeng-Nikan XV (1808–1809)**
* **1 February 1809 (Part 5 pp. 504–507):** Deployment and utilization of Jayeng-Sekan regiments in Cirebon.
* **1 September 1808 Reorganization (Articles 25–35):**
  * Reorganization of North East Coast administration: 80–100 Jayeng-Sekan troops assigned per Prefecture (pp. 164–166).
  * Placed under direct orders of the Prefect / Commandant.
  * Native Javanese officers assigned official titles (Commandant designated as *Bupati*; subordinates designated as *Jaksa* or *Patih*).
  * Officers and troops compensated through assigned ricefields (*sawahs*).
