### Archive Entries: February – May 1807 (Marriages, Magetan Incident, & Death of Wiraguna)

* **18 February 1807:** Surakarta religious officials request Yogyakarta Pengulu Muhamad Mas Supingi permit Pangeran Natapura to witness marriage of P. Natadiningrat's daughter.
* **2 March 1807 (Double Royal Marriage):**
  * HB II's daughters married: Raden Ajeng Supadari to Raden Antawiria (son of R.T. Natawijaya); Raden Ajeng Nupadrai to R. Mas Nayidin (son of Outer Regent R.T. Danukusuma).
* **25 March 1807 (Magetan Abduction Incident):** Surakarta Capitan Cina Ni Niok Liang complains that Patih of Magetan (Cakradiwiria) seized 4 unmarried nieces and 1 maid belonging to the deceased Capitan Cina's brother. R.T. Purwadiningrat explains the seizure was for an unpaid debt of 300 Np.p.
* **20 March 1807 (PB IV Marriage Gifts):** PB IV sends lavish wedding gifts for Crown Prince's children (Antawiria/Supadari): 800 *tompons* of rice, 16 buffaloes, 32 *tompons* of salt, 32 baskets of *trasi*, and 200 Ronde Reals cash.
* **30 April 1807 (Death of Patih Kadipaten Wiraguna):**
  * **DEATH OF KYAI TUMENGGUNG WIRAGUNA (PATIH OF KADIPATEN).**
  * Mas Tum. Kentadjirja (Head of Masaran / Sukawati) appointed *Kepala Pacatan* in his place.
* **14 May 1807:** In place of deceased Tumenggung of Rajegwesi (R.T. Sasradiningrat), R.T. Sasradiwiria (Bupati of Grobogan) appointed. R.T. Sasranegara appointed Bupati of Grobogan.
* **20 May 1807:** European *pesanggrahan* kampong laid out near the Fort; no sawahs in immediate fort vicinity.
