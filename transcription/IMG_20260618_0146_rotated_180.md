### Archive Entries: October 1803 (Deathbed Exhortation of Ratu Ageng Tegalreja)

* **26 October 1803 (Engelhard -> Waterloo):** News of the **Peace of Amiens** received (peace between England, France, and Holland).
* **28–29 October 1803 (Burial of Ratu Ageng Tegalreja):**
  * **Ratu Ageng Tegalreja buried at Imagiri** (4 hours distant from Yogya).
  * HB II and Crown Prince remain deeply distressed at home; entire court follows funeral cortege to Imagiri.
  * **Ratu Ageng's Deathbed Exhortation to Sultan HB II:**
    > *"Sultan: De weg die ik af te leggen heb is zwaar, en uw gevoel ik! Niets meer dan de gemeente mensch te wezen, houd dit mij zoon voor oogen en gelooft niet, dat ofschoon uw nu Vorst zijt, dat uw na u dood iets meer is dan als een gemeen Batoor (catoor), en wees daar na! —"*  
    > (*"Sultan: The path I must travel is heavy, and I feel it! Remember, my son, that you are nothing more than a common human being. Keep this before your eyes and do not believe that, though you are now a Sovereign, after your death you are anything more than a common servant — behave accordingly!"*)
  * Words make a profound impression on HB II.
* **31 October 1803:** R.T. Sumadiningrat described as a stupid antagonist (allied with DRI and Outer Regents); obstructs investigation into the murder of European Gabriel in Grobogan.
