## Archival & Historical Notes

- **Grobogan Rice Logistics (30 September 1803):** Resident Waterloo confers with Raden Tumenggung Sasrakusuma (Second Regent of Grobogan) to expedite rice shipments from Eastern Grobogan to coastal markets to supply VOC/Batavian troops stationed along the Pasisir.
- **Territorial Encroachment at Desa Luyuk (26 September 1803):**
  - First Resident J.G. van den Berg (Surakarta) writes urgently to Waterloo regarding HB II's aggressive land policy and resistance to boundary demarcations.
  - HB II forcibly annexed 100 *cacahs* of appanage land from Desa Luyuk (situated near Surakarta); all local inhabitants fled across the border into Surakarta territory and placed themselves under the protection of Surakarta Patih Raden Adipati Mangkupraja.
- **Archival Retrospective: Royal Marriages of December 1798 (AJ 1725):**
  - Extract of correspondence from Governor Johan Frederik Baron van Reede tot de Parkeler acknowledging HB II's letter of 14 Jumadilakir AJ 1725 regarding triple marriages uniting the Sultan's daughters with key Mancanagara commanders:
    1. Raden Rangga Prawiradirja III (orphan son of Rangga Prawiradirja II) married to royal princess Raden Ajeng Murniah (later Ratu Maduretna).
    2. Raden Tumenggung Natadiwiria (Raden Jayid, Mancanagara Regent of Panolan) married to Raden Ajeng Murdiah.
    3. Raden Tumenggung Prawirayuda (Mancanagara Regent) married to Raden Ajeng Sarima.
  - Solemnized on 18 Jumadilakir AJ 1725 (Year *Jimawal*).
- **Murder of European Teak Timber Merchant Gabriel Eenst (6 October 1803):**
  - Patih Danureja II writes to Surakarta Patih Mangkupraja relaying intelligence from Raden Rangga Prawiradirja III.
  - A European timber merchant from the north coast named Gabriel Eenst had traveled into the teak forests of Jipang/Panolan (under Bupati R.T. Natawijaya) to purchase timber and rented a dwelling in Desa Gelempang.
  - Eenst and his Chinese assistant were violently robbed and murdered.
  - Yogyakarta authorities order a joint investigative commission dispatched under the direct authority of Raden Rangga Prawiradirja III.
