### Memorie van Overgave: Van Overstraten (13 October 1796)

* Reference: **MvO Java's N.O. Kust No. 1 (1793–1799)**
* Document: *Memorie door Pieter Gerardus van Overstraten, afgaand Gouverneur van Java's Noord-Oost Kust, overgegeven aan Baron van Reede tot de Parkeler (13 Oct. 1796).*

#### Land Census & Register Refusals:
* **Klepu Register (1774 / Surat Ebuk Anyar):**
  * Sunan (Surakarta): 85,045 cacahs.
  * Sultan (Yogyakarta): 87,050 cacahs.
  * *Actual Census (1774):* Both courts combined exceeded 100,000 cacahs.
* **Refusal of New Census (1793–94):** Both rulers refuse a new register because each holds more land than acknowledged and fears an enforced evening-out / rebalancing of appanages.
* **V.O.C. Balance of Power:** Both courts believe V.O.C. can tip power balance. Without Dutch presence, rulers would be in open conflict. Surakarta actively plans to redress power balance in Central Java.

#### British Threat & Ceylon Exiles:
* **British Capture of Ceylon:** British take Ceylon, holding Javanese exiles from Kartasura court (including Pangeran Adipati Amangkurat / Mas Ambiya).
* **Prophecy:** Surakarta fears fulfillment of old Javanese prophecy that a descendant of the Amangkurat line would return to rule Java.
