#### 1. Land Registers & The Balance of Power (Klêpu 1774)

- Register 1774 Book of Klêpu (*Serat Èbut Anyar*) kept in a secret chest in the archives.  
- Sala / Yogya must not guarantee lands after Klêpu Register:  
  - **Sunan:** 85,450 [85,050?] cacahs  
  - **Sultan:** 87,050 cacahs  
  *(At Giyanti 1755, but by 1774 count each over 100,000 cacahs).*  

- **Reason for Rulers' refusal to allow a new Register in 1793–1794:**  
  Because each court believes it possesses more land than the other, and fears that if a new register is compiled, an **evening out / balancing of lands** would be insisted upon.  

- Sala and Yogya both believe the V.O.C. can tip the scales in favour of one or the other court (the Pakepung crisis of 1790 gave Sala a severe fright).  
- *"Without presence of Dutch, rulers would tear each other's throats."*  
- Sala is pregnant with plans to redress the balance of power in Central Java in its favour.

#### 2. British Capture of Ceylon & The Amangkurat Prophecy

- **British capture Ceylon:**  
  Javanese exiles residing there include two scions of the Kartasura court:  

  - **Pangeran Adipati Amangkurat**  
  - **Mas Ambiya**  
- **Fear in Sala:**  
  An old prophecy in Java foretells that one of the **Amangkurat family** would rule again one day in Java.
