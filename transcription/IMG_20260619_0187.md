### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.24: Ceremonial Reception Rules for Daendels' State Visit (July 1809)

* Reference: **AN Djokjo Brieven 20.24 (July 1809)**
* Document: Draft protocol for the reception of Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels at Yogyakarta.

#### Protocol Rules (Items 1–9):
* **1–2. Advance Delegations:** Minister of Yogyakarta meets Daendels at Boyolali before Daendels enters Solo. Danureja II and Yogyakarta Commander meet Daendels at Pakis to escort him to Klaten (with 2 *Bupati Wedanas*). Pangeran Mangkudiningrat, Pangeran Mangkubumi, and Minister Wiese greet Daendels at Klaten.
* **3–5. Arrival Lines:** Crown Prince and royal children meet Daendels at Kalasan to escort him to Bantulan. Dragoons escort HB II to Bantulan.
* **6–9. Formal Entry & Reksanegaran Reception:**
  * HB II and Kraton *prajurits* meet Daendels at Demangan @ 6:00 a.m. in the royal state coach *Kyai Jimat*.
  * Royal gamelan plays at Bantulan upon Daendels' arrival.
  * Crown Prince and Minister receive Daendels at Reksanegaran, riding with Daendels into Yogyakarta under Dragoon escort.
