### [Transcription]

AN Djokja Brieven no. 24.  
**1809 (July)**  
<u>CEREMONIAL for Reception of Daendels at Yogya:</u>

1. Minister of Yogya to meet Daendels at Bayalali before D -> Sala; Minister to Return on Day D -> Yogya to Yogya.  
2. Sec of Yogya -> Kartasura.  
   DR II / Comm. of Yogya -> Pakis accompany Daendels to Klaten.  

   + 2 Bupati Wêdanas.  
   At Klaten: Mangkudiningrat / Mangkubumi + Minister of Yogya.  

3. <u>Kalasan: CP & Sultan's children accompany him to Bantulan.</u>  
4. Dragoons to Kraton to accompany HB II to Bantulan.  
6. <u>HB II & all Kraton Prajurits to meet Daendels at Dêmangan at 6 a.m. in coach Kyai Jimat.</u>  
8. <u>Daendels to be greeted at Bantulan by gamêlan at Kraton / Bantulan.</u>  
9. CP & Minister to Rēksanagaran to Receive Daendels & Ride & Daendels -> Yogya escorted by Dragoons.
