### Text & Marginal Annotations

[Dutch Military Weakness:]

- **14 Jan. 1800:** Reply from Governor-General that reinforcing the Surakarta and Yogyakarta garrisons cannot be attempted until fresh troops arrive from the Netherlands.
- The V.O.C. / Batavian administration is powerless to force HB II to do anything against his will (e.g., regarding Nederburgh's proposed visit).

---

[HB II's Capriciousness toward Pangeran Natakusuma:]

[Red header: **HB II -> NK**]

- Natakusuma refuses to surrender certain princely ceremonial privileges originally accorded to him by HB I, despite the furious insistence of HB II.
- Specifically concerns **special *êbèk*** (shield / escutcheon / saddle flap decoration) on his horse [red underline & marginal note: *\* decoration on saddle*].
- At the Saturday tournament (*Senenan* / *Watangan*) on **2 May 1800**, Natakusuma was coerced into appearing with a modified/new shield.

---

[Ceremonial Clashes with Resident Johannes Gerard van den Berg:]

- **July 1800:** HB II attempts to order that the Dutch Resident must **dismount from his horse** whenever he encounters the royal *pusaka* (sacred regalia/heirlooms) being carried around town, or alternatively pull aside and hide until the cortege has passed [underlined in red].
  - Resident Van den Berg issues an absolute refusal.
  - HB II retaliates by punishing the royal messengers who conveyed the Resident's uncompromising reply.
- **January 1801:** Patih Danureja II is punished by HB II over protocol during the Resident's New Year's party:
  - HB II insisted that the toast to the Sultan must be drunk **before** the toast to the members of the High Government (Council of the Indies) at Batavia.
- HB II deliberately sends no royal gifts on the occasion of Pangeran Buminata’s third marriage.

---

[HB II's Vulnerability & Isolation:]

[Red underlines and margin line:]

- **HB II is not at all loved by his subjects.**
- Despite the formal pledging of loyalty by his royal brothers and Regents, at the slightest sign of real trouble they would instantly fall away and abandon HB II.
