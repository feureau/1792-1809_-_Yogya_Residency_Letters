### Archive Entries: 1800–1801 (Reinforcements, Court Incidents, and HB II's Capriciousness)

* **14 January 1800:** G.G. replies that reinforcing Solo/Yogya garrisons impossible until troops arrive from Holland. V.O.C. powerless to coerce HB II against his will.
* **HB II vs. Natakusuma (NK):**
  * HB II attempts to strip Natakusuma of privileges granted by HB I; Natakusuma refuses to yield.
  * **Saddle Escutcheon Incident (2 May 1800):** Natakusuma appears at Saturday tournament with a special *Ebèks* (decorated saddle shield/escutcheon) on his horse, defying HB II's orders.
* **State Pusaka Incident (July 1800):** HB II demands Resident dismount from his horse when royal *pusakas* are carried in procession through town. Resident Van den Berg flatly refuses; HB II punishes messengers who delivered Resident's refusal.
* **New Year's Toast Incident (January 1801):** Patih punished by HB II after a New Year's party at the Residency because Resident drank the Governor-General's toast before HB II's toast.
* **Court Coldness:** HB II sends no gifts on occasion of Pangeran Buminata's 3rd marriage.
* **Popular Discontent:** HB II widely disliked by subjects; despite oaths of loyalty from brothers/regents, widespread unrest feared at night.
