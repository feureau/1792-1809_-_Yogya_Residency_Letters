### Archive Entries: April – June 1809 (Gresik Court Jurisdiction & Daendels Reception Protocols)

* Reference: **A.N. Yogya Letters No. 22 (April – June 1809)**
* **26 April 1809:** Sunan and Sultan court judicial jurisdiction over Gresik formally terminated.
* **11 May 1809:** Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III refuses to surrender Panawan *Bandars* to Chinese tax farmers without a formal royal *piagem*.
* **2 June 1809 (Draft Reception Protocols for Daendels' July Visit):**
  * Draft reception protocol prepared for Daendels' July visit to Yogyakarta (*ref: 1808 Buyskes visit*).
  * **Throne Height Demand:** HB II insists he must sit on an elevated seat (*Dhampar*) higher than Daendels at both the Residency and Kraton.
  * **Stool Protocol for Crown Prince:** HB II requests a dedicated stool for the Crown Prince during audiences (*noting Crown Prince rarely sits on a stool, following Surakarta precedent*).
* **13 June 1809:** HB II agrees to travel as far as Jenu in Demangan to receive Daendels; requests a stool for Pangeran Ngabehi. Excuses himself from visiting the Residency House, offering to receive Daendels at the Kraton the following day.
