A.N. Yogya Letters No. 22. **1809 (April–May) Yogya**

*26 April 1809*  
*Wiese to Van Braam*  
PAYMENT of JUITJE to by SUNAN / SULTAN IN GRESIK ended

*11 May 1809*  
*Wiese to Van Braam*  
R. Rangga still loathe to give over Bandhars of PANAWLAN to Chinese: (without proper PIASEM)

*2 June 1809*  
*Wiese to Daendels*  

[Red ink: *Ceremonial for Reception of Daendels.*]

PROJECT CEREMONIAL TO RECEIVE Daendels AT COURT IN JULY 1809  
Expecting letter from Daendels ->  
HB II prepared to go to Demangan Curia to meet Daendels (1 hour away) similar to protocol for visit of Lt Gen Buyskes 1808.  
Asks for Procession for CP:  
HB II must sit higher than Daendels  
between Res house & Kraton  
CP only very rarely sits on a stool (cannot cpg Sala)

*13 June 1809*  
*Wiese to Daendels*  

[Red ink: *Jēnu*]

(1) HB II to come as far as JĒNU in Demangan area to meet Daendels.  
(2) CP to be given a stool: also P. Ngabèhi. Because had always been allowed one at official receptions in the past.  
(3) Asks to be excused visit to Residency house, but would invite Daendels to the KRATON on following Day.
