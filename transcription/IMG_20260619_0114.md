# Transcription of IMG_20260619_0114.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260619_0114.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (J. A. van der Chijs, *Nederlandsch-Indisch Plakaatboek, 1602–1811*, Deel XV [1808–1809], pp. 62–63)
- **Date Range:** 1808-07-27 to 1808-12-07
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official printed decrees of Governor-General Marshal Herman Willem Daendels reproduced from Van der Chijs' *Plakaatboek* (Deel XV, pp. 62–63) with Peter Carey's research annotations; includes the Semarang administrative salary decree (split between silver specie and paper credit), the decree of 27 July 1808 abolishing the second regency of Demak (*Opheffing der betrekking van tweeden regent van Demak*) criticizing former Governor Nicolaus Engelhard, the appointment of Javanese and Malay translation clerks at Semarang (28 July 1808), and the opening articles of the historic 28 July 1808 Royal Court Ceremonial Regulation (*Reglement voor het ceremonieel, in acht te nemen door de residenten bij de hoven te Soerakarta en te Djokjokarta*) establishing the elevated diplomatic status, gold-embroidered uniform, and royal parasol (*payung*) of Dutch Residents.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Nicolaus Engelhard, Prof. Peter Carey, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sunan Pakubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Semarang, Demak, Surakarta (Soerakarta), Yogyakarta (Djokjokarta).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Reglement voor het ceremonieel (1808), Plakaatboek, Payung (state parasol), Regent (Bupati), Translateur (court translator), Paper currency (papieren van credit).
