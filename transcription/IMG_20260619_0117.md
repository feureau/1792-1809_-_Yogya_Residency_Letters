### Archive Entries: July – August 1808 (Cirebon Kraman in Bagelèn & Re-appointment of Sumadiningrat)

* **Uniform Fabric Supply (25 July 1808):** 25 corges of Javanese cloth sent from Semarang to Yogya to manufacture uniforms for Daendels' troops (cost f. 1,004).
* **Cirebon Holy Rebel (*Kraman*) in Bagelèn (Aug. 1808):**
  * A 40-year-old Cirebon rebel (*kraman*) wearing a gingham *kabaya* arrives in Desa Krian-kong (Bagelèn).
  * Locals consider him holy and refuse to arrest or touch him.
* **Sumadiningrat Re-appointment & Ceremonial Resistance (3 Aug. 1808):**
  * HB II convenes *Nayakas* to evaluate Daendels' new ceremonial rules (*28 July 1808*); severe anxiety reported among courtiers.
  * **Re-appointment of Sumadiningrat:** Recently re-appointed Head Inner Regent **R.T. Sumadiningrat** counsels HB II to reject the new ceremonial as humiliating.
  * P. Ngabehi, Danureja II, Purwadipura, Mangundipura, and Nindunagara favor accepting the protocol. Raden Rongga III and R.T. Natadiningrat remain silent.
