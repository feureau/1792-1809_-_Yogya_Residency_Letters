### Diary of Marshal Daendels' State Visit to Yogyakarta: Part 1 (23–29 July 1809)

* Reference: **AN Djokjo Brieven 20.24 (17 Aug. 1809 - J. Groenhoff van Woortman)**
* Document: Official diary recording Marshal Herman Willem Daendels' state visit to Surakarta and Yogyakarta (23–31 July 1809).

#### Journey & Reception Itinerary:
* **23 July 1809:** Daendels travels on horseback from Semarang via Salatiga to Boyolali; met at Ampel by Surakarta Patih and 2 *Wedanas*.
  * *11:30 a.m. Boyolali Arrival:* Met by Ministers of Solo and Yogya, Patih Danureja II (Yogya), and Pangeran Prangwedana. Danureja II returns to Yogya; Surakarta Patih escorts Daendels to Solo.
* **24 July 1809:** Daendels departs Boyolali @ 6:30 a.m. for Banyudana; greeted by Prangwedana Legion (28-gun salute). Met at Asem by Surakarta Crown Prince and at Kartasura by Minister Van Braam; received in Solo by Sunan, Wedanas, and Nayakas.
* **28 July 1809:**
  * Daendels proceeds toward Yogyakarta; met at Boyolali by Minister G.W. Wiese.
  * Assistant Resident Groenhoff van Woortman, Kyai T. Sindunegara, and Pangeran Adinegara escort Daendels past Pakis.
  * At Klaten, Daendels greeted by Wiese, Pangeran Mangkudiningrat, and Pangeran Mangkubumi.
* **29 July 1809:** Daendels departs Klaten @ 7:30 a.m., visiting Prambanan temple ruins for breakfast dressed in full uniform as a **Napoleonic Marshal**.
