### [Transcription]

AN Djokjo Brieven no. 24. Signed Yogya. 17 Aug. 1809. J. Groenhoff van Woortmann (Asst. Res)  
**1809 (23 July – 2 Aug)**  

[Red ink: *Diary of Daendels' Visit to Yogya / Daendels' visit to Yogya. 1.*]

*23 July 1809*  
Daendels from Semarang -> Salatiga.  
Received by Secretary of Sala.  
Daendels on horseback to Bayalali.  
Ampel met by Patih of Sala & 2 Wêdanas.  
11.30 arrival at Bayalali met by Residents of Sala / Yogya ("Ministers")  
Patih of Yogya & P. Prangwēdana. Returns to Yogya.  
Patih of Sala — accompanies D -> Sala.

*24 July* — 6.30  
D from Bayalali -> Banyudana.  
met by Troops of Prang Wēdana. "Legion" 28 gun salute.  
Assêm — met by CP of Sala: -> Kartasura.  
2nd Coach Brig. de Kock.  
Kartasura met by Ridder van Braam. Sunan's Wêdanas, Nayakas.  
Visit to Sala.

*28 July*  
-> visit to Yogya — proceed to Klathen.  
G.W. Wiese meets Daendels at Bayalali. Returns 3 days later.  
J. Groenhoff in Kartasura to meet Daendels. Asst-Resident.  
Kyai Tum. Sindunēgara, P. Adinēgara } escort D passed Pakis.  
Gezworen Translateur te Semarang — C. Krijgsman.  
Klathen — Wiese — Mangkudiningrat, Mangkubumi } greet Daendels.

*29 July*  
-> Daendels leaves Klathen arrives Prambanan 7.30.  
Visit to Ruins / Breakfast — dressed in Full Uniform of Napoleonic Marshal.
