not a vindictive man.<br>  - Even if someone was caught in HB I’s seraglio, he refrained from arbitrary execution — unheard of for a Javanese ruler.<br>- **HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]’s misrule & banditry:**<br>  - Under HB II there was a great increase in **night robberies**, with many princes having a direct hand in them (e.g., Pangeran Ngabèhi) [red margin bar].<br>  - Patih at present in gaol.<br>  - Caused by the high taxes imposed by HB II; he shows no concern for the common man unlike HB I.<br>  - Described as: *"Groothartig en Oploopend van Haart"* [Arrogant/haughty and quick-tempered of heart].<br>  - No one dares to gainsay or contradict him — not even the court *panjis*.<br><br>### Yogyakarta Crown Prince (future HB III [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III]):<br><br>- Very like HB I in character.<br>- Although loved by the people, he holds **no influence on affairs of state**.<br>- Does not like going out, wandering about, or oppressing his subjects, but is forced to do so in order to keep his father (HB II) happy [underlined in red].<br><br>### Pangeran Mangkudiningrat:<br><br>- Very careful to strictly follow the old-style native Javanese customs; greatly loved by the subjects.<br>- His mother, **Ratu Anom**, descended from the old Javanese Mataram dynasty.<br>- Possesses shrewd judgement; has great skill in winning the affection of the local population.<br>- Does not appear in European circles; closely watched, especially by the Crown Prince.<br>- Nevertheless shows himself to be reasonably well-disposed towards Europeans. |
