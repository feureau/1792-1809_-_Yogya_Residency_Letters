# Transcription of IMG_20260618_0110.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260618_0110.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 48: *Lijst van de verhooging van de tolle*, 10 April 1802)
- **Date Range:** 1802-04-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation of the official tariff schedule of tollgate tax increases (*Lijst van de verhooging van de tolle*) across the Yogyakarta Residency under Resident Johannes Gerardus van den Berg; details revised transit duties on woven mats from Pesantren, cloth shipments from Yogyakarta and Bagelen, miscellaneous merchandise, and ginger shipments conveyed by pack horses, carriers (*draag off paard*), and pikul loads (*picullankers*) to Semarang and Yogyakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Johannes Gerardus van den Berg, Jan Willem Neijenheuis, Prof. Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djoyo / Djocjo), Semarang (Samarang), Bagelen, Pesantren (Passantran).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Bandar (toll farm / pagt), Picullankers (pikul porters), Picul, Stuiver, Rondemat (Spanish dollar), Pack-horse freight.

  * **Bandar / Pagt**: Listed under line 485. Tollgate tax-farm leaseholder or toll station (*tolhek*).
  * **Picul**: Measure of weight (approx. 125 lbs / 60 kg) used for commercial freight.
