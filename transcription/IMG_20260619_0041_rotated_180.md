### MvO Waterloo (1808) & Register of Deceased Yogyakarta Figures (1801–1807)

* Reference: **MvO Waterloo (4 April 1808) / Pj. Br. 20.21**
* **Transfer of Handover Papers:** Resident Matthew Waterloo officially hands over the Yogyakarta Residency to Governor Nicolaas Engelhard on 4 April 1808 (*Generaal Register van zodanige Papieren als behooren tot de Memorie overgegeven door Waterloo aan Engelhard*).

#### Register of Prominent Persons Deceased in Yogyakarta (1801–1807):
* **Ratu Bindara** (Niece of HB II and ex-wife of Mangkunagara I): Died **9 November 1801**.
* **Ratu Ageng Tegalreja** (Mother of HB II): Died **18 October 1803** at a very advanced age (*30 Dec. 1801 entry note*).
* **R.T. Natawijaya** (Bupati of Panolan): Died **20 October 1803**.
* **R.T. Natayuda**: Died **29 November 1804** very suddenly from severe colic.
* **Kyai Tumenggung Wiraguna** (Patih of Kadipaten / Crown Prince): Died **20 April 1807** at a great age.
* **Raden Ayu Nanrawijaya** (Daughter of HB II): Died **6 June 1807**.
* **R.T. Nanradiningrat** (2nd Regent of Mangkunagaran): Died **8 May 1807**.
* **Raden Ayu Nanradiningrat** (Niece of HB II and wife of above): Died **28 August 1807**.
