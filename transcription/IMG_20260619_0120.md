### Archive Entries: August 1808 (Surakarta Protocol, Prefect Titles, & Hospital Needs)

* **10–11 August 1808 (Surakarta Festivities):**
  * PB IV accepts proposed visit of the Prince of Madura and his brothers; plans a feast on 25 August for Louis Napoleon's accession. Orders tiger hunts on Surakarta territory for entertainment.
  * Patih promises to supply 500 Surakarta laborers for Batavia *Bovenlanden* public works.
* **12 August 1808 (Title Elevation to Prefects and Ministers):**
  * Governor-General's Decree (*Besluit* 8 Aug. 1808; *Plakaatboek Vol. 15 p. 87*):
  * **Title of Resident in 4 North Coast Residencies exchanged for PREFECT.**
  * **First Resident at native courts designated MINISTER; Second Resident designated SECRETARY.**
  * 2nd Resident S. van Woortman petitions for a salary increase.
* **13–14 August 1808 (Yogyakarta Hospital Facilities):**
  * Hospitaler Doctor G.W. Sentius reports current temporary hospital in European kampong is exposed to southerly winds and inadequate for sick Ambonese soldiers.
  * Recommends constructing a large permanent military hospital in Surakarta and temporary bamboo wards near the Yogyakarta fort.
