### AN Yogya Letters No. 49: Debt Policy, Loss of V.O.C. Authority, & Rise of Purwadipura (March 1807)

* Reference: **A.N. Yogya Letters No. 49 (March 1807 - Waterloo to Engelhard)**
* **18 March 1807:** Waterloo warns against over-elevating Natadiningrat, as it would excessively strengthen Pangeran Natakusuma's court faction. Waterloo plans a spring visit to Semarang.
* **22–23 March 1807 (Sultan HB II's Financial Demands):**
  * HB II accepts N. Engelhard's appointment as President in Semarang.
  * **Pasisir Rent Currency Demand:** HB II refuses V.O.C. copper duits for coastal land rent, demanding payment strictly in **Spanish Reals or Ducats**.
  * **HB II Debt Policy:** Royal children borrow 100 Ronde Reals monthly @ 2 1/2 % interest per month (ridiculed by the Pangerans).
* **Decline of Dutch Authority:** Waterloo admits that over the past 25 years the V.O.C. has **lost much of its authority in the Vorstenlanden**, a loss accelerating due to Java's rapid population growth:
  > *"War threatened from outside — weakness inside:"* (*"Oorlog van buiten — zwakheid van binnen. Is het alleen den Hemel die ons behoed!"*)
* **Rise of Purwadipura:** Sumadiningrat described as a completely broken reed. **Purwadipura** (*"een nicelle schoentje"*) now plays the most dominant role at court, with neither Danureja II nor anyone else able to oppose him.
