Not greedy / covetous for money.*<br>    - **Diplomatic maneuver:** Van Overstraten successfully headed off PB IV’s plan to appoint Pangeran Mangkubumi or Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat as Patih.<br>    - PB IV corresponds with Van Overstraten directly without the knowledge of the courtiers in order to keep the negotiations strictly secret. |
