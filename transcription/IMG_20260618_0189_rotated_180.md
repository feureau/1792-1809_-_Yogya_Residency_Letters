# Transcription: IMG_20260618_0189_rotated_180.jpg

**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260618_0189_rotated_180.jpg`  
**Folio / Reference:** A.N. Letters No. [47 crossed out] 49  
**Date Range:** 5 April 1805 – 8 April 1805  
**Primary Subjects:**  
- HB II's persistent effort to elevate Raden Ayu Sriwulan to Ratu  
- Ratu Kedhaton's staunch opposition; HB II's compromise offer of the title *Ratu Ageng* and the estate of Tegalreja (formerly belonging to HB II's mother, Ratu Kadipaten) rejected  
- HB II grants R. Ayu Sriwulan the royal privilege of a gold *payung* (parasol) at Ngawatreja and in the Kraton  
- Crown Prince's deep hatred for R. Ayu Sriwulan and desire for revenge, noticed by HB II  
- New Grobogan rent / lease terms for Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III  
- Planned hunting expedition to Klaten by Waterloo and Crown Prince; HB II at Ngawatreja orders at least 4 wild tigers captured  
- Palace tragedy and execution: Mas Panji Jayeng-Asmara (former prime royal favorite) slanders a Demang whose sister is an influential Kraton concubine  
- Concubine and R. Ayu Sriwulan engineer Panji's downfall; HB II's fury  
- Panji beheaded while fully clothed before the Srimenganti gate; his followers exiled to the wilderness of Ledaya (Lodhaya)
