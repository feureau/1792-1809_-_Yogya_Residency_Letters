### J.G. van den Berg Memorie van Overgave (August 1806): Pakubuwana IV Character Sketch

* Reference: **Van Alphen - Engelhard No. 239 (Aanw. 1900) No. XXII**
* Document: *Copia Memorie van het Hof van Soeracarta door Johannes Gerardus van den Berg aan Balthasar Fredericus von Liebeherr (Aug. 1806).*

#### Pakubuwana IV Character & Religious Devotion:
* Highly regarded and loved by Javanese subjects, especially religious scholars (*Paapen/Priests*).
* Possesses a tenacious memory (*stal Memorie*). Extremely close to Islamic priests, who exercise great influence over him.
* Large numbers of *Hajis*, *Kiyais*, and *Pradikans* flock to Surakarta from across Java prior to *Garebeg Mulud* (unlike Mangkunagaran or Yogyakarta). PB IV distributes 6–8 silver pillar dollars to each priest, spending vast sums.
* Extremely polite and shy in direct encounters with the Resident; enjoys conversing with the Resident on benches after tournament games.
* Inclined to procrastinate on state decisions.
* Not ashamed to converse with ordinary Europeans; borrows large sums from them (e.g., 68,000 silver Rixdollars borrowed from Barend Nammel).
* Readily gives his word, but breaks commitments on frivolous pretexts; feigns illness to avoid difficult negotiations.
