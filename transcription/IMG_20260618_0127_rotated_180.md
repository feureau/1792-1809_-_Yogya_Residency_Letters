### Archive Entries: July – November 1802 (Tax Farm Reorganization & Rice Yields)

* **Friction with Van IJsseldijk:** HB II ignored HB I's deathbed advice to treat Resident as a brother and heed his counsel.
* **Avarice & Fines:** HB II described as extremely avaricious; fined a palanquin bearer 1 Rupiah for arriving slightly late.
* **Tax Farm Reorganization:** Mangundipura advises HB II to raise tax-farm rates (increased in 33 years from 16,000 to 58,000 Rixdollars). HB II suspicious of advisers.
* **13 July 1802:** Tax-farm reorganization plan abandoned by HB II. Patih Danureja II ordered to cooperate with Mangkupraja regarding Raden Rongga III's excesses at Delanggu. HB II carried in palanquin (*Tandhu*) to the *Dhampar*.
* **Semarang Capitan Cina:** Jan Tian Kong appointed Capitan Cina of Semarang.
* **Trade Difficulties:** Absence of legal protection against HB II's seizures. 50 pikuls of cotton thread supplied annually by Eastern Mangkunagaran.
* **22 July / 3 August 1802:** Engelhard's journey to courts; Raden Rongga III requested to prepare rice supplies from Grobogan.
* **29 September 1802 (Grobogan & Jipang Rice):**
  * Raden Rongga III leases Grobogan tax-farm for 1,800 silver Rixdollars.
  * Delivers 550 *koyangs* of rice from Grobogan (additional supplies expected from Jipang).
  * HB II dispatches 12 detachments against bandit gangs in Yogya.
  * HB II requests abolition of Loano Indigo Factory (yielding only 3–4 pikuls p.a.) so land can be repurposed for pepper cultivation.
* **22 November 1802:** Bandit gangs quiet during W. monsoon; active during E. monsoon. HB II refuses to take measures against Raden Rongga III in Solo affair. Ni Liong Ki provides complete satisfaction as Capitan Cina in Kedu (10 months).
* **30 November 1802:** Natakusuma sends a kris in a gold box and a letter to Engelhard.
