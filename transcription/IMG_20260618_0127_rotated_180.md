## Archival & Historical Notes

- **HB II's Character & Breach of HB I's Admonition:** Resident van den Berg notes that Sultan Hamengkubuwana I had explicitly instructed his son on his deathbed to live with the Resident as a brother and heed his advice. Instead, HB II is depicted as extremely avaricious, paranoid ("one half of his body distrusts the other"), and obsessed with prestige at the expense of others, especially the VOC/Dutch government.
- **Tax-Farm Yields:** Under Mangundipura's advice, Yogya's tax-farm leases rose dramatically over 33 years from 16,000 to 58,000 *Ronde Reals*, but HB II abandoned a further planned restructuring in July 1802.
- **Rangga Prawiradirja III & Delanggu Incident:** Patih Danureja II and Surakarta Patih Mangkupraja are instructed to coordinate action regarding Raden Rangga Prawiradirja III's violent raid/murder at Delanggu. Later in November, HB II refuses to punish Rangga.
- **Chinese Captains:** Mentions Tan Jean Kong as Capitan Cina of Semarang, and Ni Liong-u (Ni Liong Ko) serving satisfactorily for 10 months as Capitan Cina of Kedu.
- **Commodities & Agrarian Policy:**
  - 50 pikuls of cotton thread supplied annually from Eastern Mancanagara.
  - Rice deliveries organized by Raden Rangga: 550 *koyangs* from Grobogan, with additional quotas expected from Jipang; Grobogan tax-farm leased for 1,800 *ronde maaten*.
  - Abolition of the Indigo Factory at Loano due to low yield (3–4 pikuls/year) in favor of expanding pepper plantations.
- **Diplomatic Gifts:** Pangeran Natakusuma's direct diplomatic outreach to Governor Nicolaas Engelhard, sending a kris in a gold box.
