### Archive Entries: April – May 1803 (Loano Pepper, Cakra-winata Execution, & Court Intrigues)

* **24 April 1803 (Loano Pepper Estates):** Pepper plantations in Loano remain owned by HB II (100 cacahs granted for upkeep of common laborers). Regent pays 240 Rixdollars tax (pepper price: 20 stuivers per pikul).
* **17 April 1803 (Kedu Tobacco Tax):** Ni Liong Ki tax on *Gedhong Tembakau* in Kedu yields 8,000–8,500 p.a. + 10,000 *Bea*.
* **19 April 1803:** Envoys of HB II and Ratu sent to Demak with funds to repair mosque (*R.T. Jayengrana*).
* **23 May 1803 (Murder/Execution of Cakra-winata):**
  * R.T. Cakra-winata of Solo killed by *towakris* 5 houses from Solo court at Naranewu. Killed for court intrigues during Resident Van Nijvenheim's tenure (similar to Arung Binang / Puspanegara during Resident Palm's term).
  * R. Ayu Cakra-winata also killed.
  * *Context:* PB IV followed Van Reede to Salatiga to take leave in 1796. Van Reede promised that if he became Governor-General, PB IV would recover part of Mataram; Cakra-winata knew too much about this secret promise.
  * PB IV attempts to poison Cakra-winata with herbal medicine (*Jamu* prepared by Ratu) for alleged illness; Cakra-winata refuses European medicines.
* **25 May 1803:** Cakra-winata strangled with cotton thread (*garrotted*).
* **31 May 1803:** Cakra-winata strangled with thread by 2 white-robed priests who pray for him using 2 strong cords.
* **PB IV Stand:** PB IV claims right on 8 May to execute subjects in his residence; gives Cakra-winata a cup of *Jamu* and ointment. On refusal, PB IV cuts off all relations and refuses food from Residency kitchen. Cakra-winata carried on intrigues with PB IV's wife. Van Nijvenheim follows PB IV to Salatiga knowing of Van Reede's secret plan to conquer Demak. Recommends Pangeran Prangwedana as possible replacement for Sunan upon PB IV's death (Prangwedana married to sister of Cakra-winata).
