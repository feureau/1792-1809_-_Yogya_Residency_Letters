## Archival & Historical Notes

- **Loano Pepper Plantations & Kedu Tobacco Tax:**
  - Pepper cultivation in Loano is maintained on land owned by HB II (100 *cacah* assigned for laborers' upkeep); Regent pays 240 *R.R.* tax, with pepper valued at 20 *stuivers* per pikul.
  - Ni Longi's tax farm on the *Gêdhong Têmbakau* (tobacco warehouse) in Kêdhu brings in 8,000–8,500 *R.R.* annually plus 10,000 *Bea*, maintained on its former footing.
  - Royal envoys from HB II and Ratu sent with funds to Raden Tumenggung Jayêngrana at Demak for repairing the Grand Mosque of Demak.
- **The Fall and Execution of Raden Tumenggung Cakrawinata:**
  - Cakrawinata, an influential Surakarta courtier closely allied with outgoing Resident Van Nijvenheim, was secretly executed (strangled/garrotted with two strong cotton threads after receiving two white-robed priests to pray for him; earlier reports claimed he was stabbed with a *towakris* at Naranèwu near the court). His wife, R. Ayu Cakrawinata, was also killed.
  - Three distinct political reasons are cited for PB IV ordering Cakrawinata's execution:
    1. An illicit love affair with a sister of Sunan Pakubuwana IV.
    2. Complicity and dangerous knowledge concerning the 1796 secret meeting in Salatiga between PB IV and Governor Pieter Gerardus van Overstraten / Van Rheede, in which Van Rheede allegedly promised that if he became Governor-General, PB IV would be given back parts of the Mataram realm (Yogyakarta). Cakrawinata and Nijvenheim were privy to these discussions, sparking rumours of PB IV's designs on Mataram.
    3. Cakrawinata's visit to Demak mosque to lobby the VOC to back Pangeran Prangwedana (Mangkunagaran heir, married to Cakrawinata's sister) as successor to PB IV upon the Sunan's death.
- **PB IV and Resident Van Nijvenheim's Rupture:**
  - On 8 May 1803, PB IV visited Resident Van Nijvenheim's residence offering a cup of herbal *jamu* (prepared by the Ratu) and ointment for his illness. Suspecting poison, Nijvenheim refused both, opting for European medicine.
  - Following this refusal, PB IV severed personal relations with Nijvenheim and refused to accept daily meals from the Residency kitchen.
