Complicity and dangerous knowledge concerning the 1796 secret meeting in Salatiga between PB IV and Governor Pieter Gerardus van Overstraten / Van Rheede, in which Van Rheede allegedly promised that if he became Governor-General, PB IV would be given back parts of the Mataram realm (Yogyakarta). Cakrawinata and Nijvenheim were privy to these discussions, sparking rumours of PB IV's designs on Mataram.<br>    3. Suspecting poison, Nijvenheim refused both, opting for European medicine.<br>  - Following this refusal, PB IV severed personal relations with Nijvenheim and refused to accept daily meals from the Residency kitchen. |
