# Transcription of IMG_20260618_0111.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260618_0111.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 48: *Lijst van de verhooging van de tolle*, 10 April 1802)
- **Date Range:** 1802-04-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Conclusion and official colophon of the 10 April 1802 Yogyakarta tollgate tariff schedule (*Lijst van de verhooging van de tolle*); establishes revised duties on rice from Payaman, Kedu, and Pesantren, cloth via Pekalongan and Kebumen, inter-court grain shipments to Surakarta, raw cotton (*kapas*) from Pepedan, warehouse tobacco (*gedhong têmbakau*), and the joint Yogyakarta–Surakarta toll stations of Subebek and Batur; concludes with the official dating at Yogyakarta on 10 April 1802 and a contemporary Dutch administrative note confirming transmission to Resident Jan Willem Neijenheuis.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Johannes Gerardus van den Berg, Jan Willem Neijenheuis, Prof. Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djoyocarta / Djoyo), Surakarta (Solo), Kedu (Kedoe), Payaman (Paijaman), Pesantren (Passantran), Kebumen (Keboemen), Pekalongan (Paccalongang), Pepedan, Subebek (Soebebek), Batur (Battoor).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Bandar (toll farm / pagt), Gedhong Têmbakau (tobacco warehouse), Gedhong Subebek, Gedhong Batur, Ronde Reaal (R.R. / Spanish dollar), Corge (score of 20 pieces), Kapas (cotton), Stuiver.

  * **Johannes Gerardus van den Berg** (`V.d.B. / vdBerg`): Listed under *Residents of Yogyakarta* (line 329). Resident of Yogyakarta (1798–1803).

* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Yogyakarta (Mataram / Djogja)** (`Yogya / Djogja`): Listed under *Places* (line 405). Capital of the Sultanate and center of residency toll administration.
  * **Surakarta (Solo / Sala)** (`Solo / Sala`): Listed under line 461. Court city and realm of the Sunanate of Surakarta.
  * **Kedu (Këdhu)** (`Këdhu / Kedu`): Listed under line 468. Rich central agricultural province traversed by the main Vorstenlanden trade artery.
  * **Pekalongan**: Northern coastal pasisir trading port and residency.
* **Terms:**
  * **Bandar / Pagt**: Listed under line 485. Tollgate tax-farm leaseholder or toll station (*tolhek*).
  * **Ronde Reaal (R.R.) / Spanish Dollar**: Standard silver coinage unit in the Netherlands East Indies.
  * **Corge**: Unit of count representing 20 pieces (used in commercial textile and tobacco trading).
  * **Gedhong Têmbakau**: Official state tobacco warehouse and transit customs depot.
