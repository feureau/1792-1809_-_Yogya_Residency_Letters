**"Suspicious nature of HB II knows no bounds!"**  
Jealous of his greatness.  
cf. Touchy about property  
cf. Pepper gardens in Loano laid out by V.O.C. = Refusal of HB II to see them extended.

---

Suggestion of Wat (& approval of Eng.) to HB II to turn over petty criminals to V.O.C. (? to work in pepper gardens) but Refusal of HB II says it would make him ashamed in front of his subjects.

HB II decides to Ban petty criminals to MN. [Mancanagara]  
& to order Rangga PwD [Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III] to see that they never entered Yogya again.  
**Rangga puts them to death** = in order that runs no risk of none Returning to Yogya & Rangga Falling into Disgrace & HB II  
HB II very little vision of / & understanding consequences of his orders.
