### Peasant Flight, Criminal Banishments, and Demographics (1806)

* **Peasant Migration & Return:** Up to 2/3 of a village will flee to escape extreme extortions, returning once exactions cease. Local *Lurahs* lend ploughs/buffaloes to farmers lacking equipment, but charge high fees.
* **HB II Suspicion & Pepper Restrictions:**
  * HB II extremely suspicious and jealous of his royal authority.
  * Refuses to allow expansion of V.O.C. pepper gardens in Loano or establishment of new pepper gardens near Yogya.
* **Criminal Banishment to Mangkunagaran:**
  * Waterloo suggests turning petty criminals over to V.O.C. to work in pepper gardens; HB II refuses, stating it would shame him before his subjects.
  * HB II orders petty criminals banished to Mangkunagaran, instructing Raden Rongga PWP to ensure they never return to Yogya. **Raden Rongga executes the banished criminals** to prevent any from returning and bringing him into disgrace with HB II.
* **Agricultural Innovations & Population Growth:**
  * Introduction of heavy Dutch deep-ploughing rejected; Javanese draft animals lack strength for deeper ploughs, and farmers resist agricultural innovations.
  * **Demographic Surge:** Rapid population growth over past 5 years evidenced by age structure (numerous children under 5). **Birth-to-death ratio: 7 births for every 5 deaths.**
