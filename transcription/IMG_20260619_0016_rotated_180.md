### Prangwedana Marriage Proposals, Financial Debts, and Exile Lines

* **Proposed Marriage (Nov. 1803):** Prangwedana attempts to secure PB IV's 13-year-old daughter in marriage to ingratiate himself with Surakarta court (Prangwedana aged 40). Engelhard advised against allowing marriage as it would create severe V.O.C. complications and increase Prangwedana's heavy debts.
* **Gambling & Entourage:** Heavy card playing and gambling at Prangwedana's residence risks financial ruin. Thieves and robbers in princely entourages steal to supply funds. Resident Van den Berg strictly bans gambling in the Residency house (unlike predecessor Van Nijvenheim).
* **Travel:** Prangwedana frequently visits Semarang (3–4x under Van Reede; 1x under Van den Berg in 1803–06), accompanied by Adjutant Descentier or Ordonnans Pontier.
* **Exile of R. Panji Manguningrat:**
  * Prangwedana's brother **Raden Panji Manguningrat** sent into exile in Batavia; receives 1,200 Ronde Reals p.a. allowance (PB IV pays 400 R.R. at Mulud and Puasa).
  * Manguningrat attempted 3 escapes from Solo under Van Nijvenheim (to Semarang, Jimangsnag, and Pepedhan), trying to assemble villagers to attack Prangwedana. Recommended that he never be allowed to return from Batavia.
