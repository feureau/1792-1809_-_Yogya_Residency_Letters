## Content

[Header in red ink:]

**MN II + m. + d. of PB IV**

---

PWD [Prangwedana] Tries to get one of legal daughters of PB IV in marriage = & Ingratiate himself & Solo court.  
Talks to N. Eng. about it in Semarang  
**Nov. 1803** [*underlined in red*] = But Eng.'s views unknown.

PB IV seems to Be prepared to allow marriage = although d. only 13 years old & PWD already 40. / But more than meets the eye to situation.  
But such a marriage would cause great Difficulty for V.O.C.  
But PWD not well-disposed to PB IV & Brothers.  
.: should Be given as little opportunity as possible To meet PB IV.

---

[*Bordered with red horizontal lines:*]

**PWD Heavily in debt & preparations for marriage.**  
**should not be allowed to contract further debts.**

[*In left margin, double red vertical rule:* **//**]

* much gambling / Card playing & at House of PWD.  
  would lead to Fin. Ruin.  

* Robbers / thieves in Entourage of princes = get money for them.  
* **Ysseldijk** = allows gambling in Res. House = **VdB** never permits it.  
* PWD loves going now / then to Semarang.  
  goes 3/4 x during period of Van Rheede.  
  & 1 x when VdB in Solo (1803–06).  

* **Adjutant Degentier / Ordonnans Pontier** [*underlined in red*] = ? accompany PWP.

---

**R. Panji Manguningrat sent by PWD to Batavia.**  
[*In left margin, in red ink:*] **Mănguningrat sent into exile in Batavia.**  

* Brother of PWP.  
* & lives on Allowance of 1,200 Rond. Real p.a.  
  PB IV pays 400 R.R. (200 at Mulud / 200 Puasa).  

* Mănguningrat had fled 3 x from Solo under **Van Ysseldijk**:  
  1 x to Nima / 1 x to Timangsung / 1 x to Pipadan.  

* Tries to gather village people & arms to attack PWP.  
* [*In left margin, red asterisk:* **\***] .: MN [Mangunadrat / Manguningrat] should never be allowed back from Batavia.
