**Primary Subjects:**  
- Proposal to deploy 800 men from Vice-Admiral Hartsinck's squadron (wintering in Oosthoek) to Central Java

### [23 December 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
Eng to Waterloo  
23 Dec. 1805

800 men might be spared from Vice Adm. Hartsinck's squadron – wintering in Oosthoek.  
Nat. Artillery Company to Bayalali.  
Adm. H's force to be conveyed down Sala river to Sala.  
Hartsinck's men to be placed between Sala / Yogya.  
necessary preparations to be made for their reception.  
to be encamped at Bayalali.

*[To right in red ink:]*  
**Idea of using Hartsinck's Bat**

---

### [27 December 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
Waterloo – Eng.  
27 Dec. 1805.

Bayalali suitable for Army camp.  
But too far (15½ hrs journey) from Yogya.  
would take 4 days march.  
& unhealthy because of winds in Rainy season.  
Prices of food in Bayalali would rise steeply as was found during recent troubles in Sala *[red underline:]* **1790.**  
Jangkiran / Delanggu more suitable.  
Better ½ Hartsinck's forces to Sala / ½ to Yogya.  
Yogya more vulnerable from S. Coast than Sala.  
Sunan / Sultan would be prepared to provide food for men of Hartsinck's squadron.  
Difficulty of getting down Sala river in Rainy season.

---

### [5 January 1806]

*[Left margin:]*  
Eng – Wat.  
5 Jan. 1806  
*[Red vertical line in margin]*

Hartsinck informs Eng. that until Java actually attacked by a foreign enemy = he could not allow sailors / marines to be garrisoned in Central Java.  
.: would have to rely on Central Jav. Rulers.  
No Reinforcements would be sent from Bat / Semarang garrisons.
