### Naval Battalion Strategy & Boyolali Encampment Disputes (December 1805 – January 1806)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 20.86 (23 Dec. 1805)**
* **23 December 1805 (Engelhard to Waterloo):**
  * Engelhard proposes deploying 800 sailors/marines from Vice-Admiral Hartsinck's naval squadron (wintering in *Oosthoek*) and a Native Artillery Company to Boyolali via the Solo River.
  * Troops to encamp at Boyolali to cover both Solo and Yogya. Red ink: **Joining of Hartsinck's Battalion.**
* **27 December 1805 (Waterloo to Engelhard):**
  * Waterloo objects to Boyolali: 15 1/2 hours (4 days' march) from Yogya, subject to unhealthy rainy-season winds, and liable to trigger severe food inflation (as during 1790 Solo troubles).
  * Recommends Tangkilan or Delanggu as better sites, or splitting Hartsinck's forces 1/2 to Solo and 1/2 to Yogya (Yogya being more vulnerable to South Coast landings). Difficulty of navigating Solo River during rainy season noted.
* **5 January 1806 (Hartsinck Refusal):**
  * **Vice-Admiral Hartsinck informs Engelhard that unless Java is actively invaded by a foreign enemy, he will not permit sailors or marines to be garrisoned in Central Java.**
  * Courts must rely on native rulers; no reinforcements sent from Batavia or Semarang garrisons.
