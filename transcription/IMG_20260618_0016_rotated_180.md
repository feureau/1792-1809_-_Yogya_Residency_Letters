### 1792 August Van Overstraten Conference & Solo Succession

* Red ink annotation: **1792 Aug. Van Ov. Conference.**

* **18 Jul. 1847 (Alg. Secr. to Res. Yogya):** Able to find Verbaal & Conference in Bundle 1743–1792 & Av. Baron v.R. tot de Parkeler 1792. Make a copy from folio 188–248.
* **9 Oct. 1847 (Id. to Res. Nala):** After long thought GG/RvI agreed to marriage MN III – d. Kedhaton.
* **Komm. Geh. 23 Sept. 1847 La E10:** PB IV & Brothers in 1792 conference (Kartasura / Solo Kraton).
* **Fol. 188:** Conference Van Ov – PB IV & Brothers 1792 (Wed. 8 Aug. 1792 at Kartasura). Various Actes for CP & MN I.
* **Fol. 190:** Decision to continue discussion in separate conference.
* **Fol. 191:** PB IV in Kartasura conf. wants eldest son – Gusti Timur recognized as CP = To be under guardianship of Van Ov & PB IV. Dies while still a minor.
* **Fol. 192:** Van Ov tells PB IV that request granted.
* **Mangkunagaran (Mon. 13 Aug. 1792):** Timur to be proclaimed CP (later PB V).
* **Fol. 154 (10 Apr.):** Reminds PB IV that in conference 10 Apr. had promised to uphold rights of Prangwedana when MN I died + 4,000 cacahs to remain in the family.
* **Fol. 195–197:** Prangwedana to be allowed title of P. Adip. or any other title govnt. wishes. Wilaran of Banyumas/Distr. Panjarlan = Pamerden + all other lands of MN. Over & Above 4,000 cacahs to be returned to PB IV on his death.
* **Fol. 199:** If PWP dies another prince from no. to be recognized.
* **Fol. 200:** PWP to be on same rank as Mangkubumi = to follow in rank immediately after Crown Prince.
* **Lands (Fol. 201):** Re new measurement of lands in Mataram. 1773 Book of Klepu = not included because uncultivated V.O.C. Not to be claimed by PB IV on instance of PB IV.
* **Fol. 202:** PB IV to drop demand for new measurement & any difficulties Klaten Yogya/Solo to be referred to V.O.C.
* **Fol. 203:** Need for Fort Between Solo / Yogya = Both courts should work to build & maintain jointly by Solo/Yogya.
