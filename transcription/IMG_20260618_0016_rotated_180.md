### Conference Record: Van Overstraten with PB IV

*(Wed. 8 Aug. 1792 at Kartasura / Solo Kraton; conference fol. 188ff. PB IV + brothers of Sunan: Buminata, Mangkubumi / Res. Solo Parkeleer)*

In conference 10 Apr. 1792 at Kartasura — various issues discussed:

#### 1. Crown Prince

- **fol. 190**: Decision to continue discussion in separate conference.
- **fol. 191**: PB IV in Kartasura conf. wants eldest son — **Gusti Timur** recognised as CP = to be under guardianship of Van Overstraten if PB IV dies while still a minor.
- **fol. 192**: Van O tells PB IV that request granted $\rightarrow$ **Mon. 13 Aug. 1792**: Timur to be proclaimed CP (*later PB V*).

#### 2. Mangkunagaran

- **fol. 194**: Reminds PB IV that in conference 10 Apr. had promised to uphold rights of **Prangwedana** when MN I died, + 4,000 cacahs to remain in the family.
- **fol. 195–197**: Prangwedana to be allowed title of *P. Adip.* or any other title govrmt. wishes.
- **Wedaran of Banyumas / Distr. Panjarlan = Pamerdèn**: + all other lands of MN over & above 4,000 cacahs to be returned to PB IV on his death.
- **p. 199**: If PWD dies, another prince from house to be recognized.
- **fol. 200**: PWD to be on same rank as Mangkubumi = to follow in rank immediately after Crown Prince.

#### 3. Lands

- **fol. 201**: Of new measurement of lands in Mataram. 1773 Book of Klèpu = not included because uncultivated; not to be claimed by PB IV.
- **fol. 202**: V.O.C. — PB IV to drop demand for new measurement // + any difficulties Yogya / Solo to be referred to V.O.C.

#### 4. Klathen

- **fol. 203**: Need for Fort between Solo / Yogya $\rightarrow$ Both courts should shoulder costs; to be built & maintained jointly by Solo / Yogya.
