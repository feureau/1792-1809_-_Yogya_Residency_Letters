## Complete Diplomatic Transcription

SUNAN PB IV gives sons of Ki TAPTAJANI 3,000 cacahs of land as mark of esteem (only 1 hour distant from Sala).

TRANSLATE a certain BOOK for PB IV:  
**SIERATTALMUSTAKKIEM:**  
which previously no one in a position to do it  
causes great success for them.

? TAPTAJANI may receive land also from PB IV.  
wishes to go to MEKKA so he can live his life in peace without being bound to either COURT.

TAPTAJANI & 2 sons must be seen as extremely dangerous SUBJECTS:  
(? Arabs / learn to speak Javanese)  
very IMPORTANT INFLUENCE on PB IV.  
THIS PRIEST must always be regarded as dangerous.  
But especially in this period: Best to exile himself & sons from Central Java –  
"would loose all his influence in courts."
