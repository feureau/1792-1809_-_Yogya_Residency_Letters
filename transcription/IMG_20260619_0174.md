### AVJ Letters: Eastern Mancanagara Timber Grievances & Dragoon Guards (April – June 1809)

* **Eastern Mancanagara Regents' Grievances:**
  * Eastern Mancanagara Regents submit formal complaints to HB II regarding government timber monopolies:
    1. Direct government timber-felling in native forests ruins regional timber commerce.
    2. Subordinate wood-cutters' production is seized directly by government officials.
    3. Regents prohibited from leasing timber rights to Chinese or coastal traders, destroying the primary rural livelihood (*few sawahs exist in timber districts*).
* **14 April 1809 (Madiun & Grobogan Discussions):** Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III and Grobogan Regent R.T. Sasrakusuma confer in Yogyakarta regarding timber grievances.
* **23 May 1809:** Recommendations submitted to Batavia for fair compensation to common wood-cutters to relieve peasant suffering while satisfying government timber needs.
* **10 June 1809 (Kraton Dragoon Escort):** HB II requests that despite temporary Dragoon troop absences, a Dragoon detachment remain at Yogyakarta to guard the water gates and escort the Sultan on the *Paseban*.
