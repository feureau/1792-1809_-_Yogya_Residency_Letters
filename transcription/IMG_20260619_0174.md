### [Transcription]

On arrival of E. MN Regents COMPLAIN of DIFFICULTIES TO HB II:  

[Red ink margin: *Complaint of E.MN Regents about Govnmt. Felling of Timber!*]

(1) Since Govnmt starts felling timber in their own woods / forests — affects their commerce.  
(2) given orders to their subordinates to fell wood but to no avail — because Govnmt takes it all for themselves.  
(3) can no longer farm out rights to fell timber to Chinese / Inhabitants of N. Coast.  
Loose foremost source of LIVELIHOOD — very Hard for COMMON MAN.  
Few ricefields — produce little  
.. For great / small wood commerce source of LIVELIHOOD.

*14 April 1809*  
*Res Yogya -> Sala:*  
Head Regent of MN R. Rangga PWD III:  
arrives in Yogya Discussion about Timber — R.T. Sasrakusuma, Regent of GROBOGAN.

*24 April 1809*  
*Pekalongan -> Yogya*  
Res of P speaks about ROAD through the wilderness of KADAWONG.

*23 May 1809*  
*-> Bat.*  
DISCUSSIONS ON TIMBER: arrangement should be made for Fair Payment to common man, & Insp. Gen. of Houtbosschen & HB II's Inhabitants as well for the CONVENIENCE of the GOVNMT & for the feelings of the SULTAN & Inhabitants.

*10 June 1809*  
*-> Bat*  
Although Dragoons must be absent for a short space of time from Yogya:  
HB II asks that Dragoons should remain behind enough to guard the kraton gates / and escort HB II when he appears on the Pasarbaan: according to old usage.
