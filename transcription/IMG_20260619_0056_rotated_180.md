### Pasisir Intermarriage Policy & Prangwedana Inheritance Claims (1808)

* Reference: **N. Engelhard MVO (May 1808) / Van Alphen - Engelhard 235**
* **Pasisir Intermarriage Restrictions:**
  * Coastal *Bupati* families resist intermarriages with royal families due to ruinous expense and danger (daughters remain locked in the *Dalem* and isolated from families).
  * Royal daughters/princesses incur heavy financial demands on coastal Regencies for court tribute.
  * **Policy Recommendation:** Marshal Daendels should break all marital connections between Pasisir Regencies and native courts permanently.
* **Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) Status:**
  * Correspondence regarding former Resident Baron van Reede's **theft and appropriation of Mangkunagara I's estate/inheritance** (Nov. 1803 / Aug. 1805).
  * Prangwedana was overly deferential to PB IV and Van Reede upon his appointment. His power and influence remain far inferior to Mangkunagara I's, though he receives 2,000 Np.D. for troop maintenance.
  * **Bitter Hatred:** Intense rivalry between Prangwedana, Pangeran Mangkubumi, and PB IV. Resident advised to mediate peace and treat Prangwedana on an equal footing with Mangkubumi and Buminata.
