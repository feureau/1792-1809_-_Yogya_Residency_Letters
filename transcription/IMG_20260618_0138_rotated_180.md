### Archive Entries: April – May 1805 (Pirate Raids on Pacitan, Cilacap, & Prigi)

* **Pacitan Shore Interactions:** 3 buffaloes exchanged by pirates for 200 Reals worth of benzoin, ginger, iron, and steel. Pirates stay 9 days watching cock-fights and evening *Tandhak* dances; locals refuse to reveal inland road routes.
* **19 April 1805:** HB II requests two 3-lb cannons and 24 cannonballs to fortify Pacitan harbor.
* **26 April 1805:** Surakarta reports Negarawedi pirates penetrating inland. On 29 Besar 1729, pirates clash with Regents of Pasuruan, Bangil, and Malang at Desa Gondang Legi (Patih of Malang wounded).
* **8 May 1805 (Reksamerta Escape Statement):**
  * Reksamerta (Cirebon Javanese) escapes at Pulo Tempo after 2 months' captivity by 19 pirate vessels (each crewed by 20 men, 2 6-lb cannons, muskets, and blunderbusses).
  * Identifies pirate leaders as **Raja Datuk Namak** and **Raja Unus** (Timorese/Bugis Malay-speakers).
  * Pirates capture inhabitants and 2 cannons at Cilacap; Reksamerta escapes by swimming to shore at Negarawedi.
* **Official Report (R.T. Purwadipura to Numadiningrat):**
  * Cilacap Demangs (Nagayuda, Martarana, Suraminggala, Kartajaya) attacked; 2 beheaded by pirates.
  * Pirate force under Raja Datuk Namak, Raja Unus, Patih Puk Rapue, and Chinese Encik Putik comprises 35 ships (50–60 men each, 4 large cannons, 20 small cannons) at Prigi; conduct nightly *Nayub* dances and daytime cock-fights.
