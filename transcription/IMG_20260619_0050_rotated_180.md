### [Diplomatic Transcription]

**Daendels Instruction for Lt. Col. Reinking & 2e Res.**  
**22 Jun. 1808**  

- To be in charge of Residency during absence of 1st Res. **van Braam**.  

---

**Waterloo to von Liebeherr**,  
**21 Sept. 1806**  
Impossible for 3rd Instructor of Marine School at Semarang **Lt. [H.P.D.] Kortzius** & Bombardier Cadet **Du Bois** to survey coast from **Mancingan - Pacitan** because of rocks.  
To continue + survey of Sunan / Sultan's Lands.  

---

**von Liebeherr to Engelhard**,  
**1 Nov. 1806**  
Need for Gov'nm't Economies in minting coins.  
200,000 Rijksdaalders less would be minted in coming year.  
To be covered by Paper / Copper money.  
Economies necessary to avoid confusion in finances.  
But Impossible to economise on expenses in Solo:  

[left margin in red ink]:  
**Res in Solo also takes over Tax Farm of Domains**  

Heavy Debt & Interest of **von Liebeherr** in Solo suffers losses through European war.  
1st Res. only paid 15,000 in paper p.a. ∴ has to get by [red underline]by taking over Tax Farm of Sunan's Domains.[/red underline]  
But heavy costs of Administration - only makes a profit out of Bird's Nests.  
All payments to Co'nat have to take place in silver money.  
[red underline]No longer able to make a profit on Opium monopoly[/red underline] = Chinese / Jav. too poor to buy opium.  
V.O.C. must pay *straatgeld* payments in order to keep up honour at court.  

---

**Id. to Id.**  
**3 Dec. 1806**  
Best part of garrison in Solo at ready in case need to take action to prevent disturbances.  

[left margin in red ink]:  
**Pellew's squadron**  

Appearance of Brit. warships in Bay of Batavia.  
Burning / destroying Dutch shipping on Pasisir.  
PBD / Sunan warned to stand by to give help to Dutch if necessary.  
Would not march ½ of Solo garrison to Semarang unless receives direct order from **Eng.**  

---

**Id. to Id.**  
**7 Dec. 1806**  
**PB IV** asks whether setting up of Civil guard in Solo composed of leading citizens not useful at this time.  
To defend Kraton / Loji.  

---

**Id. to Id.**  
**5 Jan. [1807]**  
*(see Eng. to von L., 15 Jan. 1806 \*)*  
**PB IV** & Crown Prince + rest of Royal Family to Residency House for N. Year's Day Celebration.  
Tries to repair cooled friendship between **CP** - **P. Mangkubumi**.
