### [Transcription]

**<u>Dec. 1808 – 1809</u>**  
*(ANRI Dj. Br. 22)*

- Instructions sent to Regent of Panolan, Raden Tumenggung Natawijaya, and Raden Rangga Prawiradirja (Wedana Bupati of Madiun / Mancanagara Timur) regarding stolen buffaloes:
  - 48 stolen from Tuban blandong timber-cutters.
  - 46 stolen from Trambalang.
- Regent of Wirosari, Raden Tumenggung Yudakusuma, required to make good / return 7 buffaloes.
- *19 Dec.*: Two messengers / retainers of the Yogyakarta Crown Prince (*Pangeran Adipati Anom*, later Hamengkubuwana III) arrested while travelling between Demak and Semarang.
- *24 Dec. 1808*: Governor-General H.W. Daendels issues an order strictly restricting gunpowder salutes at the royal courts.
