### ANIP Nationaal No. 22: Solo River Restitution & Gunpowder Ceilings (Dec. 1808 – Jan. 1809)

* Reference: **ANIP Nationaal No. 22 (Dec. 1808 – Jan. 1809)**
* **Restitution of Stolen Buffaloes:**
  * HB II issues orders to Panolan Regent R.T. Natawijaya and sends letters to Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III in Madiun regarding stolen livestock (48 buffaloes from Tuban *blandhongs*; 46 from Trambalang).
  * Wirasari Regent R.T. Yudakusuma required to restore 7 buffaloes to Mandutuku *blandhongs* in Rembang.
  * *Indemnity:* Chiefs of *blandhongs* paid 5 *Rondemaaten* each to purchase replacement Jipang cattle.
* **19 December 1808:** Arrest of 2 Crown Prince's messengers travelling from Demak to Semarang.
* **4 January 1809 (Daendels' Gunpowder Restrictions):**
  * Wiese receives Daendels' order of 24 Dec. 1808 (communicated via Comm. Chanteauvieux) **banning all gunpowder expenditures for 24-gun salutes on Javanese feast days**, unless specifically authorized by the Governor-General against cash payment.
