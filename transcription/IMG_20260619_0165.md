### ANIP Nationaal No. 22: Powder Magazine, Kraton Appointments, & Solo Maneuvers (Jan. 1809)

* **9–14 January 1809:** Capt. Mollet and Dragoons arrive in Yogyakarta. Wiese requests building a **new powder magazine** in the fort to house ammunition for incoming Mangkunagaran contingents attending *Garebeg Mulud*. 10 cases of opium requested for Yogyakarta.
* **19 January 1809:** Johan Redrik Bocklee (European coachman at Yogyakarta Kraton) dies. Corporal Lucas Ubenveld proposed as replacement Sergeant.
* **22 January 1809:** Daendels grants 500 lbs of gunpowder to the Sultan (50 lbs used for ceremonial salutes when Crown Prince and nobles visit Residency house post-*Besar*).
* **26 January 1809 (Surakarta Military Maneuvers Intelligence):**
  * Patih Danureja II reports rumors of unusually large military exercises (*Exercitien*) in Surakarta under PB IV.
  * **R.T. Natadiningrat and Kyai Sindunegara dispatched to Surakarta to investigate.**
