### Plakaatboek Vol. XV pp. 62–63: Official Salaries, Translators, & Ceremonial Regulations

* Reference: **Plakaatboek Vol. XV pp. 62–63 (27–28 July 1808)**
* **Salary Regulations (p. 62):** Secretarial staff salaries fixed: Secretaries-General (1,000 rds/month), 1st Commis (250 rds/month), 2nd/3rd Commis (150 rds/month), clerks (50–75 rds/month).
* **Demak 2nd Regent Abolition (27 July 1808):** Abolition of the 2nd Regent post in Demak (saves 30,000 rds/year in administrative expenses).
* **Translator Appointments (28 July 1808):** Appointment of 4 Javanese and 2 Malay translators in Semarang (16 rds/month).
* **Ceremonial Regulation (28 July 1808):** *Reglement voor het ceremonieel in acht te nemen door de residenten bij de hoven te Soerakarta en te Djokjokarta* (Articles 1–3).
