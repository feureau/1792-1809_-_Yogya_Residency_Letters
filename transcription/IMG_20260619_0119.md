### Archive Entries: August 1808 (Raden Rongga Reaction & Yogyakarta Military Hospital)

* Reference: **MvO Waterloo 1808 / Pj. Br. 23 (4–9 Aug. 1808)**
* **Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III Attitude:**
  * Raden Rongga III displays a dejected and tearful demeanor when evaluating Daendels' new ceremonial rules: *"If not a trick of this 'sly fox', indicates Rangga is prepared to counsel acceptance of ceremonial, but is highly embarrassed about it."*
* **7 August 1808 (HB II Ceremonial Objections):**
  * HB II replies to Daendels accepting the draft ceremonial in principle, but requesting revisions to 3 specific articles. Engelhard advises Daendels to grant the changes to maintain peace with HB II.
* **Grobogan Tax Farm Dispute (6 Aug. 1808):** Grobogan Regent R.T. Sasrakusuma and Raden Rongga III protest government intervention in Panawangan *Bandar* and demand the re-appointment of Chinese tax farmer Babah Hockong (who had already paid 1,500 Np.D. in fees).
* **Military Hospital Established in Yogyakarta (9 Aug. 1808):**
  * Heavy sickness (*kwaade dampen*) among Ambonese garrison troops in Yogyakarta Fort.
  * Government purchases Lt. Nehalt's 2 riverside houses for Np.D. 1,500 to establish a dedicated military hospital.
* **King Louis Birthday (9 Aug. 1808):** Celebrated with a 45-gun salute and garden illuminations.
