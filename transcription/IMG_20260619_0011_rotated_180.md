## Content

[Continuation of Waterloo's responses to Engelhard's questionnaire:]

**(10) ? Inhabitants of Desas own land / ricefields:**

[*In left margin:* red asterisk **\*** and double red vertical rule **//**]

* greatest part of Desa inhabitants own their own Ricefields  

[all land divided into cacahs owned by Sultan.]

* only mountain dwellers / Blandhong people in Teak Forests excepted.
* & coolies on Roads who find it easy to earn a living  
  often employed for Trading purposes by Chinese / Arabs.

As time goes by courts would get economically / politically stronger viz-à-viz Dutch.  
V.O.C. should bide its time in asserting its superiority to Yogya / Solo.

---

**(11) ? Nature of Land Tenure / Permanent or not?**

[*In left margin:* red asterisk **\*** and double red vertical rule **//**]

* Some Districts 50 men can live on 1 jung of land  
  in others only 2–4.  

* size of a jung in one District differs from other.
* Sawahs split up in a Heterogeneous / irregular Fashion  
  But each Farmer can recognise his own plot.

[*In left margin:*]

**Security of Tenure But not of Office**  
**See Crawfurd.** [*marked with red vertical rule // and asterisk \**]  

* Usual that a grandson remains in possession of same lands that had been owned by his grandfather.
* very little exchange of land unless Lurah / Bêkêl of a village takes the land for himself & gives it to another.  
  Reasonably common.

---

**(12) ? Size of a jung of sawah. whether proportion of Ricefields / Inhabitants in each village correct.**

* Some Desas Better administered by Lurahs and have more pop. than others.
* Some **orang mênumpang** in each village who have no land, But numbers cannot be estimated.
* Taken & mountain dwellers / Blandhong people who have no land form a very small % of whole.

* Great Differences in size of a jung in Different kabupatèn.
* Usually Jav. who own no sawah = perform corvée services for others.
* But most prajurits have land  
  also some prajurits of Sultan own no land. But few in number.
