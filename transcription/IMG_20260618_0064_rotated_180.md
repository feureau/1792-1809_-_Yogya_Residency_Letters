### Archive Entries: February 1796 (Kaliwungu Revolt & Bupatiship Corruption)

* Reference: **Van Ov. to Alting (13 Feb. 1796) - N.O.K. 44**
* **Revolt in Kaliwungu:** Local population in open revolt (*Gowinding / Patios*) against Bupati **Raden Tumenggung Natadiningrat** (formerly Sura). Javanese translator Van den Berg dispatched to investigate.
* **Corrupt Governance:** Natadiningrat dismissed large numbers of Demangs, Mantris, and Lurahs without cause, selling offices to the highest bidders in his entourage. He accepted bribes for positions without delivering appointments, debauched subjects' wives, increased corvée, and cut sawah sizes to lease roadfront plots.
* **Dismissal:** Natadiningrat confesses to abuses and is dismissed.
* **Local Demands & Prophecy:** Inhabitants demand a replacement Bupati from the traditional ruling family that governed Kaliwungu for 200 years. Reference to prophecy of Kyai Gedhe Mataram (Sultan Agung) that only descendants of Sumadiwirya (son of P. Lempeh-yangan) should rule Kaliwungu.
