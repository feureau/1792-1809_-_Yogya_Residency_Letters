| **13 February 1796** | ### [Header]<br><br>Van Overstraten to Alting, 13 Feb. 1796  <br>N.O.K. 47 ("Samarang Jan – Juni 1796")  <br>*[Red ink heading:]* **R.T. Natadiningrat / Disturbances in Kaliwungu**<br><br>### [Transcription]<br><br>#### 1. Peasant Demonstration & Open Revolt in Kaliwungu<br><br>- Severe social unrest and open demonstrations by local inhabitants against their Bupati, **Raden Tumenggung Natadiningrat**.  <br>- Districts of **Gênḍing** and **Pakis** enter into open revolt.  <br>- Translator for the Javanese Language, **H.D. van den Berg**, dispatched to investigate the grievances.<br><br>#### 2. Charges of Tyranny, Extortion, and Abuses<br><br>- Arbitrary dismissals: Bupati dismissed large numbers of traditional *dêmang*, *mantri*, *lurah*, and village officials without cause.  <br>- Offices sold to highest bidders (*places given to those who pay Bupati the most*), staffing the administration with his personal sycophants (*entourage*).  <br>- Extortion: Accepted bribe money from aspirants seeking appointments, but kept the funds without delivering the promised offices.  <br>- Personal debauchery: Accused of sleeping with and debauching the wives of his subjects.  <br>- Excessive taxation & land alienation: Increased corvée burdens and curtailed peasants' ancestral rice fields in order to lease the roadside sawahs to external tenants.  <br>- Treated senior elders and officials with contempt and arrogance.  <br>- **Outcome:** Bupati confesses his abuses; summarily dismissed by the Dutch government.<br><br>#### 3. Dynastic Legitimacy & Prophecy of Sultan Agung<br><br>- The population demands a replacement Bupati drawn strictly from the ancient lineage that had governed Kaliwungu for two centuries.  <br>- Reference to ancestral legitimation:  <br>  Sultan Agung had prophesied upon the installation of Kaliwungu's first Bupati, **Sumadiwirya** (a son of Pangeran Lêmpuyangan), that none other than direct descendants of Sumadiwirya should ever rule Kaliwungu, resting under the ancestral protection of the sacred shrine of **Batara Katong**. |
