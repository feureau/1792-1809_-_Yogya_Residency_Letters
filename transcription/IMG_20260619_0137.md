**van B -> D, 17 Oct. 1808**  
van B greeting by HB II *op zijn menier* in a friendly fashion.  
Astonishing Difference in friendliness Solo / Yogya.  
HB II cannot speak Malay ∴ Difficult to converse with him.  
<ins>Has a sort of wild look in his eyes (*verwilderd opslag van het oog*) which indicates suspicion and fear.</ins>

**Rampog macan.** = Tendons of legs of karbouw cut by tiger  
Refuses to Fight &emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp; <span style="color:red">**Rampog macan.**</span>  
\* || 2nd Tiger — jumps clean out of Ring + only caught and killed behind place where Sultan was sitting.  
This sit. which had never occurred before during Reign of HB II caused Jav. to make many conjectures with regard to me.  
(RAMPOG MACAN) &emsp; HB II makes van B. a compliment + says that had occurred in v. B's honour.

CP seems a really nice person / v. affable, talkative, but like all
