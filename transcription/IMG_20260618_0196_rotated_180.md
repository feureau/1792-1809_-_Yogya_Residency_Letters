# Transcription: IMG_20260618_0196_rotated_180.jpg

**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260618_0196_rotated_180.jpg`  
**Folio / Reference:** A.N. Yogya Letters (1805) (Continued)  
**Date Range:** 28 August 1805 – 24 October 1805  
**Primary Subjects:**  
- VOC objections to the title "Kencana" for Sriwulan; HB II stubbornly persists  
- HB II attends the wedding of Resident Waterloo's daughter (3 Sept 1805), spending the entire day at the Residency  
- Grand state visit to Waterloo (7 Sept 1805): HB II, Ratu Kencana Wulan, 5 Raden Ayus, the Crown Prince, and royal retinue  
- Marriage of Ratu Anom to Raden Tumenggung Natadiningrat (10 Sept 1805; bedhaya and srimpi performances; cf. BL Add. 12341, f. 316r)  
- Toll disputes with Raden Rangga Prawiradirja III regarding VOC rice passing through Grobogan  
- Royal pilgrimage to the South Sea (*Segara Kidul*): *sedhekah* offerings and sacrifice to Kanjeng Ratu Kidul (7–12 Oct 1805; HB II travels virtually alone with only 2 Dragoons)  
- Harem tensions: HB II's open infatuation with Ratu Kencana Wulan triggers bitter jealousy among the senior royal wives
