### AvJ Archive Entries: 1802 (Tax Lease Structure & Anti-Robbery Expeditions)

* **27 April 1802:** Note that Kertasura is a 12-day journey from Yogya.
* **26 July 1802:** Announcement of Nicolaas Engelhard's upcoming visit to the courts; timber land dispute between Tuban and Jipang.
* **7 October 1802 (Tax Farm & Toll Structure):**
  * Land leased by HB II to Regents for rent.
  * Tollgate/Bandhar land leased by Regents to Resident.
  * Resident subleases to Head Chinese, who subleases to individual Chinese tax farmers.
* **3 November 1802 (HB II Progresses):** HB II leaves Kraton for several weeks, progressing between country residences (*pesanggrahan* like Tanjung Tirto). Informs Resident via Danureja II that Mangkunagaran Regents will be allowed home upon completing work.
* **November 1802 (Expeditions Against Highway Robbers):**
  * Following Resident's complaints of rampant highway robbery, HB II dispatches commanders accompanied by European observers:
    * **R.T. Sasradiwiria:** Sent to Grobogan.
    * **R.T. Sumadipura:** Sent to Japan (Mojokerto).
    * **R.T. Pringgakusuma:** Sent to Rawa.
    * **R.T. Mertalaya:** Sent to Pajang / Sukawati.
    * **Kliwons Bp. Wiridjaya & Mangunjaya:** Sent to Kedu / Bagelèn.
* **28 November 1802:** HB II refuses to cede timber land between Tuban and Jipang, but agrees to collect wood payments there.
