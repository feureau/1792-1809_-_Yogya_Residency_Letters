- Karang Bolong  
- Kadilangu

- Rongkob

### [Assessment of Court Loyalty: PB IV & HB II]

*[Left margin bracketed with double red vertical line:]*  
PB IV. etc.  
? how far Sultan / Princes loyal to V.O.C.  
If British attack would HB II / PB IV remain at peace with each other.

Memory of 1790 still fresh = PB IV would try anything to get back other 1/2 of kingdom.  
.: whilst Dutch busy + foreign enemy = Sunan / Sultan would turn against each other.
