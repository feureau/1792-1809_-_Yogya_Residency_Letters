### AvJ Archive Entries: October – November 1804 (Royal Pilgrimages to Ancestral Tombs & Kraton Guard)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1804 (Tahun Alip / A.J. 1731)**
* **22 October 1804:** Surakarta envoys announce marriage of Patih Mangkupraja's sister (R. Aj. Varimant) to Patih Danuningrat.
* **Royal Pilgrimage along North Coast:**
  * HB II dispatches a delegation of religious scholars (*priests*) from Yogya across the North Coast (Tegal, Surabaya, Madura) to perform offerings and visit ancestral tombs and *Wali* shrines.
  * Included tombs: Amangkurat I (Tegal Arum), HB I's personal tomb, and two sacred *pusakas* (*Kyai Pamar* and *Kyai Kemiri* at Kadilangu; *Kyai Selaya* and *Kyai Amad* at Kadilangu; tomb of Pakubuwana I at Kadilangu; Demak Mosque).
  * Envoys led by Basar and 2 *Modins* (Kyai Loris and Kyai Kasatryan).
  * Pilgrimage route visits shrines at Pemalang (Barang, Pandanaran), Semarang (Cakirayara), Demak (Adilangu, Kali Nyamat), Kudus (Murya Kledaan, Bonang), Giri, Surabaya (Ampel), and Madura (Ayu Mala, Bangkul).
* **Kraton Daily Guard:** 9 Dragoons on daily guard duty at Kraton (3 on active duty: 2 at Rustgate, 1 at the cannon).
* **14 November 1804:** Surakarta envoys Mantri Martajaya and Priyayi Unawala arrive in Yogya to visit ancestral tombs at Teran.
