* **Mission & Purpose:**

  * `("graaven van eenige bijzondere HB I pesawen.")`
* **Annotations on Royal Tombs & Pusakas (in red ink):**
  * `Tombs of Amangkurat I (Têgal Arum)`
  * `2 great Pusaka which must never be alienated`

* **Personnel Dispatched:**
  * `2 kêtibs = Kyai Iman / Kyai Kêmiri`

* **Itinerary of Coastal Shrines & Holy Sites:**
  * `Têgal & Gêgèsèng in Pemalang area = Barang, Pasambaran`
  * `to graves of all great Hajis / Sarif's in Semarang — Cakrayasa (in Semarang)`
  * `Dêmak — Kadilangu / Kali Nyamat / (in Demak area)`
  * `Kudus — Murya, Kêdaan / Bonang (Kudus area)`
  * `Sidayu, Jakakat, Sêndang, Pekandangan, Giri, Bandhan, Panjang, Tanah Suci, Gapura, Krêjata`
