### AvJ Archive Entries: December 1804 (Agricultural Expansion & Tollgate Impact on Rice Prices)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1804 (29 Dec. 1804)**
* **Rice Culture Expansion:** Resident Waterloo confirms that rice cultivation across Java has increased over the past 25 years:
  > *"One has only to direct one's eyes to the lands which produce rice, and which before 20 years ago were still waste and uncultivated"* (*Men behoeft slechts zijn oog te bevestigen op die landen waar men thans rijst voortteelt, en die voor 20 jaren noch woest en onbebouwd lagen*).
  * Forests cleared to create fertile new sawah fields (e.g. Jambu Mts in Kedu).
* **Tollgate Price Multiplication:** Farmers bring surplus produce to market even from remote desas, but passing through numerous tollgates and *bandhars* significantly inflates final market prices.
* **Yogyakarta Rice Prices:**
  * White table rice transported along Kedu road from Semarang to Yogya costs 2 1/2 R.d. per horse pikul (250 lbs = 240 catties = 65 dubbeltjes / 6.50 guilders); lesser quality costs 60 dubbeltjes.
  * Standard market price in Yogya: 100 dubbeltjes per pikul (table rice); 90 dubbeltjes (lesser quality).
