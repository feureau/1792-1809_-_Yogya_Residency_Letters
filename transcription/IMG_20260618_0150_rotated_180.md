### AvJ Archive Entries: 1802 (Tegalreja Murder Expedition & Loban Border Conflict)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1802 (H 698b / LXXVII)**
* **26 January 1802:** Mangkunagaran Regents granted permission by HB II to return home.
* **17 March 1802 (Expedition against Tegalreja Murderers):**
  * Following murder of Ratu Ageng's business factor Mertalaksana at Tegalreja (14 Jan. 1802; see BM Add. MS. 12341), HB II dispatches armed expedition under P. Adinegara, R.T. Jayawinala, Kliwon Vindayuda, Panji Padmawijaya, and other heads to Kedu, Bagelèn, Sukawati, and Gunung Kendeng.
* **12 March 1802 (Loban / Palembon Boundary Dispute & Gunfire):**
  * Boundary dispute over Loban (Yogya - 300 cacahs) and Palembon (Solo - 200 cacahs). Loban ceded to Solo by Yogya due to proximity.
  * Solo inhabitants construct an irrigation canal in Amer Martuvick to the detriment of Yogya farmers.
  * Surakarta Regents revive grievances dating to 1790 Pakepung affair.
  * Conflict erupts at Pasintan / Pasampakan (jointly held desa): Yogya *Mantri* Ng. Mangurminggala (Sutawijaya) shot dead by a bullet in battle between Solo and Yogya heads. Subordinate Ki Kertayuda reports incident to Danureja II on the *Pasebaan*.
  * Joint diplomatic commission appointed consisting of **Mangkupraja** (Solo Patih) and **Danureja II** (Yogya Patih) to apply 9 articles of *Věrat Anggèr*.
