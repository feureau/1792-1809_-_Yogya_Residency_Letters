## Archival & Historical Notes

- **Nocturnal Diplomatic Conferences with Sumadiningrat:**
  - Resident Waterloo holds two extended nighttime conferences with Raden Tumenggung Sumadiningrat (Judhadaningrat / "JD"), the Sultan's chief military favorite.
  - Waterloo reproaches Sumadiningrat for his evident reluctance and lack of zeal in presenting critical colonial representations to Sultan Hamengkubuwana II.
  - He urges Sumadiningrat to overcome factional rivalries and collaborate harmoniously with Patih Danureja II, treating him as a brother in state affairs.
- **Biographical Note on J.G. Waterloo:**
  - Waterloo's deep familiarity with the Yogyakarta court derived from having served five years as Second Resident of Yogyakarta (appointed in 1798) prior to succeeding Van den Berg as First Resident in 1803.
- **Physical Characteristics:** Verso of folio 0149 showing pencil/ink ghosting and bleed-through from the recto notes regarding the Malangnegara lineage and the Kedu tobacco tolls.
