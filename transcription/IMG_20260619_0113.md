### Summary of Daendels' Decree on Court Ceremonial & Ministerial Uniforms (28 July 1808)

* Reference: **Daendels Regulation on Ceremonial and Etiquette (28 July 1808 / Plakaatboek Vol. XV pp. 62–64)**

#### Key Articles on Ministerial Uniforms & Ceremonial Protocol:
* **Art. 1 (Ministerial Designation & Official Dress):**
  * Residents at native courts elevated to **Ministers** representing His Majesty King Louis Napoleon and Governor-General Daendels.
  * *Official Dress:* Royal blue coat embroidered with gold olive-leaf borders, white cassimere breeches, white silk stockings, and a black tri-cornered hat with a black cockade.
* **Art. 2 (Royal Umbrella - Payung):** Ministers authorized to carry a grand royal *payung* (gilt on the outside, painted blue on the inside).
* **Art. 3–6 (Seating & Audiences):**
  * Public audience seating: Minister provided a chair at equal height (*Dhampar*) alongside the Sultan/Sunan.
  * Servants strictly forbidden from forcing wine or betel (*sirih*) upon the Minister.
* **Art. 7–11 (Carriage & Public Encounter Etiquette):**
  * Minister's carriage no longer required to pull over or halt when encountering the Sultan/Sunan's carriage in public.
