### Van Overstraten Memorie van Overgave 1796: Madura Regencies & Military

* Reference: **7. MvO Java's N.O.K. / Van Overstraten 1796**
* **Surrender of Jurisdiction:** Sovereign rights over Madura surrendered by Susuhunan Pakubuwana II to V.O.C. in 1743. Divided into 3 Regencies: Madura (Arosbaya/Bangkalan), Sumenep, and Pamekasan (Sumenep and Pamekasan surrendered 5 Oct. 1700).
* **Madura Regency:** 8,000 cacahs (Arosbaya, Balega, Sampang).
  * Governed by hereditary ruler **Pangeran Cakraningrat**. Well-disposed to V.O.C., but gentle, weak, and naive for Madurese governance; finances in poor state.
  * Mung bean (*kacang hijau*) cultivated in large quantities (40 Rixdollars per *koyang*).
  * **Advisers & Patih:**
    * *Mahmud:* Religious adviser who held great influence over Cakraningrat; exiled in 1782.
    * *Mangunsastra:* Sly Javanese adviser hated by Cakraningrat's family.
    * *Patih (since 1794):* Mas Aria Mancanagara, a brave Madurese married to daughter of second wife of Pangeran.
  * *Exiles:* Family members exiled at Pulau Pinang (Penang), currently held by British.
* **Madurese Military Character:**
  * Population not large enough to furnish excessive recruits.
  * **Madurese Troops:** Warlike by nature, but prone to desertion; require strict military discipline.
  * **Assessment:** **Madurese are the best soldiers among Asiatics, superior even to Javanese Sepoys.**
