#### 1. Madura: Sovereignty & Regencies

- Sovereignty over West Madura surrendered by Pakubuwana II to the V.O.C. in 1743.  
- Divided into three regencies:  
  - **Madura [Bangkalan]**  
  - **Sumenep** (ceded to V.O.C. 5 Oct. 1705 / 1700)  
  - **Pamekasan** (ceded to V.O.C. 5 Oct. 1705 / 1700)  
- **Madura (Bangkalan):**  
  - Size: **8,000 cacahs** (comprising Arosbaya, Balêga, and Sampang).  
  - Ruler: Hereditary prince **Panembahan Cakraningrat**.  
  - *Character:* Very gentle and well-disposed to the V.O.C., but weak, naïve, and far too gentle to control the fiery Madurese; state finances in a dilapidated condition.  
  - Produce: *Kacang hijau* (mung beans) grown in large quantities (40 rijksdaalders per koyang).

#### 2. Court Factions & Exiles

- **Kyai Mahmud:** Religious fanatic who held overwhelming influence over Cakraningrat as trusted adviser; banished in 1782.  
- **Mangunsastra:** Described as a sly, untrustworthy Javanese; universally hated by the Cakraningrat family.  
- **Patih:** Since 1794, **Mas Aria Mancanagara**, a brave Madurese officer (son of Panembahan Cakraningrat by a secondary wife).  
- Exiles: Several members of the dynastic family reside in exile at Bengkulu (*Benkoelen*) and Penang (*Pulo Pinang*), currently controlled by the British.

#### 3. Military Assessment: Madurese Auxiliary Troops

- Madura is not densely populated; cannot easily raise additional military levies for the V.O.C.  
- *[Red ink heading and underline:]* **Madurese Troops.**  
  - Madurese are **warlike by nature**, but prone to desertion unless kept under strict military discipline.  
  - Assessed as the **finest soldiers among Asiatics — superior even to the famed British Indian Sepoys**.
