### Translation of the Gold Farm Charter (*Piagem*) Granted by HB II to R.T. Reksanegara (Page 2)

* **Evasion Penalties:** Merchants hiding goods or bypassing tollgates to evade duties subject to royal punishment upon investigation.
* **Staffing & Military Duties:**
  * Reksanegara retains 5 *Mantris* for tollgate administration and disputes (*historiaalen*).
  * Required to march out armed *prajurits* at each *Garebeg* festival equivalent to an Outer Bupati's contingent.
* **Tenure:** Lease granted for 3 consecutive years, contingent on good conduct and prompt revenue delivery; subject to immediate dismissal for misconduct.
* **Date of Promulgation:** Issued by order of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II on **Wednesday, 1 Sawal Je A.J. 1734 (2 December 1807)**.
