### AvJ Archive Entries: July 1807 (Louis Napoleon Coronation Festival & Currency Exchange)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1807 (Notes R.p. LXXVIII)**
* **2 July 1807 (Copper Coinage Discrepancy):** Difficulty in getting HB II to accept *gebangs* (2-duit copper pieces) used on the North Coast; no local copper coins in circulation except duits and half-duits. Exchanging copper for silver Ducatoons incurs a loss of 6–7 1/2 stuivers.
* **16 July 1807:** P. Puringwidara and a European officer pass Klaten Fort on an inspection visit to Jembung.
* **25 July 1807 (Coronation Festival for King Louis Napoleon):**
  * Governor Nicolaas Engelhard notifies Yogyakarta of the coronation of **Louis Napoleon as King of Holland**.
  * **6 July 1807 Celebration:** Festival celebrated in Yogyakarta with illuminations and fireworks supplied by the Capitan Cina.
  * *Attendance:* HB II absent. Represented by 14 high nobles and Raden Adipati Danureja II. Attending courtiers: Danureja II, Purwadiningrat (HB II's father-in-law), R.T. Sumadiningrat, Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III, R.T. Natadiningrat, R.T. Purwadipura, Kyai T. Mangundipura, R.T. Prawiravala (brother of Ratu Kencana Wulan), Pangeran Adinegara, Pangeran Dipakusuma, and R.T. Sasradiningrat.
