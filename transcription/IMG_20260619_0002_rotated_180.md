### AvJ Archive Entries: 1806 (Kedu Penang Tobacco & Prince Elevations)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1806 (31 Jan. 1806)**
* **Kedu Tobacco Rotation & Production:**
  * Kedu Javanese farmers rotate fields: 2 years rice, 3rd year tobacco (experience shows tobacco cannot be planted 2 years in a row on the same soil).
  * Entire Kedu region (Solo and Yogya sectors) produces **2,000 corges of tobacco annually** (dispatched for commerce as **PENANG TOBACCO / Penang Tbak**).
  * *Volume:* 1 corge = 40 *kranjangs* = 20 pikuls (2 *kranjangs* = 1 pikul = 50 lbs man-load). Total production = 2,000,000 lbs (2.50 grams/leaf).
  * *Valuation:* Yields 200,000 Reals (each pikul 5 Reals = 10 Sp.D. wholesale).
* **Western Boundary Consultation (2 March 1806):**
  * Waterloo urges HB II to instruct all western Mancanagara inhabitants and *Great Pasar* attendees (with respective heads from Banyumas, Ledok, and Rema borders) to attend the Residency house.
* **Surakarta Princely Elevations (29 July 1806):**
  * PB IV elevates 3 sons as Princes: R.M. Husein (under) -> **Pangeran Aria Ngabehi**; R.M. Ardani -> **Pangeran Kusumayuda**; R.M. Tanadi -> **Pangeran Narabrata**.
