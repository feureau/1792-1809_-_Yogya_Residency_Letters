### 31 Jan 1806

[*In right margin:* red ink starburst symbol ✴️]

No Javanese, even without the exception in Këdhu  
never devotes all his energies / land to Tobacco production.

usually Rotation: 2 years Rice — 3rd year Tobacco: [*marked with red ink // in right margin*]  
But Javanese farmer learns from experience that he cannot plant tobacco 2 years in a row on same field. [*marked with red ink // in right margin*]

WHOLE of KËDHU including area: Solo / Yogya  
produces 2000 korfjes of Tobacco each year. [*marked with red ink // in right margin*]  
in Këdhu gekorven (? dried) and Dispatched for commerce:  
known under name of **PINANG TBAK** (Penang Tobacco) [*marked with vertical red rule in right margin*]

One corfje = 40 kranjangs = ? 20 pikuls =  
2 kranjangs = 1 pikul  
1 pikul = as much as a man can carry = 50 lbs.

**COMPLETE PRODUCTION : 2 MN lbs. (2.50 grms.)**  
gets 200,000 Reals ronde. each pikul 5 Reals. = 10 Sp. D.  
usual market price.
