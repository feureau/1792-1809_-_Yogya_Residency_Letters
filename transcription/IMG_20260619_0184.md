(4) Regent buys up 140–150 cöyangs of Rice for about 25 R.D. Hollands p. cöyang.

*29 Sept. 1809*  
*Wiese - Daendels:*  
ROBBERS from CHERIBON enter Brebes.  
Tum. Kartawijaya (? of Brebes) informs the SUNAN:  
NATIVE Oppasser — sent to Rembang murdered in (Sala) BLORA Area when travelling through forest.  
Decision to investigate closely Regency Blora & Area in which murder takes place.  
Kyai Haji MINHAD = for 6 mths in Sala Desa of DABELLAN:  

[Red ink: *Kyai Haji Minhad*]

[Red double margin line:]

receives many Javanese exercised in Robbery & using opium —  
<u>COULD BE DANGEROUS:</u>

*12 Oct. 1809*  
*Wiese - Daendels:*  
<u>CHINESE NO LONGER to BE REGARDED as a NATIVES</u> [Red underline: <u>separate NATION</u>]

*17 Oct. 1809*  
*Wiese - Daendels:*  
DR II reports that R.T. PRAWIRAWINATA goes to Demak area to Enquire into Argument between: at desa Tabasser  
Bekel Nyutra CITRA MESANA  
& 2 messengers from Yogya area Citrowijaya / Wakaranta ->  
latter sent to prison in Semarang.
