### Engelhard Report on Journey to Courts (May – July 1802) & Klaten Fort

* Reference: **Report of N. Engelhard (27 May 1802 / July 1802)**
* **Sunan Illness (p. 118–119):** Sunan falls ill and sends Crown Prince in his place to meet Engelhard at Gramas (recalling Greve's journey during PB III's final illness). Private meeting held with Sunan; Barend Jan van Nieuwkerken (Nijvenheim) present.
* **Peace of Amiens (20 July 1802):** News of General Peace in Europe received; hopes expressed that peace will prove lasting.
* **Erection of Klaten Fort (p. 120–121):**
  * Formal agreement to construct a fort at Matbo (Klaten) between Surakarta and Yogyakarta (completed 1804).
  * Both Sunan and Sultan agree to supply stone, timber, lime, and labor.
  * V.O.C. required to supply a skilled military engineer to supervise construction.
