## Archival & Historical Notes

- **Pangeran Natakusuma (Pakualam I):** Highly praised by Resident van den Berg for his exceptional courtesy and talents, strongly evoking the memory of Sultan Hamengkubuwana I. Because of HB II's unpopular and oppressive administration, Natakusuma's stature grew further, making him dangerous to the Sultan. Van den Berg recommends that the Dutch secretly nurture Natakusuma's pro-European inclination and advises Engelhard to reciprocate his gift (a kris in a gold box) with a European dagger or sword.
- **Lawlessness & Demak/Grobogan Borders:** The Regent of Grobogan captured six robbers, including a Chinese *peranakan*. Cross-border raids into Demak are noted, where local authorities were close to the bandits.
- **Points of Contention between Surakarta and Yogyakarta:**
  - Raden Rangga Prawiradirja III's raid at Delanggu and the retaliatory/related murder of HB II's mantri in Sentan by a Salanese.
  - The disputed boundary line at Lobang / Pêlembon.
  - The embezzlement charges leading to Resident Van Nijvenheim's dismissal in August 1803.
  - Healing of diplomatic scars left by the 1790–1791 *Pakêpung* crisis.
- **Kraton Affairs & Timber Access:** HB II frequently retreated to his country estates (*pesanggrahan*), preventing Dutch officials from obtaining required royal authorizations to fell timber in Jipang.
- **Dynastic Notes:**
  - Celebration of *Puasa* (Garebeg Pasa / Idul Fitri) on 28 January 1803.
  - Planned circumcision of Ratu Anom, son of the influential official consort Ratu Kencana Wulan.
  - High praise for the Crown Prince (later Sultan Hamengkubuwana III / Raja Putra Narendra), described as a "charming person."
- **Surakarta Court Politics:** PB IV opposes Van den Berg's transfer from Yogyakarta to Surakarta as First Resident, influenced by the disgraced Resident Nijvenheim, who routinely sided with the Sunan against fellow Dutch officials.
