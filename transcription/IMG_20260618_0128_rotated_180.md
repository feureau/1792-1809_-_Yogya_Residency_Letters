### Archive Entries: November 1802 – March 1803 (Natakusuma Character & Cakra-winata Murder)

* Reference: **P.J. Br. 20.48 (30 Nov. 1802)**
* **Pangeran Natakusuma Character:** Courteous and highly talented (resembling his father HB I); dangerous to HB II. Loved by the court, whereas HB II is disliked. In the event of unrest, Natakusuma would not remain passive; Dutch advised to secretly encourage him to ensure his support. Engelhard instructed to send a return present and a sword.
* **28 December 1802 (Grobogan Robbers):** Grobogan Regent captures 6 robbers (including a Chinese *peranakan*). Demak Bupati noted as close to bandits crossing over from Banda.
* **Inter-Court Dispute Points (1802–1803):**
  1. Raden Rongga III's attack on Delanggu (wounding HB II's *Mantri* in Gentar).
  2. Loban / Paremon boundary dispute.
  3. Nijvenheim dismissed in August 1803 for embezzling V.O.C. revenues.
  4. Wounds from 1790–91 Pakepung affair healing over.
* **HB II Progresses:** HB II absent from Yogya at a *pesanggrahan* estate; no wood-cutting permits in Jipang granted without his personal approval.
* **28 January 1803:** Fasting month (*Puasa*) celebrated. HB II plans to circumcise Ratu Anom (daughter of Ratu Kencana Wulan).
* **1 March 1803:** Yogya Crown Prince described as a charming person.
* **18 March 1803 (Murder of Cakra-winata in Surakarta):**
  * R.T. Cakra-winata of Solo (from Kedu family related to Natayuda; raised by Natayuda's father in Kartasura) murdered by *towakris* 5 houses away from Solo court.
  * Killed for intrigues during Resident Van Nijvenheim's tenure.
* **23 March 1803:** PB IV creates difficulties regarding Van den Berg's transfer as 1st Resident from Yogya to Solo; Nijvenheim speaks ill of him.
