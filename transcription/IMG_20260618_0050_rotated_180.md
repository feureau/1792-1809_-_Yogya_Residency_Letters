### [Transcription]

#### 1. Marriage Diplomacy: Raden Ayu Sekartaji & Sumenep

- Illegitimate sister of PB IV, **Raden Ayu Sekartaji**:  
  Brought up by Mangkunegara I, but now returns to the Sala Kraton.  

- **Plan of PB IV:** To marry Sekartaji to the Crown Prince of Yogya [Pangeran Adipati Anom / later HB III].  
  - Van Overstraten: *Must be foiled* by getting her married instead to the 2nd son of the Panembahan/Bupati of Sumenep.  
- Ruler of Sumenep is reluctant for his son to marry a Solonese princess:  
  *[Red ink note:]* *"Too expensive and too dangerous"*, but would yield to V.O.C. pressure.  

  - Eldest son of Prince of Sumenep leads a debauched life and is not suitable.  
  - Younger son conducts himself well: serves as a Major of a battalion of Sumenep troops in the Oosthoek.

#### 2. Sultan Hamengkubuwana II vs. Pakubuwana IV

- Between 1792 and 1796, HB II is hated by his brothers and Yogya Bupatis.  
- If disturbances break out in Java:  
  > Many Yogyakartans would desert HB II and join PB IV, who possesses the talent of making himself loved by his subjects.  
  *[Red underline:]* **PB IV talent for making himself loved.**  

- **Despotic / wilful character of HB II:**  
  On 26 April 1796, Van Overstraten warned HB II of the desperate situation in Java.  
  HB II is heavily tied up with his harem women.  
  However, he would not embroil himself in hostilities with Sala without V.O.C. approval; he possesses enough common sense for that.  

- In 1796, HB II has **14 sons and 30 daughters** alive.

#### 3. Recognition of the Crown Prince of Yogyakarta

- **20 Aug. 1792:**  
  At *Lêmah Dhuwur* (Kraton Yogyakarta), Crown Prince recognized by Van Overstraten:  
  Possesses a very good character — 27 years old [born c. 1769].
