### Van Overstraten Memorie van Overgave 1796 (Hamengkubuwana II & Sumenep Proposal)

* Reference: **2. MvO Van Overstraten (Oct. 1796)**
* **Raden Ayu Nokartaji:** Illegitimate sister of PB IV raised by MN I returns to Solo Kraton. PB IV's plan to marry her to CP of Yogya must be foiled by marrying her to 2nd son of Bupati of Sumenep.
  * Bupati of Sumenep reluctant due to cost and risk, but yields to V.O.C. pressure.
  * Eldest son of Sumenep Bupati leads debauched life; younger son is well-conducted (Major commanding Sumenep Battalion in *Oosthoek*).
* **Hamengkubuwana II Character & Friction:**
  * HB II hated by his brothers and Yogya *Bupatis* (1752–1796).
  * In event of general uprising, many Yogyakartans expected to desert HB II for PB IV, who possesses talent for making himself loved by subjects.
  * Describes HB II as despotic and willful (warned on 26 April 1796 by Van Overstraten of perilous situation in Java).
  * HB II heavily influenced by concubines, but cautious enough not to engage in open war with Surakarta without V.O.C. approval.
  * *Children:* 14 sons and 30 daughters of HB II alive in 1796.
* **Yogya Crown Prince:** Recognized by Van Overstraten on 20 Aug. 1792 at Lemah Dhuwur (Kraton Yogya); described as 27 years old and possessing a very good character.
