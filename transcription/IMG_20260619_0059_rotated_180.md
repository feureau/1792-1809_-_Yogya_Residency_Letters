### Speculatieve Memorie: Import Demands, Indigo Failure, & Agricultural Over-exploitation (1808)

* Reference: **Van Alphen - Engelhard No. 235**
* **Import Requests:** PB IV and HB II request Persian horses and elephants from V.O.C.; imports blocked by British naval blockade (elephants sourced from Lampung, Sumatra). Rulers frequently request gold-leaf writing paper (*goudpapier voor brieven*), which must be imported from India.
* **Pepper vs. Indigo Shift in Loano:**
  * Pepper cultivation in a degraded state.
  * 100 cacahs previously assigned to the Loano Indigo Factory returned to pepper cultivation; indigo yield never exceeded 2–3 pikuls because Javanese peasants resisted its labor-intensive preparation.
* **Agricultural Inflation & Soil Exhaustion:**
  * Rice production expanded significantly over 25 years through forest clearing and high-mountain sawah development.
  * Increased tollgates and population growth cause sharp rice price inflation.
  * Peasant expenses higher than 25–30 years prior due to tax increases and chief extortions.
  * Soil fertility declining because continuous double-cropping prevents fallowing, and artificial fertilizers are unused.
