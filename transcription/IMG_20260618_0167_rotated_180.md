### Yogya-Solo Security Accord: Articles 5–19 (27 September 1804)

* **Art. 5:** Small appanages of 2–3 jungs sawah in Tencjungan required to be guarded similarly.
* **Art. 6:** *Mantris* and *Tampings* to return to their districts immediately after being properly briefed.
* **Art. 7 (Penalties for Neglect):** Officials who fail to execute security orders subjected to public display on the *Paseban* in a timber enclosure for 1 week without a kris. Guilty *bekels* exposed to the sun on the *Paseban* from 7:00 to 11:00 a.m. for 1 week.
* **Art. 8:** Captured vagabonds handed over to local *Bupati* for punishment.
* **Art. 9:** If a village is attacked by robbers, surrounding desas must immediately rally to the rescue and pursue robbers until trail is lost.
* **Art. 10–11:** If trail is lost through negligence, *bekel* placed on a *Paseban* crossroad for 1 week. If trail remains unfound after 40 days, *bekel* beaten with 15 *tanjer* blows.
* **Art. 12:** Suspicious overnight visitors in villages must be reported immediately to the village head.
* **Art. 13:** Fencing stolen goods or harboring criminals punished by 15 *tanjer* blows, public *Paseban* exposure, and confiscation/sale of goods.
* **Art. 14 (Noble & Officer Penalties):**
  * *Prajurit* commanders / Kliwons aiding criminals stripped of state emblems (*Bawat*, *Lampit*, *Payung*) for 2 weeks until tried as ordinary subjects.
  * Families of offenders punished by confiscation of *Kinayah* and *Bawat* emblems for 2 weeks.
* **Art. 15–16:** Appanage holders penalized if robbers operate on their fields. Inter-court raids without proof dismissed; if proof established, tried under interstate law.
* **Art. 18:** *Mantris* / *Tampings* participating in robbery banished to the Woods of Kabara; wives and children imprisoned in Kraton.
* **Art. 19:** All *Bupatis*, Kliwons, and subordinates required to take an oath of cooperation with local *bekels*.
