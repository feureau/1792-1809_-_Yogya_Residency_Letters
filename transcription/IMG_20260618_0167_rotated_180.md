## Content

### [Continuation of 27 September 1804 Security Convention: Articles 5–21]

* **Article 5:**
  * `Small appanages of 3-2 jungs of Sawah Tênêjêngan [Panjênêngan]. Also to be similarly guarded.`
* **Article 6:**
  * `Once Mantris / Tampings properly briefed to Return to their Districts.`
* **Article 7 (Penalties for Official Negligence):**
  * `If fail to carry out orders / to be punished by public Display on Pasèban to be fettered in for a week long without kris // lowest bêkel guilty to be placed in sun from 7-11 on Pasèban for 1 week.`
* **Article 8:**
  * `Vagabonds to be handed over to Bp. [Bupati] for punishment.`
* **Article 9:**
  * `If Désa attacked by Robbers = Surrounding Désas to come to Rescue. To follow Robbers until trail lost.`
* **Article 10:**
  * `If Trail lost = Bêkel to be punished by being placed on crossroads of Pasèban for 1 week long.`
* **Article 11:**
  * `If Bêkel still cannot find trail after 40 Days to be beaten + Tanjêr Blows.`
* **Article 12:**
  * `If suspicious people come to spend night in Desa Report to be made to village head.`
* **Article 13:**
  * `Purchasing of stolen goods / aiding-abetting criminals to be punished + 15 Tanjêr Blows + display on Pasèban + goods to be sold.`
* **Article 14 (Sanctions on Prajurit Commanders & Kliwons):**
  * `If Heads of prajurits / Kliwon aid / abett criminals: for 2 weeks to be stripped of Bawat / Upacara / Payong / Lampit until tried by court as ordinary subjects + punished accordingly.`
  * `Family of such to be similar punished by taking away kênaya / Bawat / lampit, but not Payong * for 2 weeks.`
* **Article 15:**
  * `Punishment for appanage holder if allows Robbers onto his fields: High officials / + members of Royal family.`
* **Article 16:**
  * `If attack from Yogya on Solo by Robber but no proof found = affair to lapse; if proof found would be treated accordingly.`
* **Article 18:**
  * `If Mantris / Tampings take part + Robber in activities: to be banished to woods of Kabara + wife / children to be kept in kraton.`
* **Article 19:**
  * `Kamisedepuhs / lowest of Bupatis / Kliwons etc to take oath to co-operate + Bêkels.`
* **Article 21 (Attacks on Chinese Tax-Farms):**
  * `If Chin. Bandar attacked by Robbers = 300 Reals to be set aside to track down Robbers. / If Robbers captured within...`
