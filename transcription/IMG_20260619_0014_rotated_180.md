### Van den Berg Memorie van Overgave 1801 (Pangeran Mangkubumi & Chinese Intrigues)

* **Brothers of PB IV:** Two full brothers from same mother, including **Pangeran Mangkubumi** (proud; must be watched and kept under strict control). PB IV distrusts his brothers and excludes them from state affairs, though he takes them on nighttime strolls around town.
* **Delegation of Power:** PB IV leaves state affairs to his Patih and gives *Bupatis* a free hand until misconduct forces their removal through underhand means.
* **Mangkubumi & Capitan Cina:**
  * Mangkubumi maintains close ties with the Chinese community, sitting on the doorstep of Capitan Cina **Nie Ting Ho**.
  * Enjoys Chinese firework displays; views displays from the fort bridge wearing short breeches alongside Javanese *panakawans*.
  * *Firework Riot:* At a New Year's party, Mangkubumi drew a dagger on Patih Mangkupraja (who deflected the blow with a copper spittoon). When a firework struck Mangkubumi, he killed 2 *panakawans* in cold blood and burned one alive with gunpowder, causing a major riot.
* **Unsanctioned Travel:** Mangkubumi goes hunting at Nisa (8 hours from Solo) or stays at his Boyolali house without Resident's permission (violating rule that all royals must obtain Resident approval before leaving Solo).
* **Movement Restrictions:** Royal movement strictly forbidden when Yogyakarta Mangkunagaran Regents travel along the old Kartasura road.
