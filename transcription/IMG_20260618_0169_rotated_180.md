### Archive Entries: 1804–1805 (Batavian Flag Ceremony, Tan Tjin Sing Stadhuis Contract, & Bandhar Suicide)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 36 (1803–1810)**
* **20 March 1804 (Batavian Republic Flag Ceremony):**
  * In presence of Patih, all *Dalem* and Field Tumenggungs (Pangerans Adinegara, Dipakusuma, CP's Regent Wiraguna, and 2 senior *Panjis* of Sultan - 12 dignitaries in all representing court).
  * **21-gun salute fired from Fort to mark raising of the new Batavian Republic flag at the Residency house.**
* **22 March 1804:** HB II sends mission to Boyolali fort; postponed due to Boyolali Demang attending *Garebeg Besar* in Yogya.
* **24 June 1804 (Tan Tjin Sing Stadhuis Contract):** **Tan Tjin Sing contracts to construct the new City Hall (*Stadhuis*) in Semarang.**
* **12 December 1804 (Smuggler Capture):** Capture of 2 Javanese smugglers (Trunadangway Putra) in Yogya area; vessel had sailed from Penang, Malacca, Batavia, and Semarang.
* **10 August 1804 (Suicide of Japan Bandhar Jan Joen):**
  * **SUICIDE OF CHINESE BANDHAR JAN JOEN OF JAPAN.**
  * Accused of extortionate practices against Sultan's Mancanagara subjects; placed under arrest in the fort's lock-up (*Blokkamer*).
  * **Hangs himself by his *kabaya* garment from a nail on the door.**
