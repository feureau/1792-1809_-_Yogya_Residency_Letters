### Van den Berg Memorie van Overgave 1806 (Prangwedana Autonomy & Protocol)

* Reference: **Van Alphen - Engelhard No. 239 (Aanw. 1900) No. XXII**
* **Inter-Court Travel Guard:** Yogya Resident issues warnings when Mangkunagaran *Bupatis* return from Yogya: royal honor ceremonies on the road (*over de troontij de hand geleid*) reserved exclusively for PB IV and the Surakarta Crown Prince.
* **Pangeran Buminata:** Gentle, courteous, and discrete prince; suffers from eye disease.
* **Pangeran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II):**
  * Holds 4,000 cacahs + tollgate leases yielding substantial revenue.
  * **Never attends Surakarta court except in company of the Resident.**
  * Independence of Prangwedana from Surakarta court must be preserved by V.O.C. as far as possible.
  * Only court duties: assisting in repairs to PB IV's private apartments and Mosque. Receives 2,000 Np.D. (2,500 Rixdollars p.a.) for legion upkeep.
  * Prangwedana's *Dalem* guarded by 12 dragoons from Resident's bodyguard.
* **Patih Jayaningrat Dismissal:** Jayaningrat fell from power after failing to execute PB IV's command to strip Prangwedana of his gold *payongs* at the *Paseban*.
* **Succession:** Prangwedana's brother **Pangeran Prabuwijaya** (serving in Surakarta Kraton) selected as Prangwedana's successor (27 Oct. 1803).
