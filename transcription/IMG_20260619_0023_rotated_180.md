### Cirebon Unrest, British Reappearance, & Inter-Court Treaties (1804–1806)

* **Cirebon Disturbances & British Fleet (Oct. – Dec. 1806):**
  * Cirebon unrest continues unabated. HB II requested to prepare auxiliary troops, but resists mobilizing unless in an extreme emergency.
  * HB II instructed to seal Cirebon borders and interdict correspondence between Yogyakartans and Cirebon rebels.
  * **2 December 1806:** Reappearance of Admiral Pellew's British squadron (8 warships) in Batavia Bay. Waterloo promises to request HB II assemble auxiliary forces.
* **1804 Inter-Court Contracts:**
  * **2 February 1804 Contract:** Conference between Patihs Mangkupraja (Solo) and Danureja II (Yogya) with Residents Van den Berg and Waterloo settling all territorial disputes (Secang, Pakidulan, Pasampakkan, Gentan, Parembon, Lobang, Delanggu).
  * **27 September 1804 Local Security Accord:** Inter-village theft prevention protocol (*Nawah Pimajungan*). Village *Mantris* and *Tampings* granted full powers of arrest to maintain rural order.
