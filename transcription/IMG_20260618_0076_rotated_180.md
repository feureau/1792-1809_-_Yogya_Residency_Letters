### Patih Danureja II Appointment (1799), Gambling Report (1806), and Land Grants

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 46 (1810–11) / Solo Br. 162**
* **Appointment of Patih Danureja II (29 Aug. 1799):**
  * P.G. van Overstraten approves HB II's nomination of DRI's grandson, **R.T. Mertanegara**, as Patih (**Danureja II**) due to his trustworthiness, vigilance, and capabilities.
  * Danureja II required to take oath of allegiance to Dutch government in Semarang before Baron van Reede.
* **Gambling Investigation in Yogyakarta (8 April 1806):**
  * Moorish/Bengali trader Muhammad Mira Mohidin travels twice from Semarang to Yogya to conduct high-stakes gambling in Chinese Kampong (loses 4,200 silver Rixdollars).
  * Gambled at residence of Baba Gem Bo (Chinese Bandhar of Kamolan, brother of Surabaya Capitan Cina), Mas Ng. Danukrama, two sons of Pangeran Ngabehi, and *Mantri* of Kepatihan.
  * Loses 2,200 Reals in diamonds/cash at house of Danukrama; plays Chinese card game *Piki* and dice at residences of P. Mertapasara, R. Panji Nuryingrana, and Modin of Numadiwiria (loses 2,000 Reals taken from father).
* **Land Grants (*Piagems* - Solo Br. 162):**
  1. *R.T. Puspadipura (Bupati of Bagelèn):* Grant dated 14 Rabingulawal A.J. 1754 (1826) by HB II: 200 cacahs + 100 Sp.D. annual tax (1 Sp.D. = 2.70 guilders).
  2. *R.T. Sasrakusuma (Bupati of Brebes):* Grant dated 24 Besar 1755: 1,230 cacahs + 247 1/2 Sp.D. annual tax.
