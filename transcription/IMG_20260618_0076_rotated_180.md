### Part I: Appointment of Danureja II (23 August 1799)

- **P.G. van Overstraten to HB II:**
  - Agrees to the appointment of the grandson of Danureja I, **Raden Tumenggung Mērtanēgara**, as Patih (Danureja II).
  - Reason: Because of his trust, vigilance, and proven capabilities.
  - Stipulation: Must take the solemn oath of allegiance to the Dutch Government in Semarang at the hands of Resident Baron Johan Frederik van Reede tot de Parkeler.

---

### Part II: Report on High-Stakes Gambling in Yogyakarta (8 April 1806)

[Red marginal annotation: **Gambling in Yogya**]

- A Moor / Bengali (?) named **Muhamad Mira Mohidin** travels from Semarang twice to Yogyakarta specifically to gamble.
- Losses:
  - Loses a sum of **4,200 *Ronde maaten*** gambling in the Chinese *kampong* with:
    - Baba Gem Bo (Chinese *Bandhar* of Kamolan).
    - Brother of the Surabaya *Kapitan Cina*.
    - Mas Ngabehi Danukrama.
    - Two sons of Pangeran Ngabèhi.
    - Mantri of the Kepatihan.
  - Loses **2,200 Reals** in diamonds and money at the house of Danukrama playing the Chinese card game *Piki*.
  - Gambles with dice in the house of Pangeran Mēntapara, house of Raden Panji Nuryingrana, and house of the mother of Sumadiwiria; across various trips loses another **2,000 Reals** in diamonds and cash.
  - All money had been taken from his father.
