| **31 Dec. 1795** | ### [Transcription]<br><br>#### 1. Military Garrisons & Fortifications<br><br>- **Residency Garrisons (1796):**  <br>  - Surakarta garrison: **183 men**  <br>  - Yogyakarta garrison: **193 men**  <br>  - *Language students:* 2 *Leerlingen in de Javaansche Taal* (1 stationed at each court).  <br>  - Due to shortage of European troops, some detachments have to be recalled periodically to Semarang.  <br>  - Since Governor Van den Burgh's tenure (1780s), infantry baseline at each court was fixed at **100 men**.  <br>- **V.O.C. Relay Forts along the Post Route:**  <br>  - Ungaran: *De Ontmoeting*  <br>  - Salatiga: *De Hersteller*  <br>  - Boyolali: *De Veldwachter*  <br>  - Repairs to Fort *De Veldwachter* (Boyolali) undertaken in 1793.  <br>- **Fortifications & Warehouses:**  <br>  - HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] agreed to construct a new storehouse (*pakhuis*) for the Yogya fort using corvée labour from Mancanagara subjects.  <br>  - HB I [Sultan Hamengkubuwana I] had been too economical to fund a fort between Yogya and the Jambu hills; decided instead to build a fort at Marbon.  <br>  - Patih Danureja I postponing Yogya's cooperation on the fort until Boyolali repairs are fully completed.<br><br>#### 2. Transport Infrastructure & Intelligence (*Gladhag*)<br><br>- *[Red marginal note:]* **gladhags.**  <br>  - Establishment of a *gladhag* (relay / transport post) at **Klaten**; posts already active at **Tangkilan**, **Adilangu**, and **Marbon**.  <br>- **Religious Unrest & Holy Men:**  <br>  - Notable increase in itinerant Muslim priests and ascetics (*tapa*) roaming the North Coast districts.  <br>  - Religious figure **Kyai Pugu** active at Sala *(cf. Aparte Brief, 31 Dec. 1795)*.  <br>- Nothing further heard concerning the surviving family of **Pangeran Rangga Jajar**. |
