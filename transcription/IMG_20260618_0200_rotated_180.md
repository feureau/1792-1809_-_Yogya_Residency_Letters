## Complete Diplomatic Transcription

### [5 December 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
5 Dec 1805  
Wat – Eng

Extremely Heavy PAIN in His Head.  
Decision to go to BANTUL AFTER PUASA:  
a place just out of town of Yogya  
for a Rest.

---

*[Center in red ink:]*  
**1806 (Missing)**

---

### [4 December 1806]

*[Left margin:]*  
4 Dec 1806  
Wat – Eng

Still COMPLAINING of Headaches.

DISMISSAL of R.T. SUMADININGRAT = kept at his house.  

[? would be Resolved + fine of 1,000 Rond Realen]

Ratu Kencana Wulan using all Her Influence:  
*[Double red vertical line:]*  

[for Her brother as Head of PANJIS]

AND SON-IN-LAW (R.T. NATADININGRAT)  
TO BE RECOGNISED as First Field Tumenggung.

? Waterloo to travel to Semarang Soon.

---

### [21 January 1807]

*[Left margin:]*  
21 Jan 1807  
(Magelang)  
Wat – ENG

Waterloo + (Mrs) + TRANSLATOR LUCAS  
undertakes journey to Semarang.  
(end letters of Bundel very Damaged by Water).

---

### [12 January 1807]

*[Left margin:]*  
12 Jan 1807  
Wat – Eng  
*[Red double vertical line on right]*

New KING appointed IN HOLLAND  
(LOUIS NAPOLEON)  
Inlandsche Vorsten should know of it.

*[To right in red ink:]*  
**Apptmt of Louis Napoleon 6 June 1806.**
