### 17 January 1798 (Resident of Surakarta):

- Sunan PB IV gives permission for Mangkunegaran regents to return home.
- Reference to his sister **Raden Ayu Juta Aji** (= **Ratu Timur**).

---

### 29 January 1798 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- **Raised again / Restorations:**
  - **Raden Tumenggung Natayuda** reinstated / replaced as 2nd *kliwon* under Patih Danureja I; some of his rice fields are returned. [Red marginal annotation: *? on action of van IJsseldijk*].
  - **Raden Tumenggung Danukusuma** gets 100 *cacahs*.
  - **Raden Aria Mandura** gets 600 *cacahs*.

---

### March 1798 (Date uncertain, between 24 Feb and 6 April 1798) (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- HB II gives permission for the Mangkunegaran regents to return home.

---

### 18 June 1798 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- Arrival of a messenger and letter from Semarang: Bupati Secanegara to Bupati of Danureja II, **Karsadimeja**.

---

### 18 September 1798 (Resident of Surakarta):

- Death of **Raden Ayu Giri**, sister of the Great Modin of the Sunan's Mosque; buried at **Imagiri**.

---

### 13 October 1798 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- HB II reports the death of two royal elephants — requests replacement elephants from Batavia.
