### AvJ Archive Entries: 1798 (Natayuda Reinstatement & Royal Elephants)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1798**
* **17 January 1798 (Res. Solo):** Sultan grants permission for Mangkunagaran Regents to return home; sister Raden Ayu Suta Aji (**Ratu Timur**).
* **29 January 1798 (Res. Yogya):** **R.T. Natayuda reinstated as 2nd Kliwon under DRI** (sawah returned). R.T. Danukusuma receives 100 cacahs; Raden Aria Mendura receives 600 cacahs (action taken by Van IJsseldijk between 24 Feb. and 6 April).
* **March 1798:** HB II permits Mangkunagaran Regents to return home.
* **18 June 1798:** Envoy arrives with letter from Semarang Bupati Wecanegara to DR II's Bupati Karadimeja.
* **18 September 1798 (Res. Solo):** Death of Raden Ayu Giri (sister of the Great Modin of Surakarta Mosque); buried at Imagiri.
* **13 October 1798 (Res. Yogya):** HB II reports death of 2 court elephants and requests replacements from Batavia.
