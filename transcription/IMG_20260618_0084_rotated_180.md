| **1792 to 1809** | ### Text & Marginal Annotations<br><br>[Surakarta Court & Princely Factions:]<br><br>- **Crown Prince (CP [Crown Prince / later HB III] - future PB V):**<br>  - Makes himself loved by the common people because he is not oppressive like PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] [red margin bar].<br>  - Resides with PB IV until the new *dalem* of Pangeran Mangkubumi is completed.<br>- **PB IV's children:** 12 sons and 12 daughters living by *selir* (concubines).<br><br>[Pangeran Mangkubumi:]<br><br>- **Pangeran Mangkubumi:** Born AJ 1699 (1773 AD); very proud of spirit.<br>- Takes over most of Banyumas, which he obtained after the death of **Mangkunegara I (MN I [Mangkunagara I])** in 1795 [underlined in red].<br>- Great rivalry between Pangeran Mangkubumi and the Crown Prince.<br>- Mangkubumi has 4 official wives.<br><br>[Pangeran Buminata:]<br><br>- **Pangeran Buminata:** Born AJ 1702 (1776 AD).<br>  - 1st wife = daughter of the Bupati of Pati, Aria Megatsari.<br>  - 10 years after marriage, had no children.<br>  - Married another daughter of the Bupati of Pati, but still had no children, nor any children amongst his *selirs* (evidently Buminata is impotent).<br>  - Possesses a much gentler personality than Mangkubumi; is more loved by the Crown Prince.<br><br>[Other Princes & Dynastic Ties:]<br><br>- **Pangeran Danupaya:** Son of Amangkurat IV; banished to Ceylon.<br>  - His son, **Raden Mas Sêmantri**, was allowed to return to Java upon the personal request of PB IV.<br>- **Pangeran Aria Purbanēgara:** Son of Mangkunegara I; married legitimate sister of PB IV, **Raden Ayu Napiah**; made Bupati Wedana of Eastern Mancanagara.<br>- **Raden Ayu Sêkartaji:** Legitimate sister of PB IV; originally destined for the second son of Panembahan of Sumenep, but the marriage did not take place.<br>  - Later married the son of the Bupati of Surabaya (= later Bupati of Kudus). |
