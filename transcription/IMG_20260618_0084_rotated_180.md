### Van Reede Memorie van Overgave 1801 (Surakarta Princes & Royal Marriages)

* **Surakarta Crown Prince:** Well-liked by the common people due to his un-oppressive nature (unlike PB IV). Resides with PB IV pending completion of Pangeran Mangkubumi's new residence.
* **Pakubuwana IV Family:** 12 sons and 12 daughters living by 1773.
* **Pangeran Mangkubumi (born 1773 / A.J. 1699):** Proud spirit. Assumes jurisdiction over Mataram and Banyumas lands acquired after Prangwedana's (MN I) death in 1795. Intense rivalry between Mangkubumi and the Crown Prince; Mangkubumi has 4 official wives.
* **Pangeran Buminata (born 1776 / A.J. 1702):** Married daughter of Pati Bupati Aria Megatsari (childless after 10 years); married her sister (childless); no offspring with concubines (**impotent**). Possesses a much gentler personality than Mangkubumi and is better loved at court.
* **Pangeran Dampyak:** Son of Amangkurat (exiled to Ceylon); his son **Raden Mas Nemantri** permitted to return to Java upon PB IV's petition.
* **Pangeran Aria Purbanegara:** Son of MN I; married PB IV's daughter Raden Ayu Nupiah (appointed Bupati Wedana of Eastern Mancanagara).
* **Raden Ayu Nokartaji:** Illegitimate daughter of PB IV originally intended for 2nd son of Prince of Sumenep; marriage fails. Marries eldest son of Bupati of Surabaya (later Bupati of Kudus).
