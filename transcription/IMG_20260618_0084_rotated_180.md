### Text & Marginal Annotations

[Surakarta Court & Princely Factions:]

- **Crown Prince (CP - future PB V):**
  - Makes himself loved by the common people because he is not oppressive like PB IV [red margin bar].
  - Resides with PB IV until the new *dalem* of Pangeran Mangkubumi is completed.
- **PB IV's children:** 12 sons and 12 daughters living by *selir* (concubines).

---

[Pangeran Mangkubumi:]

- **Pangeran Mangkubumi:** Born AJ 1699 (1773 AD); very proud of spirit.
- Takes over most of Banyumas, which he obtained after the death of **Mangkunegara I (MN I)** in 1795 [underlined in red].
- Great rivalry between Pangeran Mangkubumi and the Crown Prince.
- Mangkubumi has 4 official wives.

---

[Pangeran Buminata:]

- **Pangeran Buminata:** Born AJ 1702 (1776 AD).
  - 1st wife = daughter of the Bupati of Pati, Aria Megatsari.
  - 10 years after marriage, had no children.
  - Married another daughter of the Bupati of Pati, but still had no children, nor any children amongst his *selirs* (evidently Buminata is impotent).
  - Possesses a much gentler personality than Mangkubumi; is more loved by the Crown Prince.

---

[Other Princes & Dynastic Ties:]

- **Pangeran Danupaya:** Son of Amangkurat IV; banished to Ceylon.
  - His son, **Raden Mas Sêmantri**, was allowed to return to Java upon the personal request of PB IV.
- **Pangeran Aria Purbanēgara:** Son of Mangkunegara I; married legitimate sister of PB IV, **Raden Ayu Napiah**; made Bupati Wedana of Eastern Mancanagara.
- **Raden Ayu Sêkartaji:** Legitimate sister of PB IV; originally destined for the second son of Panembahan of Sumenep, but the marriage did not take place.
  - Later married the son of the Bupati of Surabaya (= later Bupati of Kudus).
