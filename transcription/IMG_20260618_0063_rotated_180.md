| **23 Jan. 1796** | ### [Transcription]<br><br>#### 1. Internal Mangkunegaran Succession Disputes<br><br>- PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] pays no attention to Pangeran Mangkubumi's incitement against PWD [Prangwedana / Mangkunagara II] because Resident Van Reede is already in firm physical charge of the Mangkunegaran Dalem.  <br>- **Pangeran Purbanēgara**, eldest illegitimate son of MN I [Mangkunagara I], stakes a claim to inherit the **4,000 cacahs**; Resident Van Reede firmly rejects the claim, stating no change could be permitted to the established agreement with PB IV.  <br>- Widespread opposition: Not only PB IV and his royal brothers, but many members within the Mangkunegaran family itself actively oppose the succession of Pangeran Prangwedana.  <br>  *[Red ink note:]* **MN family against succession of PWD.**<br><br>#### 2. International Context & Naval Sightings<br><br>- Arrival of news confirming the alliance/peace between the new Batavian Republic and France (shifting Britain to sole enemy status).  <br>- **23 Jan. 1796 (Van Overstraten to Alting):** Severe difficulties encountered in finalizing arrangements for PWD to succeed to the 4,000 cacahs.  <br>- **30 Jan. 1796 (Id. to Id.):** Five large three-masted ships sighted sailing along the North Coast towards Bali Strait (suspected English warships or British East India Company China-men).<br><br>#### 3. Formal Investiture & Suspected Poisoning of PWD (II)<br><br>- **24 Jan. 1796:** Official appointment of PWD with the title **Pangeran Aria Prabu Prangwedana**.  <br>- **8 Feb. 1796 (Id. to Id.):**  <br>  *[Red ink heading:]* **Poisoning of PWD.**  <br>  Severe, acute sickness strikes PWD immediately following his investiture; grave suspicions that he has been **poisoned**.  <br>  PWD refuses to trust Javanese healers (*dhukun*); insists strictly on accepting treatment and medicines only from the **V.O.C. Hospitalier (surgeon) in Sala**.<br><br>#### 4. Yogyakarta Court Pledges<br><br>- **3 Feb. 1796 (HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] to Governor-General & Raad van Indië, Batavia):**  <br>  HB II formally pledges full military assistance to the V.O.C., promising to mobilize Yogyakarta auxiliary troops in the event of an English invasion. |
