### [Transcription]

#### 1. Internal Mangkunegaran Succession Disputes

- PB IV pays no attention to Pangeran Mangkubumi's incitement against PWD because Resident Van Reede is already in firm physical charge of the Mangkunegaran Dalem.  
- **Pangeran Purbanēgara**, eldest illegitimate son of MN I, stakes a claim to inherit the **4,000 cacahs**; Resident Van Reede firmly rejects the claim, stating no change could be permitted to the established agreement with PB IV.  
- Widespread opposition: Not only PB IV and his royal brothers, but many members within the Mangkunegaran family itself actively oppose the succession of Pangeran Prangwedana.  
  *[Red ink note:]* **MN family against succession of PWD.**

#### 2. International Context & Naval Sightings

- Arrival of news confirming the alliance/peace between the new Batavian Republic and France (shifting Britain to sole enemy status).  
- **23 Jan. 1796 (Van Overstraten to Alting):** Severe difficulties encountered in finalizing arrangements for PWD to succeed to the 4,000 cacahs.  
- **30 Jan. 1796 (Id. to Id.):** Five large three-masted ships sighted sailing along the North Coast towards Bali Strait (suspected English warships or British East India Company China-men).

#### 3. Formal Investiture & Suspected Poisoning of PWD (II)

- **24 Jan. 1796:** Official appointment of PWD with the title **Pangeran Aria Prabu Prangwedana**.  
- **8 Feb. 1796 (Id. to Id.):**  
  *[Red ink heading:]* **Poisoning of PWD.**  
  Severe, acute sickness strikes PWD immediately following his investiture; grave suspicions that he has been **poisoned**.  
  PWD refuses to trust Javanese healers (*dhukun*); insists strictly on accepting treatment and medicines only from the **V.O.C. Hospitalier (surgeon) in Sala**.

#### 4. Yogyakarta Court Pledges

- **3 Feb. 1796 (HB II to Governor-General & Raad van Indië, Batavia):**  
  HB II formally pledges full military assistance to the V.O.C., promising to mobilize Yogyakarta auxiliary troops in the event of an English invasion.
