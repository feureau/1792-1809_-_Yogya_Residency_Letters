### Archive Entries: January – February 1796 (Prangwedana Succession & Poisoning Allegation)

* **Mangkunagaran Estate:** PB IV ignores Mangkubumi's proposals as Van Reede is already in charge of Prangwedana's *Dalem*. Pangeran Purbanegara (eldest illegitimate son of MN I) demands 4,000 cacahs; Van Reede confirms no alteration possible to treaty with PB IV. Internal Mangkunagaran family opposition to Prangwedana's succession noted.
* **Geopolitical Context:** News received of peace between the French and Dutch Republics, ending war between Holland and France/England.
* **23 January 1796:** Procedural difficulties resolved regarding Prangwedana's succession to 4,000 cacahs.
* **30 January 1796:** 5 large 3-masted vessels observed off North Coast heading toward Bali Strait.
* **24 January 1796:** Prangwedana formally installed as **Pangeran Aria Prabu Prangwedana**.
* **8 February 1796:** Prangwedana falls ill shortly after appointment; fears arise that he was **poisoned**. Refuses Javanese physicians and insists on receiving medicines exclusively from the V.O.C. hospitalier in Surakarta.
* **3 February 1796 (HB II to Batavia):** Sultan promises auxiliary forces to assist V.O.C. and Yogya if attacked by the British.
