### Babad Ngayogyakarta Vol. I: Robbery at Tegalreja & Murder of Mertalaksana (1802)

* Source: **Babad Ngayogyakarta Vol. I, pp. 205r-v (BM Add. MS. 12341)**
* Red ink margin note: **Killing of Ratu Ageng's Business Factor Mertalaksana (14 Jan. 1802 / 8 Mar. 1802).**

#### Javanese Text Summary:
* Official letter sent by Surakarta Patih Raden Adipati Mangkupraja to Yogyakarta Patih Danureja II.
* Reports an attack by bandits (*kècu*) on the residence/estate of Ratu Ageng at Tegalreja on 14 Jan. 1802 (Nasa month).
* Ratu Ageng's business factor **Mertalaksana** was attacked and killed, and property looted.
* Sultan Hamengkubuwana II orders an immediate investigation to apprehend the culprits.
* Four primary suspects identified: **Amad Tana**, **Surayana**, **Nadongsa**, and **Kertatruna** (from Pakuncen near Tegalreja).
* Further accomplices named: **Nur Ali**, **Nuradinala**, **Pak Pakim**, **Kertapangarsa**, and **Nurayuda** (from Niti village near Tapen/Bojong). Suspects fled toward Merapi and Kedu.
