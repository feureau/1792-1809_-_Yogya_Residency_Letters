**Folio / Reference:** A.N. Letters No. 47  
**Date Range:** 12 June 1805 – 1 August 1805  
**Primary Subjects:**  
- Garebeg Mulud celebrations in Yogyakarta (12 June 1805)  
- Cross-reference to Ki Taptajani report  
- Demak *Opziener* A. Abera's report on regional insecurity shown to HB II  
- HB II orders security watch posts in Wirasari, Grobogan, and Sasajan; Grobogan regents named (Sasradiwiria, Yudakusuma, Sasrakusuma)  
- Marriage of the Crown Prince (later HB III) to the daughter of Grobogan's 2nd Bupati, Raden Tumenggung Yudakusuma (set for 15 Jumadilawal / July 1805)  
- Court festivities: "de oude Heer amuseert zich met zijn bedaya's / meisjes"  
- Notification to HB II of Governor-General Johannes Siberg's dismissal and succession of Albertus Henricus Wiese (16 July 1805)
