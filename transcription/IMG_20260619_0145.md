### Klaten Security Accord Articles (26 September 1808)

* Reference: **Yogya-Solo Klaten Agreement (26 Sept. 1808 / 16 Ruwah Dal 1733)**
* Document: Signed by J.A. van Braam, Pieter Engelhard (by order of Daendels), Patih Danuningrat (Solo), and Patih Danureja II (Yogya).

#### Inter-Court Security Articles (Arts. 1–7):
* **Art. 1 (Tollgate Defense & Raids):**
  * All *Bandhars* receive an official sealed letter from the Minister. Village heads around tollgates must provide armed guards to protect princely property.
  * If a tollgate is robbed or burned, surrounding villagers are held financially liable for the damage.
* **Art. 2 (Inter-Village Pursuit):** If a village is attacked by robbers, neighboring desas must immediately assist, tracking the robbers along visible trails to apprehend criminals and stolen property, notifying Regents and Danureja II.
* **Art. 3 (Bekel Sun Punishment):** If a village *bekel* fails to locate robbers, **he is stretched out in the sun on the *Paseban* from 7:00 to 11:00 a.m. Every day for a week**.
* **Art. 4 (Harboring Penalties):** If a *bekel* allows a robber to escape into his house, he is granted 40 days to capture the suspect; meanwhile, his family is taken hostage.
* **Art. 5–7:** *Mantris* and *Tampings* must expel suspected evil persons; overnight visitors must report to the *Pak Lurah*; disobedient Regents stripped of ornaments and suspended from office.
