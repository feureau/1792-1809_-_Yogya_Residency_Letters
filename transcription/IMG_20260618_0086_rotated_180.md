### Van Reede Memorie van Overgave 1801 (Pangeran Natakusuma & Commissioner Nederburgh)

* **Pangeran Natakusuma:** Maternal lineage tied to old Kedu dynasty; holds extensive appanage lands and great influence in Kedu (site of 1813 disturbances). Courteous and gentle manner makes him widely loved across court; acts quietly and honestly without overt ambition.
  * **Prophecy:** HB I predicted Natakusuma would one day play an important role. Many princes and *Bupatis* secretly devoted to him (especially Mangkudiningrat).
  * **Breach with HB II:** HB II had promised HB I not to diminish Natakusuma's appanages, but stripped him of court ornaments shortly after accession, breaking their friendship.
* **Commissioner-General Nederburgh's Proposed Visit:**
  * Nederburgh's planned visit to the royal courts postponed.
  * HB II refuses to accord Nederburgh higher protocol than a Governor, declining to receive him as Commissioner-General.
  * **Historic Precedent:** While Sunan was prepared for full reception, HB II refused, citing the disastrous fallout of Governor-General Van Imhoff's visit to Surakarta in 1746 (which caused Mangkubumi's departure from court and triggered the Third Javanese War of Succession).
  * HB II objects to protocol requiring 3 or 4 high-ranking Dutch officials sitting beside him, fearing it would demean his royal dignity before his subjects.
