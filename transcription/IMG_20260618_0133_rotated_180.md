## Content

### Archive Entries: 1803–1807 (Natakusuma Death, Chinese Blood Oath, Natayuda Death)

* Reference: **Solo Br. 14a / Nolo naar Yogya (1803–1807)**
* **16 August 1803 (Van den Berg -> Waterloo):** **Death of Pangeran Natakusuma** (son-in-law of Prangwedana). R.M. Gandakusuma appointed **Pangeran Adiwijaya**.
* **23 October 1803:** PB IV sends letter of condolence to HB II on death of Ratu Ageng Tegalreja (died 18 Oct. 1803).
* **9 November 1803 (Chinese Blood-Oath Plot in Banyumas):**
  * Secret conspiracy among Chinese in Banyumas (Nokaraja) involving fugitives from Cirebon and Tegal (50 men strong).
  * Initiates cut their fingers, pour blood into a pot with liquor, and drink it in a formal blood-oath binding the group.
* **15 November 1803:** Ratu Ageng Tegalreja taken ill and brought to Kadipaten where she dies. Elevations: Pangerans Natalaya, Nantakusuma, Nilarong. **R.T. Natawijaya appointed new Bupati of Panolan.**
* **28 January 1805 (Death of Natayuda):**
  * **Death of R.T. Natayuda.**
  * HB II raises R.T. Nurbekti (nephew of Natakusuma) as **R.T. Natadiningrat** to replace late Natayuda.
  * Elevates R.T. Natayuda II to Nong NY (+250 cacahs).
