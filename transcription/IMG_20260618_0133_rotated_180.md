## Archival & Historical Notes

- **Correspondence Series:** Letters exchanged between First Resident of Surakarta J.G. van den Berg and Resident of Yogyakarta J.G. Waterloo (ANRI, Solo Bundel 14a, *"Brieven van Solo naar Yogya"*, 1803–1807).
- **16 August 1803 (Surakarta Court Elevations):** Death of Surakarta prince Pangeran Natakusuma (son-in-law of Pangeran Prangwedana); Raden Mas Gandakusuma is elevated with the title Pangeran Adiwijaya.
- **Death of Ratu Agêng (Tegalreja):**
  - Ratu Agêng (consort of Sultan Hamengkubuwana I and great-grandmother/guardian of Dipanegara at Tegalreja) fell gravely ill, was brought to the Kadipatèn, and passed away on 18 October 1803.
  - Sunan Pakubuwana IV dispatched a formal letter of condolence to Sultan Hamengkubuwana II on 23 October 1803.
  - Subsequent princely elevations in Yogyakarta: appointments of Pangeran Natalaya, Pangeran Nantakusuma, and Pangeran Silarong. Raden Tumenggung Natawijaya is appointed Bupati of Panolan.
- **Chinese Blood-Oath Triad Ritual in Banyumas (9 Nov 1803):**
  - Report of a conspiracy (*complot*) among Chinese residents in Banyumas (specifically at Sokaraja) joined by 50 Chinese fugitives from Cirebon and Tegal.
  - They enacted a classical sworn-brotherhood / triad initiation ritual: slashing/cutting their fingers, collecting the blood in a bowl mixed with Chinese liquor (*tjiou* / *ciu*), and drinking it communally to seal their secrecy and allegiance.
- **Death of Raden Tumenggung Natayuda & Succession (28 Jan 1805):**
  - Death of Raden Tumenggung Natayuda I (Bupati of Kedu).
  - HB II appoints Raden Tumenggung Subêkti (eldest son of Pangeran Natakusuma / Pakualam I) as **Raden Tumenggung Natadiningrat** (later Prince Prangwedana / Pakualam II) to succeed to Natayuda's senior office.
  - Natayuda's own son succeeds to the title **Raden Tumenggung Natayuda II** and is endowed with an appanage of 250 *cacahs*.
