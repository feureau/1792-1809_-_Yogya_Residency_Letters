## Content

### Archive Entries: April – May 1804 (Dismissal of Patih Mangkupraja & Cotton Trade)

* **1 April 1804:** Resident Waterloo confers with HB II; talks restricted due to a royal wedding (Patih Danureja II's wife attends aboard a Bengal vessel).
* **6 April 1804 (Dismissal of Surakarta Patih Mangkupraja):**
  * **Patih Mangkupraja dismissed and exiled to Banyumas** on advice of Outer Regent Cakravegara and 1st *Dalem* Regent Suradilaga.
  * 10 April: Danureja II provides detailed report on Mangkupraja's fall.
* **13 April 1804:** HB II dismisses R.T. Wiriadiningrat (granted 50 cacahs); replaced by Kliwon Mangunyuda.
* **24 April 1804 (Cotton Thread Monopoly):**
  * Governor of Semarang inquires regarding purchase of cotton thread (*cattoene garens*) @ 15 guilders per pikul (125 lbs).
  * Cotton thread can only be purchased once yearly when Mangkunagaran Regents visit Yogya.
  * Thread sorted and sold @ 4 1/2 – 6 Rixdollars per 100 threads (average 5 1/2 R.R.; 600 threads = 1 pikul). Actual cost 31 1/2 – 42 Rixdollars; extremely difficult to gather 50–60 pikuls.
* **2 May 1804:** Letter from PB IV (via envoy Gardek Prayaminggala) notifying HB II of Mangkupraja's dismissal and appointment of **R.T. Cakrajaya as Raden Adipati Danuningrat**.
