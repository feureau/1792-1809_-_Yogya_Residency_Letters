### Archive Entries: 1806–1807 (Wiese Homage, Pacitan Shipwreck, & Royal Marriages)

* Reference: **Pj. Brieven No. 38 (1806–1807)**
* **8 February 1806 (Homage to Governor-General Wiese):** Patihs of Solo and Yogya offer official homage to A.H. Wiese (*ref: 27 Mar. 1802 homage to N. Ilberg*). R.T. Numadiningrat, R.T. Natadiningrat, and HB II's secretary Ng. Prawirananstra accompany Crown Prince; Surakarta Regents ride out ahead of Yogyakarta Regents.
* **29 July 1806:** PB IV elevates 3 sons as Princes; P. Aria Ngabehi scheduled to marry PB IV's sister Ratu Purbanegara in *Rabingulakir*.
* **13 September 1806:** HB II opposes V.O.C. land-surveying teams in Yogyakarta.
* **29 September 1806:** HB II appoints R.T. Pringgadiwiria as 1st Regent and Kyai T. Mangundiwiria as 2nd Regent in Loano (replacing Kliwon Mangunjaya).
* **11 January 1807 (Pacitan American Shipwreck):**
  * 10 Europeans arrive in a lifeboat in Pacitan Bay following a shipwreck.
  * Captain/Lieutenant Ntcerman born in America; 3 sailors (from Würtemberg, Copenhagen, Amsterdam, Berlin).
  * *Rescued Cargo:* Brandy, Geneva gin, cheese, butter, large mirrors, fine glassware.
* **18 February 1807 (Royal Marriage Witness):** Surakarta court figures (Mas Pengulu, Mas Keti Anom, Keti Arum) seek Yogyakarta Prince Natapura to serve as witness at the marriage of Raden Ajeng Aliya (daughter of P. Natadiningrat) to R.Ng. Wirayuda (son of R.Ria Natapada).
