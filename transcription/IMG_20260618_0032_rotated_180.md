### Archive Entries: January – May 1793 (Gresik Ordnance, Weddings, Chinese Community)

* Reference: **Port 3 No. 1. NvB 1793 (Yogya)**
* **19 January 1793:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten: HB II's envoy Gardek Jiwanaga sent to Semarang to provide information about marriage in HB II's family.
* **19 January 1793 (Ibid.):** -> Van Reede: HB II's envoy Gardek Prawirasari sent to Solo to report on wedding the following week.
* **22 January 1793:** Van IJsseldijk -> Gezaghebber Oosthoek A. van Rijck: HB II's coachman B. Lammers + Asst. Wenceslas Reves escort cannons and ammunition from Surabaya overland to Semarang.
* **22 January 1793:** Van IJsseldijk -> Resident of Gresik: Red ink: **Cannon / ammunition from Gresik.** Envoys sent to collect cannons/ammunition from Gresik via Solo River.
* **4 February 1793:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten: 50 pistols sent by Van Overstraten to Yogya as wedding present to Crown Prince (paid for by HB II).
* **13 February 1793:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten: PEPPER CULTIVATION / Indigo Fabric lands ceded by HB II.
* **14 February 1793:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Reede: Envoy of PB IV Prawiraninggala sent to report on marriage in PB IV's family (Cakarirana).
* **15 April 1793:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Reede: Reports murder of grave guard at Prambanan by Chinese *bandhar* at Brenkong Dantian. Nephew found guilty.
* **4 May 1793:** Van IJsseldijk -> Boedelmeester Semarang: Death of Kapitan Cina of Kedu, Gan Kaksing. Declaration of Head Bandhar in Yogya, Que Tjin Jing (father of Tan Tjin Sing), regarding Moorish/Arab merchant Koja Libi.
