* **Karta Truna** Jav. from Banwêrna
  * taken prisoner after Breaking in to grave of Sultan [*inserted*] **Prabu Satmata at Giri.**
  * in July had eaten a flower bloom placed on grave. (13 canari flower?)
  * lives Behind Dalêm of Bp. Prawirasêntika of Banwêrna.
  * KT [Karta Truna] Brother-in-law of Jaksa.
* Macan Gimbor / & another priest join him on leaving Banwêrna
* KT had earlier Robbed graves at Blongnong in Jipang & Tuban.
* Escapes from gaol in Banwêrna — recognized as mad
* often gives name as **R. Trunajaya.** [*encircled in red*]
* captured at Giri attempting to steal **Pusaka kris** from grave.
