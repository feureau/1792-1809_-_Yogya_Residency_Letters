## Content

### Archive Entries: August – October 1806 (Mecca Pilgrims, Giri Grave Desecration, & Naval Attack)

* **Mecca Pilgrims Update:** HB II inquires whether his Mecca pilgrims have departed Semarang. A.A. Cassa reports (4 Aug. 1806) that the pilgrims boarded an Arab vessel (*Muhammad Wasir* of Tegal) bound for Jeddah. British blockade restricts sea traffic.
* **Giri Grave Desecration Incident (8 Sept. 1806):**
  * Karta Truna (Javanese from Banwerna, brother-in-law of local *Jaksa*) arrested after breaking into the sacred tomb of **Prabu Natamata at Giri** in July 1803 and eating a flower bloom placed on the grave.
  * Accompanied by priests Macan Gimbor and Nura; previously plundered tombs at Blongnong in Jipang and Tuban.
  * Escapes Banwerna prison; recognized as insane and adopts the name **Raden Trunajaya**. Re-captured at Giri attempting to steal a *pusaka* kris from the tomb.
* **British Naval Attack on Batavia (28 Oct. 1806):**
  * **British warships attack and destroy Dutch frigate *"Maria Reigersbergen"* in Batavia Bay**; capture additional vessels at Onrust and Middelburg.
  * Engelhard instructs Waterloo to warn HB II of Admiral Pellew's British squadron in Javanese waters and prepare Yogyakarta auxiliary troops.
