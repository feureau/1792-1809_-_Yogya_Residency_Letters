### Tollgate Regulations: Dj. Br. 48 (Typewritten Transcript Page 1)

* **Document:** *Tollgate Regulations (10 Apr. 1802 sent by Van den Berg to Nijvenheim)*

```text
Chinese journeying from Yogya - Semarang with cloth (kleedjes)
  6 dubbeltjes per horse now 8.
Javanese ---------------------------- with kleedjes and garens
  17 1/2 stuivers now 22 1/2 stuivers.
Chinese ---------------------------- with ginger (gember) 12 stuivers per horse now 16 stuivers.
Javanese ---------------------------- diverse goods, indigo, oil
  18 3/4 stuivers now 22 1/2 stuivers.
Chinese from Semarang to Yogya carrying opium pays 4 rondematten
  (? - f. 3.40 - 1 ronde real) now 6 (f. 2.80 = Ronde Real).
Javanese Ibid.
Chinese or Javanese from Semarang to Kedhu and Bagelen with opium Ibid.
Chinese from Pesantren in Kedhu to Semarang with mats pays 8 dubbeltjes per horse now 12 ds.
Javanese from ---------------------------- 40 stuivers per horse now 48 stuivers.
Jav. from here to Semarang taking kleedjes 10 stuivers each pikul ? now 15 stuivers.
Jav. from here to Semarang carrying various goods in pikuls earlier 10 now 12 1/2 stuivers.
Chin. from Bagelen to Semarang carrying kleedjes pays 1 rondemat per 5 pikuls now 1 rondemat per 4 pikuls.
Jav. from Bagelen to Semarang taking clothes (? per pikul) pays 21 1/4 now 24 1/2 stuivers.
Jav. from Semarang to Yogya carrying ginger pays 18 3/4 per horse now 22 1/2.
Chin. from Semarang to Yogya bringing ginger 12 stuivers per horse now 16 (? lower Chin. rate because of monopoly).
Chin. or Jav. from Semarang to Yogya bringing ginger pays according to old rate per pikul.
Jav. from Payaman in Kedhu and Pesantren to Yogya bringing rice pays 5 stuivers per horse now 6 stuivers.
```
