### Description & Text

*[This is a second copy / carbon copy of the typed Tollgate Regulations sheet transcribed in folio 0105, without the later red ink annotations.]*

* **TOLLGATE REGULATIONS** — **Dj. Br. 48**
* **Tollgate regulations 10 Apr. 1802 sent by van den Berg to Nijvenheim.**
* Chinese journeying from Yogya – Sĕmarang with cloth (*kêeedjes*): 6 dubbeltjes per horse, now 8.
* Javanese ---------------------------- with kêeedjes and garens: 17½ stuivers, now 22½ stuivers.
* Chinese ---------------------------- with ginger (*gember*): 12 stuivers per horse, now 16 stuivers.
* Javanese --------------------------- diverse goods, indigo, oil: 18¾ stuivers, now 22½ stuivers.
* Chinese from Sĕmarang to Yogya carrying opium pays 4 rondematten (? – f. 3.40 – 1 ronde real), now 6 ".
* Javanese Ibid.
* Chinese or Javanese from Sĕmarang to Kĕdhu and Bagĕlen with opium: Ibid.
* Chinese from Pĕsantren in Kĕdhu to Sĕmarang with mats pays 8 dubbeltjes per horse, now 12 ds.
* Javanese from ------------------------------------: 40 stuivers per horse, now 48 stuivers.
* Jav. from here to Sĕmarang taking kleedjes: 10 stuivers each pikul ? now 15 stuivers.
* Jav. from here to Sĕmarang carrying various goods in pikuls earlier 10, now 12½ stuivers.
* Chin. from Bagĕlen to Sĕmarang carrying kleedjes pays 1 rondemat per 5 pikuls, now 1 rondemat per 4 pikuls.
* Jav. from Bagĕlen to Sĕmarang taking clothes (? per pikul) pays 21¼, now 24½ stuivers.
* Jav. from Sĕmarang to Yogya carrying ginger pays 18¾ per horse, now 22½.
* Chin. from Sĕmarang to Yogya bringing ginger 12 stuivers per horse, now 16 (? lower Chin. rate because of monopoly).
* Chin. or Jav. from Sĕmarang to Yogya bringing ginger pays according to old rate per pikul.
* Jav. from Payaman in Kĕdhu and Pĕsantren to Yogya bringing rice pays 5 stuivers per horse, now 6 stuivers.
