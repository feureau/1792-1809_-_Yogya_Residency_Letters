### Monopoly Terms & Labour Regulations (Loano / Pacitan)

- **Pricing:** V.O.C. to pay **7 Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.) per pikul** for pepper.
- **Anti-Smuggling Monopoly:** All pepper passing through tollgates declared property of the V.O.C. monopoly.
- Cost of exporting pepper from Loano and Pacitan to be defrayed by V.O.C.
- Target: Production to be raised to the promised **5,000 pikuls of pepper** within 2 years.
- **HB II's Reservation:** Begs to be excused from the earlier promise of expanding pepper cultivation across other districts of Java (*? promise made upon his accession in April 1792*).

### Winong Indigo Factory & Compulsory Days:

- Land / ricefields of Winong to be designated for the Indigo Factory (**100 cacahs in all**).
- Labor obligation: When villagers are not actively employed in the Indigo Factory, they are required to labour on pepper plantations for **at least 48 days per annum**.
