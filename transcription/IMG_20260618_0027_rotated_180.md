### V.O.C. Pepper Terms & Monopoly Provisions (1793)

* **Prices & Monopoly:** V.O.C. to pay 7 silver ducats (*Sp. D.*) per pikul for pepper. To prevent smuggling, all pepper passing through tollgates declared V.O.C. monopoly property.
* **Delivery Commitments:** Transport costs borne by V.O.C. Production target set at 5,000 pikuls within 2 years.
* **Exemptions:** HB II seeks exemption from earlier commitments to push pepper production in other areas of Java (made upon accession in April 1792).
* **Indigo Obligations:** Winong ricefields (100 cacahs) dedicated to Indigo Factory. When not working indigo, farmers required to work pepper plantations at least 48 days per year.
* **Pacitan Regent:** 62 Pacitan + Bupati Nitrawijaya *(See original manuscript p. 82)*.
