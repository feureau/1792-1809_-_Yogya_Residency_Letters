### Mecca Pilgrims Wahhabi Abort & Daendels Opium Monopoly Quotas (1805–1808)

* **Mecca Pilgrims Wahhabi Abort (10 Jan. 1806):**
  * V.O.C. pays Np.D. 3,360 to an Armenian merchant to transport HB II's pilgrims to Batavia.
  * While in Batavia, 7 pilgrims request permission to return to Yogyakarta after receiving news that **the Tomb of the Prophet Muhammad in Medina was destroyed by Wahhabis**.
* **25 February 1808 (Daendels' Instructions):** Daendels issues new instructions for Residents of Yogyakarta and Surakarta (*Staat p. 31*).
* **Daendels Opium Monopoly Distribution (25 May / 1 June 1808 pp. 762–766):**
  * Opium purchased in Batavia @ 3,000–7,000 Rijksdaalders per chest; smugglers supply opium for under 1,000 per chest.
  * To incentivize anti-smuggling enforcement, Opium chests are allocated among Java Residencies @ Np.D. 1,500 silver per chest (resold at Np.D. 100 profit per chest):
    * Semarang / Kaliwungu / Demak / Kendal: 54 chests
    * Oosthoek / Ungaran: 48 chests
    * Surakarta / Yogyakarta: 48 chests
    * Japara: 14 chests
    * Rembang: 13 chests
    * Tegal: 9 chests
    * Pekalongan: 9 chests
    * **Total:** **195 chests**.
