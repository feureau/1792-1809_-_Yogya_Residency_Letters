# Transcription: IMG_20260618_0199_rotated_180.jpg

**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260618_0199_rotated_180.jpg`  
**Folio / Reference:** A.N. Yogya Letters No. 47  
**Date Range:** 20 November 1805 – 30 November 1805  
**Primary Subjects:**  
- Ratu Kencana Wulan lobbying aggressively for the dismissal of Raden Rangga Prawiradirja III  
- HB II's 3rd journey to the South Coast cut short by severe South/South-West gales and the approach of Puasa (fasting month)  
- Severe storm damage in Yogya: two giant *waringin* trees uprooted in the Residency garden; wall of the Chinese Kampong collapsed  
- Governor Nicolaas Engelhard proposes traveling *incognito* to Central Java (to inspect Klaten Fortress)  
- Waterloo advises against incognito travel:  
- Javanese concepts of status preclude a ruler traveling incognito; Surakarta would take deep offense at the slight to the "oldest court in the Vorstenlanden"  
- PB IV would demand a ceremonial reception at Kartasura (as with Van Overstraten in April 1792)  
- Postal tollgates over the Jambu Hills (Secang under Solo, Bedono under Yogya) would instantly detect him  
- Both Courts would suspect Dutch territorial or military designs regarding Klaten

### [30 November 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
30 Nov 1805  
Wat – Eng

PROPOSAL of Engelhard to travel INCOGNITO to V.L. [Vorstenlanden] – but Wat says it would NOT BE POSSIBLE, easily found out:  
PB IV would INSIST on meeting Engelhard at KARTASURA just as meets van Overstraten in 1792 (April).  

[if ENG comes over JAMBU HILLS also NOT feasible (to inspect? Klaten)]

(1) Sala (if they heard about it) would regard it as a slight to the OLDEST COURT IN V.L.  

[Javanese no idea that a great man should or could travel Incognito]

(2) ENG must pass to STAGE POST toll-gates on Jambu Road:  

- SECANG (Sala)  
- BEDONO (Yogya)

(3) ? Both SULTAN / SUNAN would think Eng has Designs on Klaten.
