### Residency Protocol, Pepper Estates, and Coastal Surveys (1805–1806)

* **Residency Visit Protocols:** PB IV visits the 1st Resident's house only for Governor-General visits or New Year's Day celebrations. The Resident and his wife escort PB IV from the Kraton late at night (if unmarried, 1st Resident escorts the Ratu; 2nd Resident escorts the Sunan). Wash basins and tea presented upon arrival.
* **Pepper Estate Oversight:**
  * Tanjong / Pacitan pepper plantations managed by a European inspector (*Opzichter*).
  * Mangkunagara II's Tashawang pepper plantations contain 300,000 trees; managed by native inspector Daniel Portier.
* **North Coast Hydrographic Surveys (1 June 1806):**
  * Marine Lieutenant Kortsino and cadets from the Semarang Marine School survey North Coast bays and river mouths.
  * Survey teams operate in Banyumas surveying Cilacap Bay, coordinating with Resident Waterloo in Yogya to survey HB II's coastal lands.
* **Coastal Travel Restrictions (15 May 1805):** Delegations of PB IV's *Ratus* to North Coast ports / Madura restricted due to logistical burdens imposed on local V.O.C. officers.
* **Military Defenses:** P. Tepasana and Tum. Mangkupraja assigned to Semarang to prepare defenses against potential enemy landings.
