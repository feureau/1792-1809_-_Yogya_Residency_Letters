### Archive Entries: 1805 (Restitution of Stolen Goods & Mangkunagaran Legion Allowance)

* Reference: **B.P. 1616 N. Van B. No. 4 p. 2 / Bijdragen en Mededeelingen 1971**
* **Restitution of Stolen Property:** Governor Nicolaas Engelhard writes to Pangeran Prangwedana regarding the restitution of goods stolen from Mangkunagaran soldiers and servants by former Resident Baron van Reede tot de Parkeler (valued at 40,600 silver dollars).
* **Mangkunagaran Legion Subsidy:** Inquiries regarding the continuation of the annual 2,000 Rixdollars subsidy for the Prangwedana Legion. Signed by N. Engelhard, Governor of Java's N.E. Coast.
