### Title of the Memorie:

*Memorie voor den Weledelen Gestrengen Heer Nicolaas Engelhard, Raad Extraordinair van N.I. en aankomende Gouverneur en Directeur van Java's Noord Oost Kust, door den Weledelen Gestrengen Heer Johan Frederik van Reede tot den Parkeler, Raad Extraordinair mitsgaders afgaande Gouv. en Dir., bij deszelfs vertrek naar Batavia gearresteerd Semarang Ultimo Augustus 1801.*

---

### Character & Habits of Pakubuwana IV (f. 8):

- PB IV gives the impression of being considerably less impetuous and less inclined to intrigues than in earlier years; acts as a trusted ally of the V.O.C.
- On Saturdays, he makes tours on horseback on the *Alun-alun*.
- During the daytime, he always informs the Resident when he goes outside the kraton.
- *Secret movements (red margin bar):* Sometimes goes out at night incognito without informing the Resident, accompanied only by a very small escort.
- Inside the kraton, he is always surrounded by children; amuses himself with horse riding, watching *wayang*, and **reading Javanese manuscripts (MSS.)**.
