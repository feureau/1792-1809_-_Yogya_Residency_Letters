### Van Reede Memorie van Overgave (August 1801)

* Reference: **Java N.O.K. No. 47 MvO van Johan Frederik Baron van Reede tot den Parkeler aan Nicolaas Engelhard (Ultimo Augustus 1801)**

#### Pakubuwana IV Character & Daily Life:
* PB IV appears considerably less impetuous and less inclined to intrigue than in earlier years, acting as a trusted ally of the V.O.C.
* **Routine:** Rides on the Alun-alun on Saturdays; informs the Resident when making daytime excursions. Occasionally goes out at night incognito with a small escort without notifying the Resident.
* **In the Kraton:** Constantly surrounded by children; amuses himself riding horses, watching *wayang*, and reading Javanese manuscripts.

#### Royal Family:
* Married 3rd wife **Ratu Kencana Wulan** in 1791 (3 sons and 2 daughters born; 2 sons died in childhood).
  * Surviving children: Raden Ayu Natariah (born 6 Rabingulakir 1720 / 1793), Raden Ayu Kasbandiah / Purbaya (born 8 Ruwah 1721 / 1794), and Raden Mas Gusti Malikinsaliken (born 15 Sura 1723).
  * Education of Crown Prince neglected [1796].
