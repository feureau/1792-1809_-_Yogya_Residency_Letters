### Main Text & Margin Notes

| Margin Notes | Main Text |

| **25 March 1792** | ### Aftermath of Hamengkubuwana I's Death (March–April 1792)<br><br>#### Succession Agreement (25 March 1792 Cont.):<br><br>- According to the express wish of HB I [Sultan Hamengkubuwana I], the Crown Prince is to be recognized as Sultan immediately, having already ruled de facto beside his father.<br>- **Condition:** He should **not** be officially installed/crowned until the arrival of the Governor of Java's Northeast Coast and the formal renewal of treaties.<br>- All nobles present affirm their solemn obligation to observe this procedure.<br>- Crown Prince promises to await the arrival of the Governor before formal coronation.<br>- Direction of state affairs placed firmly in the hands of **Resident Van IJsseldijk** and **Patih Danureja I (DRI)**.<br>- No princes display inclination to rebel; precautionary measures prepared by V.O.C. are not required.<br><br>---<br><br>#### Official Notifications to Surakarta:<br><br>- **26 March 1792**: IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede (Solo):<br>  - Official letter from Crown Prince (HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]) to PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] announcing the death of HB I.<br>  - Envoys (*gandèk*): Jiwanegat sent to PB IV; Ng. Kartayuda and Trunamēnggala sent to Crown Prince of Solo.<br><br>---<br><br>#### Investiture of Royal Princes:<br><br>- **3 April [1792]** (IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten) / **13 April** ($\rightarrow$ Reede tot de Parkeler):<br>  - Investiture of 5 sons of HB I as Princes (*Pangeran*) this morning:<br>    1. Raden Aria Paninggah<br>    2. Raden Aria Mērtasana<br>    3. Raden Aria Wiramēnggala<br>    4. Raden Aria Pamot<br>    5. Raden Aria Moendiman<br><br>---<br><br>#### Defiance of Pangeran Ngabehi:<br><br>- **Pangeran Ngabèhi** declares himself entirely exempt from taking orders from HB II:<br>  > *Vrij verklaart van het droegen der lasten* (declares himself free from bearing burdens).<br><br>- *(Margin notes in red ink: **Pangeran Amardika** / Ngabehi intrigues)*. |
