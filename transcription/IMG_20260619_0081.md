### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.41: Military Reinforcements & Madura Royal Elevation (June – August 1808)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.41 (5 June – 15 Aug. 1808)**
* **5 June 1808 (Garrison Reinforcements):**
  * PB IV acknowledges Daendels' defense directives to secure Java against British invasion.
  * Ungaran Artillery company (10 cannons, 150 men) and Jager company (700–800 men) dispatched to Surakarta.
  * Infantry garrisons in Surakarta, Yogyakarta, and Klaten increased to 100 men each (cavalry similarly reinforced).
  * Salatiga Mounted Artillery reinforced to 10 cannons and 300 men.
  * PB IV constructs bamboo barracks in Surakarta for reinforcements (ready prior to 14 July 1808); keeps Surakarta troops on war footing.
* **21–23 July 1808 (Elevation of Madura Ruler):**
  * **Pangeran Cakraningrat of Pamekasan elevated to Sultan Nepuh of Madura** in recognition of supplying troops for Batavia, Ambon, and the *Groote Oost*.
  * HB II, Ratu Kedhaton, Ratu Mas, Ratu Kencana Wulan, and Crown Prince send formal congratulations to Daendels.
* **15 August 1808 (Diplomatic Protocol Clarification):** Daendels clarifies new court protocol (28 July 1808): elevating the Senior Resident to "Minister" and 2nd Resident to "Secretary" honors HB II and matches European diplomatic practice in China.
