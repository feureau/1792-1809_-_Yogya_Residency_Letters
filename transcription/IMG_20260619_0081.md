**PB IV to Daendels, 11 Jumadilawal, 1735 / 5 Jul. 1808:**  

* Receives D's letter written at Bedarah Wed. 28 Jun. 1808 (arrives at Bedarah on 28 Jun. 1808).
* In order to ensure security in Java in face of English threat =

<span style="color:red">**Troop placements in Yogya**</span>  

* Comp. of Artillery at Ungaran to be dispatched to Solo: 10 cannon / 150 men.
* Comp. of Jägers — 7-800 men to Solo.
* Infantry at Solo / Yogya / Klaten to be increased to garrisons: each 100 men. Similarly for Cavalry.
* Mounted Artillery at Salatiga reinforced + 4 cannon; now 10 cannon / 300 men.
* PB IV promises to prepare Bamboo Barracks to house garrison reinforcements. To be prepared before 20 Jumadilawal (14 July '08).
* To help transport ball / cartridges to Solo. Co-ordinates Col. Gordon in Semarang.
* Promises to keep Solo troops on war footing.

* Receives D's letter from Surabaya 16 Jul. 1808.
* Pan. Cakraningrat <span style="color:red">of Pamekasan</span> appointed as Sultan Sepuh <u>of Madura</u>.
* Because of services in providing troops for Batavia / Groote Oost / Ambon etc.

**HB II to Daendels, 29 Jumadilawal, 1735 / 23 Jul. 1808:**  

* As above on receiving D's letter of appointment of Pan. Cakraningrat as Sultan of Madura + *Wêdono* of Bps of Gresik, Sidayu + Oosthoek to Banyuwangi.
* Also Greetings of Ratu Kedhaton / Ratu Mas / Ratu Kencana Wulan / CP — Daendels.
