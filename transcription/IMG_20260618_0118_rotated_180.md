### AvJ Archive Entries: 1802 (Bandit Expeditions, Border Canals, & Pakepung Precedents)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1802**
* **4 January 1802:** Permission granted for Mangkunagaran Regents to return home.
* **17 March 1802 (Anti-Bandit Expedition):** Following the murder of Ratu Ageng's factor Mertalaksana (14 Jan. 1802), HB II dispatches a military expedition against *kècu* bandits led by P. Adinegara, R.T. Jayawinala, Kliwon Vindayuda, Panji Padmawijaya, and other heads to Kedu, Bagelèn, Sukawati, and Gunung Kendeng.
* **12 March 1802 (Border & Canal Disputes):**
  * Boundary dispute over Loban desa (Yogya - 300 cacahs) and Palembon desa (Solo - 200 cacahs). Loban ceded to Solo by Yogya due to geographic proximity to Solo.
  * Surakarta inhabitants construct an irrigation canal detouring water to the detriment of Yogya farmers.
  * Solo Regents revive grievances dating back to the **1790 Pakepung affair**.
  * Conflict at Pasintan / Pasampakan (border village shared by Solo/Yogya): Solo and Yogya heads clash over land; Yogya *Mantri* Ng. Mangurminggala (Sutawijaya) killed by a bullet.
  * Joint commission appointed consisting of **Mangkupraja** (Solo Patih) and **Danureja II** (Yogya Patih); 9 articles of *Věrat Anggèr* used to resolve the conflict.
