### [12 Mar 1802]

* **12 Mar 1802, Res. Yogya**
  * **Difficulties Sala / Yogya:**
    * Desas **Loban** (Yogya, 300 cacah) / **Palembon** (Sala, 200 cacah).
    * Loban ceded to Sala by Yogya because too far from Yogya – too close to Sala [very often the case].
    * Sala inhabitants build an irrigation canal (in Demang Hartowino in Sala) to detriment of Yogyarese.
    * Because of 1790 affair Pakepung in Sala, affair brought up again by Sala Regents.
  * **Affair of Pagêntan / Pasampakan:**
    * Desa belongs half to Sala / Yogya.
    * Yogya Mantri appointed = Ng. Mangunmênggala (Sutawijaya).
    * Sala Head of Pagêntan / Head of Sala Desa Pasampakan in conflict over piece of ground.
    * In battle: Yogya Mantri killed by a bullet between Sala Heads defending Yogya area.
    * Subordinate of Mangunmênggala — Ki Kērtayuda — reports to DR II on Pasebaan.
  * *[see xerox copy]*
    * Commission sent = Mangkupraja (Sala), DR II (Yogya).
    * None of articles of Sêrat Anggèr can decide the affair...
