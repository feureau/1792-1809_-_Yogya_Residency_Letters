### AvJ Archive Entries: 1808 (Ratu Kencana Wulan Trading Ventures & Surakarta Promotions)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1808 (H 698b)**
* **6 January 1808 (Ratu Kencana Wulan Commercial Ventures):**
  * Nicolaas Engelhard writes to Matthew Waterloo regarding trade ventures organized by **Ratu Kencana Wulan**.
  * Queen's messengers (Nyai Lidok Mirah, Ng. Risawirana, Ng. Risasentika) dispatched to Semarang to purchase luxury commercial goods from Semarang Head Regent Kyai Tumenggung Adipati Sura-Adimenggala.
* **26 March 1808 (Surakarta Pagelaran Promotions):**
  * R.T. Cakrakusuma (favorite of PB IV) elevated on the *Pagelaran* as **B.P.H. Cakrakusuma**.
  * Sunan's troop commander R. Panji Parta-Udaya promoted to R. Ria Parta-Udaya.
  * Son of deceased Ponorogo Bupati appointed new Bupati under the title R.T. Wiriadirja.
