### Djokjo Brieven No. 39: Royal Consort Titles (1805) & Mecca Pilgrims (1806)

* Reference: **Djokjo Brieven No. 39 (1805–1809)**
* **31 July 1805:** Dispatch regarding HB II's ambassadors to Mecca; embassy scheduled to travel via Semarang to Batavia.
* **5 August 1805:** Yogya Fort repairs delayed by poor quality *sirap* shingles; HB II promises repairs for 2 years without progress.
* **26 August 1805 (Elevation of HB II's Consorts):**
  * **HB II elevates Ratu Anom to Ratu Mas.**
  * *Titles conferred:*
    * R. Aj. Sriwulan = **Ratu Kencana Wulan** (*permaisuri*)
    * R.A. Numadiningrat = **Ratu Bindara**
    * R.A. Danureja = **Ratu Angger**
    * R.A. Rangga PWD = **Ratu Maduretna**
    * R. Ay. Nepuh = **Ratu Anom**
  * Resident Waterloo expresses annoyance at R. Aj. Sriwulan receiving the title of *permaisuri* (matching PB IV's chief queen).
* **4 February 1806 (Mecca Pilgrims - 27 Pilgrims):**
  * HB II dispatches 27 pilgrims to Mecca, led by **Kyai Haji Muhamad Mataram**, Benawi, Nenawi, Muhidin, Haji Mukmin, Nanap, Raphim, Haji Nur Kasim, Abdullah Imeran, Haji Aridin, Sukur, Haji Muhamad, Muhamad Namaan, Dono Nitir, Haji Ahmad, Nur Rahman, Habit, Amad Mesir, Amad Iman, Caruben, Tayar, Wirawijaya, Baki Lawi, Amad Arif, Jaka Ria, Umar, Abdullah.
  * Turned back by Batavia government due to Anglo-French naval warfare in India; 1 pilgrim dies in Batavia before returning.
* **1806 Earthquakes & Klaten Damage:**
  * 27 Feb 1806: 3,000 Cirebon rebels infest roads above Batavia.
  * 27 Mar 1806: Earthquake damages Yogya Fort; similar repairs required at Klaten Fort.
  * 23 Aug 1806: Complaints regarding Yogya workforce building Klaten Fort (planks/timber delayed, Mangkunagaran heads absent).
