### Padalangan Plunder Inventory & Peasant Wealth Indicators (12 November 1808)

* Reference: **Report on Desa Padalangan Raid (12 Nov. 1808)**
* Document: Detailed inventory of goods plundered when Dutch Jagers and PB IV's emissaries raided Desa Padalangan (Yogyakarta village belonging to Pariket Gedong Pananya) searching for military deserters.

#### Stolen Property & Personal Wealth Inventories:
* **Lurah Dipananga:** 200 *gindels* raw cotton (*kapas*), 24 woven cotton pieces, 1 piece damask, 24 sheaves (*bonschen*) of padi, 1 1/4 lbs woven cotton.
* **Priests Amad Tenani & Nitiplutaranag:** 50 *gindels* cotton each, sheaves of padi.
* **Timinggola:** **180 Ducatoons in cash** (1 Ducatoon = f. 3.20).
* **Rajawangna:** 200 *gindels* cotton, 3 *Rondemaaten* cash, 1 pike (worth 3 *Rondemaaten*), 840 rds., 1 kris, 6 gold buttons (worth f. 7).
* **Bekel Ninetrana:** 100 *gindels* cotton, 24 sheaves padi, 70 trousers/garments.
* **Ningadarana:** 100 *gindels* cotton, 30 sheaves padi, 36 garments.
* **Stredipaw:** 400 *gindels* cotton, 150 sheaves padi, 3 *Rondemaaten* cash, 4 white garments (@ 5 dubbeltjes each).
* **Angayuda & Dipadiwangna:** 100–150 *gindels* cotton, 14–100 sheaves padi.
* *Historical Analysis:* **Detailed evidence of substantial wealth accumulated by rich peasants (*sikeps*) in Javanese rural villages.**
