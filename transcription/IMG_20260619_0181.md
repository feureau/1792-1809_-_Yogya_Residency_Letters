### Diary of Marshal Daendels' State Visit to Yogyakarta: Part 3 (31 July – 1 August 1809)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 24 (31 July – 1 Aug. 1809)**
* **31 July 1809 (Inspection of Royal Pesanggrahans & Serimpi Performances):**
  * Fireworks in Residency garden organized by Capitan Cina; banquet hosted at Residency.
  * **Tour of Royal Estates:** Daendels and HB II tour Rajawinangun, Wanacatur, Purwareja, and Parangtritis; inspect royal fountains, pools, hunting lodges, and mountain-top pavilions.
  * **6:00 p.m. Srimenganti Audience & Serimpi Dances:**
    * HB II escorts Daendels to Srimenganti; introduces him to his favorite chief queen, **Ratu Kencana Wulan** (3 ornate buildings placed at Daendels' disposal).
    * Special *Serimpi* dance performances staged; lavish Javanese banquet served.
    * 9:30 p.m.: Daendels departs Kraton, warmly embraced by HB II at Srimenganti.
* **1 August 1809 (Rampok Macan & Formal Departure):**
  * Crown Prince escorts Daendels to Kraton @ 8:00 a.m.
  * **Rampok Macan Spectacle:** Staged on the Alun-alun for an hour before Daendels.
  * Official farewell taken of HB II, three *Ratus*, and royal children; 3/4 hour conference held.
