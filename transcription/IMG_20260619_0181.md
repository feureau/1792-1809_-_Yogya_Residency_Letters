### [Transcription]

AN "Djokjo Brieven" no. 24. **1809 2 Aug. (28–31 July)**  

[Red ink: *Daendels' visit to Yogya. 2.*]

Fireworks in Residency garden (?organized by Capitein Cina).  
In evening — Supper in the Residency.

*31 July:*  
Daendels rides out + HB II -> Rajawinangun.  
-> pesanggrahan: Wanacatur, Purwareja  
Inspect buildings — Fountains / Ngawatrêja.  
<u>well laid out pools. — Hunting places / underground passages / on top of mountains.</u>  

[Red ink: *pêsanggrahan of HB II*]

CP greets D at Rajawinangun:  
D returns at 10 o'clock.

6.00 In evening taken by HB II -> Kraton: meeting in Srimēnganti.  
D visits youngest and most cherished wife of HB II: Ratu Kêncana Wulan.  
<u>Sêrimpi Dances:</u> 3 Beautiful buildings placed at her disposal.  
Supper for Daendels — most Delicious Javanese Dishes:  
Friendly conversation HB II -> Daendels.  
9.30 — Daendels goes back embraced by HB II at Srimēnganti.

*1 August:*  
CP -> Residency house 8.00 accompanies D -> Kraton.  
<u>Rampok Macan:</u> watched for an hour with great pleasure.  
After Rampok official Leave Taken of HB II.  
Three Ratus present + children.  
Talk 1/2 of an hour.
