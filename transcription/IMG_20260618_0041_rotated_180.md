### Archive Entries: October – December 1794 (Loano Pepper Monopoly & Troop Mobilization)

* **29 October 1794 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Reede):** No private trade in pepper permitted in the West; V.O.C. monopoly maintained. Returning envoys of DRI (Danureja I) oversee exchange of lands in Loano.
* **4 December 1794 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Reede):** Dispatch of Dragoon Brigade to Semarang (commando): 1 Lieutenant, 1 Cornet/Sergeant, 2 Corporals, 28 Men, 2 Trumpeters/Tambours.
* **5 December 1794 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten):** Acknowledges letter of 1 Dec regarding arrival of 5 French warships in Sunda Strait.
  * HB II's troops requested to be put on stand-by under command of oldest son of DRI: **Aria Mandura**, **P. Adinegara** (+ 700 men), **R.T. Narawijaya**, **Jasrakusuma**, and **R.T. Mangunrana (Kliwon Dalem)**.
