### [Transcription]

No commerce in pepper to be allowed in the Vorstenlanden:  
so monopoly remains + V.O.C.

---

**29 Oct 1794**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede (Solo):**  
Return of messengers of Danureja I / Danuningrat to look after exchange of land in **LOANO**.

---

**4 Dec 1794**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede:**  
Following Monday: Brigade of Dragoons $\rightarrow$ Semarang (*commando*):  

- 1 Lieutenant  
- 1 Cornet  
- 1 Sergeant  
- 2 Corporals  
- 28 Men  
- 2 Pipers / Drummers (*Tambours*)  

---

**5 Dec 1794**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:**  
Letter of Van Overstraten 1 Dec = arrival in Sunda Straits of **5 French ships**.  

**HB II's TROOPS asked to be at the ready:**  
To be commanded by eldest son of Danureja I:  

- Aria Mandura [Mendura]  
- P. [Pangeran / Raden Tumenggung] Adinegara  
- R.T. Narawijaya  
- Sasrakusuma  
- R.T. Mangunrana (Kliwon Dalem)  
+ 700 men.
