### 1793 Van IJsseldijk Report 1: Loano & Pacitan Pepper Estates

* **Reference:** P.J. Br. 45 in Bundle 51 w. Van IJsseldijk *"Eerbiedige Bericht aangaande de Landen van Zijn Hoogheid den Sultan te Prodjocarta 1793."*
* **Date:** 15 Jan. 1793 (Van IJsseldijk to Van Overstraten)

#### Land Breakdown & Production:
* **Loano, Gintang, Pacitan:** 700 cacahs / 168 jungs sawah.
* **Indigo:** + 100 cacahs Indigo = Idea of setting up Indigo Factory.
* **Corvée Exemption:** Owners / farmers to be released of corvée services to Sultan.
* **Inspection:** After Van Overstraten's visit to Yogya in Apr. 1792, Van IJsseldijk visits pepper estates.

#### Regional Details:
* **Loano & Adjacent Lands (Winong / Malarang):** 350 cacahs (2 hours journey W. of Yogya).
* **Gentang:** 150 cacahs. *(Pepper not suited for cultivation here; during W. monsoon Praga floods its banks and inundates pepper gardens).*
* **Loano:** Well-suited to pepper cultivation (red / moist soil; small quantities grown for years).
* **Malarang:** Favorable, but too dry in E. monsoon.
* **Alternative:** Pepper should be planted in Nilo Tiyang, Pekalongan instead of Gentang.

#### Local Administration & Inhabitants:
* Complaints of *wong cilik* regarding oppression/extortions by local chiefs.
* Population not very large, but willing to plant more pepper if escaped oppression.
* Demangs of Loano non-cooperative; Lurahs fear giving information. Under new system, Demangs cut out of local administration of corvée deliveries. Insufficient buffaloes.
* Tumenggung Mangundipura (Tumenggung Loano) notes family relationships of Demangs (demanding 6 jungs of land each).
