## Content

### 1793 Van IJsseldijk: Loano / Pacitan Report

*(Dj. Br. 45: W. van Ysseldijk, "Eerbiedige Bericht aangaande de landen van zijn Hoogheid den Sultan te Djocjocarta", 1793 / Letter to Van Overstraten, 15 Jan. 1793)*

#### Overview of Plantations & Cacahs:

- On pepper plantations: **Loano**, **Gèntang**, **Pacitan**.
- **700 cacahs / 168 jungs sawah**.
- **+ 100 cacahs Indigo** = Idea of setting up Indigo Factory.
- Owners / farmers to be released from corvée services to Sultan.
- After Van Overstraten's visit to Yogya (Apr. 1792), Van IJsseldijk visits pepper estates.
  *(Cross-ref: BL Add MS 14397 f. 4r on pepper contingent from Loano / Pacitan; f. 50r–51v pepper contingents Gedhong Tèngen)*.

---

### Local Conditions & Soil Suitability:

- **Loano + Adjacent Lands** (= Winong / Malarang) = 350 cacahs (2 hours' journey to W. of Yogya) + **Gèntang** = 150 cacahs.
- Pepper not suited for cultivation in Gèntang: during W. monsoon Praga River floods its banks, submerging gardens.
- **Loano**: Suited to pepper cultivation: red / moist soil; small quantities grown there for years.
- **Malarang**: Similarly favourable, but too dry in E. monsoon.
- *Recommendation:* Instead of Gèntang, pepper should be planted in **Nelo Tiyang** / **Pekalongan**.

---

### Social Conditions & Administrative Tensions:

- Complaints of *wong cilik* regarding oppression and extortion by local chiefs.
- Inhabitants would plant more pepper if they could escape oppression.
- *Demangs* of Loano are unforthcoming, and *lurah*s fear to provide information.
- Under the new system, *demangs* are cut out of local administration of forced deliveries.
- Shortage of working buffaloes.
- **Tumenggung Mangundipura** (*Tum. Loano*): Entangled in family relationships with *demangs*; reports that *demangs* demand 6 jungs of land each.
