### [Transcription]

#### 1. Shipbuilding & Timber Supplies (f. 36)

- Very few ships are now being constructed for the V.O.C. at Rembang.  
- All heavy timber is now drawn from the **Blora area**, as suitable heavy timber in the Rembang and Semarang districts has become exceedingly scarce.

#### 2. Lasem & Tuban (ff. 36–37)

- **Lasem:**  
  - Size: **2,000 cacahs** under **Tumenggung Suradipura** (married to a daughter of the former first Bupati of Demak).  
  - Dues: 192½ rijksdaalders *cacah-geld*; 1 *pancalang* vessel (11–12 *vaam* in length); 6 pikuls cotton thread.  
  - Under pressure from Van Overstraten, established an **indigo factory**.  
- **Tuban:**  
  - Size: **3,000 cacahs** under **Tumenggung Purbanēgara**.  
  - Unfavourable reports regarding his conduct; recommended that close surveillance be kept over him.  
  - Dues: 60 koyangs rice; 325 rijksdaalders *cacah-geld*; 6 pikuls cotton thread; 1,000 heavy beams.  
  - Indigo factory laid out in 1795.

#### 3. Bawean Island & Sidayu (ff. 37–38)

- **Island of Bawean:**  
  - Described as a *"nest of intrigue"*.  
  - Small V.O.C. fort (*benteng*) constructed in 1786; Dutch bookkeeper (*boekhouder*) stationed there.  
  - Highly suitable for coconut plantations and copra; travel passes required.  
- **Sidayu (f. 38):**  
  - Size: **2,500 cacahs** under the supervision of the Resident of Gresik.  
  - Bupati: **R.T. Suradiningrat** (illegitimate brother of the Panembahan of Madura).  
  - Character: Very spendthrift; heavily dependent upon / subservient to local Muslim priests (*enslaved to priests*).  
  - Dues: 325 Sp.D. *cacah-geld*; 200 koyangs rice; 6 pikuls cotton thread.
