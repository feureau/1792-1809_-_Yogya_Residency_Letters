### Van Overstraten Memorie van Overgave 1796 (Rembang to Sedayu)

* Reference: **6. MvO Van Overstraten 1796**
* **Rembang Shipbuilding (p. 36):** Very few boats currently built for V.O.C. at Rembang. Timber transported from Blora because heavy timber in Rembang/Semarang area has become scarce.
* **Lasem (p. 36):** 2,000 cacahs under Tumenggung Nuradipura. Pays 192 1/2 Rixdollars cacah money + 1 *Pancalang* boat (9, 11–12 *Raam* in length) + 6 pikuls cotton thread. Regent married to daughter of first Bupati of Demak. On Van Overstraten's initiative, an Indigo Factory was established.
* **Tuban (p. 37):** 3,000 cacahs under Tumenggung Purbanegara (unfavorable reports; requires monitoring). Delivers 60 *koyangs* rice, 325 Rixdollars cash, 6 pikuls cotton thread, and 1,000 timber beams. Indigo Factory established last year.
* **Island of Bawean (p. 37):** Center of local maritime intrigue. V.O.C. fort built in 1786 with postholder stationed. Favorable for coconut/copra production; passes required for Javanese traders.
* **Sedayu (p. 38):** 2,500 cacahs under Resident of Gresik (R.T. Suradiningrat). Spendthrift regent enslaved to religious scholars. Delivers 325 Rixdollars cash, 200 *koyangs* rice, and 6 pikuls cotton thread.
