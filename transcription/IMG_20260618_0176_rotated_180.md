### History of Yogyakarta Tax Farm Administration (1762–1774)

* **Sub-leasing to Kedu Capitan Cina:** Reksanegara sub-leases the Tax Farm to Kie (Capitan Cina of Kedu), paying royal revenues to HB I while retaining a small profit. After 1 1/2 years (3 tax terms), Kie surrenders the lease.
* **Resident Lapro's Intervention:** Reksanegara, anxious over tax liabilities, seeks assistance from Resident Lapro. Lapro allows the farm to fall into disarray, then assumes direct control, advancing funds to clear deficits in exchange for a joint share. Reksanegara's son succeeds his father as Tax Farm Regent.
* **Resident Control (1762 Onward):** Tax on Royal Domains and Bird's Nests managed directly by the Resident. Mangkunagaran tax sub-leased by Lapro to the Bupati Wedana of Madiun for 1,750 R.R. p.a. (now yielding 2,500 R.R. p.a.).
* **Escalations:**
  * 1765 (4th Term): Lapro pays 23,000 at start; 31,000 at end of term.
  * 1774 (Van Rhijn): Tax-farm raised by 3,000 to 34,000; 2nd renewal raised to 44,000.
  * Lapro complains bitterly about the 10,000 increase; HB I attempts to appoint 3 Javanese Regents, but native tax administration proves inefficient.
