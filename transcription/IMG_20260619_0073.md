### Abolition of the North East Coast Governorship & Elevation of Residents to Ministers (1808)

* **Surakarta Envoys Audience with Daendels (13 June 1808):** PB IV's envoys (Pangeran Mangkubumi, Pangeran Cakrakusuma, and youngest son Gusti Timur) received by Daendels in Semarang on 31 May 1808 with high honors; return to Solo on 9 June 1808.
* **Abolition of the N.E. Coast Governorship (26 June 1808):**
  * **Daendels officially abolishes the post of Governor of Java's North East Coast.**
  * Residents at native courts elevated to **"Ministers" of King Louis**, directly representing Governor-General Daendels and corresponding directly with Batavia.
  * Residents granted significantly elevated diplomatic rank at native courts.
  * **J.A. van Braam awarded the Knight's Cross of the Order of Holland (*Ridderorde van het Koninkrijk der Nederlanden*) by Daendels.**
* **HB II Assent (28 June 1808):** HB II accepts the administrative changes and formally recognizes court Residents as Ministers.
