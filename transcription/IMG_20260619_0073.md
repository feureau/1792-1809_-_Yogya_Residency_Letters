# Transcription: IMG_20260619_0073.jpg

**Source:** Archive research notes of Peter Carey (Batch 2).  
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260619_0073.jpg`  
**Description:** Carey's archival notes from AN Djokjo Brieven 20.41 (May–June 1808): Surakarta embassy of Pangeran Mangkubumi, P. Cakrakusuma, and royal prince Gusti Timur to Governor-General Daendels in Semarang; abolition of the Directorate of Java's North East Coast; elevation of court Residents to "Ministers" representing King Louis; Daendels conferring the *Ridderorde van het Koninkrijk der Nederlanden* on J.A. van Braam; and Sultan Hamengkubuwana II's formal concurrence.

**Id. to Id.**  
Mon. 18 Rabingulakir 1735, 13 Jun. 1808  
Receives D's letter from Semarang, 7 Jun. 1808  
Telling that **Mangkubumi / Cakrakusuma** arrive in good state + seeing D & [presents] to D.  
Ambassadors Received by **Daendels** on 31 May 1808 together + **PB IV's** youngest son **Gusti Timur**.

Return on Thursday 14 Rabingulakir [9 Jun. 1808] to Solo.

**Id. to Id.**  
Sun. 2 Jumadilawal, Dal, 1735 (26 Jun. 1808)  
Receives D's letter from Semarang 17 Jun. 1808  
Telling him of **abolition of post of Gov. of Java's N.E. Coast**.  
∴ Opperhoofden at courts to correspond directly + D in Batavia.  
∴ **PB IV** prepared to regard them as "Ministers" of King Louis - directly representing **Daendels**.  
Have a much higher rank now than earlier when subordinate to Gov. of Java's N.E. Coast.
