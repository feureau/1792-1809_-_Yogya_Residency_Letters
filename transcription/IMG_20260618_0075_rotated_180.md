### Text & Marginal Annotations

[Top notes - Court protocol & Van Reede scandal:]

- **Opperhoofd Protocol:** The Dutch *Opperhoofd* (Resident) at the courts is always instructed to take up a station at the *Dalem* / Kraton upon the deathbed of rulers in order to prevent outbreaks of discontent and panic.
- **PWD's position:** PWD had to throw himself on the mercy of Resident Van Reede, who at this time was his only supporter. PB IV’s character was too indulgent/lenient, despite a heart full of bitterness.
- **Scribe Michaelis:** Michaelis, the European scribe of Van Reede, slept with one of MN I’s *selir* (concubines) during his stay in the *Dalem* and got her pregnant.

- [Red header:] **Income of PB IV (1811)**
- Tollgates farmed from PB IV for 1811:
  - Base amounts: 45,436 2/3 Sp.D. / 270 / 61,875 / 120,000 / 33,333 / 16,444.
