## Content

### Coastal Regencies: Ulijami, Wiradesa, and Pekalongan

* Reference: **5. MvO Van Overstraten**
* **Exiles:** Exile of Pangeran Brajatakusuma to Ceylon in 1788 (part of Rangga Tjarman / Nirany). Two brothers (Rangga Kusuma & Narawijaya) receive small pension from PB IV; last killed in battle between MB I and MN I in 1750.
* **Ulijami District:**
  * **Most heavily populated district** among western coastal areas; major rice producer.
  * Formerly leased to Capitan Cina of Semarang (Jan Lok to Jan. 1770) for 300 *koyangs* of rice annually at 15 Rixdollars per *koyang*.
  * Capitan Cina treats local population well and exempts them from heavy corvée.
  * Subject to Bayan; fertile land, but Capitan Cina lacks sufficient seed paddy.
  * Highly advantageous for V.O.C. to retain lease under Chinese management.
* **Pekalongan & Wiradesa:** Strikings contrast in prosperity between Ulijami and Wiradesa. Pekalongan highly suitable for rice and indigo. Raden Adipati Jayadiningrat strongly attached to Pasisir region.
