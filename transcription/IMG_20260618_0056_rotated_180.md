#### 1. Rangga Jajar Aftermath & Royal Pensions

- Exile of **Pangeran Brayakusuma** to Ceylon in 1788 (part of the Rangga Jajar affair).  
- Two brothers:  
  - **Rangga Kusuma**  
  - **Narawijaya** (*Nèrang*)  
  Both receive a small pension from PB IV.  
  *(Narawijaya was killed during a battle between HB I and MN I in 1750)*.

#### 2. The Ulujami Estate & Chinese Leaseholders

- *[Red marginal note:]* **Ulujami most heavily Pop. Rice producing area.**  
- **Ulujami:** Rice-producing district, most densely populated of all Western Pasisir districts.  
- Earlier rented to the **Capitan Cina of Semarang**; then leased to the **Ratu** for 5,000 rijksdaalders p.a.; subsequently subleased by her son **Tan To Ok** [Jan To Ok].  
- Yield: **300 koyangs of rice p.a.** at 15 r.r. per koyang.  
- Capitan Cina treats local population very well: relieves them of onerous corvée labour.  
- Land is extremely fertile, though subject to frequent river floods (*banjir*).  
- Capitan Cina suffers from shortage of seed-rice for sowing.  
- $\therefore$ Highly advantageous for the V.O.C. to continue leasing the land to a Chinese entrepreneur.  
- Sharp contrast noted between the prosperity of **Ulujami** and **Wiradesa**.

#### 3. Pekalongan & Coastal Administration

- **Pekalongan:** Very suitable for rice and indigo cultivation, though also vulnerable to periodic flooding (*banjir*).  
- **Raden Adipati Jayadiningrat** criticized as too attached to private tolls / leasing practices (*paozen / pasisir*).
