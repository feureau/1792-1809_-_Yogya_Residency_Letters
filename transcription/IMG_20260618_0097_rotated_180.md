### [19 Aug 1801]

* **van den Berg to Engelhard, 19 Aug. 1801.**
  * Ambassadors of HB II to <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Semarang for appointment of Engelhard.</ins>
    * P. Adinegara
    * R.T. Natadiwiria ?
  * To leave on 25 Aug. for Semarang.
  * *[Right margin annotation in red ink:]* **apptmnt of Engelhard takes place / Van Reede**

---

### [17 Sept 1801]

* **Id. to Id. 17 Sept. 1801**
  * Return of HB II's ambassadors from Semarang.
  * van d. B to send HB II small eats from time to time like Siberg – HB I.
  * News of van Overstraten's death reported to HB II.
  * van Reede in Sala asks for report on HB II & court in preparation for Nederburgh's visit to courts.
  * HB II asks Eng for <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Elephant / Persian & Bimanese Horse.</ins>

---

### [21 Sept 1801]

* **Id. to Id. 21 Sept. 1801**
  * HB II preparing to <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">send Embassy to pay compliments to Siberg as new G.G.</ins>
  * would leave before Puasa before end of Nov.

---

### [27 Sept 1801]

* **Id. to Id. 27 Sept. 1801**
  * Serious sickness of Natayuda – vdB visits him in secret.
  * *[Right margin annotation in red ink:]* **sickness of Natayuda**

---

### [14 Oct 1801]

* **Id. to Id. 14 Oct. 1801**
  * HB II to pēsanggrahan & bupatis panjis.
  * Rumour that HB II would refuse to accept letter Relating appointment of Siberg sitting on dhampar as had always been the case & HB I.
  * would not agree to ceremony till after Puasa in 3 mths time.
  * HB II asks that Ambassadors sent to congratulate Engelhard — DR II & 2 bupatis — <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">to be allowed to go on to Tegal Arum graves of Mangkurat Tegalwangi.</ins>

---

### [20 Oct 1801]

* **Id. to Id. 20 Oct. 1801**
  * At last allowed audience of HB II to give letters from Engelhard.
  * DR II on Tuesday had to stand in sun & rain on sit on Rier Paseban whilst HB II watched journey. [5 Dalem bupatis]
  * <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Because HB II annoyed about journey of DR II to Tegal Arum.</ins> 9 days long.
  * Argument Capitan Cina & Panji of HB II about $4,000 R.R. worth of goods given to HB II but not paid for. HB II wants Regents to pay for them.
  * would wait until HB II in better humour before broaching topic of ceremonial for apptmnt of Siberg.
