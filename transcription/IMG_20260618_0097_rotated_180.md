## Content

### Archive Entries: August – October 1801 (Embassies, Illness of Natayuda, & Tegal Arum Pilgrimage)

* Reference: **P.J. Brieven no. 48 (1801–02)**
* **12 August 1801 (Van den Berg to Engelhard):** HB II sends ambassadors (P. Adinegara, R.T. Natadiwiria) to Semarang to congratulate Nicolaas Engelhard on his appointment; depart 25 August.
* **17 September 1801:** Envoys return. News of Van Overstraten's death reported to HB II. Van Reede in Solo requests court status report in preparation for Commissioner-General Nederburgh's visit. HB II requests an elephant and Persian/Bimanese horses from Engelhard.
* **21 September 1801:** HB II prepares embassy to congratulate Niberg as new Governor-General (to depart before Puasa / late Nov.).
* **27 September 1801:** **Illness of Natayuda:** Serious illness of Natayuda; Resident visits him in secret.
* **14 October 1801:** HB II visits *Pesanggrahan* with Pasisir Bupatis. Rumor that HB II will refuse to receive Niberg's letter while seated on the *Dhampar* (as HB I had done). HB II requests that ambassadors sent to Engelhard (Danureja II + 2 Bupatis) be permitted to visit Tegal Arum (tomb of Amangkurat I).
* **20 October 1801:** Resident granted audience to present Engelhard's letters. Danureja II forced to stand in rain on River Paseban with 5 *Dalem* Bupatis while HB II watches, annoyed at Danureja II's 9-day journey to Tegal Arum. Dispute over $4,000 Rixdollars of unpaid goods supplied by Capitan Cina to HB II.
