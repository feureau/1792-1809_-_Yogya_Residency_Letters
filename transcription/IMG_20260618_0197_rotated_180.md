# Transcription: IMG_20260618_0197_rotated_180.jpg

**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260618_0197_rotated_180.jpg`  
**Folio / Reference:** A.N. Yogya Letters No. 47  
**Date Range:** 24 October 1805 – 28 October 1805  
**Primary Subjects:**  
- Court dynamics: prediction that Ratu Kencana Wulan will suffer if the Crown Prince (future HB III) ascends the throne  
- Court kinship etiquette: PB IV addressing the Sultan as "Uncle" (*Oom*) and Ratus as *"Ibu"*; Crown Prince addressing Pangeran Mangkudiningrat and Pangeran Mangkubumi as *"Adi"* (younger brother)  
- Secret offerings to the South Sea (*Segara Kidul*) presided over by Ratu Kencana Wulan; HB II visibly ashamed  
- Construction of Klaten Fort begins  
- Return of royal party from South Coast (28 Oct 1805); public appearance of HB II with Ratu Kencana Wulan  
- Ominous rejection of royal offerings by Ratu Kidul: sacrificial jackets, clothes, and breast-cloths (*borstkleedjes / kemben*) thrown back onto the beach by waves  
- Cultural interpretation: offerings spurned because HB II failed to perform a prior *slametan* and arrogantly stood on the shore under an open golden royal umbrella before the Goddess

### [28 October 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
28 Oct 1805  
Wat – Eng

Sultan Returns from S. SEA COAST + CROWN PRINCE / Ratu Kencana Wulan / Other Nobles of Kraton  
MORE PUBLIC THAN OTHER TRIPS:  
Ratu K.W. + HB II Together Publicly more often.

HB II NOT Treated very well by Goddess of S. Sea:  
Because clothes / Jackets / Kains (*Borstkleedjes*) thrown onto the sea by HB II all THROWN UP onto Beach again by the waves –

Javanese Regard HB II's OFFERINGS as NOT to the liking of Ratu Kidul:  
(1) Because HB II did not make a Slametan before casting the kains into the SEA.  
(2) Because HB II stands on Sea Shore with open golden UMBRELLA and as such appears before Ratu Kidul.
