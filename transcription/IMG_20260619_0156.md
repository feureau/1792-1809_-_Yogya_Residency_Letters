### AvJ Archive Entries: 1808–1809 (Cakrakusuma Appointment & Imagiri Burial)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1808–1809 (H 698b)**
* **3 March 1808:** Surakarta court formally notifies Yogyakarta that PB IV appointed his favorite **Cakrakusuma as Prince and Commander-in-Chief of Surakarta troops** (replacing the deceased Bupati of Ponorogo).
* **30 July 1808:** Rawa Regent R.T. Pringgakusuma arrives in Yogyakarta; 200 Yogyakarta troops (100 rifles, 100 pikes) dispatched to the area.
* **12 November 1808:** Joint Yogyakarta-Surakarta commission (including European observer Freis) dispatched to Pajang to investigate inter-court disputes following traditional procedures.
* **20 August 1809 (Royal Burial at Imagiri):**
  * **Death of PB IV's youngest legitimate daughter Nyai Putri R. Ayu Kidhaton.**
  * Funeral cortege escorted to Imagiri by Kyai Tumenggung Mangunyuda and Winarija.
