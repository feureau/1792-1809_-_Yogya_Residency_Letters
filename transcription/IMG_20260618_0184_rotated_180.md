### [10 January 1806]

*[Left margin:]*  
Wat – Eng.  
10 Jan. 1806

In order to encourage HB II to greater activity / watchfulness on S. Coast should be informed of impending danger from British.  
Dutch citizens should watch Fort = to allow military / Dragoons + Sultan's Troops to withstand attack.

Would never be able to persuade HB II to leave Kraton.  
*[Red underline:]* even if an enemy attacked on S. Coast  
Because of Treasure. too suspicious would not entrust treasure to V.O.C.  
not courageous enough to lead troops in person v. enemy.  
If HB II's force fighting far away = HB II might give himself up to V.O.C. arms.  
If kraton taken = HB II would probably retreat to Jambu Mts / Ambarawa  
would get enough food from Kedhu area.  
Proposes conditions on which Fort & V.O.C. Buildings in Yogya might be surrendered.

### [11 January 1806]

*[Left margin:]*  
Eng – Wat.  
11 Jan. 1806  
*[Red double vertical line]*

News from J.A. van Braam in Batavia  
Report Sayid Alu Nagaf & Umar from Malacca.  
No further news of proposed British attack on Java.  
merchantmen go on to China.  
10–11 men of war return to India.

### [11 January 1806]

*[Left margin:]*  
A.N.  
Eng – Wiese  
11 Jan. 1806  
*[In margin in red ink:]*  
**Farquhar's Destruction of Castle at Malaka.**

French land Reinforcements 7,000 men at Pondicherry.  
Brit. Adm. Sir Edward Pellew = Squadron of 10–11 Ships at Pulo Pinang.  
Returns to India to seek out French.

25 British Troops / 400–500 Sepoys at Malacca.  
plans of Brit. to destroy Castle at Malacca.  
would leave only small garrison there.
