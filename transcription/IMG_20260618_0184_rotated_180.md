### HB II Strategic Intentions, Naval Movement Intelligence, & Demolition of Malacca Castle (1806)

* **10 January 1806 (Waterloo to Engelhard):**
  * Waterloo advises informing HB II of British invasion threats to spur defense activity. Dutch citizens to guard the fort so regular/dragoon and royal troops can resist landings.
  * **HB II Assessment:** Waterloo notes HB II can never be persuaded to leave the Kraton in an invasion due to suspicion over his treasure and lack of personal physical courage.
  * If Kraton falls, HB II likely to retreat to Jambu Mts / Ambarawa (supplied with grain from Kedu). Proposals drafted for terms under which V.O.C. fort and buildings in Yogya might be surrendered if overwhelmed.
* **11 January 1806 (Intelligence Reports):**
  * J.A. van Braam (Batavia) forwards report of Sayyid Alwi as-Saqqaf from Malacca: 10–11 British merchantmen proceed to China, while 10–11 warships return to India.
  * A.H. Engelhard to Wiese: French land 7,000 reinforcements at Pondicherry. British Vice-Admiral Sir Edward Pellew's squadron (10–11 ships) leaves Penang for India to engage the French.
* **Demolition of Malacca Castle:** Red ink: **Farquhar's Destruction of Castle at Malacca.** British force at Malacca (25 troops, 400–500 Sepoys) under Colonel Farquhar prepares to demolish Malacca Castle, leaving only a minimal garrison.
