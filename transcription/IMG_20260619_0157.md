### Archive Entries: 1808–1809 (Cakrakusuma Army Command & Royal Burial at Imagiri)

* Reference: **AvJ 1808–1809 (H 698b)**
* **3 March 1808:** Official notification that PB IV appointed **Cakrakusuma** as Prince and Commander-in-Chief of Surakarta troops.
* **30 July 1808:** Detachment of 200 Yogyakarta troops (100 rifles, 100 pikes) stationed under Rawa Regent R.T. Pringgakusuma.
* **12 November 1808:** Joint commission sent to Pajang to settle inter-court disputes.
* **20 August 1809:** Death of PB IV's youngest daughter Nyai Putri R. Ayu Kidhaton; buried at Imagiri under escort of Kyai Tumenggung Mangunyuda and Winarija.
