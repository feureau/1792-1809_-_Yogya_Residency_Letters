### [Transcription]

**4 Mar. 1795**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:**  
Completion of buildings in Yogya fortress by Mancanagara Regents.  
Repairs to officers' buildings / *sirap* [shingle] roofs (old one torn down to build new).  
New Pepper *Pakhuis* [warehouse] completed — bleek damp.  
HB II gives orders to Danureja I to see to repairs.

---

**14 Mar. 1795**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:**  
HB II raised **Raden Mas Nuraman** as **Pangeran Dipasana**.

---

**4 May 1795**  
**Id. to Id. [Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten]:**  
Concerning troops to be sent by HB II to Batavia.

---

**22 Sept. 1795**  
**Id. to Id. [Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten]:**  
Arrival of 6 English ships at Pulo Pampang [Bandang] near Banyuwangi; Captain Sitwell demands provisions.  
Fear of attack on Oosthoek: Van IJsseldijk told to keep an eye on coast, ensuring no correspondence with the English.  
HB II to be informed + Eastern Mancanagara Regents to keep watch in Regencies and come to aid of Gezaghebber of Oosthoek with troops if necessary.  
Dutch do not want to worry HB II unduly, though the war with England and the **fall of Malacca** are already known.  
*[Red marginal note:]* Real aims / intentions of HB II a mystery; but outwardly loyal to V.O.C.  
Once English really begin attack on Java, then Dutch should press Sunan / Sultan to honour their promises to provide troops, with Dutch Dragoons to accompany them.

---

**26 Oct. 1795**  
**Id. to Id. [Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede / Van Overstraten]:**  
Death of **Pangeran Purwanēgara**, eldest son of Pangeran Ngabèhi / son-in-law of HB II.

---

**3 Nov. 1795**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede (Solo):**  
Arrival in Sala of **Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat** from Ceylon.

---

**9 Nov. 1795**  
**Id. to Van Overstraten:**  
**R.T. Mērtalaya**, grandson of Pangeran Ngabèhi, appointed as Outer Regent + 500 cacahs.
