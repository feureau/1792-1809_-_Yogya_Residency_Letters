## Content

### Archive Entries: March – November 1795 (Yogya Fort Completion, Ceylon Exiles, British Threat)

* Source: **Djokjo Brieven No. 85 (1795)** & **Solo Brieven No. 53w (10 July 1796)**
* **Solo Succession Note (10 July 1796):** R.T. Sasrawijaya installed as R. Adip. Mangkupraja replacing R. Adip. Jayaningrat. Grandson of late PB I raised as Crown Prince of Solo.
* **4 March 1795 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten):** Completion of buildings in Yogya Fortress by Outer Regents (repairing officers' quarters and roofs). New pepper warehouse completed (old warehouse too damp). HB II instructs DRI (Danureja I) to oversee final repairs.
* **14 March 1795:** **HB II elevates R.M. Nuraman as Pangeran Dipasana.**
* **4 May 1795:** Further correspondence concerning reinforcement troops for Batavia.
* **3 July 1795:** Arrival in Solo of Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat from exile in Ceylon.
* **26 October 1795:** Death of Pangeran Purwanegara, eldest son of Pangeran Ngabehi and son-in-law of HB II.
* **9 November 1795:** R.T. Mertalaya, grandson of P. Ngabehi, appointed Outer Regent (+ 500 cacahs).
* **22 September 1795:** Arrival of 6 British ships at Pulo Bawean / Banyuwangi (Capt. Sitwell requesting provisions). Fear of British attack on the *Oosthoek*; Van IJsseldijk instructed to keep watch on the coast. Eastern Mancanagara Regents instructed to remain on alert. Note on fall of Malacca to British. HB II outwardly loyal to V.O.C.
