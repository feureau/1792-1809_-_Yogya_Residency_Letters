### [Diplomatic Transcription]

**27 May - 28 Jun.**  
[top right]: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.41**  

**PB IV to Daendels,**  
**27 May 1808**  
1 Rabingulakir 1735.  
Telling Daendels that **P. Mangkubumi** [and] **P. Aria Cakrakusuma** being sent to Semarang to meet Daendels.  
ambassadors to present PB IV's compliments to "Grandfather".  
To Depart on Sat. 28 May + **J.A. van Braam**.  
Asks D's pardon if messengers do anything against usual custom.  
[red ink annotation]: **messengers of PB IV -> D**  

---

**HB II to Daendels**  
**26 Rabingulawal, Dal, 1735 (23 May 1808)**  
Receives letter of Daendels stating arrival in Semarang 12 May [in red ink: **Dismisses Eng in person**] & Dismisses **N. Engelhard** from Gov'nm't. of N.E. Coast.  
King Louis friendship to **HB II** / treat Javanese as his own children.  
& to prevent English from capturing Island.  
Daendels promises that even if English have secret friends in Java, would be able to prevent / defeat all attempts.  

[left margin in red ink]:  
**Daendels Fortifies Semarang**  

∴ D. concentrates Troops on Semarang to be able to prevent an attack on any quarter.  
3 - 4,000 Troops Reinforcement of Dutch at Semarang to prevent English landing.  
[inserted]: *not to attack Yogya.*  
Asks **HB II** to send some Yogya nobles to Semarang to witness measures D taking for Defense of Java / + sentiments of D towards Javanese.  

20 Aug. Daendels would be ready to receive Embassy from Yogya to present compliments to incoming G.G.  

---

[left margin in red ink with vertical bar]:  
**BUYSKES**  

Rear-Adm. **A.A. Buyskes** sent by King Louis to take Daendels place as G.G. in case could not get through to Java.  
Now appointed Lt. G.G. in Batavia.  

**HB II** promises to exercise troops in Yogya so that could come to D's assistance + all speed.  
Messengers from **HB II** to D to depart on [highlighted in pink]: 29 Jumadilakir. (22.8. 1808)
