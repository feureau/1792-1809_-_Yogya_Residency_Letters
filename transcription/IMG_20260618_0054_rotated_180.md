### Van Overstraten Memorie van Overgave 1796 (Yogya Court Dynamics & Resident Dispute)

* Reference: **4. MvO Van Overstraten**
* **Natayuda's Demotion:** Court rumors state HB II dismissed Natayuda from influential posts to eliminate him as a candidate for Patih upon Danureja I's death. Under HB II's despotic rule, a new Patih would be forced to submit to royal orders rather than serve as an independent bridge to the Dutch. Mandura described as courteous but lacking talent.
* **Mangkunagaran Settlement:** At Kartasura meeting, PB IV initially resists granting lands to Prangwedana. Role of **Van Nieuwkerken** praised for sound judgment, local knowledge, and trust among Javanese.
* **HB II - Van IJsseldijk Dispute (1792–1796):**
  * In 1792, HB II requests Van IJsseldijk remain as Resident, but relations deteriorate and HB II demands a replacement by August 1796.
  * Van Overstraten insists Van IJsseldijk must stay on for at least another year. Due to the threat of foreign invasion, Van Overstraten avoids provoking HB II over the Resident's replacement.
