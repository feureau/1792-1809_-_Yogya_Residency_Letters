### [Transcription]

#### 1. Succession to the Yogyakarta Grand Vizierate

- Court rumor: HB II intentionally dismissed **Raden Tumenggung Natayuda** from high administrative offices so that he would **not be in the running to succeed as Patih** upon the death of Danureja I.  
- **Despotic nature of HB II's rule:**  
  A new Patih would have no alternative but to bow completely to the Sultan's personal dictates, rather than fulfilling the traditional role of acting as an independent intermediary / bridge with the Dutch.  

- **Tumenggung Mandura:** Very obliging and courteous Javanese, but possesses little administrative talent.

#### 2. Kartasura Conference & Resident Van Nieuwkerken

- Meeting between Van Overstraten and PB IV at **Kartasura**:  
  PB IV initially attempted to repudiate his earlier promise to allocate lands (4,000 cacahs) to Prangwedana.  

- **Evaluation of Resident Van Nieuwkerken:**  
  Possesses very sound judgment, deep local knowledge, and an exceptional talent for winning the trust and confidence of the Javanese elites.

#### 3. The Rift between HB II and Resident Van IJsseldijk

- In 1792, HB II had specifically requested that **Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk** remain at court as first Resident.  
- However, Van IJsseldijk subsequently fell into severe disfavour, leading HB II to formally demand his replacement.  
- *[Red marginal note:]* **HB II – Van IJ Quarrel.**  
  - Van Overstraten corresponded with HB II regarding Van IJsseldijk.  
  - HB II replied very abruptly that he insisted on receiving a new Resident by **August 1796**, though he might permit Van IJsseldijk to stay another year if necessary.  
  - Van Overstraten replied that Van IJsseldijk must remain for at least another year.  
- **Geopolitical constraint:**  
  Because of the acute danger of a foreign [British / French] invasion, Van Overstraten dare not adopt too firm or confrontational a stance against HB II over the question of replacing the Resident.
