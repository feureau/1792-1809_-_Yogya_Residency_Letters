### Main Text & Margin Notes

| Margin Notes | Main Text |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1755** | ### Report on Pacitan Pepper Cultivation & Local Governance<br><br>#### Logistical & Social Assessment:<br><br>1. **Distance:** 40 hours' journey E. of Yogya — significant transport difficulties.<br>2. **Smuggling:** Risk of smuggling which cannot easily be prevented.<br>3. **Discipline:** Pacitan inhabitants are less disciplined; must be handled much more carefully and gently than those in Loano / Winong.<br>- *River transport:* Enquiries whether pepper can be exported along rivers.<br>- Population is already accustomed to growing pepper $\rightarrow$ difficulties in Pacitan do not outweigh benefits.<br>- Price: **2 – 2 ½ Spanish Dollars (Rijksdaalders)** to be paid per pikul of pepper.<br><br>---<br><br>### Land Tenure & Hidden Agricultural Expansion:<br><br>- **Pacitan**: 200 cacahs / 48 jungs ricefields (very suited to pepper cultivation); 200 cacahs belong to Sunan. Sawahs ringed by mountains.<br>- Mountain roads frequently impassable.<br>- Smuggling risk: Chinese tollgate keepers to be alerted and warned against illicit transit.<br>- Inhabitants possess sufficient sawahs to sustain themselves $\rightarrow$ pepper can be cultivated for the V.O.C.<br>- **Survey of Ricefields in Pacitan**: Shows that acreage has expanded greatly since the Peace of 1755 (*Giyanti*).<br>  > *Crucial note:* The increase had been concealed from the royal Courts so that local farmers could quietly appropriate newly opened-up sawahs.<br><br>- With the ending of corvée services, the Regent declares that local inhabitants can make a good living; region is more prosperous now than under Kartasura rule.<br><br>---<br><br>### Local Administration (The Netrawijaya / Wongsocitro Dynasty):<br><br>- Because of past administrative abuse and oppression by headmen and officials, populace must be handled with care to prevent flight.<br>- **Remoteness from Courts**: Regent **Netrawijaya** (now *Wongsocitro*), succeeded by his son, takes advantage of this distance. However, old Netrawijaya remains in de facto control.<br>- Resident should closely supervise the Regent's son (who is far less fair-minded than his father) to avert peasant flight.<br>- To satisfy father and son, their official sawah holding is to be raised from 6 to 12 jungs, while retaining local labour obligations for pepper plantations.<br><br>#### Final Land Note:<br><br>- **Loano**: 26 jungs could all be cleared and planted with pepper. |
