### Archive Entries: December 1808 (Buffalo Restitution, Teak Mutilations, & Crown Prince Envoys)

* **16 December 1808 (Rembang Buffalo Restitution):**
  * Theft of 48 buffaloes from Rembang by Yogyakarta *blandhongs*. Panolan Regent R.T. Natawijaya instructed to resolve the matter.
  * HB II writes to Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III (acting as Vice-Patih); villagers agree to pay 5 Ronde Reals per buffalo, resolving the claim (48 buffaloes from Tuban *blandhongs*; 46 from Trambalang).
  * **Mancanagara Teak Protection:** Wirasari Regent R.T. Yudakusuma instructs Raden Rongga III to prevent Mancanagara subjects from mutilating government teak trees during forest sorties.
* **19 December 1808 (Arrest of Crown Prince Messengers):** Dutch dragoons arrest 2 messengers of the Crown Prince travelling from Demak to Semarang.
