### N.O.K. 47 Van Reede 4: Marriage Proposals & Prangwedana Allowance (1798–1800)

* Reference: **N.O.K. 47 MvO Van Reede 4**
* **Natakusuma Position:** Natakusuma and brothers positioned to overthrow HB II; HB II aware of threat and monitors Natakusuma closely.
* **Yogya-Solo Marriage Proposals (1798–1799):**
  * Proposal to marry Yogya Crown Prince to PB IV's sister **Raden Ayu Nokartaji** (later Ratu Timur; married son of Surabaya Bupati, now Bupati of Kudus).
  * In 1799, Surakarta envoys (led by PB IV's brother-in-law R. Panji Kuda Nalamitra and Parta Wikrama) visit Yogya to request hand of Ratu Kedhaton's daughter for Solo Crown Prince. Envoys stay 5 days, but proposal declined as princess is only 8 years old.
* **Prangwedana Military Allowance (28 Aug. 1798):**
  * Prangwedana granted annual V.O.C. allowance of 2,000 guilders (*Np.D.*) for war duration.
  * Prangwedana complains sum is insufficient to maintain troops in readiness and requests 10,000 guilders to clear debts; V.O.C. refuses.
* **Marriage & Succession:** Prangwedana requests permission to marry daughter of a Pasisir Bupati (wife R.A. Sugi, daughter of Nindwireja, died in 1800). If Prangwedana dies, youngest brother **Pangeran Aria Prabu Wijaya** designated to succeed him.
