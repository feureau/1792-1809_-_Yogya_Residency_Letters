### Court Tensions & Regency Politics:

- **Pangeran Natakusuma's strength:** Natakusuma and his brothers would be in a position to **topple HB II** [red margin bar].
- HB II realizes this danger and keeps a wary, watchful eye constantly on Natakusuma.

---

### Inter-Court Marriage Proposals:

[Red header: **Marriage Proposal Yogya CP + PB IV's sister**]

- **Proposed match:** Yogya Crown Prince and PB IV's sister, **Raden Ayu Sêkartaji**.
  - Later raised with the title **Ratu Timur**; subsequently married to the son of the Regent of Surabaya (who was later appointed Regent of Kudus).
- **1798–1799 Sala Mission to Yogya:**
  - Messengers arrive from the Surakarta court to Yogyakarta to solicit the hand of the daughter of Ratu Ibu for the Surakarta Crown Prince.
  - Surakarta envoys: Sent by the brother-in-law of PB IV, Raden Panji Kuda Samitra, accompanied by Raden Panji Partawijaya / Parta Wikrama.
  - Received in Yogyakarta by HB II’s royal officer, Raden Panji Padmawijaya.
  - Surakarta envoys stay 5 days; the proposal is declined because the royal princess is only 8 years old — far too young to be married off.

---

### Legions of Pangeran Prangwedana (Mangkunegara II):

- Van Reede notes that the auxiliary troops of Pangeran Prangwedana could be treated and relied upon like those of the V.O.C. itself.
- **28 August 1798:** PWD is granted a yearly allowance of **2,000 Sp.D.** for the duration of the European war.
  - PWD protests that this sum is insufficient to maintain his troops in an adequate state of military readiness.
  - PWD requests a special grant/loan of **10,000 Sp.D.** to clear his pressing debts.
  - The V.O.C. Government refuses the request.

---

### PWD Marriages & Succession:

- PWD soon to request permission to marry a daughter of a *Pasisir* (North Coast) Regent.
  - Occasioned because his principal wife, **Raden Ayu Sêpuh** (daughter of Patih Danureja I / Sindureja), died in 1800.
  - Succession clause: If PWD dies without issue, his youngest brother, **Pangeran Aria Prabu Wijaya**, is designated to succeed him.
