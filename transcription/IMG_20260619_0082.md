### Ministerial Rank Protocols & Minister Wiese Appointment (August – September 1808)

* **HB II Protocol Protest:**
  * **HB II protests against the new Dutch "Minister" sitting beside him on the *Dhampar* (royal throne) at left-hand level (1–1 1/2 feet away).**
  * Demands that as immediate representative of Daendels, the Minister be accorded the same ceremonial protocol previously granted to the Governor of Java's N.E. Coast.
  * Crown Prince and Princes of the Blood required to be received by the Minister, though blood princes are lower in rank than Patih Danureja II.
  * Both courts accept equal protocol; PB IV requests no changes.
  * Minister's carriage no longer required to pull over when meeting HB II's carriage; HB II required to notify the Minister whenever he leaves the Kraton to prevent awkward encounters.
* **Appointment of Minister G.W. Wiese:**
  * Nicolaas Engelhard requests transfer to Batavia for family reasons.
  * **G.W. Wiese (brother of Governor-General A.H. Wiese) appointed Minister to Yogyakarta.** Praised as a man of noble character.
* **9 September 1808:** PB IV acknowledges Daendels' note explaining that the Semarang audience was postponed from 20 August to 25 September to allow Daendels to confer with North Coast Regents regarding native welfare reforms.
