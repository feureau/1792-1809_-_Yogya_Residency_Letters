### 1793 Loano / Pacitan Report 3: Yields & Official Decrees

* **Pacitan Bay & Maritime Risk:** Bay 1/2 hour across, surrounded by mountains, open to South wind. Boats can load pepper, but risk of pirates in large 2-masted proas (*mayang/prahau*) makes overland export safer.
* **Sultan's Suspicion:** Warning that V.O.C. maritime surveying of Pacitan Bay would arouse suspicion in Sultan that V.O.C. intends to annex it.
* **Tree Yields:**
  * Loano: 8,000 old pepper trees producing only 70–80 pikuls/year.
  * Pacitan: 10,000 trees in flourishing condition (local population cares for them better). 300 pikuls expected this year from Yogya Pacitan; 370 pikuls from Solo Pacitan.
* **Acreage & Care:** Total Pacitan/Loano pepper lands: 800 cacahs (1,000,000 pepper trees in Loano on 192 jungs + 144 jungs potential). Pepper gardens enclosed with low walls; 100,000 *dadap* shade trees planted (60,000–70,000 new shoots expected annually).

#### Decrees (*Piagem*):
* **DRI - Demang Gagah Kemitir, Mangundiwiria (22 Sept. 1792):** Loano & Malarang freed from corvée services and dedicated to pepper under supervision of Tumenggung Mangundiwiria; local villagers protected from oppression.
* **DRI - Van IJsseldijk (28 Jan. 1793 / 15 Jumadilakir Dal 1719):** For Pepper/Indigo: Lands in Loano, Nilo Tiyang, Pekalongan, Malarang, Plaosan, Winong (700 cacahs) + 200 cacahs in Pacitan allocated to V.O.C. HB II exempts inhabitants from corvée to focus on pepper and indigo.
