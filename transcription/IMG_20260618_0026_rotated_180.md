| **22 Sept. 1792** | ### 1793 Loano / Pacitan Report 3 (Van IJsseldijk)<br><br>*(Dj. Br. 45: W. van Ysseldijk, 'Eerbiedig Bericht')*<br><br>#### Bay of Pacitan & Maritime Considerations:<br><br>- **Bay of Pacitan**: ½ an hour's journey across; hemmed in by mountains on both sides; completely open to south wind.<br>- Feasibility of coastal shipping: Possible to use boats to load pepper, but significant **danger of pirates** using large two-masted brigs $\therefore$ safer to transport pepper overland.<br>- *Geopolitical sensitivity:* If V.O.C. conducts a survey of the Bay, it would arouse acute suspicion in the Sultan and Sunan that the Company intends to seize it.<br><br>---<br><br>#### Tree Counts & Expected Yields:<br><br>- **Loano**: 8,000 old pepper trees $\rightarrow$ only produce 70–80 pikuls per annum.<br>- **Pacitan**: 10,000 trees in much more flourishing condition; inhabitants are better able to tend them.<br>- Immediate harvest expectations:<br>  - 300 pikuls from Yogya Pacitan.<br>  - 370 pikuls from Solo Pacitan.<br>- **Total Lands Suitable**:<br>  - Pacitan / Loano: **800 cacahs (192 jungs)**.<br>  - Room for 1,000,000 pepper vines in Loano.<br>  - Additional 144 jungs across both districts could likely be brought into cultivation (60 square roods = 1 jung).<br>  - Cultivation techniques: Gardens surrounded by low walls; planting of **dadap trees** to provide shade for over 100,000 pepper bushes.<br>  - Well maintained in Pacitan; 60,000–70,000 new cuttings expected annually.<br><br>---<br><br>#### Official Decrees & Letters from Danureja I (DRI):<br><br>- **22 Sept. 1792**: Danureja I to *Demang* Gajah Kèmitèr and *Demang* Mangundiwiria:<br>  - Loano / Malarang freed from all royal corvée services and reserved exclusively for pepper under **Tumenggung Mangundipura**.<br>  - Strict injunction: local villagers must not be oppressed.<br>- **28 Jan. 1793 (15 Jumadilakir Dal 1719)**: Danureja I to Resident Van IJsseldijk:<br>  - For Pepper & Indigo: **700 cacahs** in Loano, Nelotiyang, Pekalongan, Malarang, Plaosan, and Winong (*+ 200 cacahs in Pacitan*) ceded to V.O.C.<br>  - HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] exempts all cultivators from customary corvée duties so that they can concentrate entirely on crops. |
