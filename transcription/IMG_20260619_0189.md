### AN Djokjo Brieven No. 24: Summary of Marshal Daendels' Visit to Yogyakarta (August 1809)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 24 (17 Aug. 1809 - J. Groenhoff van Woortman)**
* Document: *Dagverhaal van het voorgevallene ter gelegenheid van de komst alhier van Z.E. Den Heer Maarschalk en G.G. H.W. Daendels*.

#### Summary of Royal Encounters & Estate Visits:
* **Meeting at Kartasura (23 July 1809):** HB II meets Daendels at Kartasura in the presence of Brigadier-General De Kock, Cikranegara, and Pangeran Mangkubumi.
* **Tour of Royal Estates & Gardens:** Daendels tours HB II's royal hunting lodges and gardens at Wonagung, Purwareja, Kerta Winangun, and Wonocatur, inspecting fish ponds, mountain-top pavilions, and the Crown Prince's private inner gardens.
* **Audience with Ratu Kencana Wulan:** Daendels visits HB II's youngest and most cherished chief queen, **Ratu Kencana Wulan**, in her 3-pendhapa pavilion.
* **Policy Discussions:** Discussions held with HB II regarding expanding agricultural production (*cultures*) in the Vorstenlanden and broader political relations with the French-Dutch colonial administration.
