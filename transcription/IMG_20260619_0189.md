A.N. Djogja Brieven naar Batavia 1809–11  
**1809 (Yogya) Aug Letters**  
**DAENDELS VISIT TO DJOGJA 1809**

<u>Meeting of Sunan with Daendels at Kartasura:</u> Marshal  

[25 July 1809.]

Brigadier General De Kock in attendance [Dagverhaal] 23 July 1809.  
Tjokra Negoro from Solo:  
and MANGKU BUMI at reception.

<u>Daendels visits Royal Hunting Lodges / Gardens in company of CROWN PRINCE</u>  

[to PURWAREOJA / KERTA WINANGOON / WANA TJATOOR]

GARDENS have: fish ponds, HUNTING LODGES, well-laid out gardens. Crown Prince in this garden.

<u>VISIT TO SULTAN'S "youngest and most beloved wife RATU KENCANA WULANG."</u>  
3 pendopos.

[Signed: 17 Aug. 1809]

<u>TALK TO SULTAN ABOUT POSSIBILITY:</u>  
of broadening cultivation in V.L.  
as well as POLITICAL DISCUSSIONS.

<u>AN Djokja Brieven no. 24</u>  
*Dagverhaal van het voorgevallene ter gelegenheid van de komst alhier van Z.E. den Heer Maarschalk en G.G. H.W. Daendels.*
