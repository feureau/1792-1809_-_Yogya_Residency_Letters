### AvJ Archive Entries: January – April 1807 (Court Protocol, Marriage Witnesses, & Robbery Investigations)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1807**
* **6 January 1807:** New Year's Day reception at Residency House attended by Crown Prince and Yogyakarta Princes.
* **8 January 1807:** R.T. Pringgakusuma appointed Regent of Rawa (Rema).
* **18 February 1807 (Royal Marriage Witness):**
  * Patih Danuningrat dispatches Mas Keti Anom with a letter to Danureja II containing instructions from PB IV and the Surakarta *Pengulu*.
  * Pangeran Natapura (bearing the religious name **Pangeran Abdul Kadir**) requested to act as witness (*wakil*) for the marriage of Raden Ajeng Aliya (daughter of P. Natadiningrat) to R.Ng. Wirayuda (son of R.Ria Natapada).
* **21 February 1807:** R.T. Purwadipura appointed 2nd *Dalem* Regent.
* **9 March 1807 (Wirasari Robbery Investigation):** European gunner and HB II's envoys sent to Rembang to investigate a major robbery at Wirasari in the Rembang area.
* **3 April 1807:** Note on P. Cakravingrat and P. Pakuningrat (whose father still resides with Ratu Mas; Pakuningrat has no sons).
