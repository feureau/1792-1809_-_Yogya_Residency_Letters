### <u>Gold Farm Letter Lease Terms:</u>

* **Scope & Lessee:** Renting out of Bandhars / Tollgates / Rangkas / Selarongs, Gêdhong Têbak [tembakau] in Kêdhu to **Reksanêgara** for **60,000 Sp.D.** in silver money.  
  *Buffalo Tax on S. Coast.*  
  *Conversion:* 1 Sp.p = 28 Dubbeltjes.  
  *Duration:* **For 3 years.**  
  *Installments:*  
  — Puasa: 30,000  
  — Mulud: 30,000  
  \* Pasars.  

* <u>Exceptions:</u> Tanjung Tirta / Têgalsari + Tollgates in Pasar Pêrdikan Belonging to Priests;  
  *also:* Pasars / Bandhars / Tollgates on Sala river.  

* **Birds' Nests:** 2x per year gathering of Bird's Nests. Farm Rent of Birds nest harvest = **12,000 Sp.D. p. ½ year.**  
* **Royal Cattle Exemption:** 110 Pangluput Buffalo / Sapi Belonging to HB II can go through Tollgates without paying Toll.  

---

### <u>Pangirings / Presents to HB II:</u>

Pathoolen / gold thread / silk / Tea / candy / sugar / Chinese confiture:  

* 10 prs Pathoolen Jêlamprang, Each 25 Sp.D.  
* 10 prs Pathoolen Kanigara, Each 15 Sp.D.  
* + Pathoolen Kênanga  
* **1,230 Sp.p worth** of 10 ponds gold thread of 12 Pypen + Heavy thread (18 Reals per cattie).  
* **20 Katies Silk:** 9 green / 9 Red / 2 Jambu (Pink) @ 8 Reals per Cattie.  
* Candy / sugar / confiture: 430 reals per ½ year.  
* **Patompono:** 10 per 1,000 of chest of Surat.  
* **Papak:** 60 Piculen.  
* **Penglamar:** 30 Piculen.  

---

### <u>Toll Exemptions:</u>

* <u>If Inhabitants pass Tollgates not carrying goods for market — no Tax can be levied on them.</u>  
* <u>All members of HB II's family exempt from Tolls,</u> i.e. bringing in Rice from Lands.
