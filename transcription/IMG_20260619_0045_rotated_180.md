### Summary of Gold Farm Charter (*Piagem*) Lease Terms (2 Dec. 1807)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.86 (2 Dec. 1807 / 1 Nawal 1734)**
* **Lease Terms:**
  * Renting of tollgates (*bandhars/rangkahs/salarangs*) and Kedu tobacco warehouses (*gedhong tembakau*) to R.T. Reksanegara for **60,000 silver Sp.D. per annum** over a 3-year term.
  * Payable in 2 installments of 30,000 Sp.D. at *Puasa* and *Mulud*.
  * *Exceptions:* Tanjung Tirto, Tegalsari tollgates, priestly *pasar perdikan*, and Surakarta river tollgates along the Bengawan Solo.
  * Birds' nest harvest farmed separately @ 12,000 Sp.D. per half-year.
  * 110 royal *sapi pengluput* draught beasts exempt from toll fees.
* **Required Annual Ceremonial Tribute (*Pangirings*):**
  * *Pathoolen* textiles: 10 pairs *Jelamprang* (@ 25 Sp.D. = 250); 10 pairs *Kanigara* (@ 15 Sp.D. = 150) + *Kenanga* cloth.
  * Gold thread: 10 lbs of 12-thread/18-real quality (@ 24 Reals = 240).
  * Silk: 20 catties (9 green, 9 red, 2 pink) @ 8 Reals/catty (= 160).
  * Confections/Sugar/Tea: 430 Reals per half-year (= 860). Total tribute value: 1,230 Sp.D.
  * *Administrative Fees:* 1% *Patompono* chest fee, 60 pikuls *Serat Papak*, 30 pikuls *Penglamar*.
* **Tax Exemptions:** Non-merchandise transport, royal family members, and estate rice/padi exempt from tolls.
