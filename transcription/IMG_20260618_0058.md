| **October 1796** | ### [Header]<br><br>6. MvO Van Overstraten 1796.<br><br>### [Transcription]<br><br>#### 1. Shipbuilding & Timber Supplies (f. 36)<br><br>- Very few ships are now being constructed for the V.O.C. at Rembang.  <br>- All heavy timber is now drawn from the **Blora area**, as suitable heavy timber in the Rembang and Semarang districts has become exceedingly scarce.<br><br>#### 2. Lasem & Tuban (ff. 36–37)<br><br>- **Lasem:**  <br>  - Size: **2,000 cacahs** under **Tumenggung Suradipura** (married to a daughter of the former first Bupati of Demak).  <br>  - Dues: 192½ rijksdaalders *cacah-geld*; 1 *pancalang* vessel (11–12 *vaam* in length); 6 pikuls cotton thread.  <br>  - Under pressure from Van Overstraten, established an **indigo factory**.  <br>- **Tuban:**  <br>  - Size: **3,000 cacahs** under **Tumenggung Purbanēgara**.  <br>  - Unfavourable reports regarding his conduct; recommended that close surveillance be kept over him.  <br>  - Dues: 60 koyangs rice; 325 rijksdaalders *cacah-geld*; 6 pikuls cotton thread; 1,000 heavy beams.  <br>  - Indigo factory laid out in 1795.<br><br>#### 3. Bawean Island & Sidayu (ff. 37–38)<br><br>- **Island of Bawean:**  <br>  - Described as a *"nest of intrigue"*.  <br>  - Small V.O.C. fort (*benteng*) constructed in 1786; Dutch bookkeeper (*boekhouder*) stationed there.  <br>  - Highly suitable for coconut plantations and copra; travel passes required.  <br>- **Sidayu (f. 38):**  <br>  - Size: **2,500 cacahs** under the supervision of the Resident of Gresik.  <br>  - Bupati: **R.T. Suradiningrat** (illegitimate brother of the Panembahan of Madura).  <br>  - Character: Very spendthrift; heavily dependent upon / subservient to local Muslim priests (*enslaved to priests*).  <br>  - Dues: 325 Sp.D. *cacah-geld*; 200 koyangs rice; 6 pikuls cotton thread. |
