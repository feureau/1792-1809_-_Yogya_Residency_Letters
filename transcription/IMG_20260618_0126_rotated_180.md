### Secret Complaints of Patih & Nayakas Against HB II

* Patih + Nayakas complain about HB II in secret to vdB.
* **HB II's aims / transgressions:**
  1. To get Crown Prince appointed Sultan on his death without permission of V.O.C.
  2. Refusal to accept Nederburgh as being more senior to himself.
  3. Refusal to allow subjects implicated in affairs + V.O.C. subjects to be judged in Semarang according to govnt. law.
  4. Refusal to allow DR II to take oath of allegiance to V.O.C. on appointment. (*Still not done.*)
  5. When krises / swords (*Pusakas*) being carried in state about Yogya = V.O.C. officials should get down off horses & take off hats or hide behind walls of streets!
  6. V.O.C. officials to get out of way when wives / concubines of HB II going to *pêsanggrahan*.
* HB II shown lack of respect for V.O.C. in many ways.
* But no use falling out + HB II as van IJ[sseldijk] had done *(red note: **van IJ falls out + HB II**)* — vd Berg always on cordial terms + HB II.
