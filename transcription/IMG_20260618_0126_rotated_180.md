### Archive Entries: 1802 (HB II Tax Reforms, Fortifications, & Resident Conflicts)

* Reference: **P.J. Br. 20.48 (Van den Berg / Waterloo to Engelhard - 4 Apr. 1808 / 5 Nawal 1802)**
* **Tax Farm Strategy:** HB II attempts to replace Resident in tax-farm administration to elevate royal prestige; seeks to emulate HB I's earlier policies.
* **Dutch Impression:** HB II appears more friendly and engaging to Engelhard than the Sunan, but lacks urbanity and polish.
* **New Fortifications (1785 / Ricklefs p. 278):** New ramparts and gun batteries constructed at Yogya Kraton.
* **Secret Grievances of Patih & Nayakas (to Van den Berg):**
  1. HB II aims to appoint Crown Prince as successor upon his death without V.O.C. approval.
  2. Refusal to receive Commissioner-General Nederburgh as a superior.
  3. Refusal to allow subjects implicated in V.O.C. offenses to be tried in Semarang under colonial law.
  4. Refusal to permit Patih Danureja II to take the official V.O.C. oath of allegiance (still unsworn).
  5. Royal Protocol: When royal krises/swords (*pusakas*) are carried in state through Yogya, V.O.C. officials required to dismount, remove hats, or wait behind walls.
  6. V.O.C. officials required to step aside when HB II's concubines travel to *pesanggrahan* residences.
* **Lack of Respect:** HB II demonstrates lack of respect for V.O.C., but avoids open breach. Resident Van den Berg remains on courteous terms.
* **Van Overstraten Advice:** Advises HB II to manage concubines within Kraton rather than taking them to *pesanggrahan* estates. Advises HB II to stop taking subjects' wives for his Amazon guard (*Prajurit Estri*); notes one man was killed by being thrown down a well for refusing to surrender his wife. Recommends sending expeditions to capture Chinese women rather than disrupting local subjects.
