### 1804 Mack. Pr. Vol. 21: Numadiningrat Appanages & Pacitan Agriculture

* Reference: **Mack. Pr. Vol. 21 (p. 199–202)**
* **Numadiningrat Appanage Charter (20 Jan. 1804):**
  * Numadiningrat elevated to **Bupati Dalem Gedhong Tengen** (similar to office formerly held by Natayuda under the title R.T. Numadiningrat, *"Flower of the Nation"*).
  * Granted 1,000 cacahs of land. *Gawening wong* lands in Wadawala, Kalirat, Wanatu, Neyegan, Tulung, and Kasontan (50 cacahs).

* **Coal Discovery:** Discovery of a new coal bed at Maja, North of Pacitan.
* **Corn Measure Definitions (p. 201):**
  * *Pochong:* Quantity of unthreshed rice stalks a man can embrace in one hand.
  * 2 *Pocongs* = 1 *Geding*; 5 *Gedings* = 1 *Nanga*; 5 *Nangas* = 1 *Mamist*.
