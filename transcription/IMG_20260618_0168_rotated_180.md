### Yogya-Solo Security Accord: Articles 21–23 (27 September 1804)

* Reference: **P.J. Br. 42a (pp. 87–96)**
* **Art. 21 (Bandhar Attacks & Indemnity):**
  * If a Chinese *Bandhar* is attacked by robbers, 300 Reals allocated to track down the criminals.
  * If robbers are not captured within 30 days, the 300 Reals shall **not** be paid as indemnity to the *Bandhar*, but distributed as a reward (*douceur*) to villagers who assisted in the search.
  * Non-tax-farmer Chinese living in villages treated under same security rules as Javanese.
* **Art. 22:** If robbers are not captured within 40 days, local heads punished; search terminates immediately upon capture of robbers.
* **Art. 23 (Jurisdiction over Chinese Offenders):**
  * Chinese toll-collectors (*Pabean*) involved in criminal activity punished by Dutch Resident.
  * Minor offenses punished by short-term imprisonment in the Dutch fort in Solo; serious offenses referred to the Court of Justice (*Raad van Justitie*) in Semarang.
