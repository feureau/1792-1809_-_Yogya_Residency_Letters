# Transcription of IMG_20260618_0109.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260618_0109.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 48: *Lijst van de verhooging van de tolle*, 10 April 1802)
- **Date Range:** 1802-04-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official tariff schedule of tollgate tax increases (*Lijst van de verhooging van de tolle zoo als die thans ingevoerd staat te worden*) instituted across the Yogyakarta Residency under Resident Johannes Gerardus van den Berg and Jan Willem Neijenheuis during the reign of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II; establishes revised transit tax rates on Chinese and Javanese merchants conveying cloth, yarn, ginger, indigo, lamp oil, opium (*amphioen*), and woven mats between Yogyakarta, Semarang, Kedu, Bagelen, and Pesantren.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Johannes Gerardus van den Berg, Jan Willem Neijenheuis, Prof. Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djogja), Semarang, Kedu (Cadoe), Bagelen, Pesantren (Passantran).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Bandar (tollgate tax farm / pagt), Amphioen (opium monopoly), Dubbeltje, Stuiver, Rondemat (Spanish dollar / real), Vorstenlanden trade routes.
