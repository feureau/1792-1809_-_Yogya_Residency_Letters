### Archive Entries: September 1803 (Volcanic Eruptions & Royal Marriages)

* Reference: **A.N. Yogya Letters No. 49 (Sept. 1803)**
* **15 September 1803 (Waterloo -> Engelhard):** Waterloo meets HB II at Purworejo country pavilion; HB II impressed with English horses.
* **22 September 1803:** 1,366 Rixdollars paid to Raden Rongga III for public works. Grobogan rice trade expected to recover, but newly appointed 2nd Regent (Sasrakusuma) remains in Yogya.
* **Volcanic Activity (1803):** **Eruptions recorded at Mount Merapi, Mount Sumbing, and Mount Sundara.**
* **Court Bias against Raden Rongga:** Raden Rongga III commits excesses at court; Crown Prince strongly biased against him. Rumors of marriage between HB II's daughter R.A. Clairah and R.T. Natadiningrat.
* **28 September 1803 (Trouw Feest & Natadiningrat Marriage):**
  * Birthday feast for Sultan's children. Patih confirms R.T. Natadiningrat to marry Raden Ajeng Supirah.
  * Pangeran Natakusuma circumspect, but Waterloo fears marriage will increase Natakusuma's court faction. Crown Prince and Ratu Kencana Wulan strongly favor the marriage.
