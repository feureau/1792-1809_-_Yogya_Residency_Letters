## Archival & Historical Notes

- **Audience at Purworejo Pavilion (15 September 1803):** Resident Waterloo meets Sultan Hamengkubuwana II at his country estate/pavilion at Purworejo to deliver European/English horses, with which HB II is exceptionally delighted. However, delivery of the Bimanese horse via Sidayu fails to materialize.
- **Rangga's Accounts & Grobogan Rice (22 September 1803):** Raden Rangga Prawiradirja III pays 1,366 *ronde maaten* for timber allocations. Waterloo expects the Grobogan rice supply trade to normalize, though the new Second Regent of Grobogan, Raden Tumenggung Sasrakusuma, delays in Yogyakarta.
- **Volcanic Eruptions in Central Java (September 1803):** Remarkable simultaneous volcanic disturbances recorded: Mount Merapi is actively erupting, and volcanic smoke/eruptions are observed on Mount Sumbing and Mount Sundara (Sindoro).
- **Rangga's Court Standing:** Raden Rangga faces escalating hostility among court factions, with the Crown Prince (later HB III) exhibiting intense personal prejudice against him.
- **Dynastic Marriage of Raden Tumenggung Natadiningrat:**
  - Patih Danureja II confirms plans for the marriage of HB II's royal daughter, Raden Ajeng Jupirah, to Raden Tumenggung Natadiningrat (son of Pangeran Natakusuma / Pakualam I, later Pakualam II).
  - While Pangeran Natakusuma acts with extreme caution, HB II harbors deep anxiety that this royal union will swell Natakusuma's prestige and political following.
  - Conversely, the Crown Prince and the Sultan's influential consort, Ratu Kencana Wulan, strongly champion the marriage.
