### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.86: Peasant Character, Floods, and Livestock (1806)

* **Flooding in Lowland Districts:** Lowland areas in Bagelèn, Pamekasan, Juwana, Kudus, and Pati suffer months of flooding; fields cannot be planted until floodwaters subside and irrigation walls are rebuilt.
* **Peasant Character:** Javanese farmers praised as diligent and hard-working (far superior to their rulers/administrators); would produce far more if exempt from chief extortions. V.O.C. advised against interfering in HB II's internal administration.
* **Livestock & Draught Animals:**
  * Javanese peasants generally poor; little progress in livestock breeding.
  * Draught animals heavily worked (cows worked even immediately after calving, degrading livestock quality). Buffaloes brought from Eastern/Western Mancanagara to Yogya for slaughter.
  * *Crawfurd's Note (Mack. Pr. Vol. 21 p. 114):* Javanese nobles avoid beef; cows used extensively in agriculture (often cross-bred with wild *Banteng* bulls, producing strong draft cows).
