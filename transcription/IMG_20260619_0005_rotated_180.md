[Continuation from folio 0004 on lowlands of Bagêlen, Grobogan, etc.:]

For months on end suffer from floods.  
And just as in Dêmak / Joana / Kudhus / Pathi / Cêngkalsewu cannot be planted. [*underlined: cannot be planted.*]  
Even after floods subside irrigation walls have to be built up again & systems strengthened.

**Jav. Farmer** much more Hardworking / diligent than those who rule / administer over him.  
If not so oppressed / extorted by chiefs would certainly produce more.

Av. Jav. Farmer would always be prepared to work himself out of any predicament — religious convictions do not militate against this.

Would not be Politic at this Time for V.O.C. to interfere further & Internal Admin. of HB II.

---

**(4) If population in Raising of cattle facilitated in each District & enough ploughing animals in**

* Jav. in general v. poor = little progress made in Buffalo / cattle Rearing.
* No lack of Beasts of Burden — mainly Because Jav. Farmer makes great use of those that he already has.
* Even when cow has just had a calf still expected to work — this has Deleterious effects on Quality of Livestock.
* many Buffaloes Brought from E / W MN [Eastern / Western Mancanagara] to be slaughtered in Yogya for Food.
