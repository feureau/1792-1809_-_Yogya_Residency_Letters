### Comprehensive Inventory of Sultan HB II's 22 Royal *Pesanggrahans* / Pleasure Houses (1808)

* Reference: **Pj. Br. 23 (28 June – 22 Aug. 1808 - Van Woortman to Daendels)**
* Document: *Notitie van de lustplaatsen van Z.H. den Sultan te Djocjocarta (1808)*.

#### Inventory of Royal Estates & Country Retreats (*Lustplaatsen/Pesanggrahans*):
* *East of Kraton:*
  1. Raja Katawang
  2. Winangun
  3. Purwa (formerly called Babadan)
  4. Wonocatur (1 1/2 hours' ride from Kraton)
  5. Ngawatreja / Mawatreja (2 hours' ride)
  6. Tanjung Tirta (2 1/2 hours' ride)
  7. Fruit orchards at Kali Ajir, Jenu, and Paltrane.
* *North of Kraton:*
  8. Old Krapyak (1/4 hour from Kraton)
  9. Bantul Karang (2 1/2 hours)
  10. Namas (South Coast; 4 1/2 hours)
  11. Kepik fruit garden (2 1/2 hours)
* *West of Kraton (Gamping Limestone Range):*
  12. Nana Newu (1 1/4 hours)
  13. Nana Pakis (1 1/2 hours)
  14. Ambarketawang
  15. Tegal Pingawi (behind Ambarketawang; 3/4 hour)
  16. Karang Reja (pepper garden; 1 1/2 hours)
  17. Kawarasang (pepper garden)
  18. Krensing (formerly Kanigara)
  19. Banyu Binumpang (2 1/2 hours)
* *North of Kraton (Ruins):*
  20. Ruins of Rusterburgh (3/4 hour; called Bulu by Javanese).
* **Total:** **22 Royal *Pesanggrahans* belonging to Sultan HB II**.
* *Crown Prince's Pesanggrahans:* Rejakusuma (1 1/2 hours North) and Wanayasa (1 1/2 hours North).
