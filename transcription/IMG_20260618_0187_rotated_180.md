### [21 January 1805]

21st Jan 1805  
Waterloo to Eng.

Border Incident KENDAL / KALIWONGU:  
Proposal of Van den Berg to come to Yogya from Sala  

[but Waterloo still indisposed]

Attempt to get NEW BEKTI CONTRACT

### [31 January 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
31 Jan 1805  
Waterloo -> Engelhard

Message from DR II: 2 MANTRIS because of Bad behaviour had been taken into CUSTODY IN KRATON but escaped.  
SUTADIWIRIA flees to father in Sala desa Bulus = (12½ hrs journey from Yogya)  

[SUMADININGRAT planning to send troops to Bulus to Reseize him]

Refusal of Waterloo to allow armed men to be sent but asks Representations at Sala COURT  
-> Danuningrat PATIH of Sala.  
(Yogya - Sala Relations in jeopardy)

But 50 KATANGGUNG soldiers -> KLATEN:  

[Representations to DR II to get them Recalled]

SUTADIWIRIA + other flees from BULUS.
