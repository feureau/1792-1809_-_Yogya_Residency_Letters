### [Mar 1802] — Ritual Execution of the Murderers of Ratu Agêng's Factor

* **Orders of HB II to Sumadiningrat conveyed by Panjis: Mar. 1802**
  * *[Right margin annotation in red ink:]* **Execution of Criminals who kill Factor of Ratu Agêng**
  * **Amad Tono / Nuryasa** [and Jo Truna / Wangsatruna] = to be executed for attack on Ratu Agêng's trader + special ceremony.
  * **Ceremony / Attire of the Condemned:**
    * Clothed in yellow trailing *dodot*.
    * White trousers / Belt / Body smeared + yellow *boreh*.
    * *Sumping* behind ears; *lang* + flowers.
    * \+ white hats (*reges*) + bobble on top.
    * Kris + gold *pondhok* at side + *Tunggak Semi* hilt (normally only to be carried by Sultan). Kris to be tied up.
    * Put on 2 horses + Red *payongs*.
    * Led from prison westwards across Pasebaan as gamelan plays *Ladrang Pangantèn*.
  * **Execution on Pasebaan:**
    * In middle of Pasebaan: gamelan to stop / krises taken / payongs shut.
    * *Bikêl* to stab them + kris (not a kraton *pusaka*).
    * Bodies to be cut into pieces / Heads + *sumpings* to be placed on stakes at cross-roads.
    * [Jo] Tatruna = Hand + Foot to be cut off.
    * After execution: money to be distributed to people from 2 *panggung*. Order: 2 *panggung* placed E / W of Waringin Kurung.
  * *[Left margin annotation:]* **see Louw, Vol. III, p. 244. Decapitation of Brandals during Java War.**
  * *[Right margin archival citations:]*
    * **see BL Add. MS. 12341, ff. 223r–228r**
    * **see BL Add. MS. 14397, f. 59r–v**
