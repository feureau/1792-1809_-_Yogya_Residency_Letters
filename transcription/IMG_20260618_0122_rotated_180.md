### Archive Entries: February – May 1802 (Execution of Tegalreja Robbers & Harvest Reports)

* Reference: **P.J. Br. no. 48**
* **1 March 1802 (Van den Berg to Engelhard):** Danureja II and Regents sent to Semarang to greet incoming Governor-General Niberg.
* **17 March 1802:** Danureja II, Natayuda, and Purwadipura arrive at Semarang.
* **Execution of Tegalreja Murderers (March 1802):**
  * HB II orders execution of bandits **Amad Tana** and **Nurgayasa** for the attack on Ratu Ageng's business factor **Mertalaksana**.
  * Executed at Truna Wangsatruna in a public ceremony.
  * *Attire & Protocol:* Dressed in trailing yellow *dodot*, white trousers, belt, body smeared with yellow *boreh*, *sumping* behind ears, wearing white hats (*regez*) with bobble on top.
  * *Kris Protocol:* Golden *pendhok* at side; *Tungak Nemi* hilt carried exclusively by the Sultan.
  * Lead from prison westward across *Paseban* as gamelan plays *Ladrang Panganten*.
  * At middle of *Paseban*, *Gronilanti* stops; krises removed and payongs shut. *Bekel* to stab them with kris (not a Kraton *pusaka*). Bodies cut to pieces; heads and limbs placed on stakes at cross-roads. **Jatruna** had hands and feet cut off.
  * Executions funded by money distributed from 2 *Panggonan* under *Waringin Kurung* trees.
* **14 April 1802:** Feast of Binar celebrated. Roads in Yogya in terrible state.
* **9 May 1802:** Pending arrival of Engelhard and wife; horses dispatched from Kedu. Sultan's stud farm noted.
* **24 April 1802:** PB IV shelters individuals implicated in attack/murder on Ratu Ageng's factor (*"paras ati" to PB IV*).
* **26 May 1802:** Van den Berg returns from pepper gardens. Pepper harvest for the year expected to reach 1,000 pikuls (smaller than 1801).
