* **Id. to Id. 1 Jul. 1802**

  * Idea of HB II himself administering Domains (? hitherto farmed out):

    * Mataram Domain to Sumadiningrat, 1st Inner Regent.

    * Kêdhu to Natayuda, administering Tolls / Birds Nests in Bagêlèn.

  * <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">If HB II takes Tax Farm out of hands of Resident = great loss of Rev. for Dutch</ins> (Rēksanēgara) + Disharmony Sala / Yogya + Resident (much of salary from Tax Farm).
