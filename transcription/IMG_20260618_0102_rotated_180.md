### Archive Entries: December 1801 – July 1802 (Kedu Tobacco, Bird's Nests, & Domain Reform)

* **9 December 1801:** Embassy to congratulate Niberg scheduled to depart in *Sawal* (Dec. 1802).

* **19 December 1802:** Written appointment of Niberg presented at a Medono sugar mill. Chinese manager loses goods. Natayuda warned not to display pro-V.O.C. bias openly at court. HB II to send embassy in *Dulkangidah*.
* **27 February 1802:** HB II appoints Natayuda, Purwadipura, and Ng. Prawirasentra to accompany Danureja II to Semarang.
* **30 May 1802 (Kedu Tobacco Export):** 2,500 corges of tobacco exported annually from Kedu. Chronology of Kedu/Yogya Capitans Cina: Jan Lekko (1776–?), Gan Ke Ko (1764–1776), Ni Liong Ki (–1803), Que Tjin Ning (Yogya), **Tan Tjin Sing** (1803–1813).
* **29 June 1802 (Bird's Nest Taxes):** Bandhar tax 21,333.16. HB II aware Resident makes enormous profits from bird's nests and prepares to raise taxes.
* **1 July 1802 (Secret Domain Admin Plan):**
  * Secret conference between HB II and *Nayakas*. HB II proposes directly administering royal domains instead of tax-farming them:
    * *Sukawati Domain:* Assigned to Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III.
    * *Mataram Domain:* Assigned to Numadiningrat (1st Inner Regent).
    * *Bagelèn Domain:* Assigned to Mangundipura (+ Pangeran Dipakusuma administering tolls/bird's nests in Bagelèn).
    * *Kedu Domain:* Assigned to Natayuda.
    * *Frontier Tolls:* *Mantri Tamping* (frontier village heads) to manage tollgates instead of Chinese tax farmers.
  * *Warning:* Taking tax farms out of Resident's hands would cause severe revenue loss for Reksanegara, the Dutch, and the Resident (whose salary depends on tax farms), creating major friction between Solo, Yogya, and the Residency.
