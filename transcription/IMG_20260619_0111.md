### Salatiga Tax Office Raid, Japanese Samurai Capture, & Daendels Travel Route (June – July 1808)

* Reference: **Pj. Br. 23 (15 June – 15 July 1808)**
* **Salatiga Bandar Attack:**
  * Attack on Chinese *Bandar* tax office at Salatiga by a 20-man armed *kècu* gang from Yogyakarta led by Nunggaminggala.
  * **Captured Weaponry:** Search party captures a **Japanese broadsword (*samurai katana*)** valued at 200 Rijksdaalders and a Japanese musket.
  * Special investigation commission dispatched under Cornet Fion and R.T. Wiryaguna; 200 Sp.D. Bounties placed on the gang leader and 50 Sp.D. On gang members.
* **Daendels Escort & Travel Route:**
  * HB II and PB IV advise Daendels against bringing a large military escort on his journey to Solo and Surabaya (Daendels limits escort to 200 European cavalry, artillery, and infantry).
  * Daendels plans an incognito return visit to HB II in Yogyakarta; road and bridges between Solo and Yogyakarta ordered to be repaired.
