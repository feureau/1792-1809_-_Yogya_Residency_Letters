### Daendels Orders on Prangwedana Troop Deployment to Klaten and Yogya (22 June 1808)

* Reference: **Solo Br. 55 (22 June 1808 - W.N. Servatius)**
* **Deployment Instructions:** In order not to weaken the Surakarta garrison during First Resident Van Braam's absence, Lt.-Col.
