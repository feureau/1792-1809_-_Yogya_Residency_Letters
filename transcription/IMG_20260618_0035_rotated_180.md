### Archive Entries: October 1793 (Troop Pay Negotiations & Pay Scale)

* **Financial Burden:** High cost of troop payments during English War of 1781 recalled. HB II had promised in 1792 to make reasonable arrangements for reinforcement troops in case of need.
* **30 October 1793 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten):**
  * Acknowledges letter of 22 Oct attempting to reduce 4,000 Sp. D. allowance for 1,000 troops.
  * Resident manages only a minor reduction of 200 Sp. D. (to 3,800 Sp. D.). HB II insists his officers and *mantris* must pay their subordinates fixed rates and cannot accept lower figures without risk of desertion in Batavia.
  * R.T. Wiraguna and Kliwon Mangkuwijaya confirm common soldiers received 5 Sp. D. in 1781 vs. 3 1/2 Sp. D. now.

#### Pay Scale for Troops in Semarang / Surabaya (After 20 Days):
* **Major:** 15 Sp. D. / month
* **Captain:** 10 Sp. D. / month
* **Lieutenant:** 7 2/4 Sp. D. / month
* **Ensign:** 5 Sp. D. / month
* **Sergeant:** 3.36 Sp. D. / month
* **Corporal:** 2 Sp. D. / month
* **Ordinary Soldier:** 1 Sp. D. / month + 40 lbs Rice + 3 lbs Salt.
