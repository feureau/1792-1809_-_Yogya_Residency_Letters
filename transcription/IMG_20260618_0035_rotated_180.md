**30 Oct 1793**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:**  
Letter of Van Overstraten 22 Oct.  
Attempt to get better arrangement than 4,000 Sp.D. promised for 1,000 troops.  
But despite arguments of Van IJsseldijk only reduction of 200 Sp.D. $\rightarrow$ **3,800 [Sp.D.]**  
because HB II says that his people need this sum for their upkeep.  
Mantris + small followings: have to pay the salaries of their people / salaries fixed.  

Earlier audience informed by:  

- R.T. Wirajuwana [Wiraguna / Wirajuwa]
- Kliwon Mangkuwijaya  
Each common man in English War 1781 gets 5 Sp.D. ? / now $3\frac{1}{2}$ Sp.D. ?  

HB II says that he would feel ashamed if his troops deserted in Batavia because of low wages.  

HB II more accommodating about troops in Semarang / Surabaya:  
after 20 days should receive following payments:  

- Major: 15 p.m.  
- Captain: 10 p.m.  
- Lieutenant: 7.24 p.m.  
- Ensign: 5 p.m.  
- Sergeant: 3.36 p.m.  
- Corporal: 2 p.m.  
- Ordinary soldier: 1 p.m.  
+ 40 lbs rice / 3 lbs salt.
