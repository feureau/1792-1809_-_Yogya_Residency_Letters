### [Transcription]

**5 Oct.** Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:  
letter 2 Oct from Van Overstraten: arrival of French ships in Sunda Strait.  
Sultan [HB II] prepares promised reinforcement troops.  
Sultan promises cavalry company to Semarang if necessary.

---

**8 Oct.** Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:  
Letter of Van Overstraten 6 Oct. demands audience with Sultan $\rightarrow$ delivery of forced deliveries of food (*gebidt*).  
Regarding payment of troops: Sultan asks them to be considered *not* as mercenaries, but as reinforcements:  
> *"niets als huur maar als hulp-troepen"*  

Payment same as for English War in 1781.  
Sultan takes counsel with Regents.  
Sultan: reinforcement troops should be kept under their own commanders, to be paid a lump sum each month to be divided up amongst Regents.  
Van IJsseldijk suggests 3,000 Sp.D.; Sultan rejects it as insufficient.  
Agreement reached: **4,000 Sp.D. per month** for **1,000 men** who would march to Batavia in times of need. (Less than in 1781 when V.O.C. more prosperous and paid 5 Sp.D. per man per month).  
First 20 days in Semarang / Surabaya: no payment demanded by Sultan.  
Document (*Bewijs*) given to Van IJsseldijk about the agreement.
