### Archive Entries: October 1793 (Defense Requisitions & Reinforcement Troops)

* Reference: **Port 3 No. 1. NvB 1793 (Yogya)**
* **5 October 1793 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten):**
  * Acknowledges letter of 2 Oct regarding arrival of French warships in Sunda Strait. HB II instructed to prepare promised reinforcement troops. HB II offers cavalry company to Semarang if required.
* **5 October 1793 (Ibid.):**
  * Audience with HB II regarding timber deliveries and military pay.
  * **Status of Troops:** HB II requests troops be regarded as allied auxiliary forces (*niets als hunne maatschappelijke hulp-troepen*), not mercenaries, under payment terms matching the English War of 1781–84.
  * **Command & Structure:** HB II insists auxiliary forces remain under Javanese command, with monthly lump-sum payments distributed among Regents.
  * **Financial Terms:** Van IJsseldijk proposes 3,000 Rixdollars (*Sp. D.*); HB II rejects this as insufficient, demanding 4,000 p.m. for 1,000 men marching to Batavia in time of need (comparing with 5 Sp. D. p.m. paid in 1781). No payment demanded for first 20 days in Semarang/Surabaya. Official agreement document (*Bewijs*) delivered to Van IJsseldijk.
