#### 1. Demak & Adilangu

- *[Red underline:]* **Dêmak Area & Adilangu:**  
  Noted as a place of refuge / sanctuary for many Javanese fugitives and criminals.  

- Ruled by **Pangeran Wijil**:  
  Old Pangeran died in 1784; succeeded by his son.  
  Described as a very fine, brave Javanese gentleman from an ancient and eminent family; recommended to be given a regular Regency when a vacancy occurs.  

- Pays an annual tribute to the V.O.C. of **6 koyangs of rice**.

#### 2. Japara, Kudus & Pati

- **Declining timber yield in Japara:**  
  Production originally fixed at 2,000 teak planks, but recently cut by 500 planks.  

- **Kudus:**  
  Very heavily populated district, encompassing the surrounding territory of Japara.  

  - Bupati: **R.T. Suradiningrat** (a Madurese), recognized as one of the most diligent and capable Bupatis in the region.  
- **Pati:** Administered by two Bupatis:  
  - **R.T. Aria Mêgatsari**  
  - **R.T. Mangunkusuma** (married to a daughter of Mangkunegara I, by whom he has many children).

#### 3. Rembang & The Blandong Timber Workers

- *[Red underline:]* **Rembang:** One of the most sparsely populated areas (*f. 38*).  
  - Bupati: **Tumenggung Puspadipura**.  
  - Inhabitants earn their livelihood through carpentry and shipbuilding (*timmerwerf*), though work has fallen off considerably in recent years.  
  - Forest-cutter (*blandong*) population drawn from **Trambalang**, **Waru**, **Resserman**, and **Morototo**.  
  - Required to deliver timber to Rembang yards as well as to the Sunan of Surakarta for state boat-building.
