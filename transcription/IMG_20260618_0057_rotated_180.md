### Coastal Regencies: Demak, Kudus, Japara, Pati, and Rembang

* **Demak & Adilangu:** Place of refuge for Javanese fugitives/criminals. Ruled by Pangeran Wijil (died 1784; succeeded by son). Pangeran Aria Riu (Brang), from an eminent Javanese family, recommended for future regency. Pays V.O.C. tribute of 6 *koyangs* of rice.
* **Japara Teak Production:** Teak timber production in Japara declines to 2,000 planks (cut by 500).
* **Kudus & Demak Population:** Kudus, Demak, and Japara heavily populated. Regent R.T. Suradiningrat praised as one of the most diligent *Bupatis* in the region.
* **Pati Administration:** Governed by 2 *Bupatis*: R.T. Aria Megatsari and R.T. Mangunkusuma (married to daughters of MN I and Prangwedana).
* **Rembang:** One of the most sparsely populated areas (Tumenggung Sumadipura). Inhabitants rely on carpentry and timberwork, but economic activity has declined in recent years. Timber supplied from Blora, Trambalang, Waru, Tesneran, and Moroteto to Rembang and Semarang for boat building.
