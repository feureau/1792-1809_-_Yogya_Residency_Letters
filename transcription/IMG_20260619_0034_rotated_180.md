## Content

### Archive Entries: July – November 1807 (Opium Lease Rates & Cilacap Piracy Operations)

* **HB II Illness:** HB II claims illness due to old age and remains confined to the Kraton; Crown Prince remains at his side. Certified by Patih Danureja II under official seal.
* **31 July 1807 (Opium Monopoly Rates):**
  * Opium retail price across Yogyakarta and Kedu set at **12 Ronde Reals (24 Sp.D.) per catty** (1,600 silver Rixdollars per chest).
* **28 August 1807:**
  * Surakarta marriage: PB IV's daughter R. Ayu Jatirah marries Young Pangeran Mangkubumi (R. Mas Aria Natakusuma).
  * Death of R. Ayu Jasradiningrat (sister of HB II) after a protracted illness.
* **29 August 1807:** HB II dispatches Lurah Mantri Anom Mangkuwijayat with wedding presents to Surakarta.
* **20 October 1807:** Nicolaas Engelhard notifies HB II of the elevation of R.T. Aria Magetsari to Pangeran.
* **27 October 1807 (Cilacap Anti-Piracy Operations):**
  * On HB II's orders, Danureja II issues instructions to border districts near Banyumas and Cilacap (*see Galuh/Ciamis Wetan annexation in 1811*).
  * Coastal patrols ordered to suppress pirate raids disrupting local Javanese communities around Cilacap.
* **2 November 1807:** Envoys of Ratu Kencana Wulan (Nyai Sedah Mirah, Mantris Ng. Risawirana and Risasentika) dispatched to Semarang to purchase luxury imported goods.
