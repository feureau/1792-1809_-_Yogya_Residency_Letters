### AN Djokjo Brieven No. 86: History of Yogyakarta Tax Farm Administration (1754–1762)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 86 (19 Jan. 1805 - Waterloo to Engelhard)**
* **Accession Leases:** Immediately upon ascending the throne, HB II farmed out regional tollgate and tax revenues in Mataram, Pajang, Bagelèn, Sukawati, Rema, Kedu, and Mangkunagaran lands to the Capitan Cina of Mataram.
* **1754–1755 First 3-Year Term:**
  * Rate fixed at 16,000 Ronde Reals annually (@ 28 dubbeltjes / 2.80 guilders), paid in equal installments at *Puasa* (8,000) and *Mulud* (8,000).
  * **Royal Ceremonial Tribute:** At each payment, tribute delivered to HB I: 34 small *Patholen* cloths, 34 small chintz cloths, 1 gold payong, 1 yellow payong, 1 white payong with gold bands and fittings.
  * **Hang Bikti:** 4,000 paid for 3 years upon receipt of the gold charter (*piagem*).
* **1757–1758 Second Term:** Tax-farm raised to 6,000. Ki Mantu (ordinary Chinese tax farmer) defaults.
* **1762 Reorganization:**
  * **Tax Farm placed under direct charge of Patih Danureja I and favorite Inner Regent Nindudidja.**
  * **Kyai Tumenggung Reksanegara appointed Tax Bupati (*Tax Regent*).**
  * Gold *piagem* granted to Reksanegara every 3 years.
