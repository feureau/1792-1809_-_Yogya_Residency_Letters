### [Historical Retrospective: Origin of the Yogyakarta Tax Farm under HB I]

* **Source & Dating:**
  * `Concept. Aparte Secret Missive, 19 Jan. 1805`
  * `M. Waterloo to N. Engelhard.`
  * `at time Major Donkel Resident.`

* **1755 (HB I's Accession & Initial Lease):**
  * `Immediately HB I ascends throne:`
  * `Farms out Tax farm to Capt cina in Mataram — of Mataram, Pajang, Bagalèn, Sukawati, Romo + Bird's Nests chipping + Kêdhu / MN [Mancanagara] Lands.`
  * `1754-1755: 1st 3-year Tax Farm 16,000 Ronde Reaalen @ 28 dubbeltjes p.a.` *(marked with double red bars: `|| 2.80`)*
  * `8,000 paid at Puasa`
  * `8,000 paid at Mulud.`

* **Ceremonial Offerings & Uang Bekti:**
  * `Every Time Tax paid = presents to HB I =`
  * `34 small Pathoolen`
  * `34 small sorts of Chintz`
  * `1 gold payong`
  * `1 yellow payong`
  * `1 white payong + gold Bands + confiture.`
  * `+ Payment of Uang Bêkti 4,000 for 3 years on Receipt of gold piagêm.`

* **1757–1758 (Second 3-Year Term):**
  * `2nd 3 years: Raised to 6,000 for Uang Bêkti.`
  * `Ki Mantu, a v. ordinary Chinese Tax Farmer. But cannot pay Sum Required.`

* **1762 (Reorganization under Kyai Tumenggung Reksanegara):**
  * `1762: .: Tax farm placed in Hands of DR I + favourite / Inner Regent Sindureja.`
  * `+ order to get administration of Tax farm given to a capable person.`
  * `.: Kyai Tum. Rêksanêgara appointed as Tax Bupati.`
  * `.: gold Piagêm given to RN every 3 years.`
  * `Even if Rêksanêgara no longer Directly in charge of Tax farm, HB I no longer bothers.`
