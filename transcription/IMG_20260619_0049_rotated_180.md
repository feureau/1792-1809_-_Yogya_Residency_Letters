### Tan Tjin Sing Shortfalls & Surakarta Court Festivities (1806–1808)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.86 / Solo Br. 20.55**
* **Tan Tjin Sing Tax Farm Shortfalls:**
  * Non-payment and tax collection deficits average approx. 1,000 Ronde Reals annually.
  * **Tan Tjin Sing makes up all tax shortfalls out of his own pocket.** Yields little direct profit as tax-farmer, but the position provides immense commercial advantages for his private trade network.
* **Residency Personnel:**
  * Frederik Willem Baumgarten (born Brunswick, arrived Java 1784): Residency Surgeon and private surgeon since 1805.
  * Lucas Ubenfeld (born Batavia, entered service 1777): 51-year-old Corporal.
* **1806 Surakarta Court Festivities (15 Jan. 1806 - Liebeherr to Engelhard):**
  * New Year celebrations passed off smoothly; strained relations between Crown Prince and Pangeran Mangkubumi somewhat restored.
  * **6 July 1807 Accession Festival for King Louis Napoleon:**
    * Special banquet hosted at the Residency.
    * **PB IV, Pangeran Mangkubumi, Patih Danuningrat, and P. Cakrakusuma dance the *Pawiring* (Javanese Fighting Dance)** to elaborate gamelan music (*PB IV dancing Pawiring at Resident's house unprecedented since Resident Palm*).
    * PB IV presents Resident Liebeherr with a gold tobacco box; hot air balloons released (rising 200 ft).
    * *Serimpi* dances in morning; 12:00 banquet surrounding a mock Greek temple built by Jr. Cornelius featuring statues of Bacchus, Appis, Minerva, and Juno.
