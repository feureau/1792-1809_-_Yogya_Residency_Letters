## Content

### Archive Entries: January – April 1796 (Hague Treaty, Malacca Fall, & Diplomatic Letters)

* **20 February 1796:** Outposts notified of Franco-Dutch peace; National Guard Commander Biwon in Batavian Republic.
* **Banyumas Appanage:** *Piagem* of Wedanaship of Banyumas (500 cacahs of Kadahuwang) restored to PB IV (PB IV to Van Overstraten, 19 Jan. 1796).
* **16 May 1795 Treaty:** Franco-Dutch Treaty of Peace and Friendship (signed in The Hague) translated into Javanese by J.G. van den Berg.
* **27 February 1796 (Van Overstraten to Alting):** Formal request for Surakarta Crown Prince to receive the title *Pangeran Adipati Anom*.
* **7 March 1796:** Pangeran Aria Nunya Prangwedana sends letter of thanks to W.A. Alting for formal installation as PWP.
* **Fall of Malacca (1795):** Note that Malacca fell to the British in 1795 without resistance (see De Haan, *Priangan*).
* **2 April 1796 (Van Overstraten to Alting):** Envoys including Sayyid Alwi as-Saqqaf dispatched in 1795 to Malacca, Riau, and Borneo. British fleet sails from Malacca toward *Groote Oost* to capture Ambon.
