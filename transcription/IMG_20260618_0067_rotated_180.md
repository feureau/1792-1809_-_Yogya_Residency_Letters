### Text & Marginal Annotations

- **20 Feb. 1796:**
  - News given to North Coast outposts that peace was signed between France and Holland.
  - National Convention becoming the Batavian Republic.

- **Project Ceremonial for Voorstelling of PWD:**
  - *Piagem* of the *wedana*-ship of Banyumas.
  - 500 *cacahs* of Kaduwang to be given back to PB IV.

- **PB IV to Van Overstraten (19 Jan. 1796):**
  - Refers to **16 May 1795 Treaty of Peace / Friendship** signed at The Hague between France and the Dutch (ratified 4 June 1795).
  - Translated by J.G. van den Berg (*Trans. in Javaans Taal*).

- **Van Overstraten to Alting (27 Feb. 1796):**
  - Request for the Sala Crown Prince to receive the official title/name of **Pangeran Adipati Anom**.

- **P. Aria Surya Prangwēdana at Sala to W.A. Alting (15 Feb. 1796 / rec’d 7 Mar. 1796):**
  - Official letter of thanks for his appointment as PWD [Mangkunegara II].

- **Fall of Malacca (1795):**
  - *Malakka falls to English 1795 without a shot being fired* (see De Haan, *Priangan*).

- **Van Overstraten to Alting (2 Apr. 1796):**
  - Sayyid Alwi as-Saggaf sails to Pontianak, Riau, Malacca, coast of Borneo (sent in 1795 to Malacca // coast of Borneo).
  - English fleet from Malacca heads to the *Groote Oost* (Great East / Moluccas) to capture Ambon, Banda, etc.
