### Van den Berg to Engelhard (12 November 1801 Cont.) & Evaluation of Danureja II

* Reference: **Dj. Br. No. 48 (12 Nov. 1801 cont.)**
* **Resident's Interface:** Van den Berg forced to converse with HB II personally in nervous Javanese; takes advantage of official occasions (e.g., *strandgeld* presentations) to address the Sultan.
* **Sultan's Demands:** HB II informs Nederburgh of his desire for the Crown Prince to ascend the throne without V.O.C. approval, and demands the 1st Resident sit in the *Pagelaran* on Mondays, Thursdays, and Saturdays to receive royal commands.
* **Niberg Appointment Celebrations (6 Dec. 1801):** Postponed by 2 1/2 months to *Garebeg Puasa*; no noble dares gainsay HB II. HB II refuses to allow Danureja II to receive an official V.O.C. Act of Appointment (repeating his stance with Danureja I).
* **Resident's Comparison:** *"Despite all his whims and caprices, HB II pleases me better than that hypocrite of a Solo Sunan."* HB II criticizes the V.O.C. publicly, whereas the Sunan does so in secret. HB II disliked by subjects, with few loyal followers in event of war.
* **Patih Danureja II Character:**
  * HB II excludes DR II from major state decisions, limiting his purview to Surakarta and V.O.C. affairs (while HB II secretly controls everything).
  * *"DR II is a very obliging and very genial person who has stood the test and on whose loyalty we can depend."*
  * Initially considered a bungler before appointment, DR II has matured in office and equals Natayuda in skill. Lack of worldly experience/naïveté improving with time; beginning to show firmness with HB II while maintaining proper distance.
* **V.O.C. Position:** *"The times are certainly miserable and our strength is weak"* :: firm tone toward HB II necessary. Nederburgh's open admission of V.O.C. financial bankruptcy only emboldened the Sultan.
