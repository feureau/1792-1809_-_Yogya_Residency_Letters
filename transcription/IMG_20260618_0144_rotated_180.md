### Archive Entries: August – September 1803 (Severe Earthquakes & Rangga-HB II Rift)

* **12 August 1803 (Waterloo -> Engelhard):** Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III's tax payments 16 months in arrears (1,300 silver Rixdollars). Waterloo severely reprimands him.
* **20 August 1803 (Waterloo & Domis -> Engelhard):** Audience with HB II, Ratus, and Crown Prince. Extreme lack of economy (*Verregaande inzaagligheid*) noted on part of Sultan in financial matters; Crown Prince powerless to intervene.
* **7 September 1803 (Severe Earthquakes in Yogyakarta):**
  * **At 9:57 a.m. (7 Sept. 1803), a very severe earthquake strikes Yogyakarta** (strongest in living memory).
  * **Damage:** Major structural damage in Kraton and **Taman Sari**; Ngawatreja building damaged; Chinese temple destroyed and pools drained; N.W. bulwark of Fort cracked. 4 major earthquakes within 17 days.
* **13 September 1803 (Quarrel between Raden Rongga III and HB II):**
  * Quarrel erupts because Raden Rongga III seized land granted in perpetuity to a court Javanese.
  * HB II summons Rongga's wife (**Ratu Maduretna**) back to the Kraton; Rongga's mother-in-law excluded from court affairs.
  * Rongga pursues other women; HB II summons Rongga's concubines and Ratu to court.
