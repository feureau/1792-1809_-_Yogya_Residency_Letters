### Klaten Fort First Stone & Loano Indigo Factory Yields (1804)

* **Klaten Fort Foundation Stone (25 July 1804):**
  * Under the flag of the Batavian Republic and a 21-gun salute, the **FIRST STONE OF KLATEN FORT WAS LAID**.
  * 28 July: Resident Waterloo and Yogyakarta *Priyayis* return to Yogya.
* **Loano Indigo Factory (1789–1802):**
  * Only indigo factory on HB II's personal domain; operated for 13 years (1789–1802) before being dismantled at HB II's request in 1802.
  * *Production Record:* Dec. 1796 (125 lbs); July 1798 (625 lbs); July 1800 (488 lbs); May 1801 (250 lbs). Total: 1,488 lbs.
  * 100 families in Loano assigned to cultivate indigo plants; production remained small relative to labor input because Loano land produced only 2 pikuls of indigo vs. 10 pikuls in Pekalongan or Surabaya.
* **Cotton Yarn Trade (30 Sept. 1804):**
  * Resident requests exemption from buying cotton yarn @ 15 Sp. D. per pikul, as actual market cost is 31 1/2 – 42 Sp. D. (1 Ronde Real = 2 Sp. D. / 2.80 guilders).
  * HB II notes DR II's son supplied 60 pikuls @ 21 2/3 Ronde Reals (80 Sp. D.) per pikul; total payment 1,800–2,400 Sp. D., though some pikuls were missing.
  * Cotton thread cheaper along the North Coast; suggests Resident of Tegal supply the thread. Raw cotton imported from Banyumas/Kedu into Yogya incurs at least 40 Sp. D./pikul in tolls, doubling or trebling material costs.
