### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.86: Fort Progress, Resident Bupatis, & Road Taxes (1808)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.86 (May – June 1808)**
* **16 May 1808:** Daendels demands an immediate financial audit and status report from Engelhard regarding the Yogyakarta Residency office.
* **8 June 1808 (Fort & Road Report):**
  * Engelhard reports on Yogyakarta Fort: HB II's Mangkunagaran subjects required to work 90 consecutive days on the fort post-*Garebeg*, but HB II diverts them to other royal building projects, hindering progress.
  * Klaten Fort construction suffers from similar labor diversions. Boyolali Fort maintenance shared 1/2 by Semarang Regency and 1/2 by HB II.
* **Bupatis Resident in Yogyakarta:** Bupatis of Kedu, Bagelèn, Mataram, Pajang, and Sukawati maintain residences in their Regencies, but **all reside permanently in Yogyakarta**.
* **Purwadipura Gatekeeping:** 1st Inner Regent **Purwadipura** serves as sole gatekeeper—all communications to HB II must pass through him.
* **Road Conditions & Road Tax Extortion (*Hang Kerigaji*):**
  * Roads connecting Yogyakarta to Semarang and Surakarta in very poor condition with almost no river bridges.
  * **Road Tax Extortion:** Special road tax (*Hang Kerigaji*) levied on the local population for road construction, **but HB II hands over very little of it to Patih Danureja II, retaining most in the royal treasury** (violating the 1792 Treaty obligation).
