### Archive Entries: June – August 1808 (Louis Napoleon Festival & Prangwedana Troops Arrival)

* **26 August 1808 (Louis Napoleon Accession Festival in Yogyakarta):**
  * Grand festival held at Yogyakarta Residency featuring illuminated garden displays (*"Vivat Louis Napoleon Koning van Holland"* arranged in firework letters by Lt. Schrones).
  * Attended by HB II, Crown Prince, and all Yogyakarta Mancanagara Regents: R.T. Sasradiningrat (Rajegwesi), Sasranegara (Grobogan), Prawirasentika (Banwerna), Pringgolaya (Kartasura), Sumorigana (Padangan), Natadiwiria (Duri), Natawijaya (Panolan), Prawirayuda (Niharan), Mas T. Sumadipura (Japan), Narakusuma (Grobogan).
  * HB II retires early from the festivities.
* **28 June 1808 (Prangwedana Troops Arrival):** Prangwedana's troops arrive in Yogyakarta under Capt. Janitz; confirm complete peace in Yogyakarta and Surakarta garrisons.
* **Inter-Court Grievance:** Yogyakarta Patih lodges a formal complaint against PB IV and Patih Danuningrat.
