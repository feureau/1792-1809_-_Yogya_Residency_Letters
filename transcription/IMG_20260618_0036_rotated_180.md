### [Transcription]

**4 Nov 1793**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:**  
Payment of 1,000 men sent to Batavia:  

- **1.** 2 Leaders (*eerste hoofden*):  
  - R.T. Danukusuma  
  - Raden Ria Sindureja  
  *(in view of large following)*: 100 Sp.D. p.m. $\times 2 = \mathbf{200\text{ Sp.D.}}$  

- **2.** R.T. Jayawinata: $\mathbf{60\text{ Sp.D.}}$  
- **3.** Tum. Mas Aria Mandura [Mendura] & Kliwon Mangunrana: 38 Sp.D. each $= \mathbf{76\text{ Sp.D.}}$  
- **4.** Radens: Martapura, Sasratmaja, Jayasèntika: 20 Sp.D. each $= \mathbf{60\text{ Sp.D.}}$  
- **8** Heads (? Captains): 10 Sp.D. each $\dots \mathbf{80}$  
- **66** Heads (? Sergeants): 6 Sp.D. each $\dots \mathbf{396}$  
- **49** Heads (? Corporals): $4\frac{1}{2}$ Sp.D. each $\dots \mathbf{220\frac{1}{2}}$  
- **869** Men: 3 Sp.D. each $\dots \mathbf{2607}$ [noted as 260.]  
*Extra:* 100 Sp.D. to be used on extraordinary occasions.  
*Provisions:* Each man 40 lbs Rice, 3 lbs Salt.

---

**31 Oct [1793]**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:**  
Disposition for PLANTING of Pepper (? Loano) Returns.

---

**18 Nov [1793]**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede tot de Parkeler (Solo):**  
Visits in Loano (Pepper Plantations).  
News that Empress [Ratu Kencana] of Solo gives birth to a daughter.

---

**20 Nov [1793]**  
**Ibid [Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede]:**  
At BANJAKKAN / GUNUNG KRUNJONG:  
Difficulties Solo / Yogya.
