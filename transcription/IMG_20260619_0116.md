### Archive Entries: July 1808 (Cirebon Rebel Movements & Military Leather Orders)

* **25–27 July 1808 (Cirebon Rebel Incursions):**
  * 500 Surakarta troops dispatched to Tegal under a Surakarta prince to suppress Cirebon rebels (**Kyai Kalur, Bagus Rangin, Prachman**).
  * HB II expresses surprise at Dutch inability to capture the rebels and orders border patrols in Banyumas and Yogyakarta territories to capture crossing fugitives.
* **Grobogan Tax Farm Lease:** Panawangan tax farm (*Bandar*) in Grobogan leased to Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III; Parawangan tax farm leased to R.T. Sasrakusuma.
* **Leather Equipment Order (19 July 1808):**
  * Engelhard orders leather cavalry equipment manufactured in Yogyakarta: 198 casques (*kasketten*), 198 curb reins, 198 spurs, 190 currycombs (*ronskammen*), 190 brushes, command holsters.
  * 2nd Resident Groenhoff van Woortman delivers 60 black saddles, whips, and boots to Semarang.
* **Pirate Raid at Nagara Wedi (30 July 1808):** Rawa Regent R.T. Pringgakusuma reports pirate landings at Nagara Wedi; dispatches 80 men with firearms and pikes. Artillery sent from Yogya garrison.
