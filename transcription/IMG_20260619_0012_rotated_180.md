### Wat. – Eng. | 28 Feb. 1806.

Still some Lands Fallow suitable for sawah.  
But usually in Remote areas.  
But, all land usually v. scrupulously used.  
If no sawahs laid out:  
1) Because of lack of people  
2) Lack of water or lack of skill of villagers in constructing irrigation schemes.

? If HB II would be constrained to Force excess pop. in heavily populated areas to move out to remote lands to open up Ricefields:

HB II v. clever at squeezing most profits from Land.  
3 years ago new measurement of land comes into force:  
**Pancas (cutting) = all land remaining over had been taken over by HB II & income directly paid to him** [*underlined in red*]  
**Land left over by new Tax / land assessment** [*underlined in red*]

Sometimes Day Labourers used for projects But rarely.  
If number of Idlers / Lay-abouts get too great can be sent to Batavia.

[*In left margin, red asterisk \* and red vertical rule:*]

* Some Idlers taken on as Followers of Jav. Nobles.  
* Others Become **wong Durjana** / Bandits  
* Idlers mostly too lazy to work in sawah  
* prefer to work as coolies  
* Transport goods for v. small payment  
  cf. van Sevenhoven.

---

### Eng – Wat. | 15 Jul. 1806

* State of affairs in Cirebon = getting gradually worse each day.  
* Eng. chosen to go on Commission to Cirebon to help put down Disturbances.

---

### Wat – Eng. | 23 Jul. 1806

* Wat. to tell HB II of Eng's departure for Cirebon  
* & necessity for Better watch on Borders [Boundary Division] Between Yogya / Cirebon lands.  
* for Fugitives from Cirebon & Robbers.
