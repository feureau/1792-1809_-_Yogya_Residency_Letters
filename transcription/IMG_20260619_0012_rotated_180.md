### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.86: Fallow Lands, Tax Assessment, & Cirebon Border Security

* **Fallow Lands:** Fallow sawah lands remain mainly in remote districts due to population or water shortages, or lack of local engineering skill in constructing irrigation works.
* **Land Assessment (*Pancas*):** HB II squeezes maximum profits from land following the new land assessment implemented 3 years prior. All surplus acreage (*Pancas*) seized by HB II for direct royal income.
* **Idlers & Banditry (*Wong Durjana*):**
  * Unemployed idlers become followers of Javanese nobles or turn to highway robbery (*Wong Durjana*).
  * Many work as transport coolies for minimal pay under Van Nivenhoven. Excess idlers banished to Batavia.
* **Cirebon Border Crisis (15–29 July 1806):**
  * Deteriorating security in Cirebon; Engelhard heads a commission to suppress Cirebon rebellions.
  * Waterloo instructs HB II to reinforce border patrols between Yogya and Cirebon to capture fleeing robbers and fugitives.
