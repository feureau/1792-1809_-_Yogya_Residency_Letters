### AN Djokjo Brieven No. 22: Wiese Installation & Solo River Commerce (December 1808)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 22 (4–9 Dec. 1808)**
* **4 December 1808 Installation:** Formal installation ceremony for Minister G.W. Wiese conducted in the Residency garden in the presence of HB II, Crown Prince, and court dignitaries.
* **Deserter Death:** Sick military deserter with close-cropped hair captured at Kutu; transferred to Yogyakarta garrison hospital, where he dies.
* **9 December 1808 (Solo River Grain Trade):**
  * Solo River boats loading salt from the *Oosthoek* proceed to Rajudan (Tuban) to load coke, paddy, and chaff.
  * Patih Danureja II sends a commission to Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III in Madiun to arrange downriver shipments of rice, timber, and coolies.
  * Solo River *Bandhars* instructed that no transit tolls are to be levied on rice, grain, or salt shipments between Tuban and the *Oosthoek*.
