### Archive Entries: September 1805 (Frans Paul Incident & Chinese Murder)

* **23 September 1805:**
  * **Frans Paul Incident:** Former soldier Frans Paul settles at the foot of Mount Merapi making and selling butter.
  * Takes in a girl named **Kuwak**, an escaped *Tandhak* court dancer belonging to Sultan HB II (*ref: Indonesia No. 37 article on Tandhak*).
  * Court officials **Rinawijaya** and **Netradirana** (in charge of Mataram province *Tandhak* dancers) locate Kuwak at Pasar Trowulan and seize her.
  * Frans Paul pursues them, shoots Rinawijaya dead at Nitis, and severely wounds Netradirana.
  * Paul arrested and detained in the Fort; Sultan informed of proceedings.
* **Murder of Chinese Merchant:** Chinese merchant murdered near *Pesanggrahan* Karangmojo (*Bagong/Mulud*); 2 Javanese arrested for the crime.
