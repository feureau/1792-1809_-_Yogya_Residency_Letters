### Van Overstraten Memorie van Overgave 1796 (Surakarta Court Dynamics)

* **British Danger:** If British invade Java, they could reinstate Ceylon exiles and fulfill Javanese messianic prophecies. V.O.C. must exercise double prudence regarding court relations and prevent all British-Court contacts.
* **Pakubuwana IV:** Van Overstraten notes PB IV is not as superstitious as reported in 1790, but describes him as *"young, shrewd and full of fire"*.
* **Influence:** Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat viewed as a bad influence on PB IV.
* **Family & Succession:**
  * PB IV marries 3rd wife (daughter of Bupati of Pamekasan) in 1791.
  * Crown Prince born 1785; appointed CP in Aug. 1792 by Van Overstraten. CP doted on by PB IV, but education neglected (*zeer verwaarloosd*).
  * 3rd wife's children: Ratu Pembayun (born 1795) and Prince Purbaya (born 1796).
  * Nusateng Ratu granted to P. Mangkubumi (died pre-1796).
  * Marriage link between Ratu Kencana and Pangeran Mangkubumi.
  * P. Buminata married daughter of Bupati of Pati.
  * *Comparison:* Mangkubumi is fiery and energetic; Buminata gentle and subtle. V.O.C. must preserve friendship with both princes as PB IV is fond of them.
