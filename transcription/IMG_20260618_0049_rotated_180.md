### [Transcription]

#### 1. Geopolitical Threat: The English & The Prophecy

- If the English reach Java, they would be in a position to **make the prophecy come true** [regarding the Amangkurat restoration].  
- Present parlous condition of the V.O.C. requires **doubling of prudence** with regard to the courts.  
- Guard especially against any contacts between the English and the courts.

#### 2. Character of Pakubuwana IV & Royal Family Dynamics

- Van Overstraten doubts PB IV is merely credulous / superstitious (*bijgeloovig*), as Resident Van Greven made him out to be during the 1790 Pakepung crisis:  
  > He is *"young, shrewd, and full of fire"*.  

- **Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat:** a very bad influence on PB IV.  
- PB IV married his 3rd wife (daughter of Bupati of Pamekasan / Ratu Kencana) in 1791.  
  - **Crown Prince [later PB V]:** Born c. 1788; appointed Crown Prince in August 1792 by Van Overstraten.  
  - Doted on by PB IV, but little done about his education (*zeer verwaarloosd*); character of CP might be spoiled by excessive doting.  
- **Two children of 3rd wife (Ratu Kencana):**  
  - Princess: **Ratu Pembayun** (born 1795).  
  - Prince: **Pangeran Purbaya** (born 1796).  
- **Marriage link:** Sister of Ratu [Kencana] was given to **Pangeran Mangkubumi**, but had since died (pre-1796).  
- **Pangeran Buminata:** married daughter of the Bupati of Pati.  
  - *Mangkubumi:* more fiery and energetic.  
  - *Buminata:* gentle and subtle.  
  - V.O.C. must maintain the friendship of these two princes, especially as PB IV is very fond of them.
