### Archive Entries: January – June 1802 (Raden Rongga Delanggu Incident & Earthquakes)

* **Heavy Rains:** Heavy rains continuing into May; fear of damage to pepper trees.
* **3 January 1802:** **Heavy earthquake in Yogya on 28 Dec. 1801** (5 shocks). Street walls damaged; pyramid built by HB II at a *pesanggrahan* destroyed. Death of Ratu Bendara noted.
* **Appointments (2 May 1792 / 1802):** Capitan Cina of Kedu (Gan Tiek Ko) dies; succeeded by Gan Tak Nung. Capitan Cina of Yogya (Que Tjin Ning) appoints Ni Liong Ki in Kedu. 7,708 Rixdollars annual tax-farm yield for Kedu.
* **Raden Rongga Incident at Delanggu (30 May 1802 / 27 Sura 1720):**
  * Mangkupraja to DR II: Journeying to Yogya; Raden Rongga III halts outside *pesanggrahan* Delanggu.
  * **Conflict:** Rongga demands a grazing goat near *pesanggrahan*; owner warns him off. Rongga orders dogs to attack the goat.
  * Deputy Demang **Jingadipa** tries to remonstrate; Rongga attacks Ki Kertasari, killing him with a pike (7 wounds in body after death). Houses plundered by Rongga's men.
  * Resident Nijvenheim files legal complaint (*gugat aluwaris*) in Yogya against Rongga.
* **17 June 1802 (Van den Berg to Engelhard):**
  * Describes **Raden Rongga III** (young man around 23): *"Die zijn hart vrij hoog draagt"* (arrogant/proud bearing).
  * Sumadiningrat conducts inquiry at Delanggu on HB II's orders, attempting to minimize the incident.
  * Incident triggered because Rongga took a goat for his daughter; when Javanese protested, Rongga attacked and killed Ki Kertasari.
  * Describes Rongga as a lawless, proud young man addicted to drink (*"kwaad jongen"*); planned to cause bad blood between Yogya and Solo. HB II refuses to punish Rongga despite religious and V.O.C. complaints.
