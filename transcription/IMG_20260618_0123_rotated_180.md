### [30 May 1802] — The Rangga Incident at Dêlanggu

* **Mangkupraja to DR II, 30 May 1802 / 27 Sura AJ 1729 [1728?]**
  * *[Left margin annotation in red ink:]* **Rangga Incident at Dêlanggu**
  * *[Right margin note:]* **on this see Suradipura, *Bêdhahipun Kraton Ngayogyakarta***
  * At Dêlanggu journeying to Yogya: Rangga halts outside *pêsanggrahan*.
  * Wants a goat [grazing nearby] — taken by Rangga.
  * Orders dogs to warn off anyone else.
  * Jingadipa (wakil Demang) attacked when tries to remonstrate + Ki Kērtasari killed + a pike = 7 wounds in body after death.
  * Houses plundered by Rangga.
  * Res. Nijvenheim sends *gugat aluwaris* to Yogya [legal complaint / *doodslag*].

### [17 Jun 1802] — Character of Raden Rangga Prawiradirja III

* **van den B – Eng., 17 Jun. 1802**
  * <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">R. Rangga young man of about 23 die zyn hart vrij hoog draagd.</ins>
  * Sumadiningrat making Inquiry at Dêlanggu on orders of HB II; tries to put different cast on affair:
    * Starts because Rangga takes little goat for his daughter.
    * When Javanese remonstrate, Rangga attacks them & kills Ki Kērtasari.
  * > "Rangga is a lawless proud person, attached to drink, a 'kwaadje jongen'"
  * Planned for a long time to put Bad Blood between Yogya / Sala, because of problems between kingdoms.
  * HB II would not allow Rangga to be punished by Jav. / Religious law for attack on Dêlanggu.
