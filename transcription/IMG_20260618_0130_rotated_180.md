### De Jong Opkomst pp. 118–234: Engelhard's Journey to the Courts (1802)

* Source: **De Jonge, *Opkomst*, Vol. XIII, pp. 118–283 (Engelhard Dated Semarang 27 May 1803)**
* **1802 Illness of Emperor:** Sunan ill; official business transferred to Crown Prince.
* **Grelengrert of Marbon [Klaten]:** Fort construction at Klaten.
* **24 August 1802 (Visit to Yogyakarta):**
  * Audience with Sultan HB II attended by Resident Van den Berg, Crown Prince (Raja Putra Narendra / HB III), and Danureja II.
  * Purpose: Secure troop commitments in case of British invasion and arrange installment payments for Governor-General's visits.
* **Complaints Regarding Protocol & Delays:**
  * **Paseban Protocol:** Complaint that court envoys were kept waiting on open *Paseban* in rain.
  * **Audience Delays:** Complaint that Resident was made to wait 3–4 months before securing an audience with Sultan.
  * **Speedy Execution:** Demand that court business receive prompt execution.
  * **Company Oath:** Danureja II takes oath to V.O.C. Company and agrees to accept letters on land grants.
* **Infrastructure & Roads:**
  * Planting of pepper and indigo; insistence on repairing bridges and maintaining roads in good condition.
  * Complaint regarding ruined road at Boyolali (neglected due to lack of materials).
* **Key Role of Sumadiningrat:**
  * All official business handled through **R.T. Sumadiningrat** (**Key man in Kraton**).
  * Sunan embraces Engelhard; past misunderstandings forgotten.
  * Sultan HB II remains unyielding; Engelhard realizes Sultan cannot be easily moved.
* **Crown Prince Comparison:**
  * **Yogya Crown Prince (HB III):** Very different character from his father HB II. Much easier in conversation, courteous, open, friendly, and talkative. Time will show if he resembles his father or grandfather upon ascending throne.
