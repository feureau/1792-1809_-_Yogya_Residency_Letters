## Diplomatic Transcription

Sending out of Solo mission to investigate plundering.  

[See another letter of E-D, dated 11 Aug. 1808 below.]

**Eng — Daendels,**  
**11 Aug. 1808**  
In *Besluiten* of G.G. P. Eng. sees his promotion as President of *Collegie van Schepenen* [above *Schepenen* in red: *College of Magistrates*] in Batavia.  
Thanks Daendels for apptmt. But Eng. to stay on as Res. in Yogya until <ins>end</ins> Nov.  
1 in order to have the honour of receiving Daendels in Yogya.  
2 to be present at paying of homage by Rulers.  
3 to work out agreement + Solo on *Roof & moord* in Bovenlanden (Klatèn Agreements Sept. / Oct. 1808)  
4 to get income from Bird's nests harvest in Autumn ||\* [in margin: *see 102.*]  
to defray enormous costs of his position as Opperhoofd during next 4 months.  
\*\ // <ins>Income as Minister at Yogya not enough even for running an economical household / & cannot pay for Daily Bread.</ins>

**v. van Braam -> D,**  
**11 Aug. 1808**  
PB IV accepts all <strike>the</strike> clauses of new Regulation on Ceremonial & Etiquette without demur.  
makes gamelan play on *Lama Dhuwur* as sign of acceptance.  
As usual PB IV v. friendly + pleased at D's expression of friendship & in appting Gusti Timur (? <strike>PB IV</strike> son of PB IV + Ratu Pamêkasan) as Lt. of Cavalry. [underlined in red; note in red: **Nata Purbaya**]  
gives Brevet of apptmt. at same time — causes PB IV great pleasure + tears start from eyes of Ratu.  
PB IV seems pleased at new ceremonial in that it increased his Prestige.  
If PB IV comes out of kraton + res. usually accompanied by women (old / young) in that case one of Kraton Nyais would carry Res' payong.  
Death of Maitre d'hotel (*Hofmeester*) causes difficulties for van Braam: another " " van der Ley to be sent from Salatiga.

**PB IV**  
Wants to know if Daendels still needs proa *pinglumpat* of Solo in Surabaya for another expedition (had taken D) + Patih Danuningrat to Surabaya) if not wants it returned to Solo + merchandise.
