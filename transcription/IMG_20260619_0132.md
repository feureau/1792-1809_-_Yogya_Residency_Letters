### Archive Entries: August 1808 (Engelhard Resignation, PB IV Protocol Acceptance, & Solo Gamelan)

* **11 August 1808 (Engelhard Resignation & Direct Financial Needs):**
  * Pieter Engelhard appointed President of the *Collegie van Schepenen* in Batavia; requests permission to remain in Yogyakarta until November to:
    1. Receive Governor-General Daendels in Yogyakarta.
    2. Participate in the royal homage ceremonies.
    3. Finalize the Solo-Yogya Klaten Accord on Bovenlanden robberies.
    4. Collect Autumn birds' nest revenues.
  * Notes his Minister's salary is insufficient to cover basic household expenses.
* **11 August 1808 (PB IV Unconditional Protocol Acceptance):**
  * Resident Van Braam reports PB IV accepts all clauses of Daendels' new ceremonial etiquette without objection.
  * Orders gamelan performances on *Lamo Dhuwur* to mark acceptance.
  * PB IV delighted that Daendels appointed his young son Gusti Timur (by Ratu Pamekasan) as Lieutenant of Cavalry; tears shed by Ratu Pamekasan during brevet presentation.
  * PB IV requests return of Surakarta proas previously used for the Surabaya military expedition.
