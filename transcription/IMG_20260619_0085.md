### Debt Settlement of Ex-Resident Waterloo & Surakarta Army Reorganization (April – May 1808)

* Reference: **Pj. Br. 23 'Bundel Djokjo Brieven 1808' (14 Apr. – 22 May 1808)**
* **Waterloo Debt Settlement (14 April 1808 - Engelhard to Daendels):**
  * Upon assuming office, Engelhard receives a note from Danureja II stating ex-Resident Matthew Waterloo owes HB II **8,000 Spanish Dollars @ 3/4% interest per month**.
  * Debt notes: (1) Dated 10 Aug. 1803 (2,000 Np.P.); (2) Dated 19 Jan. 1807 (6,000 Np.D.).
  * HB II insists Waterloo clear the debt prior to departing Yogyakarta; rejects Capitan Cina Tan Tjin Sing's offer of security.
  * Waterloo unable to pay; **Engelhard personally goes surety for Waterloo's debt**.
* **Departure Gifts Precedent:** Danureja II notes G.G. Wiese provided no farewell gifts upon departing Semarang, whereas G.G. Niberg presented: 1 piece coarse coat-cloth, 1 piece black velvet, 1 piece green cashmere, gold thread, red armozijnen, and 2 cannons.
* **Surakarta Army Reorganization (24 April 1808):**
  * PB IV appoints young **Pangeran Cakrakusuma as Army Commander-in-Chief**.
  * Cakrakusuma vigorously drills Surakarta troops, though Dutch observers question their combat effectiveness.
