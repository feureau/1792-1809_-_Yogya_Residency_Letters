# Transcription: IMG_20260618_0191_rotated_180.jpg

**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260618_0191_rotated_180.jpg`  
**Folio / Reference:** A.N. Yogya Letters No. 49  
**Date Range:** 22 June 1805  
**Primary Subjects:**  
- Flight of prominent Islamic scholar Ki Taptajani from Yogyakarta to Surakarta  
- Secret report to Waterloo by Outer Bupatis and Patih Danureja II  
- Grievances leading to Ki Taptajani's departure: confiscation of maintenance ricefields by Danureja II; Yogya Pengulu forbids him from meeting Yogya princes  
- Biographical profile of Ki Taptajani: born in Yogya, resided at Mlangi (*Melangi*), master of Javanese and Islamic jurisprudence (*Fiqh*)  
- Ki Taptajani as former *guru* (teacher) of Danureja II  
- Patronage ties with Pangeran Buminata (Abuminata) of Surakarta, who employed Taptajani's young sons  
- Sociological observation on young courtiers leaving Yogya for Sala becoming permanently alienated from their place of birth

### [22 June 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
22 June 1805  
Waterloo -> [crossed out: G.G. Asiese] Engelhard in Semarang  
(+ Notes of Engelhard in the margin.)

OUTER Bupatis / PATIH ->  
tell Waterloo in secret Priest named Ki TAPTAJANI leaves Yogya area for Sala.  
If Taptajani does not return HB II would ask questions of VOC.

*[Underlined in red:]*  
Taptajani leaves Yogya area:  
(1) because ricefields given to him for his upkeep by PATIH DR II taken away from him.  
(2) Ki Pengulu of Yogya refuses Ki Taptajani to meet any of the Yogya Princes.  
.: COMPLETELY ROBBED of means of LIVELIHOOD.

TAPTAJANI = born an Inhabitant of Yogya area.  
Lived in MELANGI.  
was held in High Regard by Nobles of Yogya COURT. Learnt to speak Javanese.  
was the guru of the present PATIH DR II.  
Extremely Learned in Islamic Law.

manages to get positions for his sons when they were still very young with *[underlined in red:]* **ABUMINATA**.

*[Left red vertical margin line; quoted text underlined with red ink:]*
