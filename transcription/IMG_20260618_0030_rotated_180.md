## Content

### Archive Entries: April – July 1792 (Gifts, Envoys, and Biography of HB I)

* Reference: **Port 3 No. 1. NvB. 1792 (Yogya)**
* **15 April 1792:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Reede: Reports on robberies in Mawi area.
* **27 April 1792:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten: Returning Regents Aria Mandura & R.T. Suraduningrat of Semarang. Presents given to HB II: a carriage, 150 cabines, 4 Danko'ter (9 grapefruit trees).
* **4 June 1792:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten: Expression of gratitude for Governor's role in HB II's succession and maintaining peace. Letters received from Van Overstraten and Council of the Indies.
* **18 June 1792:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Reede: Envoy Gardek Jiwanaga sent to Solo; presents sent for 7th-month pregnancy of Ratu Kencana.
* **21 June 1792:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten: **Haji Abdul Wahit returns from Mecca.** Brought letter from widow of Pangeran Surya Adikusuma in Ceylon to DRI (Haji Abdul Wahit visited Ceylon on return journey).
* **14 July 1792:** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Reede: Ng. Prawirasari, Ng. Mangkuwijaya sent. Letter from HB II to PB IV containing official biography of late Sultan Hamengkubuwana I (*levensbeschrijving van wijlen S.H. vader*). Red ink: **B. Mangkubumi?**
