### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.41: Departure of Daendels to Batavia & Van Braam Audiences (Oct. 1808)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.41 (9 October 1808)**
* **Daendels' Sudden Departure:**
  * Yogyakarta embassy arrives in Semarang on 25 September 1808 to present homage and gold tributes, but discovers Marshal Daendels had already departed for Batavia.
  * Daendels received the royal gold tributes prior to embarking, but returned to Batavia before the envoys' arrival.
* **Interim Response by Van Braam:**
  * Acting Resident J.A. van Braam replies to HB II on behalf of Daendels, confirming receipt of the gold regalia and birds' nests.
  * Reassures HB II of Daendels' personal affection, promising the Governor-General will write a formal letter of thanks upon his return.
