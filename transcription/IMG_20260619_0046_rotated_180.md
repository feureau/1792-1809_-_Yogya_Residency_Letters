### [Gold Farm Regulations (Cont.) & Tan Jin Sing's Mataram Tollgate Revenue Register (1 May 1808)]

If Bupatis / + others coming to Yogya for Garêbêg  
i.e. to pay Taxes / But have no negotiable goods,  
then can pass Tollgates without charge.  
*But Pikuls should be allowed to be examined by Bandhar.*  

<u>Difference Negotiable / Non-Negotiable Quantities:</u>  
i.e. small amount of cloth / Tobacco as presents for Lords:  
<u>no Toll to be levied.</u>  
<u>If Toll attempted to be levied unjustly = Bandhar would be punished.</u>  

* Every Garêbêg = **Reksanêgara to produce as many prajurits for parade as an Outer Regent.**  
* *If RN does not pay Tax on time would be dismissed.*
