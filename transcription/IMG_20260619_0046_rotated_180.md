### Gold Farm Terms (Cont.) & Tan Tjin Sing 1808 Revenue Report for Mataram Tollgates

* **Tollgate Rules (Cont.):**
  * Attendees travelling to Yogyakarta for *Garebeg* festivals carrying non-merchandise goods allowed free passage through tollgates (subject to inspection by *Bandhar* officers).
  * Small personal quantities of cloth or tobacco intended as presents for appanage lords exempt from tolls; unjust toll extortion penalized.
  * Reksanegara required to produce a contingent of armed *prajurits* for *Garebeg* parades equivalent to an Outer Bupati. Failure to pay tax results in dismissal.
* **Tan Tjin Sing Report on Mataram Tollgates (1 May 1808):**
  * Revenue breakdown of Mataram tollgates leased by Capitan Cina Tan Tjin Sing to pay HB II:
    * Warak, Bligo, Relaten, Norolang, Kamlaka (plus subordinate pasars/rangkahs): 7,000
    * Bantul: 4,800
    * Gamping: 2,800
    * Kadilangu: 2,000
    * Brosot: 900
    * Kalasan: 1,350
    * Jeru: 200
    * Wates: 2,900
    * Kritek: 1,500
    * **Mataram Tollgate Total:** **23,450 Ronde Reals**.
