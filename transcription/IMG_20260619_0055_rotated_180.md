### PB IV Fratricide, Poisoning Attempts, & Forced Tax Levies (1807–1808)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.86 (1806–1808)**
* **Murder of R.A. Numa:**
  * PB IV feigns pardoning his 16-year-old sister R.A. Numa, then attempts to poison her 7 times (failing due to her strong constitution).
  * **PB IV enters her bedroom at night and strangles her with his own hands.** Disguises his barbarism under a veneer of polite friendliness.
* **Attempted Poisoning of Resident Van Nijvenheim:** PB IV attempts to poison Resident Van Nijvenheim (1796–1803) by personally administering poisoned *jamu* herbal medicine.
* **Execution of Cakra-winata:** Arrested after being entertained by PB IV; wife and children ordered to wear white mourning clothes. Held in prison until 1:30 p.m., then driven toward Lodhaya and **murdered on the road**; all property and money chests confiscated.
* **Downfall & Murder of Patih Mangkupraja:**
  * Mangkupraja (Surakarta's only competent Patih) dismissed and murdered due to PB IV's suspicion of his links to Prangwedana.
  * Banished to Banyumas and **murdered by Yudanegara** alongside his wife and children.
* **100,000 Np.D. Forced Tax (1807–1808):** PB IV levies an arbitrary forced tax of 100,000 Np.D. on his subjects to clear personal debts; retains most funds for himself while creditors receive almost nothing.
