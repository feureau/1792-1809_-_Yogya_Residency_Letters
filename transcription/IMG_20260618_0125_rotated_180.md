### [May 1793] — Piagem HB II to Rēksanēgara (1 Sawal AJ 1719 / May 1793)

* Scope of lease / tax farm:
  * Tollgates, Rangkas, *Gêdhong Têmbakau* (tobacco warehouses).
  * Buffalo slaughter, Salt making N. coast, Bird's Nests.
* 20 new Pasars / Tollgates / Rangkas / Salarans can be set up.
* Bird's Nest Harvest: 3 x p.a.
* <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">46,000 Ronde Reals (23,000 at Mulud / 23,000 at Puasa).</ins>
* Markets exempt / specified:
  * Rejakatawang, Babadan, Rejawinangun, Rejakusuma, Têgalsari: 7 pasars in Yogya to be set up with no tax.
  * \+ 20 Rangkas / Salarans / Pasars / Tollgates in Yogya.
  * Free Pasars / Bandars / Birds Nests in MN not included.
* Exemptions:
  * Any subject of HB II on mission for Him / i.e. any official of HB II not to be taxed at tollgates (except if trading).
  * \+ no tax on villagers coming to Court + presents for Garebegs (Mulud / Puasa), except negotiable goods.
  * Pikulans can be inspected; if negotiable goods found, can be taxed.
* 6,000 per ½ year Tax for Bird's Nests.
* <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">77 of HB II's Drag oxen = to be allowed free passage to Semarang 3 x p.a. for purposes of Trade.</ins>
* 5 Mantris to be appointed as subordinates of Rêksanêgara.
* Some prajurits to be produced each year by Rêksanêgara at court.
* *[Left margin annotation in red ink:]* **see also pranpêring-liput Sala R. 18 Apr. 1805, Dj. Br. 49**

### [8 Mar 1799] — Piagem HB II to Rēksanēgara (1 Sawal AJ 1725 / 8 Mar. 1799)

* Piagem for 3 years — but would be withdrawn by HB II if tax not paid in time / lapse.
* Terms as above.
* <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">56,000 in silver money p.a. (1 Ronde Real = 28 dubbeltjes / f. 2.80).</ins>
* Pasars of HB II: add Rejakatawang, Purwareja, Wanacatur, Pakuncèn; also outside: Pasar Perdikan (Gatak Pesantrèn).
* Birds nests: 1,100 in silver ½ year.
* \+ various presents: *Pênompon*. *(see Back of p.)*
