### Charters (*Piagems*) for Reksanegara (1807–1811)

* Reference: **P.J. Br. 25 (1810–1811)**
* **HB II – Reksanegara Grant (1 Nawal 1719 / May 1793):**
  * Tollgates, *Rangkas*, *Gedhong Tembakau*, buffalo slaughterhouses, salt-making on North Coast, and bird's nest revenues.
  * **Revenue Targets:**
    * 1807: 60,000 Rixdollars.
    * 1810: 62,000 Rixdollars.
    * 1810 / 1811: 62,600 Rixdollars.
  * 20 new *Pasars* / Tollgates / *Rangkas* allowed to be set up.
  * Bird's nest harvest (3x p.a.): 46,000 Rixdollars (23,000 at Mulud and Puasa).
* **Tax Exemptions & Markets:**
  * Markets at Rija, Katawang, Babadan, Ryawihangun, Ryatawarna, Tegal Sani, and Pasars in Yogya defined.
  * Free Pasars, Bandhars, and Bird's nests in Mangkunagaran excluded.
  * HB II's official envoys exempt from tollgate taxes (unless engaged in trade).
  * No taxes on villagers bringing court presents for Mulud/Puasa (except commercial goods). *Pikuls* inspected for taxable goods.
  * Bird's nest tax fixed at 6,000 per 1/2 year.
* **Transit Rights:** HB II's drag oxen allowed free passage to Semarang 3x p.a. for trade purposes. 5 *Mantris* appointed as subordinates to Reksanegara.
* **3-Year Grant Renewal (8 Mar. 1799 / 1 Nawal 1725):**
  * Fixed at 56,000 in silver money p.a. (1 Rixdollar = 28 *dubbeltjes* / 2.80 guilders).
  * Markets at Rija, Katawang, Purwarija, Wanacatur, and Pakuncen included (*Pasar Pardikan / Gatak Pisantren*).
  * Bird's nest revenue fixed at 1,100 per 1/2 year + various presents (*Pinompon*).
