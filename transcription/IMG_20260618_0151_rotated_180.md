### Archive Entries: October – November 1803 (Teak Requisitions & Royal Fictions)

* Reference: **A.N. Yogya No. 49 (1803)**
* **20 October 1803:** Patih Danureja II issues circular letter on security across the *Vorstenlanden* following conferences between Patihs Mangkupraja (Solo) and Danureja II (Yogya).
* **20 November 1803 (Engelhard -> Waterloo):** Regents of Blora and surrounding timber districts prohibit sales of teak. Engelhard insists teak sales must continue on old contract basis given impending war with the British.
* **23 November 1803 (Waterloo -> Engelhard):** Official remonstrances sent to HB II regarding necessity of keeping teak trade open.
* **9 November 1803 (Waterloo -> Engelhard):**
  * Waterloo's mother-in-law visits Kraton. Waterloo meets HB II in an *"extremely good mood"*.
  * Sultan pleased because Waterloo quickly constructed and painted a pleasure boat for royal outings on the *Pondo* at **Taman Sari**.
  * HB II's English horse remains his favorite mount (decked in elaborate trappings; preferred over Bimanese horses).
* **10 November 1803:** News received of European peace treaty signing.
* **16 November 1803:** Waterloo sends Engelhard 2 live mouse-deer (*kandjils*) caught during a hunting trip in Gunung Kidul.
