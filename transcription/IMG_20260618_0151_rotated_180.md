## Archival & Historical Notes

- **Vorstenlanden Security Protocols (20 October 1803):** Resident Waterloo addresses a circular letter to Patih Danureja II regarding overarching security throughout the Principalities (*Vorstenlanden*), regulating discussions and intelligence sharing between the Prime Ministers of Surakarta (Raden Adipati Mangkupraja) and Yogyakarta (Danureja II).
- **Strategic Teak Crisis & Napoleonic War Preparations (20–23 November 1803):**
  - Governor Nicolaas Engelhard alerts Waterloo that the Regents of Blora and adjacent timber-rich districts have unilaterally halted the sale of teak timber.
  - Teak supplies are of critical naval and fortification importance in anticipation of resumed war with the British.
  - Waterloo is instructed to remonstrate vigorously with Sultan Hamengkubuwana II to ensure timber deliveries continue strictly according to the terms of earlier treaties.
- **Residency-Kraton Social Diplomacy (9 November 1803):**
  - Resident Waterloo's mother-in-law pays a formal social visit to the Kraton to meet the royal consorts (*Ratus*).
  - Waterloo finds HB II in an exceptionally buoyant mood because Waterloo had expediently arranged for a leisure boat to be constructed, painted, and decked out with decorative flags for the Sultan's personal use on the lake/pools at **Taman Sari**.
  - The Sultan expresses immense fondness for the prized English horse presented to him by the Dutch, prizing it well above his Bimanese mounts.
- **Diplomatic Curiosities (10–16 November 1803):**
  - News of the European peace treaty (Treaty of Amiens) circulating at court.
  - Waterloo sends Governor Engelhard a diplomatic gift of two live mouse-deer (*kancil*) captured during an aristocratic hunting expedition in the rugged hills of Gunung Kidul.
