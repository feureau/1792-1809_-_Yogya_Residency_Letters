### Register of Yogyakarta Royal Marriages (1801–1807)

* Reference: **Waterloo to Engelhard (5 Sept. 1807)**

#### Record of Marriages Contracted in Yogyakarta Court:
* **27 Aug. 1803:** Pangeran Mangkuningrat married daughter of Mas Ng. Nitipura.
* **2 Sept. 1803:** Pangeran Natabaya married granddaughter of R.T. Natayuda (daughter of Mas Rara Nangedoh).
* **2 Sept. 1803:** Pangeran Nilarong married daughter of Mangkunagaran Regent Mangundiraza.
* **2 Sept. 1803:** Raden Ajeng Modinah (daughter of HB II) married son of Mas Ng. R. Cakrakusuma.
* **2 Sept. 1803:** Raden Ajeng Nupiah (daughter of HB II) married R. Panji Jayingrana (son of P. Adinegara).
* **26 Mar. 1804:** Pangeran Narakusuma married daughter of R.T. Pringgadiwiria of Loano.
* **26 Mar. 1804:** Raden Ajeng Ragil (younger sister of HB II) married R.T. Purwadipura.
* **30 Mar. 1804:** Pangeran Mangkubumi married daughter of Regent of Padangan (2nd wife).
* **4 Sept. 1805:** Crown Prince married daughter of Regent of Wirasari (3rd wife).
* **7 Sept. 1805:** Ratu Anom married R.T. Natadiningrat.
* **7 Sept. 1805:** Pangeran Jayakusuma (later P. Ngabehi) married daughter of P. Ngabehi.
* **7 Sept. 1805:** Raden Ajeng Mariem (daughter of HB II) married son of R.T. Manghadipura.
* **13 Oct. 1806:** Pangeran Nartawijaya (son of HB II) married daughter of R.T. Purwadipura.
* **13 Oct. 1806:** Raden Ajeng Ngarenah (daughter of HB II) married son of P. Adinegara.
* **13 Oct. 1806:** Raden Ajeng Nurdinah (daughter of HB II) married son of R.T. Nasurekusuma.
* **13 Oct. 1806:** Raden Ajeng Natipah (daughter of HB II) married son of Raden Adipati Purwadiningrat.
* **27 Aug. 1807:** Pangeran Mangkudiningrat married daughter of R.Ay. Nerang (4th marriage).
* **25 Feb. 1807:** Crown Prince's children: Antawiria married R.Aj. Nupadmi; R.Aj. Murtinah married R.M. Nahidin (son of Danukusuma).
