### Archive Entries: December 1795 – January 1796 & Prangwedana Protocol (*Nota van Schikking*)

* Reference: **Solo Br. 131 / AN N.O. Br. No. 53v**
* **25 December 1795 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Reede):**
  * Son of Raden Rongga Prawiradirja II marries Raden Ajeng Natijah, daughter of HB II.
  * Pangeran Dipasana marries sister of Raden Rongga Prawiradirja II.
* **26 December 1795:** Van IJsseldijk informs HB II of British ships in Banyuwangi Bay.
* **14 January 1796:** Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III appointed Bupati Wedana due to father's eye affliction.
* **Nota van Schikking (17 Dec. 1795 / 5 Jumadilawal Dimakir 1722 - PB IV & Van Overstraten):**
  * Protocol governing Pangeran Prangwedana's court appearances in Surakarta:
    * **Saturdays:** Appears on Alun-alun in tournament dress using grandfather's small *Paseban*.
    * **Mondays & Thursdays:** Appears at *Paseban* alongside Pangeran Mangkubumi and Pangeran Buminata to greet Sunan Pakubuwana IV.
    * **Feast Days:** Accompanies Governor and dignitaries; granted equal seating status (*stool*) with Mangkubumi and Buminata; wears *wedung* dagger.
    * **Exemptions:** Exempted from *Jayasturun* feudal taxes and corvée.
    * **Contributions (*Numbang*):** Required to contribute on royal marriages, Governor-General visits, Kraton repairs (*Prabayeksa*), and *Masjid Agung* maintenance. Pays 50–60 Rixdollars twice yearly (Mulud) to be exempted from *Gladag* services.
