Winter, pp. 32–31 on MN – PB Relations; signed Van Reede).*<br><br>1. **Tournaments & Pasebaan Attendance:**  <br>   - On Saturdays, Pangeran Prangwedana to appear in usual court clothes on Alun-alun to take part in Tournament (*senenan*).  <br>   - To use same small *Pasebaan* as grandfather [Mangkunegara I]; could erect another in same place if necessary.  <br>   - Also on Mondays and Thursdays to appear at *Pasebaan* to stand in same place as Pangeran Mangkubumi and Pangeran Buminata to greet PB IV when he comes out of Kraton.  <br>   - *(Note: After PB IV kept PWD [Prangwedana / Mangkunagara II] waiting, PB IV agreed to send messengers to announce whether he would appear or not on that day; if not, PWD could go home).*  <br>   - On all Feast Days, PWD to appear on *Pasebaan*, as well as when Governor-General or other dignitaries visit Court.<br><br>2. **Precedence & Court Honours:**  <br>   - PWD to take precedence immediately after Pangeran Mangkubumi and Pangeran Buminata.  <br>   - If a stool is given to these two princes, a stool is also given to PWD.  <br>   - Permitted to carry *wedung* at his side.  <br>   - If PB IV hosts a *Bêdhaya* performance in Kraton, PWD to attend along with princes and Regents.<br><br>3. **Taxes, Corvée & Financial Contributions (*Numbang*):**  <br>   - Excused from *Pajeg/Têkes-turun* taxes and corvée services to the Sunan.  <br>   - Expected to make financial contributions (*numbang*) only on the following occasions:  <br>     1. Marriage of the Sunan, or of one of his children or brothers.  <br>     2. When the Governor-General visits the Court.  <br>     3. If the *Prabayeksa* (private apartments of the Sunan) is repaired or rebuilt.  <br>     4. If the *Mesjid Ageng* (Grand Mosque) is repaired or rebuilt.  <br>     5. If the Dutch Residency (*Loji*) is repaired or rebuilt.  <br>     *(Like others, to give 2 buffaloes per annum to Buminata).*<br><br>- Twice per annum (*Garebeg Puasa* and *Maulud*) to pay 50–60 Ronde Reals to be free of *Gladhag* (transport/porter) services.<br><br>4. **Jurisdiction & Authority:**  <br>   - To pay same honours to the Sunan as Buminata and Mangkubumi, and conduct himself like those princes.  <br>   - Only Sunan and Resident could give PWD orders; Sunan's orders not to exceed the usual honours enumerated above. |
