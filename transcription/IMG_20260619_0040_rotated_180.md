### Archive Entries: December 1807 – May 1808 (Gresik Naval Raid, Klaten Earthquake, & Chinese Kongsi Raid)

* **17 December 1807 (British Attack on Gresik):**
  * Rumors of impending British attack on Gresik to destroy remaining ships of Vice-Admiral Hartsinck's squadron wintering in the *Oosthoek* and sever Solo River communications.
  * 24 December: British squadron withdraws from Javanese waters.
* **16 Dulkangidah 1734 / 25 January 1808:** Death of PB IV's aunt (daughter of P. Mangkupraja of Demak) from pneumonia / T.B.
* **1 March 1808 (Severe Earthquake Damage at Klaten Fort):**
  * **28 February 1808 (Early Morning): Severe earthquake causes extensive structural damage to Klaten Fort.**
  * Reported by Lt. W. Schraag and Lt. Petele to Resident Waterloo.
* **3 March 1808 (Cakrakusuma Head of Surakarta Army):**
  * **PB IV appoints his favorite brother-in-law Cakrakusuma as Prince and Commander-in-Chief of Surakarta troops.**
  * Son of recently deceased Tumenggung of Ponorogo (Wiriadiningrat) succeeds his father.
* **4 April 1808:** J.A. van Braam succeeds Von Liebeherr as Acting Resident of Surakarta.
* **31 May 1808 (Chinese Kongsi Raid at Bander, Surabaya):**
  * Statement of Radawijaya: 250 armed men with pikes, clubs, and torches attack Bander village on 25 May.
  * **Plunder and burn Chinese houses and the Chinese *Kongsi* (tax office).**
  * *Stolen Loot:* 200 Np.P., 13 pieces of European white cloth, 5 Surat garments, 1 gold stomach plate, ginger, benzoin, 4 bars of iron, 2 copper pots, 5 packets of cotton (*kapas*), and 1 betel box. Conspirators retreat into Kertawana forest.
