### Main Text & Margin Notes

| Margin Notes | Main Text |
| :--- | :--- |
| **20 February 1796** | ### Text & Marginal Annotations<br><br>[Top left / marginal notes:]<br><br>- Succession / Regency of Kaliwungu:<br>  - Sons of Sumadiwirya exiled to Ceylon in 1794: Mas Wiriarijaya (28 yrs old) & Mas Sumawijaya (21 yrs old).<br>  - Mas Wiriawijaya appointed as Regent (Bupati) of Kaliwungu with title **Mas Tumenggung Adinegara**.<br><br>[Main text - Red ink heading:]<br><br>**apptmt. of PWD [Prangwedana / Mangkunagara II].**<br><br>**20 Feb. 1796: Van Ov. to Alting**<br><br>PWD’s health improves.<br><br>Three difficulties arise over the succession / investiture:<br><br>1. **Choosing a suitable name:**<br>   - PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] refuses to allow him to succeed to his grandfather’s name (*Mangkunegara*).<br>   - PB IV suggests four alternative names, but all are considered too low in rank/stature.<br>   - PWD then suggests: *Aria Prabu Amangkurat* or *Aria Prabu Mataram*.<br>   - *Amangkurat* is unsuitable for both Surakarta and Yogyakarta; *Mataram* would deeply annoy Yogyakarta.<br>   - Governor Van Overstraten therefore himself suggests the name **Aria Prangwēdana** to avoid all arguments in Surakarta and to expedite the appointment.<br><br>2. **The 4,000 Cacahs:**<br>   - The three eldest illegitimate sons of MN I [Mangkunagara I] press for a division of the 4,000 cacahs among the family members.<br>   - Pangeran Mangkubumi had put them up to asking for lands.<br>   - The sons desist after the eldest apologizes to Resident Van Reede.<br><br>3. **Ceremonial Swearing-in:**<br>   - PB IV makes difficulties about the ceremonial swearing-in of PWD; it eventually takes place in the Dutch Fort under a durian tree.<br>   - PWD does not want to take an oath of allegiance to PB IV, but is forced to do so (cf. MN I had previously taken an oath twice to PB III [Sunan Pakubuwana III]).<br>   - Present at the swearing of the oath:<br>     - Pangeran Ngabèhi<br>     - Sala Patih [Raden Adipati Mangkuprojo]<br>     - Tumenggung Nastrawijaya |
