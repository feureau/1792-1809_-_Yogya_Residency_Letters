  * **Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus)** (`PB IV / Sunan`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 18). Sunan of Surakarta (reigned 1788–1820), prominent rival to HB II.
  * **Sunan Pakubuwana VI (Radèn Mas Saperdan)** (`PB VI`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 20). Sunan of Surakarta (reigned 1823–1830), exiled to Ambon in 1830.
  * **Radèn Mas Said (Mangkunagara I / Prince Mangkunegara)** (`MN I`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 26). Legendary founder of the Mangkunagaran Principality (reigned 1757–1795).
  * **Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II)** (`MN II / PWD`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 27). Ruler of the Mangkunagaran (reigned 1796–1835), creator of the Mangkunagaran Legion.
  * **Pangéran Natakusuma (Pakualam I)** (`PA / PA I`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 23). Son of HB I, created first independent ruler of the Pakualaman (reigned 1812–1829).
  * **Ratu Kencana Wulan (Ratu Wétan)** (`Ratu Kencana Wulan`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 31). Influential favorite consort of HB II.
