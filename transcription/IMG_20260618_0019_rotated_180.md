### Historical Survey of Solo–Yogya Marriage Proposals & Balance of Power

- **1770s (18th c.)**: V.O.C. prevents marriage between HB II and daughter of Sunan PB III (2nd); claims of Yogya on Solo throne dashed.
- **1790**: PB IV asked a daughter of CP (HB II) in marriage (1790), but refusal of V.O.C.
  - *Pakepung Incident*: Greeve's idea of replacing PB IV with MN I. Peace restored $\rightarrow$ MN / Yogya foreswear old hatred.
- **1798**: PB IV wants to marry unofficial sister to CP of Yogya; Nederburgh / V.O.C. to allow it.
- **1823–1830**: Proposal: P. Ria (grandson of MN II, 47 years old) marries only official sister of PB VI (PB VII 54 years old).
- **Structural Position of MN vs PA**:
  > *MN set up as a balance for Yogya, not against Sunan.*  
  > *PA = Independent from Sultan + Sunan (1813).*  
  > *Different from MN who was Independent of Sultan, but NOT of Sunan — Pangeran Pinisepuh (see Rouffaer V.I. p. 604); P. Muji for Sunan / P. Mardika for V.O.C.*  

- **Recent Dynastic Marriages**:
  1. Recently Resident Van Nes proposed marriage: Crown Prince of Yogya $\rightarrow$ daughter of P. Ngabehi (Solo); goes through. Sunan is therefore now uncle of HB V (son of PB VII / Ratu Kencana).
  2. MN III – R. Nikar marriage.
- **Strategic Implications**:
  - Both go against old political system of divide and rule.
  - Anticipated consolidation / blending of Solo–Yogya Royal houses.
  - Break with the past $\rightarrow$ Should Governor-General Rochussen seek advice of Minister of Colonies?
