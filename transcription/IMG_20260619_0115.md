# Transcription of IMG_20260619_0115.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260619_0115.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (J. A. van der Chijs, *Nederlandsch-Indisch Plakaatboek, 1602–1811*, Deel XV [1808–1809], pp. 64–65)
- **Date Range:** 1808-07-28 to 1808-07-29
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Conclusion and core provisions of Governor-General Marshal Herman Willem Daendels' landmark 28 July 1808 Royal Court Ceremonial Regulation (*Reglement voor het ceremonieel, in acht te nemen door de residenten bij de hoven te Soerakarta en te Djokjokarta*) spanning pp. 64–65 of Van der Chijs' *Plakaatboek* (Deel XV); specifies the radical diplomatic protocol mandating that rulers rise when the Resident approaches, seating the Resident on a par with the rulers at their left hand, banning Residents from serving wine or sirih to rulers, requiring grand viziers (*rijksbestuurder* / patih) and regents to pay homage to the Resident, ordering military carriage escorts with 12 dragoons, and commanding that royal letters be conveyed in advance in a separate carriage; documents the profound historical note recording that while Sunan Pakubuwana IV yielded, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II openly resisted these demands; concludes with the 29 July 1808 decree authorizing 20,000 rijksdaalders in credit paper currency for Banda.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus), Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Prof. Peter Carey.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Djokjokarta / Craton), Surakarta (Soerakarta), Banda, Batavia.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Reglement voor het ceremonieel (1808), Kraton protocol, Craton audience, Dragonders (cavalry escort), Rijksbestuurder (Patih), Papieren van credit (credit currency), Plakaatboek.
