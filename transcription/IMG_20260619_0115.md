### Archive Entries: July 1808 (Madura Elevation Reactions & Fort Lodewijk Timber Requisitions)

* **22 July 1808 (Reactions to Madura Elevation):**
  * HB II and PB IV feel humiliated by the elevation of Pangeran Cakraningrat to Sultan Nepuh of Madura.
  * Daendels reassures HB II that both courts are treated objectively.
  * Orders issued to Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III to permit timber felling in Eastern Mancanagara.
* **Fort Lodewijk Requisitions:**
  * Daendels requests permission from HB II to cut teak in Eastern Mancanagara for 6 months to construct pillars for **Fort Lodewijk in Surabaya**.
* **Opium Supply (25 July 1808):**
  * 5 chests of opium arrive in Yogya from Semarang (retailing @ 20 Np.D. Per catty).
  * Capitan Cina Tan Tjin Sing given the opium on credit (@ 2,300 Sp.D. per chest, vs. 1,800 in Semarang's Kampong Cina).
