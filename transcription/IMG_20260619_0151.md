### Archive Entries: December 1808 (Rembang Teak Audits, Bantam Uprising, & Timber Thefts)

* **11 December 1808 (Rembang Teak Audits):** Minister Wiese opens correspondence with Prefect Eckenholm of Rembang regarding unfulfilled teak timber orders (*Daendels 27 May decree*). Civil commission dispatched to survey Rembang Regencies for teak productivity.
* **12 December 1808 (Surgeon Salary Revision):** Payment roll revision requested for 2nd Class Surgeon G.W. Sentius (salary increased from Rds. 50 to Rds. 100 per month).
* **14 December 1808 (Bantam Uprising Intelligence):**
  * Official disclosure delivered to HB II regarding the uprising in Bantam (*Banten*) and the **banishment of the Sultan of Bantam**.
* **16 December 1808 (Rembang Timber & Buffalo Theft):**
  * Investigation opened into the theft of 48 buffaloes by Yogyakarta forest workers (*blandhongs*) in the Rembang forest district.
  * Written explanation demanding restitution submitted to HB II.
