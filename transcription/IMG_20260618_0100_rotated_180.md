### Archive Entries: 1801–1806 (Banditry, Cirebon Security, and Tan Tjin Sing)

* **Eastern Banditry (1728 / Regeb):** DRI orders Raden Rangga to cooperate with Kyai Adipati Purwadiningrat of Magetan to capture robbers. Captured robbers sent to Yogya (killed on spot if they resist). If bandits flee into Surakarta territory, Rangga to coordinate with Raden Adipati Mangkupraja.
* **Cirebon Border Security (2 March 1806 - Waterloo to Engelhard):**
  * Resident confers with HB II on border security along Preanger, Cirebon, Banyumas, and Ledok. HB II instructed to order Western Mancanagara subjects on *Garebeg Besar* to capture fugitives from Cirebon.
  * HB II questions why V.O.C. cannot drive bandits out of Cirebon itself. V.O.C. reassures HB II that troops are reserved for potential conflict with the British, and promises reinforcements from Batavia to Cirebon. HB II issues orders via the Crown Prince.
* **Textile Trade & Tan Tjin Sing (9 May 1803):**
  * Head Bandhar **Tan Tjin Sing** reports difficulty purchasing required volume of long cloths for Batavia, as local weavers prefer short cloth.
  * Price per corge raised from 16.32 to 18.32 Rixdollars to compensate weavers.
  * **3 August 1803:** **Tan Tjin Sing appointed Capitan Cina of Kedu**, succeeding Nie Longie (dismissed).
  * **3 July 1803:** 10 pikuls cotton thread and 6 corges flag material supplied for cruiser squadron under Vice-Admiral Wiese in Semarang.
