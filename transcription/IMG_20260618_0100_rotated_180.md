### [2 Mar 1806]

* **Waterloo to N. Engelhard, 2 Mar. 1806.**
  * *[Left margin annotation in red ink:]* **Cirebon / Bagus Rangin (1806) / see BLADD 12341 f. 125r.**
  * Talks w to HB II about security on Borders + Preanger / Cirebon.
  * HB II to give orders to W. MN subjects on occasion of Garebeg Bēsar to keep watch on Boundaries: Rêma / Banyumas / Lēdok for Fugitives from Cirebon.
  * HB II asks why couldn't V.O.C. drive out Bandits from C. and if Dutch no longer in Cirebon, ? not powerful enough to drive out Bandits.
  * Then HB II says: "Perhaps V.O.C. doesn't lack strength, But keeping it to decide more important issues + British."
  * HB II then promises to give necessary orders via Crown Prince.
  * In order to Reassure HB II of power of V.O.C., tells of Reinforcements from Batavia -> Cirebon.

### [9 May 1803]

* **[vanden Berg crossed out] Waterloo to Raad van Politie (Semarang), 9 May. 1803**
  * Head Bandhar (Tan Jin Sing).
  * <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Difficulty Buying up quantity of long clothes demanded by Batavia.</ins>
  * *[Double red vertical line in left margin]*
  * <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Ordinary people no longer like to weave long cloth because not in demand from Local Traders.</ins>
  * Raising also of price per Corge from R. R. 16.32 -> 18.32 per corge.
  * used to pay weavers higher prices then given by V.O.C. (cf letters 7 Oct. 1798 / 5 Apr. 1799).
  * Otherwise Refusal of weavers to make that sort of cloth for V.O.C.
