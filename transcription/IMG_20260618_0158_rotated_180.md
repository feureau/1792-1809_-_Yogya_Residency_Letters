### AvJ Archive Entries: 1804 (Tuban Robbery & Alienation of Pusakas)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1804**
* **12 February 1804 (Inter-Court Disputes Resolved):** Longstanding boundary disputes over Secang, Pakidulan, Pasampakkan, Gintan, Parimban, and Lobang, plus the Delanggu incident involving Prangwedana III, resolved by formal treaty between Solo and Yogya Patihs.
* **5 March 1804 (Tuban Robbery Assistance):** Major robbery in Tuban; European sergeants dispatched from Rembang, Yogya, Solo, and Surabaya. Assistance provided by R.T. Natawijaya (Panolan), Mas T. Sumawigana (Kapadangan), R.T. Sasradiningrat (Rajegwesi), R.T. Prawirasentika (Banwerna), R.T. Natadiwiria, Mas T. Sumadipura (Japan), and R.T. Jayakusuma (Kertasara).
* **22 May 1804:** *Garebeg Besar* celebrated.
* **26 May 1804 (Pusaka Alienation Restrictions):**
  * Inquiry into pawning/sale/alienation of *pusaka* heirlooms belonging to Sultan's family.
  * Yogya Regents possess only official ornaments granted by HB II for their rank.
  * Tri-weekly state ceremony meetings enforced. Sultan HB II affirms he will never alienate any royal *pusakas*.
