Rangga Jajar Aftermath & Royal Pensions<br><br>- Exile of **Pangeran Brayakusuma** to Ceylon in 1788 (part of the Rangga Jajar affair).  <br>- Two brothers:  <br>  - **Rangga Kusuma**  <br>  - **Narawijaya** (*Nèrang*)  <br>  Both receive a small pension from PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV].  <br>  *(Narawijaya was killed during a battle between HB I [Sultan Hamengkubuwana I] and MN I [Mangkunagara I] in 1750)*.<br><br>#### 2. Rice producing area.**  <br>- **Ulujami:** Rice-producing district, most densely populated of all Western Pasisir districts.  <br>- Earlier rented to the **Capitan Cina of Semarang**; then leased to the **Ratu** for 5,000 rijksdaalders p.a.; subsequently subleased by her son **Tan To Ok** [Jan To Ok].  <br>- Yield: **300 koyangs of rice p.a.** at 15 r.r. per koyang.  <br>- Capitan Cina treats local population very well: relieves them of onerous corvée labour.  <br>- Land is extremely fertile, though subject to frequent river floods (*banjir*).  <br>- Capitan Cina suffers from shortage of seed-rice for sowing.  <br>- $\therefore$ Highly advantageous for the V.O.C. to continue leasing the land to a Chinese entrepreneur.  <br>- Sharp contrast noted between the prosperity of **Ulujami** and **Wiradesa**.<br><br>#### 3. Pekalongan & Coastal Administration<br><br>- **Pekalongan:** Very suitable for rice and indigo cultivation, though also vulnerable to periodic flooding (*banjir*).  <br>- **Raden Adipati Jayadiningrat** criticized as too attached to private tolls / leasing practices (*paozen / pasisir*). |
