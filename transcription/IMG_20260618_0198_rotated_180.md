## Complete Diplomatic Transcription

JOURNEY to be UNDERTAKEN a THIRD TIME to appease Ratu Kidul.  
HB II very attentive to Waterloo (who accompanies him to S. Sea).  
sends him a large section of CATCHING FISH.

### [3 November 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
3 Nov 1805  
Wat – Eng

AFTER TOURNAMENT ON ALON-ALON:  
HB II calls DR II and all Regents and Enquires INTO CAUSES FOR SLACKNESS of WORK ON FORTRESS AT KLATEN:  
WIRAGUNA (PATIH of CP) to produce all COMPLAINTS from Bupatis in WRITING -> HB II.  
CP to make STRICT INQUIRY into work at Klaten [whether large Quantities of wood appear at Klaten].

2nd Res DOMIS of Yogya placed as PAKHUISMEESTER at CHERIBON:  
2nd Res of SALA Van Meemert.

---

### [7 November 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
7 Nov 1805  
Wat – Eng

BRIDAL PARTY from KRATON for marriage of Waterloo's Daughter:  
DR II + KRATON NOBLES / CP who always comes with full ENTOURAGE.

*[Margin note with asterisk:]*  
Interesting CP never usually goes to BRIDAL parties – NOT EVEN to marriage of RATU ANOM to Natadiningrat.
