### Archive Entries: April – June 1796 (Signet Ring, Sayyid Alwi, & Patih Mangkupraja)

* Reference: **Van Ov. - Alting (Apr. - Jun. 1796) - N.O.K. 47**
* **23 April 1796:** Engraving of official signet ring for Surakarta Crown Prince.
* **21 April 1796:** *Procès-Verbaal* of **Sayyid Alwi as-Saqqaf** (born at Terim in Hadhramaut; *Inche / Encik*). Red ink: **Térim / Hadhramaut enclaves.**
* **21 May 1796:** Formal dismissal of Surakarta Patih Jayaningrat and appointment of **Raden Adipati Mangkupraja**. Jayaningrat dismissed due to heavy gambling and oppression of the common people.
* **Background of Mangkupraja (Sasrawijaya):**
  * Son of Sasradiningrat I (dismissed by PB III as Bupati of Kediri; later Outer Bupati). Married two sisters of PB IV (both deceased).
  * Accused during 1790 disturbances by Tumenggung of Kalangs; fell into disfavor with PB IV's *Mantri* advisers, but reinstated post-1790 due to firm V.O.C. loyalty.
  * Described as intelligent, humorous, soft-tempered, and uncovetous.
* **Diplomacy:** Van Overstraten successfully blocks PB IV's plan to appoint Pangeran Mangkubumi or P.A. Kusumadiningrat as Patih. PB IV corresponds directly with Van Overstraten in secret without knowledge of courtiers.
