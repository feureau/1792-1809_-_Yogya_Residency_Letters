- **21 May 1796 (Van Overstraten to Alting):**
  - **Dismissal of Sala Patih Jayaningrat; appointment of Raden Adipati Mangkupraja [Nastrawijaya]:**
    - Jayaningrat had lost all standing/authority (*"no face seen at Sala court"*); engaged in gambling and frequent oppressions of the common people.
    - **Nastrawijaya:** Son of Sasradiningrat I (who had been dismissed by PB III on the recommendations of Ratu Beruk).
    - First served as *Ap. kĕdhin* [Bupati Anom / Nayaka?], then Bupati of the inner court.
    - Had married successively two sisters of PB IV, both of whom had died.
    - During the 1790 Disturbances (*Pakepung*), he was demoted/abased to Tumenggung of the Kalangs because he fell into disfavour with PB IV’s *santri* advisers due to being considered too loyal to the V.O.C.
    - After 1790, he regained his former position in the court.
    - **Character assessment (red underline):** *Intelligent, shrewd Javanese, of a soft character & humour. Not greedy / covetous for money.*
    - **Diplomatic maneuver:** Van Overstraten successfully headed off PB IV’s plan to appoint Pangeran Mangkubumi or Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat as Patih.
    - PB IV corresponds with Van Overstraten directly without the knowledge of the courtiers in order to keep the negotiations strictly secret.
