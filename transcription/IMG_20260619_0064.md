### Ambtgeld Allowances & Mecca Pilgrims Transit Regulations (1793–1805)

* **7 September 1793 Ambtgeld Provision (Vol. XI pp. 566–568):**
  * Chief officers (*Opperhoofden*) required to supply wine and European luxury goods for Surakarta and Yogyakarta rulers (f. 1,000 fine linen, iron nails).
  * *Surakarta Allowance:* 84 cans wine, 60 lbs candles, 150 lbs Dutch butter, 12 cans olive oil, 24 cans vinegar.
  * *Yogyakarta Allowance:* 150 cans wine, 60 lbs candles, 24 cans olive oil, 45 cans Dutch vinegar.
  * Also applies to Fort Commandants in Yogyakarta and Surakarta.
* **17 September 1796 Resident Emoluments (Vol. XII p. 295–297):** Resident emoluments set at f. 1,000 Indiasch; 2nd Residents receive f. 1,644.
* **12 July 1805 Mecca Pilgrims Transit (Vol. XIV pp. 194–195):**
  * HB II's Mecca delegation (9 priests + 26 followers = 35 total) brought to Batavia at V.O.C. expense, or permitted to sail from Semarang directly to Sumatra/Riau on native vessels at own expense.
  * *Council of the Indies Order:* Council prefers HB II's pilgrims travel directly to Batavia under V.O.C. supervision rather than mixing with coastal trading communities on native vessels.
