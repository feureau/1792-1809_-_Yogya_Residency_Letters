- **Date Range:** 1809-07-29 to 1809-07-30
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official ceremonial state visit of Governor-General Marshal Herman Willem Daendels to Yogyakarta on 29–30 July 1809; met at Kalasan by the Crown Prince (later HB III) and at Bantulan by Sultan Hamengkubuwana II; received at the Rèksanagaran residence amidst full muster of inner and outer regents and Mangkunagaran bupatis; first solemn meeting in the Throne Room with Daendels seated directly on the right of HB II on the royal dais under 28-gun salutes; Fort Commandant Willem Driessen tenders the keys of Fort Rustenburg; Daendels inspects the fortifications accompanied by the Sultan and Crown Prince; following day protocol review at the Residency House; second meeting at the Srimenganti pavilion preceded by 28-gun artillery salutes and reviews across the northern alun-alun; Sultan Hamengkubuwana II embraces Daendels with tears in his eyes; HB II orders an extraordinary one-hour mock tournament display by his 40-strong female bodyguard regiment (*Prajurit Èstri* / "Amazon" corps).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Crown Prince / Pangéran Adipati Anom), Albert Henricus Wiese, Bartholomeus Jacobus Driessen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kraton Yogyakarta, Kalasan, Bantulan, Rèksanagaran (Kepatihan), Fort Rustenburg, Northern Alun-alun, Srimenganti Pavilion, Residency House.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Daendels court ceremonials, 28-gun fortress salutes, Prajurit Èstri ("Amazon" female guard corps), Keys of the Fort presentation, Alun-alun troop reviews.

### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Marshal Herman Willem Daendels** (`Daendels / D`): Listed under *Governors-General* (line 282). Franco-Dutch Governor-General of the East Indies (1808–1811) who overhauled diplomatic protocol between the colonial power and the Central Javanese courts.
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh)** (`HB II / Sultan`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 12). Second Sultan of Yogyakarta (reigned 1792–1810, 1811–1812, 1826–1828).
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Crown Prince / Pangéran Adipati Anom)** (`CP`): Listed under line 13. Third Sultan of Yogyakarta (reigned 1812–1814), escorted Daendels throughout the ceremonial procession.
  * **Albert Henricus Wiese** (`Wiese`): Listed under line 281. Preceding Governor-General (1805–1808) who facilitated the diplomatic reception of Daendels.
  * **Bartholomeus Jacobus Driessen (Willem Driessen)**: Commandant of Fort Rustenburg in Yogyakarta.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Kraton Yogyakarta (Srimenganti)**: Listed under lines 405, 518. Fortified palace of the Sultanate; Srimenganti is the ceremonial waiting hall and throne pavilion.
  * **Alun-alun (Northern)**: Listed under line 479. The great northern parade ground fronting the Kraton, lined with boxed waringin trees.
  * **Fort Rustenburg (Fort Vredeburg)**: Listed under line 631. Dutch fortress situated directly opposite the Kraton Yogyakarta.
  * **Kalasan (Kalassan)**: Listed under line 468. Historic royal staging post east of Yogyakarta.
  * **Rèksanagaran (Kepatihan)**: Line 516. The official estate and office of Radèn Adipati Danureja II (earlier titled Radèn Tumenggung Rèksanagara).
* **Terms:**
  * **Prajurit Èstri (Amazon Corps)**: Elite royal female bodyguard regiment of the Yogyakarta Kraton, trained in horsemanship, musket drill, and combat lance tournaments.
  * **Alun-alun**: Listed under line 479. The expansive royal square used for troop musters, parades, and state receptions.
  * **Kraton**: Listed under line 518. Seat of the sovereign and sacred center of the realm.
  * **Prajurit**: Royal troops and guardsmen of the Sultanate.
