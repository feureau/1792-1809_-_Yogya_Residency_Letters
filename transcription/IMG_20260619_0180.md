### [Transcription]

**2.**  
meets CP & brothers at Kalassan.  
met at 10.00 by Sultan at Bantulan:  
CP & Wiese -> Rēksanēgara to Receive Daendels.  
all HB II's prajurits — inner / outer Regents & MN bupatis ready at Rēksanēgaran.  
*1.00* Wiese welcomes Daendels -> Yogya.  
meeting in Throne Room + HB II / Both sitting on throne: Daendels on right of HB II.  
28 gun salute  
Willem Driessen presents Keys of Fort via Wiese -> Daendels.  
Daendels inspects Fort accompanied by HB II / CP.  
HB II met by Wiese in Kraton: to make arrangements for the following day — "never in such a good humour" claps his hands, slaps his knees.  
pleased + 21 gun salute from the Fort & CP + 9 gun salute } + small arms fire.

*30 July*  
2nd meeting of HB II & Daendels in Throne Room of Residency House.  
Daendels escorted to Kraton — passed the Fort.  
28 gun salute for D from Fort.  
passes Broad Row of Sultan's Troops on alun-alun.  
meet HB II & D. on Srimēnganti — sit on throne: Refreshments.  
[Red asterisk & red lines]:  
Visit to Ratus — children of HB II  
Daendels embraced by Sultan who has tears in his eyes.  
Happy to have the chance to meet D.  
[Red underline & margin line]:  
<u>40 "Amazon" Corps of women — ordered to appear by HB II  
1 hour of mock tournaments.</u>  
D accompanied back to Residency house by CP.
