### Diary of Marshal Daendels' State Visit to Yogyakarta: Part 2 (29–30 July 1809)

* **29 July 1809 (Arrival in Yogyakarta):**
  * Met at Kalasan by Crown Prince and brothers; met @ 10:00 a.m. at Bantulan by Sultan HB II.
  * Crown Prince, Wiese, and Reksanegara receive Daendels at Reksanegaran amidst full arrays of HB II's *prajurits* and Mancanagara Bupatis.
  * **1:00 p.m. Throne Room Audience:** **HB II and Daendels seated together on the throne (Daendels on HB II's right); 28-gun salute fired.** Willem Driessen presents fort keys to Daendels via Wiese.
  * Daendels inspects Yogyakarta fort accompanied by HB II and Crown Prince.
  * *Kraton Conference:* Wiese confers with HB II; Sultan noted as *"never in such a good humor"*, clapping hands and slapping knees; 21-gun salute fired.
* **30 July 1809 (Kraton Procession & Amazon Corps):**
  * 2nd audience in Residency Throne Room; Daendels escorted to Kraton under a 28-gun fort salute, passing double ranks of royal troops on the Alun-alun.
  * HB II and Daendels meet on the *Srimenganti* and take seats on the throne.
  * **Royal Family Meeting:** Daendels visits royal *Ratus* and children; **HB II embraces Daendels with tears in his eyes**, expressing joy at the meeting.
  * **40-Woman "Amazon" Guard Review:** **HB II orders his 40-woman "Amazon" prajurit corps (*Prajurit Laki/Langenkusuma*) to perform mock tournament maneuvers before Daendels.**
