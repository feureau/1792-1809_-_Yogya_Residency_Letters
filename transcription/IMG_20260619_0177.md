### AVJ Letters: Demangan Meeting Protocols, Throne Height Disputes, & Chinese Nation Prohibition (1809)

* **10 June 1809 (Daendels Meeting Protocols):**
  * **Demangan Meeting Limit:** HB II refuses to meet Daendels at Jenu, insisting on meeting only at Demangan (*as done for Lt. G.-G. Buyskes in Dec. 1808*).
  * **Throne Height & Stool Disputes:**
    * HB II demands a dedicated stool for the Crown Prince at Residency and Kraton audiences (*as PB IV grants the Surakarta Crown Prince*).
    * HB II refuses to allow Minister Wiese to stand during ceremonies.
    * HB II insists on sitting on an elevated throne (*Dhampar*) raised higher than Daendels' seat by a bench.
* **30 June 1809:** Pemalang-Yogyakarta territorial boundaries negotiated at Tegal.
* **23 September 1809 (Prohibition of "Heads of Chinese Nation" Title):**
  * **Daendels issues a circular forbidding Chinese Lieutenants and Capitans from titling themselves "Heads of the Chinese Nation"**, ruling that Chinese residents do not constitute a sovereign nation.
