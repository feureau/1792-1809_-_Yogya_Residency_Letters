H 698 b AVJ 1809 (Yogya) **-1809-**  

[Red ink: *Ceremonial for visit of Daendels*]

*10 June 1809*  
*-> Bat.*  
\* Discussion of Daendels' proposed visit to Yogya.  
Refusal of HB II to meet Daendels at Jēnu.  
But when he met Lt. G.G. Buyskes in Decemb. 1808 only go as far as Dêmangan.  
\* According to Engelhard : Dêmangan — one hour's distance from Yogya.  
HB II would go no further to meet Daendels.  
Ask for a STOOL for CP at Reception at Residency and at Kraton the following day  
Because CP named as successor for a LONG TIME:  
HB II would not allow Wiese to STAND —  
"in all ceremonies & HB II usually sits next to the Sultan."  
.. asks Daendels for a STOOL for himself & CP / Ngabèhi.  
HB II already wants to sit on THRONE = in THRONE ROOM in Residency house  
& in Kraton — Dampar Raised higher than Daendels' seat by a bench.  
HB II would take it hard to see his beloved Crown Prince sitting on the FLOOR:  
in Sala: CP always gets a STOOL from his father: PB IV.

*30 June 1809*  
*Procès-Verbal PREFECTURE TEGAL:*  
Boundary + Sultan's Lands and District of Pemalang discussed.

*9 Aug. 1809*  
*from Bat*  
NOTE: CELEBRATION / Name Day but of BIRTHDAY of L. NAPOLEON usually occurs 2 September.

*23 Sept. 1809*  
*from Bat.*  
CIRCULAR from Daendels = Lt. / Capitein Cina:  
NOT permitted to DESCRIBE themselves as *"Heads of Chinese Nation"*  
Because Chinese cannot pass here for a NATION.
