### [Transcription]

*Wiese to HB II, 29 Jul. 1809:*  
500 logs being Detained by Bupatis in Jipang / Japan although had promised free Transport to coast.

*Wiese to DR II, 24 Sept. 1809:*  
Attacks on Tegal from Cirêbon + Sala / Yogya lands.  
500–700 banditti.  
Robbers fall on Regency of Brebes.  
Wood of Kadang centre of Robberies.

*ID. to ID. 22 Oct. 1809:*  
Chinese make profit out of Changing Ronde Realen / Ducatoons into Duits.  
Employ Jav. women to sit in market to change money.

*ID. to ID. 28 Oct. 1809:*  
1st Lt. Bossart of Semarang Marine School to N. Coast to survey area.

*ID. to ID. 14 Jan. 1810:*  

[Red double margin line & red ink:]

<u>Ratu Kêncana Wulan trading in opium via middleman, Prayadiwangsa — Kota Gede.</u>

[Red underline:]

<u>Engelhard = Ridder van de Koninklijke Orde der Unie.</u>

*Eng. to DR II, 14 Sept. 1810:*  

[Red double margin line:]

Work on Fort at Klaten since 1808 v. little done on Yogya side — Surakarta work far more advanced.  
Despite Terms of Contract 1808 v. little advance on work.  
See lol "Papers of Robison at time of capture!"

*ID. to ID. 14 Sept. 1810:*  
Time of Renewal of Lease of Pachtgeld falling Due.  
Eng. asks Rēksanēgara to ask HB II to allow Tax to Remain at 60,000 Sp.D. for period of 3 years.

*ID. to HB II, 23 Sept. 1810:*  
If HB II does not provide "Eclatante Satisfactie" for attack on Dēmak  
Daendels would march + large army on Central Java.  
The least opposition on part of HB II would be taken as a Declaration of War.

*ID. to ID. 17 Sept. 1810:*  
Kliwon of Dēmak + Naying Sekar to Kayumas.  
Find only village Inhabitants who flee along River.  
J. Sekar go to mosque find 9 Deserters from Dēmak amongst them 2 from Semarang Garrison Regt.  
DR II gives new orders to Yogya Commissioners to Kayumas  
R. Sasrakusuma / Ng. Nindijaya  
to arrest Pêmang of Kayumas + head of Wirosari & order R.T. Sasranēgara of Grobogan to return to Yogya to answer for Bad Govnmt.
