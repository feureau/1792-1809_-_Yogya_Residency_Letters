### Pj. Brieven No. 39: Ratu Kencana Wulan Opium Trade, Klaten Fort Delays, & War Declaration Threat (1809–1810)

* **24 September 1809:** Cirebon bandit raids on Tegal and Brebes; Kadawon forest identified as bandit center.
* **14 January 1810 (Ratu Kencana Wulan Opium Monopoly):**
  * **Ratu Kencana Wulan engages in large-scale illicit opium trading via her middleman Prayadiwangsa at Kota Gede.**
  * Nicolaas Engelhard awarded the Knight's Cross of the Order of Union (*Ridder van de Koninklijke Orde der Unie*).
* **14 September 1810 (Klaten Fort Construction Delays):**
  * Engelhard reports that since 1808 **very little progress has been made on the Yogyakarta side of Klaten Fort**, while Surakarta's sector is far advanced (*ref: Robinson papers*).
  * 3-year tax farm renewal falling due; Engelhard requests Reksanegara petition HB II to maintain the lease rate @ 60,000 Np.D.
* **23 September 1810 (Daendels Declaration of War Ultimatum):**
  * **DAENDELS WARNS HB II THAT UNLESS HE PROVIDES "ECLATANTE SATISFACTIE" (FULL SATISFACTION) FOR THE ATTACK ON DEMAK, DAENDELS WILL MARCH A LARGE ARMY INTO CENTRAL JAVA.**
  * Any further opposition from HB II will be treated as a **formal Declaration of War**.
* **17 September 1810 (Demak Incursion & Deserters):** Demak Kliwons and *Naying Sekars* track fleeing villagers along the river to Kayumas; locate 9 military deserters in a mosque (including 2 from the Semarang garrison). Danureja II orders the arrest of the Demang of Kayumas and orders Grobogan Regent R.T. Sasranegara to Yogyakarta to account for bad governance.
