### Archive Entries: May – September 1803 (Surakarta Friction & Crown Prince Character)

* Reference: **P.J. Br. 20.48**
* **31 May 1803:** PB IV believes he was promised a portion of Mataram upon HB I's death. Discontented with V.O.C. Governor's position; quarrel between PB IV and Resident Van Nijvenheim.
* **26 August 1803 (Van den Berg Leaves Yogya):**
  * Van den Berg arrives in Solo (18 Aug.) to take leave.
  * **Conflict between Solo Crown Prince and Pangeran Mangkubumi:**
    * Pangeran Mangkubumi described as possessing *"a charming heart"* to be pitied; tries to reconcile with Crown Prince.
    * Crown Prince described as a vindictive, rancorous manikin (*"wraakzuchtig en hart dragende mannetje"*) devoid of intelligence.
    * Mangkubumi approaches Crown Prince in Van den Berg's presence asking for reconciliation with tears in his eyes, calling him *"Gusti"*.
    * Crown Prince turns his back on his uncle, only toasting Van den Berg.
    * Mangkubumi remarks despondently: *"I must confess I fear for unpleasant consequences at some time of this oneenigheid, which seems to be unamendable and bodes ill for the injudicious Crown Prince."*
* **September 1803 Negotiations:**
  * PB IV goes fishing at Boyolali for 3 days. Prangwedana confers with Van den Berg regarding grievances against Sunan.
  * Marriage negotiations between Yogya princess (Ratu Anom) and Solo Crown Prince.
  * One of Solo Crown Prince's concubines pregnant (*"The young Prince wat veel naar de vrouwen begint te zien"*), disproving rumors of impotence.
  * 30,000 men under arms escort PB IV to Pengging (see Louw Vol. VI p. 109).
