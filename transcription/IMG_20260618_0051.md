| **2 April 1792** | ### [Transcription]<br><br>#### 1. Factions within the Yogyakarta Royal Family<br><br>- **Pangeran Mangkudiningrat:**  <br>  In disfavour with HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]; lives in very reduced circumstances (*in a sober fashion*), but conducts himself quietly.<br><br>- **Pangeran Ngabèhi & Pangeran Demang:**  <br>  Pangeran Ngabèhi hoped to see his son **Pangeran Demang** appointed over the head of the Crown Prince.  <br>  Demang is married to a daughter of Danureja I. Governor Van den Burgh had reported very negative things about him as early as 1780. However, Demang made no move during the 1792 succession.<br><br>- **Ratu Bèndara:**  <br>  Very widely respected by other members of the royal family.<br><br>#### 2. Protocol: Felicitations to Incoming Governor-General<br><br>- Historical refusal of HB I [Sultan Hamengkubuwana I] to pay respects in person to an incoming Governor-General in Semarang.  <br>- Resolved by **Article 24 of the Treaty of 2 April 1792**, permitting felicitations to be delivered in Semarang rather than traveling all the way to Batavia. (In 1788 PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] had also agreed to a similar article).<br><br>#### 3. Succession of Mangkunegara II (Pangeran Prangwedana)<br><br>- **28 Dec. 1795:**  <br>  **Mangkunegara I dies** aged 71 years old.<br><br>- **Pangeran Prangwedana:**  <br>  Allowed to succeed to the **4,000 cacahs**, but *not* permitted to inherit MN I [Mangkunagara I]'s personal title of *Pangeran Adipati*.  <br>  To enjoy court precedence immediately after the Sala Crown Prince.<br><br>- *[Red ink note:]* **Trouble to get agreement of PB IV to successions:**  <br>  Great difficulty in winning over PB IV in 1795 to consent to Prangwedana's inheritance.<br><br>- **25 Jan. 1796:**  <br>  PB IV formally allows him to succeed to the 4,000 cacahs with the title **Pangeran Aria Prabu Prangwedana**.<br><br>- Strategic imperative: Need of the V.O.C. to use PWD [Prangwedana / Mangkunagara II] as a counterweight / balance against the Sunan. |
