### Archive Entries: May – June 1808 (HB II Reaction to Daendels & Rajawinangun Troop Inspection)

* Reference: **Pj. Br. 23 (29 May – 4 June 1808 - Engelhard to Daendels)**
* **HB II Reaction to Daendels' Military Assembly:**
  * Engelhard reports HB II was initially alarmed by Daendels' sudden arrival in Semarang (12 May) and troop mobilization, viewing the invitation to send courtiers as contrary to custom.
  * After receiving Daendels' letter of 16 May, HB II relaxes and agrees to dispatch envoys and allow Colonel Von Winkelmann to inspect royal troops.
  * Engelhard reassures HB II that the 3,000–4,000 troops in Semarang are assembled to defend Java against the British, not to attack Yogyakarta.
* **Yogyakarta Envoys & Troop Inspection (1–2 June 1808):**
  * 19 cavalry horses sent from Yogya to Semarang. Due to Pringgakusuma's illness, Pangeran Adinegara leads the delegation.
  * **Rajawinangun Troop Review (1 June 1808):** General inspection (*monstering*) held at Rajawinangun (N.E. of Kraton) under the Crown Prince and 9 young *Panjis*.
  * *Panji Regalia:* Clothed in yellow chintz *kambens*, green velvet jackets trimmed with gold lace (*gold passement*), black velvet caps (*mutsjes*), riding matched grey horses (*Schimmels*).
  * HB II, Ratus, and royal women watch the parade from the Kraton's West bastion; HB II warmly greets the marching contingents.
