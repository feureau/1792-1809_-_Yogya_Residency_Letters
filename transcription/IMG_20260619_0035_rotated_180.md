### AN Yogya Letters No. 49: Sumadiningrat Fall & Ratu Kedhaton Influence (Feb. 1807)

* Reference: **A.N. Yogya Letters No. 49 (Feb. 1807 - Waterloo to Engelhard)**
* **12 February 1807 (Sumadiningrat Dismissal Context):**
  * Pending dismissal of R.T. Sumadiningrat. Elderly Ratu Kedhaton vigorously defends her son-in-law.
  * *Waterloo's Characterization of Ratu Kedhaton:* *"De oude heeft een Madoureesch hart, en dat scheind in haar ouden dag noch weinig versleten te zijn."* (*"The old queen has a Madurese heart, which in her old age appears little worn down."*)
  * HB II entirely dominated by the counsels of **Ratu Kencana Wulan**. In court emergencies, HB II and Ratu K.W. retreat to one of his country *pesanggrahans*.
* **16 February 1807:** Von Liebeherr installed as 1st Resident of Surakarta.
* **19 February 1807:** *Garebeg Besar* celebrated in Yogyakarta on 18 Feb. 1807.
* **1 March 1807 (Crown Prince Marriage Trousseau):**
  * 29 children of Crown Prince married (Dipanasara).
  * *Trousseau:* 2 pairs gold *Allagiasen* earrings, 2 large silk *Patholen* cloths, 2 *Armozijnen* silks, 2 chintzes, 21 ells of fine parchment/trimmings.
  * Sultan also marries off several of his own children.
