### N. Engelhard MvO (May 1808): Natayuda Fall, Klaten Fort Origins, & Earthquake Damage

* Reference: **N. Engelhard MVO (May 1808) / Van Alphen - Engelhard 235**
* **Natayuda Disgrace & Nuwadireja:**
  * **Natayuda** originally designated as successor to Patih Danureja I, but fell into disgrace for being too close to the V.O.C.
  * **R.Ng. Nuwadireja** (son of murdered Natayuda) had no prospects at the Yogyakarta court. Van Overstraten took him to Semarang in 1802. Engelhard appointed him Demang of Siderga *Blandhong* teak forests.
* **Klaten Fort Origins (1802 Visit):**
  * During 1802 court visit, Engelhard secured HB II and PB IV's agreement to build **Klaten Fort** (constructed by Capt. Ir. A.C. Cornelius under Lt.-Col. Carel von Woltzogen in 1806).
  * Promised Jambu hills forts never built by HB II.
* **Earthquake Damage to Yogyakarta Fort (19 March 1806):**
  * **19 March 1806 earthquake heavily damaged Yogyakarta Fort and cracked its foundations.**
  * Minor repairs executed by HB II, but complete rebuilding required. Government approved complete reconstruction on 10 April 1807; repairs underway.
* **Surakarta Fort:** Surakarta Fort maintained in excellent condition.
