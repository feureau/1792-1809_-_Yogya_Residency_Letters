## Content

### 1792 Conference: Van Overstraten with PB IV / HB II (Aug. 1792)

*(Ref: Komm. Geh. 23 Sept. 1847 La L10)*

#### Fort at Klaten & Roads:

- **p. 231**: Promise of HB II to lay out 3 small forts along road to Jambu – Ungarang.
- **fol. 232**: But Van O promises not to press HB II too far on this $\therefore$ abandons idea of 3 small forts, but building of fort...
- **p. 233**: ...at **Klaten** instead; promise of PB IV to bear ½ costs.
- **p. 234**: **Marbon** chosen as suitable place = good water / view / on a height / water supply / equidistant Solo / Yogya; promise of HB II to bear ½ costs...
- **fol. 235**: ...+ to maintain fort = V.O.C. to take decision later on 3 small forts.
- **fol. 236**: Gladag to be set up at Klaten.

#### Pepper & Indigo Cultivation:

- **fol. 237–238**: Of Pepper cultivation: promise of HB II to encourage inhabitants to plant pepper.
- **fol. 239**: **Pacitan / Loano / Sintang** to be set aside entirely for pepper. Total 700 cacahs. To be under overview of Res / Patih = pop. to be freed from corvée labour.
- **fol. 240**: Lands to be viewed as having been given over entirely to V.O.C. During harvest European *opzieners* to be placed.
- **fol. 241**: All pepper from lands in future to be monopoly of V.O.C. Restrictions on HB II's undertakings to V.O.C.
- **fol. 242**: Necessary arrangements to be made by Patih / Res regarding Indigo cultivation. 100 cacahs to be given for Indigo cultivation.
- **fol. 243**: Corvée services remitted + Patih to provide batons from Gladag.

#### Pangeran Ngabehi & Royal Princes:

- **fol. 244–245**: *(Red margin marker)* Of **P. Ngabehi**: in previous conference, HB II shows understanding = to be given 1,000 cacahs free of corvée + to be allowed to sit on a stool not on ground in presence of Sultan (like CP).
  - *Condition:* But on understanding Ngabehi would make no further demands for increase in his landholding / prestige.
- **fol. 246**: + same corvée / military duties as CP.
  - Pringgalaya / P. Anom given same status as P. Natakusuma.
- **fol. 247**: + to be given names PA / MA.
- **fol. 248**: Signed / sealed HB II / Van O / Van IJ / Van R. Yogya, 19 Aug. [1792].

#### Mangkunagaran Acte:

- **fol. 249**: *Acte in forme* = for MN signed / sealed + PB IV / Van O = 8 Aug. 1792.
  - Guaranteeing grandson **PWD** (Prangwedana) the succession + same rank as brother of PB IV (P. Aria Mangkubumi) + guaranteed **4,000 cacahs**.
- *(Bottom annotation: See Kuitenbrouwer / Rouffaer, p. 604; Signed / sealed Van Ov 14 Aug. 1792 Surakarta)*
