### 1792 Conference Notes (Folios 231 to 249)

* **Fol. 231:** Promising HB II to lay out 3 small forts along road to Jambu – Ungarang (Komm. Geh. 23 Sept 1847 La E10 Conference Van Ov – PB IV / HB II Aug. 1792).
* **Fol. 232:** But Van Ov promises not to press HB II too far on this = abandons idea of 3 small forts but building of fort at Klaten instead = promising PB IV to bear 1/2 costs.
* **Fol. 233–234:** Matbox chosen as suitable place = good water supply / river / open height / equidistant Solo / Yogya, promising HB II to bear 1/2 costs.
* **Fol. 235:** To maintain fort = V.O.C. to take decision later on 3 small forts.
* **Fol. 236:** Gladag to be set up at Klaten.
* **Fol. 237–238:** Re Pepper cultivation: promising HB II to encourage inhabitants to plant pepper.
* **Fol. 239:** Pacitan, Loano, Gintang to be set aside entirely for pepper. Total 700 cacahs. To be under overview of Res / Patih = pop. to be freed from corvée labour.
* **Fol. 240:** Lands to be viewed as having been given over entirely to V.O.C. During harvest Eur. opzieners to be placed.
* **Fol. 241:** All pepper from lands in future to be monopoly of V.O.C. Restitutions HB II's undertakings to V.O.C.
* **Fol. 242:** Necessary arrangements to be made by Patih / Res regarding Indigo cultivation. 100 cacahs to be given for Indigo cultivation.
* **Fol. 243:** Corvée services remitted & Patih to provide Baton from Gladag.
* **Fol. 244:** Re P. Ngabehi = in previous conference = HB II shows understanding = to be given 1,000 cacahs free of corvée & to be allowed to sit on a stool not on ground in presence of Sultan = like CP.
* **Fol. 245:** But on understanding Ng. would make no further demands for increase in his landholding / prestige.
* **Fol. 246:** Same corvée/mil. duties as CP. Pringgalaya / P. Anom given name / status as P. Natakusuma.
* **Fol. 247:** To be given names MD / MB.
* **Fol. 248:** Signed / Sealed HB II / Van Ov / Van IJ / Van R. Yogya 19 Aug. Signed / Sealed Van Ov 14 Aug. 1792 Surakarta.
* **Fol. 249:** Acte = in form signed/sealed PB IV / Van Ov = 8 Aug. Guaranteeing grandson PWP the succession & name Rank as brother of PB IV = P. Aria Mangkubumi & guaranteed 4,000 cacahs.
