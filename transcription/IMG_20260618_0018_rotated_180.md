* **People:**
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh)** (`HB II / Sultan`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 12). Second Sultan of Yogyakarta (reigned 1792–1810, 1811–1812, 1826–1828), primary sovereign in the residency letters.
  * **Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus)** (`PB IV / Sunan`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 18). Sunan of Surakarta (reigned 1788–1820), prominent rival to HB II.
  * **Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II)** (`MN II / PWD`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 27). Ruler of the Mangkunagaran (reigned 1796–1835), creator of the Mangkunagaran Legion.
  * **Pangéran Mangkubumi** (`MB / Mangkoeboemie`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 63). Son of HB I, uncle of Dipanagara, key advisor during the 1825 court crisis.
  * **Pangéran Natakusuma (Pakualam I)** (`PA / PA I`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 23). Son of HB I, created first independent ruler of the Pakualaman (reigned 1812–1829).
  * **Pieter Gerardus van Overstraten** (`Van Overstraten`): Listed under *Governors-General* (line 279). Governor of Java's Northeast Coast (1791–1796) and Governor-General (1796–1801).
  * **Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk** (`Van IJsseldijck`): Listed under *Residents of Yogyakarta* (line 327). Resident of Yogyakarta (1786–1798), author of the 1798 Memorie van Overgave.
* **Places & Institutions:**

  * **Surakarta (Solo / Sala)** (`Solo / Sala`): Listed under *Places* (line 461). Court city and realm of the Sunanate of Surakarta.
  * **Klaten (Klathèn)** (`Klattèn / Klaten`): Listed under *Places* (line 423). Border garrison town and fortress site between Yogyakarta and Surakarta.
