### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.86: Sawah Expansion & Irrigation Systems (1806)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.86 (28 Feb. 1806 - Waterloo to Engelhard)**
* **Sawah Expansion (Nagara Agung):**
  * 25% increase in cultivated sawahs in Yogyakarta *Nagara Agung* over the past 10 years.
  * Land around Gunung Gamping (N.W. of Yogya along Kali Bedhog) transformed from jungle into extensive sawahs under HB II's orders.
  * **Irrigation Works:** Dam constructed across the Winorga River (Kali Noka) with skillfully engineered irrigation canals; roads west of Yogya thronged with farmers and traders (*Ref: Stat. Yogya 1838 p. 147; Winorga Dam behind Krapyak*).
  * Sawahs expanding up the slopes of Mount Merapi with new canals opened alongside old ones.
* **Rice Market Prices:** Rice prices lower than in preceding years (2 Rijksdaalders per pikul = 2.40; see Raffles).
* **Crop Rotations:** Irrigation allows continuous cropping without fallow periods; 5 rice harvests gathered every 2 years in Mataram and Kedu.
* **Regional Variations:** Lowlands in Bagelèn, Grobogan, and Eastern Mancanagara present different challenges.
