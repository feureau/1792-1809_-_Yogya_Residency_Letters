### Wat. – Eng. | 20 Feb. 1806.

**(1) What Ricefields laid out in VL during past 10 years & suitable for what Expansion?**

* 25% increase in vicinity of Yogya lands (Nagara agung) during past 10 years. [*marked with red vertical rule //*]
* **Around Kalk Berg : Gunung Gamping** (1 hr. N.W. of Yogya | Kali Bêdhog).
  * Earlier just jungle But on HB II's instructions
  * Dam Built in River (? Kali Noka) **Winanga.** [*'Winanga.' in red ink*]
  * & Irrigation of sawahs skilfully laid out.
  * Roads so thronged & merchants / traders / Farmers to W. of Yogya that can scarcely get by on Horseback. [*marked with red vertical rule //*]

[*In left margin, red ink:*]

**On Irrigation see Stat. Yogya 1838**

[*In right margin, red ink with arrow pointing from Winanga:*]

**cf. Alg. Verslag 1847**  
**Kali Winanga Dam Irrigates sawahs Behind Krapyak.**

* E. / N. of Yogya many new lands laid out:
  * sawahs spreading up Mêrapi.
  * new sawahs opened up alongside old ones.

---

**(2) How much value of Harvest in each District:**

* Impossible to answer Question accurately
* But rice much cheaper now than had been for a long time (1806) (only 2 Ryksdaalders per pikul)

[*In right margin, red ink:*]

**see Raffles**  
**// Rix Dollar = 2.40**

---

**(3) If Farmers take care to plant 2nd Harvest in Time:**

[*In left margin, red ink written diagonally:*]

**see St. Yogya 1838**

* Yes.
* sawahs very rarely left fallow.
* When one end of a field still being harvested sometimes see the other end already being prepared for next Harvest.
* usually in space of 2 years = 5 Rice harvests gathered in in Mataram / Këdhu.
* Enough water / irrigat. so as to plant in Dry season.

* But low lands in Bagêlen in W. / Grobogan / E. MN [Eastern Mancanagara]  
  v. different situation.
