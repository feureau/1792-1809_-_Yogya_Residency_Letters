### <u>Inner Court Factions and Character of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II:</u>

<u>Apart from Ratu Kêncana Wulan, no-one at court has Real Trust of HB II; clearly the most favoured person at the court.</u>

* **R.T. Natadiningrat:** son-in-law of HB II also v. highly favoured.  
* **R.T. Purwadipura:** also often called close to HB II; knows HB II's character v. well + has most say in financial matters.  

*(Marked with double red vertical line in margin)*  
> *a very ordinary trader knows how to give advise most pleasing to HB II.*  
> But like SD [Sumadiningrat] has to pay fines; not so long ago had to pay **1,000 R. Reals** [PWP: Purwadipura] because without permission of HB II had used a certain gamêlan.  
> ∴ Not Really a favourite or someone who has HB II's full Trust.  

*(Marked with continuous red vertical line in left margin)*  
<span style="color:red"><u>no-one in Yogya kraton really trusted by HB II, still less a favourite.</u></span>
