### Waterloo Memorie van Overgave (1807): Factions and Power Dynamics at the Yogyakarta Court

* Reference: **Waterloo MVO (5 Sept. 1807 - Waterloo to Engelhard)**
* **Document Appendices:** Contains lists of HB II's wives/Ratus, legitimate/illegitimate children, brothers, nephews, appanages, Patih/Bupati/Nayaka registers, Mangkunagaran ranks, deaths (1801–07), and marriages (1801–07).

#### Inner Court Power & Trust:
* **Ratu Kencana Wulan:** The **ONLY person in whom HB II reposes genuine trust**; indisputably the most powerful figure at court.
* **Raden Tumenggung Natadiningrat:** Son-in-law of P. Natakusuma; highly favored by HB II.
* **Raden Tumenggung Purwadipura:**
  * Formerly an ordinary market trader; skilled in offering financial advice pleasing to HB II.
  * Exercises primary influence in financial matters, though subjected to arbitrary fines (e.g. fined 1,000 R. Reals for playing a court gamelan without royal authorization).
  * Neither Purwadipura nor any other courtier enjoys HB II's complete trust.
* **Absence of True Favorites:**
  * Waterloo concludes after 9 years in Yogyakarta that **HB II trusts no one at court**—neither his brothers, nephews, sons, nor even the Crown Prince.
