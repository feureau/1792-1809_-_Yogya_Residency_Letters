### Archive Entries: August 1808 (PB IV Animosity toward HB II & Jager Brawl at Fort)

* **18 August 1808:** HB II accepts new ceremonial without protest. Departure of Daendels' 3,000 troops creates immense relief at court. 380 Javanese garments sent to Semarang to make military uniforms.
* **23 August 1808 (PB IV Inter-Court Animosity):**
  * PB IV plans marriage of P. Panular to late P. Sumadiningrat's daughter during Louis Napoleon festival; fails to notify or invite HB II (his uncle).
  * Resident Van Braam notes PB IV's growing animosity toward HB II and encourages these feelings to keep Surakarta allied with the Dutch.
  * Absence of Prangwedana deprives Van Braam of Yogyakarta intelligence (Prangwedana previously passed court gossip regarding Ratu Bindara to the Resident).
* **26 August 1808 (Dutch Jagers Brawling in Kampong):**
  * 4 Dutch *Jagers* severely wounded in a brawl in a kampong near the fort over a *ronggeng* dancer.
  * Jagers paid a husband money for a *ronggeng* dancer; when the door was shut in their faces, 6–7 comrades returned unarmed to force entry and were attacked by armed villagers with rifles. Patih institutes an investigation.
