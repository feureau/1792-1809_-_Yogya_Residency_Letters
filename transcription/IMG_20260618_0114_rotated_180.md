### [29 Jan 1802] — Appointment of R. Rangga as Wedana of Eastern Mancanagara

* **Mack. Pr. 21, pp. 254–5:** Nawala for R. Rangga for E. Mancanagara (12 Puasa AJ 1728 / 29 Jan. 1802).
* *[Left margin annotation in red ink:]* **apptmnt. R. Rangga as Bp. Wdn.**
* Appointment as Bupati Wedana of E. MN:
  * **10,000 cacahs** of Royal lands in Madiun:
    * <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">5,700</ins> cacahs <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">nominal</ins> (dead) ? not worked.
    * 5,000 productive (*kuring*):
      * 2,000 assigned for maintenance.
      * 3,000 charges + rent = 1,675 Sp. D. annually:
        * Mulud: 837½ Sp. D.
        * Puasa: 837½ Sp. D.
  * Each dollar to consist of 30 *wangs*.
  * Office fee: <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">1 wang</ins> = each dollar.
  * Each year account to be rendered to kraton of increase / decrease of sawahs.
