### Appointment of Raden Rongga III (1802) & Licensing of Wayangs (1814)

* Reference: **29 Jan. 1802 Nask. Pr. 21 (Nuwala for R. Rangga for E. Mancanagara - 12 Puasa 1728)**
* **Appanage Breakdown for Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III:**
  * Appointed **Bupati Wedana of Eastern Mancanagara**.
  * Total Appanage: 10,000 cacahs of Royal lands in Madiun.
    * 5,700 cacahs nominal (*pead*, unworked).
    * 5,000 cacahs productive (*wring*).
    * 2,000 cacahs assigned for maintenance.
    * 3,000 cacahs charges + rent = 1,675 Sp.D. annually (payable at Mulud: 837 1/2, Puasa: 837 1/2).
  * Rate: 1 dollar = 30 *wangs*.
  * Account of increases/decreases in land charters (*Nuwala*) to be rendered annually to the Kraton.

#### 1814–1815 Wayang Licensing & British Textile Speculation:
* **22 Dec. 1814:** Implementation of licensing fees for *wayang* performances across Java.
* **22 Dec. 1814:** Orders placed in England to manufacture cotton textiles imitating traditional Javanese batik patterns as a commercial speculation.
* **15 Feb. 1815:** Official advertisements for sale of British-made Javanese-style textiles posted in Javanese, Chinese, and Malay at Surabaya, Solo, Yogya, Kedu, and Gresik.
