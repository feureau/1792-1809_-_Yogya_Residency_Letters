### Blora Teak Forests & Regeneration Protocols (August 1806)

* Reference: **Van Alphen - Engelhard No. 239 (MvO Van den Berg, Aug. 1806)**
* **Blora Teak Leases:** PB IV permits V.O.C. to fell teak in Blora forests.
* **Chinese Sales Prohibited:** Bupati of Blora prohibited from selling timber to Chinese merchants, but authorized to cut teak for local needs and Surakarta Kraton requirements.
* **Forest Reserves:** Vast Blora teak reserves estimated as inexhaustible (not 1/4 felled in 1,000 years).
* **Agricultural Regeneration:** Local populations plant maize (*jagung*) or rice on cleared forest tracts; once *jati* or *glaga* shoots re-emerge, farmers shift to new plots.
* **European Restrictions:** No European permitted to enter Blora without explicit knowledge and authorization of the Resident.
