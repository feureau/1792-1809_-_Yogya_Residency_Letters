### AVJ Letters: Pekalongan Robberies, Daendels Kedu Annexation Threat, & Citra Wiguna Arrest (1809)

* **30 December 1809 (Daendels Ultimatum on Pekalongan Robberies):**
  * Daendels demands HB II surrender guilty Yogyakarta village heads involved in Pekalongan highway robberies.
  * Expresses outrage that HB II stalls decision pending further investigation.
  * **ANNEXATION ULTIMATUM:** **Daendels warns that if Yogyakarta robberies recur, Dutch troops will invade Yogyakarta territory within 14 days and permanently annex Kedu province without returning it.**
* **Citra Wiguna Incident:**
  * Envoy Citra Wiguna (sent by Yogyakarta Bupati R.T. Prawirawirata to investigate a robbery in Demak) arrested and detained in Semarang for unseemly conduct.
  * Patih Danureja II demands Citra Wiguna's extradition to Yogyakarta; Daendels refuses, citing European procedures where offenders are punished on the spot.
