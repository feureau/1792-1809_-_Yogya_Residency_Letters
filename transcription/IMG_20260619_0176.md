*20 Mar. 1809*  
R.T. Sasrakusuma — mentioned as Regent of Grobogan.

*30 Nov. 1809*  
District Tersana, Desa Warakrama in Yogya area:  
Ngabèhi mentioned as Head of District.

*30 Dec. (Wintermaand) 1809*  
*Daendels -> HB II*  
Daendels writes about ROBBERIES in Pekalongan: and Demand of HB II to hand over guilty heads of désas.  

[Red ink: *Robberies in Pekalongan.*]

[Margin: *See BL ADD 12341 240v–243v*]

Surprised that HB II wants to put off decision until a further Investigation.  

- Personal visit of Daendels — Yogya (July 1809) } 2 strong examples of Friendship by Daendels.  
- Lessening of Troops (Semarang)  
If Insult Repeated Sultan's Lands would be fallen on by Daendels' Troops within 14 Days.  
at least Kèdhu would NOT be evacuated.  
Glory / greatness of HB II depends on attitude to Dutch Govnmt i.e. behaviour.

*28 Dec. 1809*  
*Wiese -> HB II*  
Reports on proposed attack on Kèdhu:  
would occupy Kèdhu without intention of handing it back.  
Messenger Citra Wiguna sent by Yogya Bupati R.T. Prawirawinata -> Desa Gribegan (Demak) to Investigate Robbery.  
Because of unseemly conduct captured and taken in detention to Semarang.  
Wiese Reports to Daendels: cites system in Europe where wrong-doers punished on the spot for deed.  
Daendels would await decision on Citra Wiguna:  
Because of demand of DR II to deliver up this person to Yogya.  
[Asterisk / red note:] To see what HB II does about Robberies in Pekalongan mounted by Yogya Inhabitants of Warakrama, Tersana.
