## Complete Diplomatic Transcription

### [16 April 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
16 April 1805  
Waterloo -> Engelhard

LAWICK VAN PABST to Yogya.  
Brings Japanese PRINTS – most beautiful that of BAY of NAGASAKI.

---

### [19 April 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
19 April 1805  
Waterloo -> Engelhard  
*[In red ink in margin, with long vertical red line:]*  
**Importance of Prau Pengluput**

CONCERNING BOATS allowed to sail without paying dues on Sala River: PRAU PENGLUPAT  
(PB IV has 10 such praauws).

[crossed out: Annoys R Rangga +] CAPITAN CINA of JAPARA [in charge of MN Tollgates for Yogya]

annoyance that these Boats make journey to GRISEK + back passing Sultan's Tollgates without Tax.  
Usually SUNAN does not use these PRAAUWS himself  
but Radèn Rangga lends them out to merchants for 200 Reals p.a apiece.  
NOW P.B. IV making HIS OWN PROFITS by using them Directly.

---

### [17 April 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
17 April 1805  
Wat – Eng

MAS PANJI fined 300 Reals =  

+ allowed back to POST.

---

### [2 May 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
2 May 1805  
Wat – Eng

Waterloo makes outing + CP to Klaten (spelt MALATTEN) garden of woods there.  
CP tells Waterloo that the SULTAN would make many SORTIES during the DRY SEASON.  
Fear that it would make Waterloo bankrupt because of expenditure for presents.
