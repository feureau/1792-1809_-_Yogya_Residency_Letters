# Transcription: IMG_20260618_0201_rotated_180.jpg

**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260618_0201_rotated_180.jpg`  
**Folio / Reference:** H 698 b, AvJ 1805 (Yogya)  
**Date Range:** 7 January 1805 – 18 April 1805  
**Primary Subjects:**  
- Sudden death (? poisoned) of Raden Tumenggung Natayuda (Nayaka Bumiya Tengen)  
- Appointment of Raden Mas Subekti (son of Pangeran Natakusuma) as Raden Tumenggung Natadiningrat (future Paku Alam II); eldest son of Natayuda receives his father's name and 250 cacahs  
- Military expedition against *wong durjana* (brigands) to Desa Ngalian on the Kendal / Kaliwungu border: Kyai Tumenggung Mangunnegara with 35–40 Sultan's prajurits, 4 mantris, 1 European sergeant, and 4 dragoons  
- Mancanagara regents granted leave to return home  
- Inter-court pact between Patih Danureja II and Patih Danuningrat to eradicate *"kwaad gespuys"* (*wong durjana*) across Central Java  
- Coastal defense alarm at Pacitan: Regent reports 32 foreign ships sighted off the coast at Segara Wedi (? British / pirates); Raden Tumenggung Purwadipura with 15 Gedhong Kiwa mantris and 200 prajurits dispatched to Pacitan (18 April 1805)
