### Historical Correspondence: Simon Schama to Peter Carey (22 March 1976)

* **Sender:** Simon Schama, Director of Studies in History, Christ's College, Cambridge (CB2 3BU).
* **Recipient:** Peter Carey, Trinity College, Oxford.
* **Text Summary:**
  * Schama discusses Louis Napoleon's creation of Dutch chivalric orders:
  * Louis Napoleon created the **Orde van de Unie** on 14 Feb. 1807 (not the *Orde van de Nederlandse Leeuw*).
  * The *Almanac de Cour* (1809) divides the *Orde van de Unie* into three classes:
    1. **Grand Croix:** Reserved for Cabinet Ministers (*Ministerraad*), Council of State members, foreign ministers, and imperial princes.
    2. **Commanders:** Includes Council of State members (Appelius, Calkoen) and notable Dutch figures (Anthony Hope, Hodshon, Pieter van de Kasteele, Gerrit Pijman, Robert Voute).
    3. **Ridderschap (Chevaliers):** 700–800 members in 1809 (nearly all native Dutch, no Indonesian potentates listed).
  * Notes that Daendels held the Grand Cross of the Order along with Gogel and Schimmelpenninck. Under the 1810 annexation terms, Napoleon abolished all Louis-era orders.
