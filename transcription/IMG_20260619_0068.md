### Daendels Forestry Regulations & Native Auxiliary Regiments (1808–1809)

* **Java Forestry Regulations (26 May 1808 – 22 Jan. 1809):**
  * Inspection General of Forests under Const. Carel von Woltzogen; Forestry Administration Board chaired by J. Knops (members: ex-Resident Matthew Waterloo, Semarang Fiscal N. Minnaut).
* **Indonesian Auxiliary Troops under Daendels (19 June 1808 pp. 836–837):**
  * **Strengthening Central Java Garrisons:**
    * Ambonese troops garrisoned in Yogyakarta (2/3 Infantry Regiment; 2 Grenadier companies recruited partly from Ambonese).
    * Stationed at court forts and **Klaten Fort**.
    * 1,000 Buginese dispatched from Makassar; 750 Balinese recruits.
    * Governor Engelhard purchases Balinese slaves via Gezaghebber Rothenbühler in Oosthoek.
    * 1,500 men brought from Groote Oost (slaves from Makassar and Bali).
