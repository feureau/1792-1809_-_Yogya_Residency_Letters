- **Date Range:** 1806-02-28
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Resident Matthijs Waterloo reports to Governor Nicolaus Engelhard regarding the salt trade monopoly and demographic expansion across the Vorstenlanden; contrasts North Coast salt consumption (Mataram, Solo, Pajang, Boyolali) with South Coast sources supplying Kedu, Bagelen, and Banyumas; presents vital census statistics estimating an increase of 488,472 inhabitants (over 50% growth) between 1760 and 1806; documents high infant mortality under age 12; records the proliferation of new agricultural desas into waste lands.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Matthijs Waterloo, Nicolaus Engelhard, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Radèn Mas Said (Mangkunagara I).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Mataram), Surakarta (Solo), Pajang, Boyolali, Këdhu (Kedu), Bagelèn (Bagelen), Banyumas, Lêdhok, Rêma, Eastern Mancanagara.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Salt trade monopoly, Vorstenlanden census, Treaty of Giyanti population baseline, Desa clearing, Hearth counts.
