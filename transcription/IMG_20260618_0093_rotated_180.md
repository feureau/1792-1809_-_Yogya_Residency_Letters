### Van Reede Memorie van Overgave 1801: Tobacco Monopoly & Tollgate Abolition

* Reference: **N.O.K. 47 MvO Van Reede 6**
* **Cloth Pricing (p. 197):** V.O.C. purchase price for Javanese cloth raised from 11.32 to 18.32 Np.D. per corge due to increased production costs.
* **Tobacco Trade (p. 228–229):** Javanese tobacco unsuited for European tastes; most tobacco for European consumption imported from America. Trade depressed by wartime hostilities.
* **Tollgate Abolition in Oosthoek:**
  * Van Overstraten refuses to establish *Gedhong Tembakan* tobacco tollgates in Kedu.
  * **Tollgates along Solo River:** Internal trade severely depressed by tollgates erected by native rulers along the Bengawan Solo.
  * Van IJsseldijk, as Commissioner in Surabaya, orders the **abolition of tollgates in the Oosthoek** to protect small traders from depression.
* **Language Appointment (p. 340):** Official Javanese language translator appointed at court.
