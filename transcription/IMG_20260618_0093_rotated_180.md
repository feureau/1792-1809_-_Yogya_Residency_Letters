### Textile Prices (f. 197):

- V.O.C. procurement price paid for *kleedjes* (woven cloths) was deemed too low, despite being produced in greater quantities than previously.
- Price officially raised from **16.32 Sp.D. to 18.32 Sp.D.** per *corge*.

---

### Tobacco Trade & Quality (f. 228, 229):

- **European taste:** Javanese tobacco evaluated for European consumption, but no local strain proved completely suitable; most fine tobacco consumed by Europeans in Java is imported from the Americas.
- Trade in tobacco falls off markedly due to the international wartime / hostilities situation.

---

### Tollgates & Internal Commerce (Oosthoek & Kedu):

- **Gêdhong Tembakau:** Refusal of Governor Van Overstraten to countenance the establishment of tollgates / tobacco warehouses (*gedhong tembakau*) in Kedu because they would function as extortionate toll barriers.
- **Economic impact:**
  - **\*** *Tollgates severely affect internal trade in tobacco.*
  - Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, acting as Commissioner in Surabaya (*Oosthoek*), was ordered to abolish tollgates in the Eastern Salient to prevent the ruin and economic depression of small local trade.
  - [Red marginal annotation:] **Tollgates in East along Sala R[iver]. Placed by Rulers. \***

---

### Language Training / Javanese Cadets (f. 340):

- Appointment of Dutch student interpreters (*Elèves in de Javaansche Taal*) attached to the Residencies to master the Javanese language.
