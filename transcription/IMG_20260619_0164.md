### Wiese Remonstrance on Gunpowder Cuts & Crown Prince Demak Incident (Jan. 1809)

* **Salute Protocol Precedent:** Wiese notes that Daendels' gunpowder ban does not apply to personal salutes for the Sultan, Chief Queen (*Permaisuri*), or Crown Prince passing the Residency house (*established 10 July 1796 by Van Overstraten and Van IJsseldijk*).
* **28 January 1809 (*Garebeg Besar* Exception):**
  * Wiese argues that cancelling traditional *Garebeg Besar* salutes would severely dishonor HB II and his family.
  * Wiese continues customary salutes while awaiting explicit orders from Daendels, advising against hasty actions that could alienate the Sultan.
* **2 January 1809 (Demak Kecu Raid Incident):**
  * Two messengers sent by the Crown Prince organize a bandit (*kècu*) gang to raid Desa Jebajjan in Demak.
  * Crown Prince's *Mantri* Tirtadiwiria claims the raid occurred without the Crown Prince's knowledge; Crown Prince warned that such incursions must not recur.
