### [Transcription]

**<u>Dec. 1808 – Jan. 1809 (cont.)</u>**  
*(ANRI Dj. Br. 22)*

- Gunpowder salutes on Garebeg festivals (*Garebeg Besar* & *Garebeg Mulud*): Resident G.W. Wiese requests that ceremonial gun salutes remain on their customary old footing to avoid alarming the Court and population.
- *2 Jan. 1809*: The two captured retainers/messengers of the Crown Prince reveal under interrogation that they were sent to organize a *kètsju* (robbery/raid) party on Desa Jebugan in Demak.
- Mantri of the Crown Prince, Tirtadiwiria, asserts that the raid took place without the personal knowledge or sanction of the Crown Prince.
