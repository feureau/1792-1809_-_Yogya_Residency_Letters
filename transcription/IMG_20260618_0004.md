## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260618_0004.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 41: *Ingekomen stukken wegens opgenomen Vorsten Landen*, 1770 / Book of Klèpu)
- **Date Range:** 1770-10-01 to 1770-11-09
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official survey and demarcation of disputed agricultural desas and cacah appanage holdings in Pajang and Gunung Kidul following the post-Giyanti land commissions; documents reports submitted by Dutch commissioners C.P. Reygen, A. van Rijck, and C.H. Boltze to Nicolaas Vos on 9 November 1770 (Kanaja Bagan); records precise fiscal measurements across 15 agricultural villages (Pengkol, Limpar, Kadung kris, Mungi, Koripan, Jimbaran, Wanadaya, Cuwela, Gebang, Puwat, Kebun Agung, Ponjong, Bolotan, Karang Pung, and Pakapapan); catalogues cacah household totals, jung/bau acreage, village tax collectors (bèkèl), and contested princely ownership partitioned among Sultan Hamengkubuwana I, Sunan Pakubuwana III, and Pangéran Mangkunagara I.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana I, Sunan Pakubuwana III, Radèn Mas Said (Mangkunagara I), Nicolaas Vos, C.P. Reygen, A. van Rijck, C.H. Boltze.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Pajang, Gunung Kidul, Ponjong, Kebun Agung, Pengkol, Limpar, Kadung kris (Yogya), Cuwela, Gebang.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Book of Klèpu (1770), Cacah household counts, Jung / Bau acreage metrics, Bèkèl revenue collectors, Appanage territorial partition.

### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Sultan Mangkubumi (Hamengkubuwana I)** (`HB I`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 11). First Sultan of Yogyakarta (reigned 1749–1792).
  * **Sunan Pakubuwana III** (`PB III`): Predecessor of PB IV (reigned 1749–1788). Signatory to the 1755 Treaty of Giyanti and 1770 Book of Klèpu boundary settlements.
  * **Radèn Mas Said (Mangkunagara I)** (`MN I`): Listed under line 26. Founder of the Mangkunagaran line (reigned 1757–1795).
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Pajang**: Listed under lines 434, 468. Ancient agricultural domain partitioned between the courts.
  * **Gunung Kidul**: Listed under line 462. Rugged southern karst province of Yogyakarta.
  * **Ponjong & Kebun Agung**: Major agrarian revenue centers in Gunung Kidul.
* **Terms:**
  * **Cacah**: Listed under line 489. Peasant household unit used for measuring land productivity and taxation (approx. 5 people).
  * **Jung**: Listed under line 504. Javanese land measurement unit (approx. 4 *bau* or 2.8 hectares).
  * **Bau**: Agrarian land area unit (1/4 of a *jung*).
  * **Bèkèl**: Listed under line 485. Village-level tax collector operating on behalf of royal apanage holders.
  * **Book of Klèpu (1770 / 1773)**: Official inter-court land register establishing territorial jurisdictions and cacah allocations between Yogyakarta, Surakarta, and the Mangkunagaran.
