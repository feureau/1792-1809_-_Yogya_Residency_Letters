### Continuation of Van Overstraten – Solo Conference (1792)

- **fol. 205**: Estimation of cost to be made = part borne by Solo, part by Yogya.
- **fol. 206**: Patihs to co-op over coolies from Gladag. V.O.C. to provide militia / ammunition.
- **fol. 207**: PB IV in beginning expresses fear that it would be a new burden on Solo, but seeing it would weigh equally heavily on Yogya agrees.
- **fol. 208**: Of Auxiliary Troops from Solo. Same as those which had been sent by PB III in Recent War + England.
- **fol. 209**: Promise of payment / upkeep by V.O.C.
- **fol. 211–212**: Cultures pepper in PB IV's lands / PB IV promises to co-operate. Promises full co-op + V.O.C. Would set aside Paji / Tanjong for cultivation; 800 cacahs most suitable lands for pepper cultivation.
- **fol. 215**: Inhabitants to be freed from corvée to attend to cultivation. Apart from local food needs, to be devoted to pepper.
- **fol. 216**: Eur. opzieners to see that product produced.
- **fol. 217**: Of Indigo in Solo — Van Rheede tries to get indigo produced for V.O.C., but initial expenses so great that has to abandon idea.
- **fol. 218–219**: PB IV promises to try out indigo in his lands, but not under V.O.C. supervision.
- **fol. 221**: Signed, sealed + signatures: PB IV / Van Ov / P. MB / P. BN / Patih Jayaningrat. (Oct. 5).
- **p. 220**: Copy of this in Dj. Br. 42.

### Van Overstraten Conference with HB II at Yogya Kraton (Aug. 1792)

*(Ref: BL Add MS 12303/12 fol. 164v)*

- **Sunday 19 Aug. 1792**: Meeting with HB II in presence of W.H. van IJsseldijk / Van R tot de Parkeleer in Kraton Yogya. To discuss affairs touched on in conference HB II / Van O of 2 Apr. 1792.
  - In presence of 5 Nayakas: Secodiningrat, Mangundipura, Natayuda / Danukusumo / Dipo Kusumo / P. Mangkubumi.
- **fol. 223**: Ratification Treaty of 2 Apr. 1792 (p. 113).
- **fol. 224**: HB II thereupon says that [son] should be appointed Crown Prince — Van O had promised to appoint MB on his return.
- **fol. 225**: If MB not acceptable, then P. Anom or P. Pringgalaya (*later P. MB II*).
- **fol. 226**: Van O says had taken no steps viz-à-viz Anom $\therefore$ MB to be appointed CP $\rightarrow$ on 20 Aug. Project Ceremonial Act: MB to be P. Adip. Anom.
- **fol. 227**: Van O accepts HB II's request made earlier that Van IJsseldijk should be allowed to stay on as Resident for at least another 2–3 years.
- **fol. 228**: *(Marked with red line)* Of measuring of lands now cultivated which had been uncultivated in 1755 – 1773 time of Book of Klèpu. To prevent arguments between Solo / Yogya; Van O wants permanent survey.
- **fol. 229**: HB II.
- **fol. 230**: Of renewal of Fort "De Herdwachter" at Boyolali. HB II promises help at earlier conference = costs to be borne by HB II. Van O inspects fort on journey to courts + specialist materials / workforce from HB II.
