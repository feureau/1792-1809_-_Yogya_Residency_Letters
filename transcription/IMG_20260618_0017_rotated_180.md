## Content

### 1792 Conference Notes (Folios 205 to 230)

* **Fol. 205:** Estimation of cost to be made = part borne by Solo, part by Yogya.
* **Fol. 206:** Patih to co-op over coolies from Gladag. V.O.C. to provide militia/ammunition.
* **Fol. 207:** PB IV in beginning expresses fear that it would be a new burden on Solo, but seeing it would weigh equally heavily on Yogya agrees.
* **Fol. 208:** Re Auxiliary Troops from Solo. Same as those which had been sent by PB III in recent war & England = Promise of payment/upkeep by V.O.C.
* **Fol. 209–211:** Cultivation of pepper in PB IV's lands. PB IV promises to co-operate. Promises full co-op with V.O.C. Would set aside Paji / Tanjong for cultivation. 800 cacahs = most suitable lands for pepper cultivation.
* **Fol. 215:** Inhabitants to be freed from corvée to attend to pepper.
* **Fol. 216:** Eur. opzieners to see that product produced.
* **Fol. 217:** Re Indigo in Solo – Van Reede tries to get indigo produced for V.O.C., but initial expenses so great that has to abandon idea.
* **Fol. 218:** PB IV promises to try out Indigo in his lands.
* **Fol. 219:** But not under V.O.C. supervision.
* **Fol. 221 / Act 5:** Signed, sealed & signatures: PB IV / Van Ov / P. MB / P. BN / Patih Jayaningrat. Copy of this in Dj. Br. 42.
* **Fol. 220 (Sun. 13 Aug. 1792):** Meeting with MB II in presence of W.H. Van IJ, Van Ov tot Dep in kraton Yogya. To discuss affairs. Touched on 13 Apr. conference MB II & Van Ov on 2 & 3 Apr. 1792 in presence of P. Nayakas: Natayuda / Danukusuma / Dipakusuma / P. Mangkubumi.
* **Fol. 223:** Ratification of treaty 2 Apr. 1792 p. 113.
* **Fol. 224:** MB II requests that CP should be apptd = Van Ov had promised to appoint MB on his return.
* **Fol. 225:** If MB not acceptable then P. Anom or P. Pringgalaya (later P. MB II).
* **Fol. 226:** Van Ov says had taken 20 steps viz-a-viz Anom. MB to be apptd CP = on 20 Aug. Project ceremonial act.
* **Fol. 227:** Van Ov accepts MB II's request made explicit that Van IJ should be allowed to stay on as Res. for at least another 2–3 years.
* **Fol. 228:** Re measuring of lands now cultivated which had been uncultivated in 1755–1773 (1779 Book of Klepu). To prevent arguments between Solo/Yogya. Van Ov wants permanent survey.
* **Fol. 229:** HB II.
* **Fol. 230:** Re Renewal of Fort "De Herdwachter" at Boyolali, promise help at earlier conference = costs to be borne by HB II & Van Ov inspect fort on journey to courts & specialist materials work done from HB II.
