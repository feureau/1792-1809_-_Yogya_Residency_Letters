## Content

### AvJ Archive Entries: 1795–1796 (Fortress Completion & Appointments)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1795–6**
* **4 March 1795:** **YOGYAKARTA FORTRESS ALMOST COMPLETE.** Quarters for Sergeants and Bookkeeper built by Eastern Regents. V.O.C. personnel move into fort. Pepper warehouse additions delayed pending arrival of Eastern Mancanagara Regents. Two officers present completion report.
* **14 March 1795:** HB II receives unofficial brother Raden Mas Wiranegara as **Pangeran Dipasana** (later rebel in 1822; exiled to Moluccas).
* **3 July 1795:** Arrival in Solo of Pangeran Adipati Natakusuma (previously exiled to Ceylon; see Ricklefs).
* **26 October 1795:** HB II reports death of son-in-law Pangeran Purwanegara (only official son of Pangeran Ngabehi).
* **9 November 1795:** HB II appoints Pangeran Ngabehi's grandson **R.T. Martalaya** as Outer Bupati (+ 500 cacahs).
* **25 December 1795:** Marriage of HB II's daughter (Raden Ajeng Katijah) with son of Madiun Regent **Raden Rongga Prawiradirja II** (Raden Maduretna). Younger brother of HB II (Pangeran Dipasana) marries sister of Raden Rongga II.
* **15 January 1796:** Raden Rongga Prawiradirja II pensioned due to near total blindness; succeeded as Bupati Wedana of Madiun by his son **Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III** (son-in-law of HB II).
