### [Transcription]

**4 March 1795**  
**Resident Yogya $\rightarrow$ Governor Java's N.E. Coast [Semarang]:**  
**FORTRESS IN YOGYA ALMOST COMPLETE:**  
Houses for Sergeants / Book-keeper (*Boekhouder*) BUILT BY Eastern Regents.  
$\therefore$ V.O.C. servants can easily move into the FORT.  
**PEPPER WAREHOUSE renewals / additions:**  
Cannot be begun until Eastern Mancanagara Regents reach Yogya at the end of this year.  
2 Officers report on *"COMPLETION of FORTRESS"*.

---

**14 March 1795**  
**Resident Yogya $\rightarrow$ Governor N.E. Coast:**  
HB II raises unofficial brother **Raden Mas Nuraman** [Uluranan] $\rightarrow$ **Pangeran Dipasana**.  
*(Note: Rebels in 1822 — exiled to Moluccas / Kebumen / Ambon).*

---

**30 July 1795**  
**Resident Sala $\rightarrow$ Governor N.E. Coast:**  
Arrival in Sala of **Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat**, previously exiled to Ceylon *(see Ricklefs)*.

---

**26 Oct 1795**  
**Resident Yogya $\rightarrow$ Governor N.E. Coast:**  
HB II reports death of son-in-law, only official son of Pangeran Ngabèhi: **Pangeran Purwanēgara**.

---

**9 Nov 1795**  
**Resident Yogya $\rightarrow$ Governor N.E. Coast:**  
HB II reports appointment of grandson of Pangeran Ngabèhi as Outer Bupati with title **R.T. Martalaya** [Mērtalaya] (+ late Ratu's lands, 500 cacahs of ricefields).

---

**25 Dec 1795**  
**Resident Yogya $\rightarrow$ Governor N.E. Coast:**  

- Marriage of daughter of HB II (Maduretna), **R. Ayu Natijah**, to son of Mancanagara Regent **Raden Rangga Prawiradirja II**. *(See letter of Van Reede, 1795)*.  
- Younger brother of HB II, **Pangeran Dipasana**, marries sister of **Raden Rangga Prawiradirja II**.

---

**15 Jan 1796**  
**Resident Yogya $\rightarrow$ Governor N.E. Coast:**  
HB II reports Raden Rangga Prawiradirja II, because of eye sickness, nearly completely loses eyesight.  
$\therefore$ Pensioned — position given to **Raden Rangga Prawiradirja III**, son-in-law of HB II.
