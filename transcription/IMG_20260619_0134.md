### Archive Entries: August 1808 (Prangwedana Fears & Daendels Direct Banishment Order)

* **14 August 1808 (Prangwedana Legion Fears):**
  * HB II fears Pangeran Prangwedana's legion harbors hostile intentions against Yogyakarta.
  * Sultan cancels planned dispatch of Pangerans Dipakusuma, Mangkukusuma, and Mangkudiningrat to outer provinces, retaining all troops in Yogyakarta to meet potential threats.
  * Engelhard reassures HB II that Prangwedana acts under strict government orders and confirms Prangwedana's legion is assigned garrison duty in Klaten.
* **Daendels Demand for Sumadiningrat's Banishment:**
  * **Daendels demands the immediate banishment of R.T. Sumadiningrat.**
  * Engelhard advises against banishment, noting accusations against Sumadiningrat rely on single-witness testimony. Engelhard monitors R.T. Natadiningrat closely.
* **15 August 1808 (Daendels March on Klaten/Yogya):**
  * Daendels dispatches orders from Bojong (13 Aug.) for **3,000 troops to march on Klaten and Yogyakarta**.
  * HB II summons Engelhard to court to explain Daendels' suspicions. HB II publicly declares before *Nayakas* that he does not mistrust Daendels, and agrees to send Engelhard and Danureja II to Klaten to await Daendels.
