### [Chronology of Yogyakarta Tax Farm Renewals (1774–1804)]

* **Resident Lapro's Re-entry:**
  * `.: given Back to Lapro who eventually accepts Raising of Tax to 10,000 Sp. D.`

* **3rd Renewal under Resident Van Rhijn:**
  * `3rd Renewal of Tax-Farm under van Rhijn: no raise But v. R. forced to give a "present" to HB I (i.e. Uang Bêkti):`
  * `2 combs set + jewels worth 1,200 R.R. each`

* **1786 (Van IJsseldijk's 6-Year Renewal):**
  * `Remains as such Till van IJ [Van IJsseldijk] takes over from van Rhijn in 1786.`
  * `Renewal for 6 years @ 44,000 [Ronde Realen]`
  * `But Uang Bêkti Raised to 1,500 + clothes / + Beautiful Cane set + diamonds.`
  * *(Exchange rate in red ink:)* `1 ronde real = f 2.80`

* **1792 Renewal:**
  * `1792 Renewal for 3 years: Raised to 46,000; no Uang Bêkti.`

* **1795 Renewal:**
  * `1795: Tax Farm Raised to 56,000 by 10,000 to enormous sum.`

* **1798–1804 (Van den Berg & Waterloo Tenures):**
  * `No change 1798 - 1803 under van den Berg on renewal Date.`
  * `Dec. 1804: Waterloo presses for Tax to be lowered, But Tax would probably remain as 56,000.`
