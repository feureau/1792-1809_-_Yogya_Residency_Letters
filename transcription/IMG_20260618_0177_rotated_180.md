### History of Yogyakarta Tax Farm Escalations (1786–1804) & Strandgeld

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 20.86 (1805)**
* **Lease Escalation Chronology:**
  * **1786 (Van IJsseldijk):** 6-year renewal @ 44,000 R.R., but *Hang Bikti* raised to 1,500 + luxury garments and a diamond-studded cane (1 Ronde Real = 2.80 guilders).
  * **1792 Renewal:** 3-year renewal raised to 46,000 + 20 *Hang Bikti*.
  * **1795 Renewal:** Tax farm raised by 10,000 to an enormous **56,000 Rixdollars**.
  * **1798–1803 (Van den Berg):** Fixed @ 56,000.
  * **Dec. 1804 (Waterloo):** Waterloo petitions for tax reduction, but rate remains fixed @ 56,000 Rixdollars per Sultan's insistence.
* **Royal Domain Autonomy:** Resident never interferes directly in Royal Domain management or bird's nest harvesting.
* **Navy Strandgeld:** 10,000 Sp. Real p.a. paid by Navy; 1,000,000 Np.P. Total *strandgeld* payments.
