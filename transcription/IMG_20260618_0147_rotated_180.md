### Archive Entries: November 1803 (Malangnegara Lineage & Kedu Toll Taxes)

* Reference: **A.N. Yogya Letters No. 49 (Nov. 1803)**
* **Tobacco Toll Taxes (4 Nov. 1803):** Taxes on tobacco at Kedu tollgates set at 2 dubbeltjes / 1 duit per pikul; 3 dubbeltjes in Mataram. Tax per pikul increased by 1–2 dubbeltjes in Kedu.
* **Bupati of Pacitan:** Raden Ngabehi Jasrawijaya (Regent of Padangan; later Patih to R. PWD III, killed 17 Oct. 1810).
* **Malangnegara Lineage & Royal Immunity:**
  * Mas Tumenggung Jumanagara and Mas Ngabehi Jumadirja held in extraordinary regard by Sultan HB II.
  * Raden Ayu Rangga Jasrawijaya is eldest daughter of Ratu Ageng Tegalreja (HB II addresses her as "mother").
  * **Lineage:** Grandson of Malangnegara, famous in Mataram for accompanying HB I throughout the Third Javanese War of Succession (1749–1755). HB I declared: *"The blood of Malangnegara is the same as my blood."*
  * Extremely difficult to punish Malangnegara descendants for crimes (including murdering Europeans in Kedu with their own hands).
  * Sister of Mas Ng. Jumadirja is HB II's 1st concubine (R.A. Nepuk); her children are deeply beloved by HB II.
  * Sultan pardons Sumanasara, Ustandi, and Sumadirja (noted for good looks at court).
* **Court News:** HB II gradually recovers from grief over Ratu Ageng's death and moves to Ngawatreja.
