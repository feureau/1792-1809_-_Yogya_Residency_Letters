## Archival & Historical Notes

- **Deathbed Timeline of Ratu Agêng Tegalreja:**
  - 11 October 1803: Ratu Agêng shows temporary slight improvement in health.
  - 17 October 1803: Erroneously reported to have passed away at 7:00 AM; Raden Rangga's political standing begins to recover upon Danureja II standing surety for his future conduct. Rangga receives his official pardon through the intercession of his two royal brothers-in-law, Raden Tumenggung Sumadiningrat and Patih Danureja II; his wife, Ratu Maduretna, is allowed to return home.
  - 18 October 1803: R.T. Sumadiningrat informs the Resident that the queen had suffered a prolonged swoon/coma (*"long fainting spell"*) rather than dying in the morning. She officially expires at 3:00 PM on 18 October 1803. Resident Waterloo pays a formal visit of condolence to HB II in the Kraton.
- **Funeral Rites and State Burial (25 October 1803):**
  - Ratu Agêng lies in state in the Kadipatèn (the residence of the Crown Prince, later HB III, who had been raised under her care alongside Prince Dipanegara).
  - Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and all members of the royal family attend the lying-in-state.
  - The burial cortege proceeds to the royal graveyard at **Imagiri**.
  - Strict ceremonial protocol: Crown Prince accompanies the funeral procession to the southern entrance (*Rear Passe Baan* / South Alun-alun). Upon returning to the Kadipatèn at 5:00 PM, he is directed by two elder royal wives to enter through the rear gate, but refuses to accept any diminution of prestige and insists on entering through the main front entrance.
