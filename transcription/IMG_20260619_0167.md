### Raden Rongga River Proa Seizures, Etiquette Incident, & Public Apology (1808)

* **Solo River Proa Seizures:**
  * Surakarta court complains that Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III detained 35 Surakarta *prau pingluput* boats on the Solo River.
  * During his return journey from Surabaya, Daendels allowed his flotilla to pass custom-free, but Rangga's toll collectors detained the proas. Joint commission sent to investigate (*BL Add. MS 12303 f. 118v*).
* **Carriage Etiquette Incident & Public Apology:**
  * Resident Van Braam rides out of the fort in his carriage wearing his official gold *payung*. **Raden Rongga III cuts across Van Braam's path, forcing the Resident's coachman to stop dead.**
  * Engelhard advises Van Braam to lodge a formal complaint with HB II. HB II severely criticizes Rangga.
  * Rangga apologizes in Javanese/Malay, claiming no insult was intended. Van Braam rejects the private apology and demands a public apology.
  * **Public Apology on the Paseban:** **HB II orders Rangga to apologize in a loud, clear voice before the entire court (*Bodi-Dalems*, courtiers, and Crown Prince) on the Paseban.** Rangga executes the order with intense humiliation and red-faced embarrassment, creating a sensation across the court.
* **Oct. 1808 Klaten Meeting:** Van Braam and Engelhard meet Rangga and Prangwedana at Klaten to conclude the *Gladhag* agreement.
