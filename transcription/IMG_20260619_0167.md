### [Transcription]

Arsip Nasional No. 22.] **1809 (Yogya)**

*9 Jan. 1809*  
*Wiese to Sec. Gen. Veekens*  
Arrival of CAPT. MOLLET & Dragoons to Yogya.

*13 Jan. 1809*  
*Wiese to Daendels*  
MULUD / April — Regents & families & followers from MN to Yogya.  
<u>Need for BUILDING of a NEW POWDER MAGAZINE:</u>

*14 Jan. 1809*  
*Wiese to Sec. Gen. Veekens*  
10 cases of OPIUM needed for Yogya.

*19 Jan. 1809*  
*Wiese to Daendels*  
European Coachman at Yogya Kraton Johan Hendrik Boekhee dies.  
On advice of Comm. Chastelenvieux — Corporal Lucas Lebeveld to be appointed Sergeant [But too old for military service?]

*22 Jan. 1809*  
*Wiese to Sec. Gen. Veekens*  
500 lbs of GUNPOWDER allowed by Daendels for SULTAN.  
After CEREMONY of BESAR at to PASEBAN CROWN PRINCE & various Yogya nobles to RESIDENCY HOUSE:  
50 lbs used for CEREMONIAL SALUTES.

*26 Jan. 1809*  
*Wiese to Daendels*  

[Red ink: *Rumour of large mil. Exercise (Sala)*]

Rumour transmitted by Danureja that at SALA unusually large MILITARY EXERCISE (exercitien) takes PLACE.  

[R.T. NATADININGRAT, Kyai SINDUNEGARA Dispatched to Sala to ascertain TRUTH.]
