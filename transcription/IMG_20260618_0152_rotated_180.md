### Archive Entries: November 1803 (Inter-Court Rivalry & Banyumas Chinese Plot)

* **30 November 1803 (Waterloo -> Engelhard):**
  * Circular letters sent to Patihs of Solo and Yogya to settle outstanding inter-court disputes following royal marriages.
  * *Assessment of Surakarta Sunan (PB IV):* *"The Sunan is a dissembler in his heart, who will never be on good terms with Van den Berg, unless taken into his confidence... Van den Berg must truly in Solo practice more politics and hypocrisy whenever he talks with the Patih."*
  * *Surakarta Antagonism:* Deep-seated hatred from Solo toward Yogyakarta court.
  * **Quote of Sunan Pakubuwana IV:**
    > *"Men zeggen van de Keizer goed opgehoord, zoo dat er al dadelijk in de zaaken gewerkt is, zoo moet ik mij volkomen vertrouwen op de Compagnies stellen, dat zij nimmer zal gedoogen dat mijn hof voor dat van Yogya zal vernederd worden, want ik ben Vorst die zich over geschillen tusschen hier en Yogya met het regt niet inlaat..."*
* **Pangeran Mangkubumi & Banyumas Plot:**
  * Trustworthy *Bupati* **Sindunegara** reports Pangeran Mangkubumi (Solo) in league with Javanese conspirators in Banyumas.
  * Mangkubumi's ill-temper noted: *"A prince that would be capable of anything."*
  * Spy dispatched to Banyumas reports the Chinese plot involves only 27 *peranakans* who swore a mutual brotherhood (*kongsi*) without political danger.
