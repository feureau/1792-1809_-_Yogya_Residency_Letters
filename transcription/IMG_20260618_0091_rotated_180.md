### The "Leen" Controversy & Danureja II's Appointment:

- Van Reede considers it far better to leave Danureja II without an official *Acte van Aanstelling* (Act of Appointment) rather than engage in an open diplomatic breach and quarrel with HB II over the Dutch feudal term *"leen"* (fief) [red margin bar].
- **HB II was terribly exercised and agitated over the word *"leen"*** [underlined in red].
- The formal *Acte* had consequently **not yet been delivered as of 1801**.
- Resident Johannes Gerard van den Berg is noted for possessing unusual skill and fluency in the Javanese language.
- **August 1797:** Matthijs Waterloo takes over from Second Resident Palm in Yogyakarta due to the latter’s failing health.

---

### Military Garrisons & Pepper Warehouses:

- **Garrison strengths:**
  - Yogyakarta: 164 men.
  - Surakarta: 188 men.
- Nederburgh fixed the planned garrison strengths:
  - Surakarta / Boyolali / Salatiga / Ungaran line: 300 men (3 companies).
  - Yogyakarta: 200 infantrymen + 50 cavalry + 25 artillerymen.
- **Pepper Warehouses (*Pakhuizen*):**
  - Both Sunan and Sultan asked to construct pepper warehouses.
  - The location proposed by the Yogyakarta Residency was better suited than Surakarta’s.
  - However, Eastern Mancanagara regents suddenly marched back home when the British fleet blockaded Batavia; consequently, the warehouses were left unfinished.

---

### V.O.C. Cannons & British Invasion Fears:

[Red annotation: **V.O.C. Cannons**]

- HB II requested old, unserviceable V.O.C. cannons which the company was preparing to melt down.
- Cannons were sold to HB II **at the cost of scrap metal** [red underline: *sold at cost of scrap metal*].
- Consideration of constructing defensive fortifications on the Jambu hills revived due to continuous dread of a British invasion, as British warships appeared incessantly along the coast of Java.

---

### Religious Fanaticism & Suborned Prophets:

- Marked decline in the numbers of religious fanatics / ascetics (*tapas*).
- **Prophet Kyai Iwen / Shen:** Suborned and bought out by the V.O.C. government so that his public prophecies in Surakarta were carefully tailored to be favourable to the Dutch Company.
