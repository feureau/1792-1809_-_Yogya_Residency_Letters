| **August 1797** | ### Text & Marginal Annotations<br><br>[Top notations:]<br><br>- `N.O.K. 47`<br>- `MvO van Reede 5.`<br><br>### The "Leen" Controversy & Danureja II's Appointment:<br><br>- Van Reede considers it far better to leave Danureja II without an official *Acte van Aanstelling* (Act of Appointment) rather than engage in an open diplomatic breach and quarrel with HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] over the Dutch feudal term *"leen"* (fief) [red margin bar].<br>- **HB II was terribly exercised and agitated over the word *"leen"*** [underlined in red].<br>- The formal *Acte* had consequently **not yet been delivered as of 1801**.<br>- Resident Johannes Gerard van den Berg is noted for possessing unusual skill and fluency in the Javanese language.<br>- **August 1797:** Matthijs Waterloo takes over from Second Resident Palm in Yogyakarta due to the latter’s failing health.<br><br>### Military Garrisons & Pepper Warehouses:<br><br>- **Garrison strengths:**<br>  - Yogyakarta: 164 men.<br>  - Surakarta: 188 men.<br>- Nederburgh fixed the planned garrison strengths:<br>  - Surakarta / Boyolali / Salatiga / Ungaran line: 300 men (3 companies).<br>  - Yogyakarta: 200 infantrymen + 50 cavalry + 25 artillerymen.<br>- **Pepper Warehouses (*Pakhuizen*):**<br>  - Both Sunan and Sultan asked to construct pepper warehouses.<br>  - The location proposed by the Yogyakarta Residency was better suited than Surakarta’s.<br>  - However, Eastern Mancanagara regents suddenly marched back home when the British fleet blockaded Batavia; consequently, the warehouses were left unfinished.<br><br>### V.O.C. Cannons & British Invasion Fears:<br><br>[Red annotation: **V.O.C. Cannons**]<br><br>- HB II requested old, unserviceable V.O.C. cannons which the company was preparing to melt down.<br>- Cannons were sold to HB II **at the cost of scrap metal** [red underline: *sold at cost of scrap metal*].<br>- Consideration of constructing defensive fortifications on the Jambu hills revived due to continuous dread of a British invasion, as British warships appeared incessantly along the coast of Java.<br><br>### Religious Fanaticism & Suborned Prophets:<br><br>- Marked decline in the numbers of religious fanatics / ascetics (*tapas*).<br>- **Prophet Kyai Iwen / Shen:** Suborned and bought out by the V.O.C. government so that his public prophecies in Surakarta were carefully tailored to be favourable to the Dutch Company. |
