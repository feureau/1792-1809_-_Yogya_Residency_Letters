### Van Reede Memorie van Overgave 1801: Garrisons, Ordnance, and Patih Title Dispute

* Reference: **N.O.K. 47 MvO Van Reede 5**
* **Patih Title Dispute (*Leen*):** Van Reede considers it better to leave Danureja II without an Act of Appointment (*Aanstelling*) rather than provoke a quarrel with HB II over the term *"Leen"* (fief), which greatly exercises HB II (appointment act still withheld in 1801).
* **Javanese Language Expertise:** J.G. van den Berg noted for unusual fluency in Javanese.
* **Residency Succession:** In August 1797, **Mr. Waterloo** replaces 2nd Resident Palm in Yogya due to Palm's ill health.
* **Garrison Strengths:**
  * Yogya Garrison: 164 men.
  * Surakarta Garrison: 188 men.
  * Regional Netherlands Forces (Solo, Boyolali, Salatiga, Ungaran): 300 men (3 companies).
  * Yogya Forces: 200 men + 50 cavalry / 25 artillerymen.
* **Pepper Warehouse:** Both courts requested to construct a pepper warehouse adjacent to Yogya Residency (preferred over Solo). Project interrupted when Eastern Mancanagara Regents suddenly return to Surakarta due to British naval blockade of Batavia.
* **Scrap Metal Ordnance:** HB II requests old V.O.C. cannons slated to be melted down, purchasing them at scrap metal cost.
* **Fortification Strategy:** Plan for Jambu Hills fort reconsidered due to persistent fears of British invasion along the coast.
* **Religious Movements:** Decline noted in numbers of *fanatics* / *tapas*. Prophet Kyai Tjen bought out by V.O.C. to stop issuing anti-Dutch prophecies in Surakarta.
