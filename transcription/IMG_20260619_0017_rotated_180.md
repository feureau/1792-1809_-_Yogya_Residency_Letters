### Surakarta Crown Prince (Future PB V) & Patih Danuningrat Evaluation

* Reference: **Van Alphen - Engelhard No. 239 (Aanw. 1900) No. XXII - 3**
* **Crown Prince (Future Pakubuwana V) Behavior:**
  * Highly unsociable character. Prefers European dress and **wears Dutch attire when visiting the Resident**, causing resentment among traditional courtiers.
  * At loggerheads with Pangeran Mangkubumi (who mocks PB IV's harem). If Crown Prince ascends throne, Mangkubumi expected to flee court.
* **Surakarta Royal Children:** Gusti Timur (future Purbaya) and 2 daughters born of chief queen; other children by secondary wives politically unimportant, though holding high self-opinions (hardly deigning to move aside for Resident's carriage).
* **Patih Danuningrat Evaluation:**
  * **Incompetent Patih:** Procrastinates on all state affairs; favorite of PB IV, but unintelligent.
  * **Religious Extremism:** Champion of Islamic priests and "fanatical man"; attends mosque every Friday and takes part in prayers, but drinks wine.
  * Resident-Patih relations governed strictly by the *Wěrat Anggèr*.
* **1st Outer Regent R.T. Cikranagara:** Friendly toward Resident; frequently borrows large sums of money.
