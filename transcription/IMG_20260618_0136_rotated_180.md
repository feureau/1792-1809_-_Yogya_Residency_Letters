## Archival & Historical Notes

- **Surakarta Harem Friction:** Crown Prince (future PB V) blocks his uncle Pangeran Mangkubumi from visiting PB IV's private chambers at the Pengging country estate, furious at Mangkubumi's frequent presence in the royal harem.
- **Prangwedana's Claim:** Pangeran Prangwedana demands restitution of 27,000 *ronde maaten* in gold jewelry held from Governor Pieter Gerardus van Overstraten / Van Rheede.
- **Eurasian Secretary d'Abo:** Mentions the Eurasian clerk/translator d'Abo insinuating himself into Resident Van den Berg's entourage in Surakarta, praised for having a "quick intelligence" and being retained for translation and copying.
- **HB II's Fine on Patih and Regents (11 Jan 1802):**
  - Sultan Hamengkubuwana II imposes a harsh fine of 2,000 *ronde maaten* on Patih Danureja II and several bupatis because they stayed enjoying the European New Year's party at the Residency and failed to attend the palace funeral memorial (*sedekah*) for the Sultan's deceased elder sister, Ratu Bêndara (died 30 Dec 1801).
  - The Resident notes exasperatedly that the Company ought to strip HB II of his excessive wealth and instill fear of the VOC.
- **Opium Smuggling Economics (3 Feb 1802):**
  - Illicit opium trade thrives due to price arbitrage: smuggled chests sell at 450–500 reals in Yogyakarta versus 700 reals in Batavia.
  - Yogya consumes approximately 40 chests per annum.
  - The Chinese Head *Bandhar* is driven into bankruptcy due to exorbitant official kickbacks demanded by the Governor of Java's Northeast Coast (250 *R.R.* per chest). The Captains of Yogya and Kedu sell 4,000 *R.R.* worth of opium annually.
- **Medono Sugar Mill Raid (14 Feb 1802):** The plundering of the Chinese Captain's sugar mill at Medono yields 4,000 Spanish Dollars, stolen by Panji Padmawijaya, which reportedly ended up in Sultan Hamengkubuwana II's private treasury.
- **Dramatic Escape from Taman Sari (24 Feb 1802):**
  - Royal commanders Panji Jayêngrana and Kuwon Sindureja are dispatched to the slopes of Mount Merapi to hunt down bandits who murdered and robbed a trade agent belonging to Ratu Agêng (Tegalreja).
  - Three captured bandits had been incarcerated by HB II inside a 40-foot deep subterranean well located on an island within the **Taman Sari** water palace complex; remarkably, they managed to scale out and climb over three extremely high perimeter walls to escape.
