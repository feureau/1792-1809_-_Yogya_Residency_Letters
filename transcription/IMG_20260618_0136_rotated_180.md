## Content

### Archive Entries: 1802–1803 (Pengging Incident, Opium Trade, & Taman Sari Escape)

* **Pengging Harem Incident:** Crown Prince refuses to permit Pangeran Mangkubumi to visit PB IV in his bedroom at Pengging, as Mangkubumi was too frequently in PB IV's harem.
* **Prangwedana Jewels:** Prangwedana demands return of 27,000 Rixdollars worth of gold jewels from Van Reede's estate.
* **11 January 1802:** **HB II fines Patih Danureja II and Regents 2,000 Rixdollars** for remaining too long at the Residency's New Year party and missing the *Sedekah* ceremony for late Ratu Bindara in the Kraton (died 30 Dec. 1801). Resident notes Company must curb HB II's financial arrogance.
* **3 February 1802 (Opium Trade in Yogya):**
  * Opium smuggling widespread; sold at 450–500 Rixdollars per chest in Yogya vs. 700 in Batavia. 40 chests imported annually into Yogya.
  * Chinese Head Bandhar goes bankrupt paying excessive cuts to Governor (250 R.R./chest). Yogya/Kedu Capitan Cina sells 4,000 R.R. worth of opium annually.
* **14 February 1802 (Medono Sugar Mill Plunder):** Medono sugar mill plundered; Capitan Cina loses 4,000 Np.D. (stolen by Panji Padmawijaya; eventually finds its way into HB II's coffers).
* **24 February 1802 (Bandit Escape from Taman Sari):**
  * Detachment sent to Merapi foothills to recapture bandit who murdered Ratu Ageng's trader.
  * 3 bandits imprisoned by HB II in a 40-foot deep well on an island in **Taman Sari**, but scale 3 high walls and escape.
