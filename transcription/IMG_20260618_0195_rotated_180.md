# Transcription: IMG_20260618_0195_rotated_180.jpg

**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_20260618_0195_rotated_180.jpg`  
**Folio / Reference:** A.N. Yogya Letters No. 49  
**Date Range:** 27 June 1805 – 26 August 1805  
**Primary Subjects:**  
- HB II's mission to Mecca (June 1805): request for Dutch financial assistance for pilgrim passports (*Pass*)  
- Detention of Mecca envoys in Semarang for 2 months; Ki Sindunegara and R.T. Natadiningrat convey HB II's shame  
- Engelhard's explanation: Batavia government's refusal due to mission preparations and danger of British naval blockade  
- Official elevation of HB II's consorts and daughters (26 August 1805): Danureja II, Raden Rangga Prawiradirja III, and R.T. Natadiningrat inform Waterloo  
- Conferred royal titles:  
- Ratu Anom -> **Ratu Mas**  
- Raden Ayu Sriwulan -> **Ratu Kencana Wulan**  
- Raden Ayu Sumadiningrat -> **Ratu Bendahara**  
- Raden Ayu Danureja -> **Ratu Angger**  
- Raden Ayu Rangga -> **Ratu Maduretna**  
- Raden Ayu Supirah (eldest daughter of Ratu Kencana Wulan) -> **Ratu Anom**  
- Engelhard's marginal annotation: title *Kencana Wulan* provokes Surakarta (PB IV's consort bears the same title); notes Sriwulan is a concubine of low birth
