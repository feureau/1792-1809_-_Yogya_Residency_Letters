### Continuation of Dutch Manuscript Tollgate Tariff Increases

1. <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Een Javaan van Passantran na Samarang meede neemende matjes betaalt 40 st[ui]v[ers]: per paard word thans verhoogt met 8 stuivers — [tot 48 stuivers].</ins>  
   *(A Javanese from Pesantren to Semarang conveying mats pays 40 stuivers per horse; this is now increased by 8 stuivers [to 48 stuivers].)*

2. <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Een Javaan van hier na Samarang meede neemende kleedjes à 10 st[ui]v[ers]: ieder draagpaard word thans verhoogt met 5 stuivers — [tot 15 stuivers].</ins>  
   *(A Javanese from here to Semarang taking cloths at 10 stuivers per pack horse; this is now increased by 5 stuivers [to 15 stuivers].)*

3. <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Een Javaan van hier na Samarang diverse goederen draagende [en] voerende betaalt 10 st[ui]v[ers]: word verhoogt met 2½ stuivers — [tot 12½ stuivers].</ins>  
   *(A Javanese carrying and transporting diverse goods from here to Semarang pays 10 stuivers; this is increased by 2½ stuivers [to 12½ stuivers].)*

4. <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Een Chinees van de Bagalen na Samarang meede neemende kleedjes betaalt aan post 1 rondemat voor 5 picul, word verhoogt met 1 rondemat voor de 4 piculs —</ins>  
   *(A Chinese from Bagelen to Semarang taking cloths pays at the post 1 rondemat per 5 piculs; this is increased to 1 rondemat per 4 piculs —)*

5. <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Een Javaan van de Bagalen na Samarang meede neemende kleedjes betaalt aan post 21¼ st[ui]v[ers]: word verhoogt met 3¼ stuivers — [tot 24½ stuivers].</ins>  
   *(A Javanese from Bagelen to Semarang taking cloths pays at the post 21¼ stuivers; this is increased by 3¼ stuivers [to 24½ stuivers].)*

6. <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Een Javaan van Samarang na Djocjo meede brengende gember, betaalt aan post 18¾ st[ui]v[ers]: per paard word verhoogt met 3¾ stuivers — [tot 22½ stuivers].</ins>  
   *(A Javanese from Semarang to Yogya bringing ginger pays at the post 18¾ stuivers per horse; this is increased by 3¾ stuivers [to 22½ stuivers].)*

7. <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Een Chinees van Samarang na Djocjo meede brengende gember, betaalt aan post 12 st[ui]v[ers]: per paard word verhoogt met 4 stuivers — [tot 16 stuivers].</ins>  
   *(A Chinese from Semarang to Yogya bringing ginger pays at the post 12 stuivers per horse; this is increased by 4 stuivers [to 16 stuivers].)*

8. <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Een Chin: of Javaan van Samarang na Djocjo meede brengende gember betaalt aan post na de oude gebruiken / NB: per picullankers /</ins>  
   *(A Chinese or Javanese from Semarang to Yogya bringing ginger pays at the post according to old custom / NB: per pikul carriers /)*
