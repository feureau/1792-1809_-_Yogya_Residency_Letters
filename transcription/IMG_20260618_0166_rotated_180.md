### Yogya-Solo Treaties & Security Agreement (February – September 1804)

* Reference: **P.J. Br. 42a (Yogya-Solo Agreements 1792–1804)**
* **2 April 1792 Precedent:** *Acte van Verband* granting hereditary succession to Yogyakarta Crown Prince (Pangeran Adipati Anom), passing over 3 older brothers.
* **11 February 1804 Treaty (pp. 76–79):**
  * *Acte van Overeenkomst* signed by Patihs Mangkunkusuma (Solo) and Danureja II (Yogya) to settle all outstanding disputes: Nclang, Pakidulan, Pasampakkan, Gentan, Paremban, Lobang, and Raden Rongga III's Delanggu incident.
  * Negotiated on specific orders of HB II, PB IV, and N. Engelhard (6 copies executed; 2 per court, 2 for Engelhard).
  * Signed 2 Feb. 1804 (2 Nawal 1730) by J.G. van den Berg and M. Waterloo; ratified in Surakarta on 9 Feb. 1804.
* **Importance of Klaten:** Klaten designated as the neutral meeting place (*rendezvous*) for joint inter-court negotiations.
* **27 September 1804 Security Accord (pp. 87–96):**
  * Conference between Danureja II and Patih Danuningrat in Surakarta (signed 27 Sept. 1804 / 21 Jumadilakir Alip 1731 by J.G. van den Berg).
  * **Articles 1–4:**
    1. *Mantris* and *Tampings* granted right to arrest vagabonds and extradite them to Surakarta.
    2. Officials required to swear an oath never to aid or abet *kècus* (bandits).
    3. Royal orders issued to actively hunt out *kècus*; all appanage holders required to instruct lowest village *bekels* to maintain local security.
