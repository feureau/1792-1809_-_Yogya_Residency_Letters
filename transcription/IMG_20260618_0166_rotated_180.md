### [11 February 1804: Inter-Court Settlement Treaty (pp. 76–79)]

* **Date & Source:** `(11 Feb. 1804)`
* **Header (in red ink):** `Yogya / Solo agreement (pp. 76-79)`
* **Treaty Terms:**
  * `Acte van overeenkomst R. Adip. Mangku[praja] / Danureja II`
  * `to settle quickly as possible outstanding issues between 2 courts:`
  * `|| Sêcang, Pakidulan, Pasampakkan, Gintan, Parembang, Lobang.` *(marked with double vertical bars)*
  * `-> + Delanggu affair + R. Rongga in interests of peace between 2 courts.`
  * `.: Patihs discuss outstanding matters + all differences settled.`
  * `meetings arranged + special orders of HB II / PB IV / N. Engelhard.`
  * `6 copies made: 2 for each of courts, 2 for N. Engelhard.`
* **Signing & Ratification:**
  * `2 Sawal 1730 (2 Feb. 1804) signed by MR / DR`
  * `J.G. van den Berg / M. Waterloo`
  * `signed in H.O. & Res. Solo -> 9 Feb. 1804`
  * `at // FORT at KLATÈN not yet built //`
* **Marginal Annotation:**
  * `IMPORTANCE of KLATHÊN as a Rendezvous.`

### [27 September 1804: Vorstenlanden Security Convention (pp. 87–96)]

* **Date & Context:** `(27 Sept. 1804)   (pp. 87-96)`
* **Signing:**
  * `Meeting DR II / Danuningrat at Solo. Signed J.G. van den Berg.`
  * `21 Jumadilakir Alip 1731 (27 Sept. 1804)`
  * `For local security in VL [Vorstenlanden].`
* **Four Security Articles:**
  1. `Mantris / Tampings to have Right to Round up Vagabonds + send them to Solo.`
  2. `To swear an oath never to aid / abett kècus.`
  3. `Measures taken to Deliver Royal orders to seek out kècus.`
  4. `Every appanage holder to give order to lowest bekel to maintain local security.`
