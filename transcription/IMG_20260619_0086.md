### Surakarta Embassy to Semarang & Requisition of Cavalry Horses (May 1808)

* Reference: **Pj. Br. 23 (22 May 1808 - Van Braam to Daendels)**
* **PB IV & Surakarta Delegation:**
  * PB IV reads Daendels' missive to assembled princes and officials; visits Van Braam incognito that evening to discuss envoys to Semarang.
  * PB IV selects Pangeran Mangkubumi (despite noting his lack of refined manners); Van Braam suggests adding **Pangeran Cakrakusuma** (young troop commander, brother-in-law, and confidante).
  * *Delegation:* Pangeran Mangkubumi, Pangeran Cakrakusuma, and Prangwedana depart for Semarang on Friday under 2nd Resident Servatius' escort; lodging provided near Bojong.
* **Cavalry Horse Requisitions (5 May 1808 - Engelhard to DR II):**
  * Engelhard requests Danureja II's assistance in purchasing cavalry horses in Semarang (150 horses still undelivered).
  * HB II reminded of treaty obligation to assist Dutch forces during foreign invasions (*ref: 1806 British attack on Gresik*).
  * **Yogyakarta subjects compelled to sell cavalry horses to the Dutch @ 50–70 Spanish Dollars per horse.**
