### Archive Entries: January 1796 (Death of Mangkunagara I & Mangkunagaran Succession)

* Reference: **N.O.K. 47. "Samarang VI Jan - Juni 1796"**
* **2 January 1796 (Van Overstraten to W.A. Alting):**
  * **Death of Mangkunagara I:** Mangkunagara I dies early in the morning on **28 December 1795**.
  * Resident Van Reede goes immediately to Mangkunagaran *Dalem* accompanied only by a slave; secures the residence with grenadiers and piquet guards.
  * **Raden Ayu Nokartaji:** Brought up by MN I; transferred to Surakarta Kraton in a carriage.
  * **Yogyakarta Intentions:** Pangeran Mangkubumi of Yogya planned to enter the Mangkunagaran *Dalem* to claim R.A. Nokartaji and take revenge on Prangwedana.
  * **Burial:** Body of MN I buried at Mengadeg (*Matesih*), brought up by Ratu Bendara (**NOT Imagiri**).
  * **Surakarta Demands:** Susuhunan Pakubuwana IV demands return of all Mangkunagaran lands exceeding the baseline 4,000 cacahs.
  * **Maritime Threat (23 Dec. 1795):** Gezaghebber of Oosthoek reports 7 British ships anchored at Pulo Bawean / Banyuwangi (taking on water and firewood at Pampang).
  * **Prangwedana Duties:** Agreement reached on 17 Dec. 1795 regarding duties of PWD.
  * **5 January 1796:** Pangeran Mangkubumi (Yogya) urgently petitions PB IV to take action against Prangwedana.
