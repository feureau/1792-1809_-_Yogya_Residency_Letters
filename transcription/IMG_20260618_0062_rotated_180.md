### [Transcription]

#### 1. Death of Mangkunegara I (28 December 1795)

- **28 Dec. 1795:** Mangkunegara I dies early in the morning.  
- Resident Van Reede proceeds accompanied only by a slave to the Mangkunegaran Dalem to secure the doors; grenadiers and a military picket had been dispatched earlier to preserve order.  
- **Raden Ayu Sekartaji:**  
  *[Red ink note:]* **R.A. Sekartaji Brought up By MN I.**  
  Sister of PB IV, brought up by MN I; escorted back to the Surakarta Kraton in a carriage.  

- **Pangeran Mangkubumi's Plot:**  
  Pangeran Mangkubumi planned to force an entry into the Mangkunegaran Dalem to seize R.A. Sekartaji and wreak vengeance upon Prangwedana.  

- **Burial of Mangkunegara I:**  
  Body of MN I buried at **Mangadeg** (Matesih).  
  *[Red marginal notes:]* Land bought by Ratu Bèndara; **NOT IMAGIRI**; *(cf. Mangkunegaran oral tradition claiming the body of MN I was buried secretly in the MN Dalem)*.  

- Resident Van Reede maintains continuous vigil at the Mangkunegaran Dalem, leaving only briefly for the New Year's reception at the Residency to avoid telegraphing any panic or fear of political upheaval.  
- PB IV immediately demands the return of all lands held by MN I in excess of 4,000 cacahs.

#### 2. English Threat & Court Intrigues

- **23 Dec. 1795:** Gezaghebber of the Oosthoek reports that 7 English warships have anchored at Ulu Pampang (Banyuwangi) taking on fresh water and firewood.  
- **17 Dec. 1795:** Agreement formally reached on the ceremonial duties and status of Pangeran Prangwedana (*Nota van Schikking*).  
- **5 Jan. 1796 (Van Overstraten to Alting):**  
  *[Red ink heading:]* **MB urging attack on PWD.**  
  Pangeran Mangkubumi makes urgent representations to PB IV to take aggressive military action against Prangwedana.
