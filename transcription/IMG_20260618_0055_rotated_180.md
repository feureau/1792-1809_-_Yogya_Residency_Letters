### Garrisons, Fortresses, Warehouses, and Gladag Systems (1793–1796)

* **Garrison Strengths (1796):**
  * Surakarta Garrison: 183 men.
  * Yogyakarta Garrison: 193 men.
  * 2 Dutch language students (*leerlingen in de Javaansche taal*) stationed (1 per court).
  * Due to shortage of European troops, no reinforcements sent to Semarang. In 1780s (Van den Burgh), infantry strength was 100 per court.
* **Regional Forts:**
  * Ungaran: *"De Ontmoeting"*
  * Salatiga: *"De Hersteller"*
  * Boyolali: *"De Veldwachter"* (repairs initiated in 1793).
* **Mancanagara Warehouse:** HB II orders new warehouse constructed for fort by Mancanagara inhabitants.
* **Jambu Fort Rejection:** HB I considered building a fort between Yogya and Jambu hills too expensive; decision taken to build fort at Matbon instead. Patih Danureja I delays Yogya cooperation until Boyolali fort repairs are completed.
* **Gladag Network:** Gladag established at Klaten; existing gladag posts functioning at Tengkirang, Adilanglu, and Matbon.
* **Taxation & Clergy:** Increased prices/taxes across North Coast districts. Note on priest Kyai Pangu in Surakarta (31 Dec. 1795).
