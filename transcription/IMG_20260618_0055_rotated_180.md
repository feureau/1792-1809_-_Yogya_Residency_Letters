#### 1. Military Garrisons & Fortifications

- **Residency Garrisons (1796):**  
  - Surakarta garrison: **183 men**  
  - Yogyakarta garrison: **193 men**  
  - *Language students:* 2 *Leerlingen in de Javaansche Taal* (1 stationed at each court).  
  - Due to shortage of European troops, some detachments have to be recalled periodically to Semarang.  
  - Since Governor Van den Burgh's tenure (1780s), infantry baseline at each court was fixed at **100 men**.  
- **V.O.C. Relay Forts along the Post Route:**  
  - Ungaran: *De Ontmoeting*  
  - Salatiga: *De Hersteller*  
  - Boyolali: *De Veldwachter*  
  - Repairs to Fort *De Veldwachter* (Boyolali) undertaken in 1793.  
- **Fortifications & Warehouses:**  
  - HB II agreed to construct a new storehouse (*pakhuis*) for the Yogya fort using corvée labour from Mancanagara subjects.  
  - HB I had been too economical to fund a fort between Yogya and the Jambu hills; decided instead to build a fort at Marbon.  
  - Patih Danureja I postponing Yogya's cooperation on the fort until Boyolali repairs are fully completed.

#### 2. Transport Infrastructure & Intelligence (*Gladhag*)

- *[Red marginal note:]* **gladhags.**  
  - Establishment of a *gladhag* (relay / transport post) at **Klaten**; posts already active at **Tangkilan**, **Adilangu**, and **Marbon**.  
- **Religious Unrest & Holy Men:**  
  - Notable increase in itinerant Muslim priests and ascetics (*tapa*) roaming the North Coast districts.  
  - Religious figure **Kyai Pugu** active at Sala *(cf. Aparte Brief, 31 Dec. 1795)*.  
- Nothing further heard concerning the surviving family of **Pangeran Rangga Jajar**.
