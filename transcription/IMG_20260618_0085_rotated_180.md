### Comparison between Hamengkubuwana I and Hamengkubuwana II:

- **HB I’s character:**
  - Very rarely sentenced anyone to death unless absolutely necessary; not a vindictive man.
  - Even if someone was caught in HB I’s seraglio, he refrained from arbitrary execution — unheard of for a Javanese ruler.
- **HB II’s misrule & banditry:**
  - Under HB II there was a great increase in **night robberies**, with many princes having a direct hand in them (e.g., Pangeran Ngabèhi) [red margin bar].
  - Patih at present in gaol.
  - Caused by the high taxes imposed by HB II; he shows no concern for the common man unlike HB I.
  - Described as: *"Groothartig en Oploopend van Haart"* [Arrogant/haughty and quick-tempered of heart].
  - No one dares to gainsay or contradict him — not even the court *panjis*.

---

### Yogyakarta Crown Prince (future HB III):

- Very like HB I in character.
- Although loved by the people, he holds **no influence on affairs of state**.
- Does not like going out, wandering about, or oppressing his subjects, but is forced to do so in order to keep his father (HB II) happy [underlined in red].

---

### Pangeran Mangkudiningrat:

- Very careful to strictly follow the old-style native Javanese customs; greatly loved by the subjects.
- His mother, **Ratu Anom**, descended from the old Javanese Mataram dynasty.
- Possesses shrewd judgement; has great skill in winning the affection of the local population.
- Does not appear in European circles; closely watched, especially by the Crown Prince.
- Nevertheless shows himself to be reasonably well-disposed towards Europeans.
