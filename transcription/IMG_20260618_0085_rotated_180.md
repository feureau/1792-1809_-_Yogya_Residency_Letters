### Van Reede Memorie van Overgave 1801 (Hamengkubuwana II Governance & Mangkudiningrat)

* Reference: **Java N.O.K. 47 MvO Van Reede - 2**
* **Raden Ayu Nusia:** Illegitimate daughter of PB IV married to Regent of Pamekasan.
* **Contrast between Sultans:**
  * **Hamengkubuwana I:** Rarely pronounced death sentences unless strictly necessary; not a vindictive ruler. Capital punishment within his harem (*Nereglio*) unheard of for a Javanese monarch.
  * **Hamengkubuwana II:** Sharp increase in night robberies, with various princes involved (including Pangeran Ngabehi, currently imprisoned). Imposes heavy taxation with no regard for the common people (*"Groot-hartig en oploopende van aard"*). Unquestioned authority; no noble dares gainsay him.
* **Yogyakarta Crown Prince:** Resembles HB I; popular among the people, but holds no sway over state affairs. Dislikes public wandering or oppressing subjects, but complies to keep his Patih satisfied.
* **Pangeran Mangkudiningrat:** Strictly observes traditional Javanese customs; highly popular among subjects (mother **Ratu Anom** was from old Mataram line). Possesses sharp judgment and skill in winning popular affection. Avoids European social circles and is closely watched by the Crown Prince, but remains well-disposed to Europeans.
