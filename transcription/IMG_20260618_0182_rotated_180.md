## Complete Diplomatic Transcription

*[Top center in red ink:]* **1805 (Dec.)**

---

### [Continuation of Coastal Defense Plan: Items 3–11]

3. + European N.C.O.s.  
   Sultan / Sunan should send troops to places on S. Coast most likely to be attacked.  

4. Royal Troops to be placed on prepared footing for attack.
5. European soldiers to be kept together to be used in a body v. enemy.
6. MN [Mancanagara] Regents to be ordered to hold themselves in readiness to march + troops to Yogya / Sala.
7. Yogya / Sala to be provisioned for possibility of long campaign.
8. Preparation for ammunition.
9. Yogya closest to S. Coast in case of attack = artillery should be strengthened + capable artillery officer should be sent to Yogya.
10. Residents Yogya / Sala to co-operate with each other.
11. any new 2nd Residents appointed to courts should be briefed on situation / thoroughly acquainted + kratons.

---

Waterloo – DR II,  
24 Puasa, 1732  
16 Dec. 1805.

Warning v. Pirates on S. Coast.  
Asks measures to be taken to strengthen coastal areas.  
Fear that even Europeans would land as Pirates on S. Coast.  
would try to capture kraton.  
Nusa Kambangan would be a prime target for a Landing place.
