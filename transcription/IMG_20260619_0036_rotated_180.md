### Dismissal of Sumadiningrat & Re-alignment of Yogyakarta Court Factions (May 1807)

* **7 May 1807 (European & American Intelligence):**
  * Official news received of Louis Napoleon's accession as King of Holland and Wiese's appointment as Governor-General.
  * 11 May: Reports received of revolutionary developments in America.
* **13 May 1807 (Formal Dismissal of Sumadiningrat):**
  * **ON THE EVENING OF 12 MARCH 1807, R.T. SUMADININGRAT WAS DISMISSED FROM ALL HIGH COURT OFFICES.**
  * Stripped of his grand commander rank and demoted to an Outer *Bupati Miji* under Patih Danureja II's orders (*Luipt loopende Tumenggung*).
  * Position left temporarily vacant, but **R.T. Natadiningrat** (son-in-law of Pangeran Natakusuma and favorite of Ratu Kencana Wulan) tipped as successor.
  * **Ria Sindureja** (brother of Ratu Kencana Wulan) to be appointed 1st Outer Regent (Prawiranata), with R.T. Prawiranata transferred to 4th Inner Regent.
  * Pangeran Dipakusuma also marked for promotion.
  * **Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III** expected to serve well as Chief Mancanagara Bupati alongside R.T. Natadiningrat.
