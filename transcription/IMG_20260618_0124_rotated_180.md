### Archive Entries: July – December 1802 (Royal Illness, Road Conditions, & Murders)

* **18 July 1802 (Van den Berg to Engelhard):** HB II ill (loss of appetite); PB IV suffering from rheumatic pains.
* **20 July 1802:** DR II's cousin (Danukrama) visits aunt (DR II's aunt) and husband Patih of Kendal (Cakrasimita).
* **26 July 1802:** Engelhard plans visit to North Coast; inspects Prambanan temple (cleared of wild grass by Van den Berg). Spends a day at Tangkilan.
* **10 August 1802 (Road Conditions):**
  * Road between Delanggu and Tangkilan impassable for carriages.
  * For 10 years, HB II performed no road repairs; backbreaking work to cross in a carriage (bridges consist only of palm tree trunks). Recommends traveling by palanquin.
  * Phaeton carriages meet ladies at Tangkilan for trip to Prambanan. Deep valley at **Papringan** on road to Yogya remains in terrible state.
* **17 December 1802:** Murder of European gardener at Solo by bandits. Two cousins of Pangeran Mangkudiningrat involved in murder of HB II's *Mantri* at Pasampakkan. HB II demands culprits be executed and their heads displayed on bamboo poles (power reserved exclusively to Sultan).
* **18 December 1802:** V.O.C. requests permission to cut timber in Jipang. HB II progresses from one *pesanggrahan* to another, making it difficult to hold audiences.
