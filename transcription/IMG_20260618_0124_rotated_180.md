### [10 Aug 1802]

* **Id. to Id. 10 Aug. 1802**
  * *[Double red vertical line in margin]*
  * Road Dêlanggu – Tangkisan not passable for a carriage – if for 10 years HB II makes no repairs on roads.
  * It is Back Breaking work to cross the Road in a carriage – Bridges only Palm Tree Trunks.
  * <ins style="text-decoration: underline; text-decoration-color: red;">Better to go in Palanquin.</ins>
  * Phaeton: to meet ladies at Tangkisan to take them to Prambanan.
  * Tangkisan – Yogya: have to cross Deep Valley of Papringan. Road in same terrible state.
  * *[Right margin annotation in red ink:]* **PAPRINGAN. on Rd. to Yogya**

---

### [17 Dec 1802]

* **Id. to Id. 17 Dec. 1802**
  * Murder of Eur. gardener at Sala by Bandits.
  * 2 cousins of P. Mangkudiningrat involved in murder of HB II's Mantri at Pasampakkan.
  * HB II wants to see them executed & Heads on Bamboo poles; only Sultan able to do that.
