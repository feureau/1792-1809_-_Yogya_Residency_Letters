*27 June 1809*  
*Wiese to Daendels*  
HB II pleased CP allowed STOOLS: Ngabèhi

*30 June 1809*  
*Wiese - Van Braam*  
Bogaard letter complaining of an attack  
Robbery in Brebes (Pekalongan / Tegal) made by Yogya based men.  
ORDERS to be given by BLORA regents to hand over cutting of teak wood to Dutch Forestry officials.  
DR II sends letters to R. Rangga about this:

*18 July 1809*  
*Wiese - Daendels*  
4 CHILDREN of HB II get married.  
Necessary presents demanded.  
CONTINUAL bothering of Inhabitants of PASISIR by Yogya Inhabitants .. : Necessary measures to be taken in conjunction + HB II.

*24 July 1809*  
*Wiese to Daendels*  
Commissioner assisted by a European -> Japan & Jipang  
5,000 daggers / knives (for woodcutting?)  

[to be handed over by Regents there]

Daendels Demand asks for satisfaction for murder of Europeans in that area.  
HB II serious talk + R. Rangga over it:
