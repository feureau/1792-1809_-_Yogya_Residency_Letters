### [Transcription]

A.N. No. 22. **1809 (Yogya)**  

[Red ink: *1809 (Feb-April)*]

CROWN PRINCE visits WIESE & Sec. WOORTMAN  
and Comm. De CHASTEAUVIEUX  
makes TOUR ON HORSEBACK

*2 Feb. 1809*  
*Wiese to Daendels*  
LETTER HB II to DR II CANCELLING ARMY MANOEUVRES:  

[If he should proceed to SIMILAR (ridiculous) ACTION should be cleared from Java.]

*8 Feb. 1809*  
*Wiese to Van Braam (Minister Solo)*  
Collection of money for WAR TREASURY from CITIZENS / Eur. Officials of Yogya:  
Lt. Col. de Chasteauvieux : 20,000 (Rendmatten)  
Lt. Beer : 20,000  
Hospitaler Baumgarten : 15,000  
C.M. Klein : 25,000  
Capt. Baksman : 10,000  
Citizen E. Klaarvig : 15,000.

*11 Feb. 1809*  
*Wiese to Daendels*  
Desa PADEREGAN (in Yogya territory) plundered from SURAKARTA.  
Solo Inhabitants followed up by a  
Detachment of mounted troops from Yogya.

*17 Mar. 1809*  
*G.W. Wiese to J.A. van Braam*  
[Red asterisk & double margin line]:  
<u>Will of Baron van Rheede tot de Parkeler leaves 39,000 Sp. matten for P. Prangwedana</u>

*22 Mar. 1809*  
*G.W. Wiese to Van Braam*  
Each year SUNAN / Sultan allowed to send 6 Prauws Tax free up Sala River to Gresik for purposes of Trade.  
Fact that Sunan raises *his* number to 10 causes annoyance / jealousy of the SULTAN  
asks to be allowed 10 also —
