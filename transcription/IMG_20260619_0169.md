### Archive Entries: February – March 1809 (Cancellation of Army Drills, War Tax, & PWD Bequest)

* Reference: **A.N. Yogya Letters No. 22 (1809)**
* **2 February 1809 (Cancellation of Army Drills):** Wiese reports HB II issued a formal letter to Danureja II **cancelling all urban military maneuvers**. Wiese notes it is far better to proceed cautiously than provoke an open conflict.
* **8 February 1809 (War Treasury Levies on European Residents):**
  * Minister Wiese collects forced contributions for the Dutch war treasury from European officials and citizens in Yogyakarta:
    * Lt.-Col. De Chanteauvieux: 20,000 *Rondemaaten*
    * Lt. Beer: 20,000 *Rondemaaten*
    * Hospitaler Baumgarten: 15,000 *Rondemaaten*
    * C.H. Klein: 25,000 *Rondemaaten*
    * Capt. Baksman: 10,000 *Rondemaaten*
    * Citizen E. Kleung: 15,000 *Rondemaaten*
* **11 February 1809 (Paderesan Raid):** Surakarta inhabitants plunder Desa Paderesan in Yogyakarta territory; pursued by a detachment of Yogyakarta mounted troops.
* **17 March 1809 (Van Reede Bequest):** **Will of Baron van Reede tot de Parkeler bequeaths 39,000 Spanish Dollars to Pangeran Prangwedana.**
* **22 March 1809 (Solo River Trade Allocations):**
  * Annual allowance of 6 tax-free trading proas sent down the Solo River to Gresik increased by PB IV to 10 proas, exciting HB II's jealousy; HB II demands a matching allocation of 10 tax-free proas.
