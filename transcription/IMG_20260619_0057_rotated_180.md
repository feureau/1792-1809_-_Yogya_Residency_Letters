### Mangkunagaran Succession, Coastal Regent Appointments, & Van Reede Interventions

* **Mangkunagaran Succession Risks:**
  * **Pangeran Prabuwijaya chosen as Prangwedana's successor.**
  * PB IV seeks to reclaim 4,000 Mangkunagaran cacahs. If Prangwedana dies unexpectedly, P. Mangkubumi will attempt to seize Mangkunagaran lands and attack the *Dalem* (as attempted in 1796 when Van Reede occupied the Mangkunagaran *Dalem* in the V.O.C.'s name).
* **Mangkunagaran Daughters' Marriages:**
  * (1) R.A. Nolim (daughter of Mangkunagara I) married Bupati of Tegal.
  * (2) R.A. Nici married R.T. Reksanegara (Fiscal of Landraad).
  * (3) R.A. Ningnim married Raden Rongga (brother of Demak Bupati); resided at PB IV's court, but returned to Pasisir due to insufficient allowance.
  * (4) R.A. Nri married Wirakhonma (Bupati of Lasem; vigilant and capable official).
* **Case of Nataningrat (Terbaya):**
  * R. Panji Nataningrat (Bupati of Kaliwungu) dismissed by Van Overstraten for abducting a woman and her daughter. Case unproven; reappointed 2nd Bupati of Batang by Van Reede. Proves himself an excellent factory manager and recommended for promotion to full Bupati.
