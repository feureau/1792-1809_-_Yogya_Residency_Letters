### [Transcription]

H 698 b **-1809-** AVJ 1809 (Yogya)  

[Red ink: *Concerning Estate of Baron van Rheede tot de P. / Estate of P.W.P.*]

*12 Feb. 1809*  
[Red double margin line] *Besl. G.G. 31 Jan. 1809*  
Sunan / Sultan informed that 39,800 Sp. Reales to be paid out to P. Prangwēdana by Executors of Baron van Rheede tot de Parkeler, because the sum was unlawfully retained by Rheede from MN I's Estate.  

[1st 1/2 of Besl. van G.G. 31 Jan 1809: included in Bundel 2nd 1/2 destroyed.]

During Van Rheede's period as 1st Res. at Sala:  
under pretext of doing it under orders of N.I. Govmt and as a guarantee of good behaviour of P. Prangwēdana seizes whole Inheritance of MN I.  
Keeps gold, precious stones and other pretiosas to sum 39,800 Sp. Ds.  
Refusal to return this sum: after a small payment to surviving Inheritors.  
P. Prangwēdana claims it back thro' N. Engelhard — Reported in N. ENG geheim / apart missive 23 Nov. 1803.  
But claim Dismissed 1 Aug. 1805:  
(1) Because Prangwēdana had only Reclaimed money after death of Rheede and until then been silent.  
(2) That N. ENG who reported claim to N.I. Govnmt was the declared enemy of Van Rheede.  
.. Request Repeated by Prangwēdana: 24 April 1808 -> J.A. Van Braam: sends Request to G.G. 15 May 1808.

*1 Mar. 1809*  
*Van Braam (Semarang) -> Wiese:*  
Levies of Daendels on Rich Yogya Citizens (Decreet Besl. 2 Oct. 1808)  
in 3 installments: 1 April / 1 May / 1 June 1809  

- Burger Kleijn = 3,000  
- Baumgarten = 3,000  
- Klaring = 1,500  
- Capt. Beksman = 1,000  
- Capitein Cina = 3,000
