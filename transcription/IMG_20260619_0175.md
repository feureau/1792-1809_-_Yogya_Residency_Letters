### AVJ Letters: Legal Dispute over Baron van Reede's Seizure of Mangkunagaran Estate (1809)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1809 (H 698b)**
* **12 February 1809 (Daendels Decree on Van Reede Estate):**
  * **Governor-General's Decree (31 Jan. 1809):** Informs Sunan and Sultan that **39,800 Spanish Reals must be paid to Pangeran Prangwedana by the executors of Baron van Reede tot de Parkeler**.
  * *Background:* During his tenure as 1st Resident of Surakarta, Van Reede unlawfully seized and retained Mangkunagara I's entire personal estate (gold, gems, valuables worth 39,800 Sp.D.) under the pretext of holding security for Prangwedana's good conduct.
  * Prangwedana's original 1803 claim was dismissed by Engelhard on 1 Aug. 1805 because Prangwedana remained silent until Van Reede's death, and Engelhard was Van Reede's personal enemy.
  * Claim resubmitted by Prangwedana to Van Braam on 24 April 1808 and approved by Daendels.
* **1 March 1809 (War Treasury Assessment):**
  * Van Braam levies war contributions on rich Yogyakarta citizens in 3 installments (*Decree 2 Oct. 1808*): Burger Kleyn (3,000 rds), Baumgarten (3,000 rds), Kleung (1,500 rds), Capt. Beksman (1,000 rds), Capitan Cina Tan Tjin Sing (3,000 rds).
