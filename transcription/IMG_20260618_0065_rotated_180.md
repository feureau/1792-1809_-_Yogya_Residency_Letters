## Content

### Archive Entries: February 1796 (Kaliwungu Appointment & Prangwedana Naming Title)

* **Kaliwungu Succession:** Sumadiwirya's sons (exiled to Ceylon in 1754): Mas Wiriawijaya (28) and Mas Sumawijaya (21). Mas Wiriawijaya appointed Bupati of Kaliwungu under the title **Raden Tumenggung Adinegara**.
* **Prangwedana Title Disputes (20 Feb. 1796):**
  * Prangwedana's health improves.
  * Disagreement over a suitable title: PB IV rejects granting him his grandfather's title (*Mangkunagara*), proposing 4 minor titles instead.
  * Prangwedana suggests *Aria Prabu Amangkurat* or *Aria Prabu Mataram*; rejected as unsuitable/offensive to Solo and Yogya.
  * **Van Overstraten's Intervention:** Van Overstraten proposes the title **Pangeran Aria Prangwedana** to resolve dispute.
* **Appanage & Oath:**
  * Three eldest illegitimate sons of MN I press for division of 4,000 cacahs (encouraged by Mangkubumi), but withdraw claims and apologize to Resident Van Reede.
  * PB IV raises procedural obstacles regarding ceremony; swearing-in takes place in Fort under a durian tree. Prangwedana takes oath of allegiance to PB IV (as MN I had done twice to PB III). Present: Pangeran Ngabehi, Patih Sasrawijaya.
