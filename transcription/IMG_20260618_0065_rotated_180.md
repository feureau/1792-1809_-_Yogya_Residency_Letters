[Main text - Red ink heading:]

**apptmt. of PWD.**

**20 Feb. 1796: Van Ov. to Alting**

PWD’s health improves.

Three difficulties arise over the succession / investiture:

1. **Choosing a suitable name:**
   - PB IV refuses to allow him to succeed to his grandfather’s name (*Mangkunegara*).
   - PB IV suggests four alternative names, but all are considered too low in rank/stature.
   - PWD then suggests: *Aria Prabu Amangkurat* or *Aria Prabu Mataram*.
   - *Amangkurat* is unsuitable for both Surakarta and Yogyakarta; *Mataram* would deeply annoy Yogyakarta.
   - Governor Van Overstraten therefore himself suggests the name **Aria Prangwēdana** to avoid all arguments in Surakarta and to expedite the appointment.

2. **The 4,000 Cacahs:**
   - The three eldest illegitimate sons of MN I press for a division of the 4,000 cacahs among the family members.
   - Pangeran Mangkubumi had put them up to asking for lands.
   - The sons desist after the eldest apologizes to Resident Van Reede.

3. **Ceremonial Swearing-in:**
   - PB IV makes difficulties about the ceremonial swearing-in of PWD; it eventually takes place in the Dutch Fort under a durian tree.
   - PWD does not want to take an oath of allegiance to PB IV, but is forced to do so (cf. MN I had previously taken an oath twice to PB III).
   - Present at the swearing of the oath:
     - Pangeran Ngabèhi
     - Sala Patih [Raden Adipati Mangkuprojo]
     - Tumenggung Nastrawijaya
