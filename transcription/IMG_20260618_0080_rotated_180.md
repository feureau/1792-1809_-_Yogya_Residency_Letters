## Content

### AvJ Archive Entries: 1799 (Death of Danureja I & Installation of Danureja II)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1799**
* **30 January 1799 (Res. Yogya):** HB II grants permission for Mangkunagaran *Bupatis* to return home.
* **3 August 1799 (Res. Solo):** Arrival of Eastern Mancanagara Regents in Surakarta to provide corvée labor for Kraton.
* **19 August 1799 (Res. Yogya):** **DEATH OF PATIH DANUREJA I AT 2:00 P.M.** (aged 84 Javanese years). HB II selects Mertanegara (grandson of DRI, married to HB II's daughter) to succeed him.
* **20 August 1799:** **Burial of Danureja I at Imagiri** at the personal request of late Sultan Hamengkubuwana I.
* **21 August 1799:** Patih affairs temporarily entrusted to Mertanegara.
* **9 September 1799:** Official letter received from Batavia authorizing appointment of **Danureja II (DR II)**.
  * **R.T. Natayuda** re-appointed as 1st Kliwon to Patih.
  * **Aria Mandura** (eldest son of DRI) appointed 2nd Kliwon.
  * **R.T. Danukusuma** (second son of DRI) appointed Assistant to DR II.
* **20 September 1799 (Res. Solo):** Patih Danureja II departs for Semarang accompanied by 3 Tumenggungs (Kyai Tum. Jendunegara, Mas Tum. Sumadipura, R.T. Purwadipura of Japan) to deliver letters to Batavia and Governor of Java's N.E. Coast regarding his appointment.
