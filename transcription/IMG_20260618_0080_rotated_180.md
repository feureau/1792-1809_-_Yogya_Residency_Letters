### 30 January 1799 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- HB II gives permission for Mangkunegaran bupatis to return home.

---

### 3 August 1799 (Resident of Surakarta):

- Arrival of Eastern Mangkunegaran Regents in Sala to provide compulsory labour services for the kraton.

---

### 19 August 1799 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- **Death of Patih Danureja I (DR I):**
  - Dies at 2:00 p.m. at **94 Javanese years of age** [underlined in red].
  - HB II wishes **Raden Tumenggung Mērtanēgara** to succeed him:
    - Married to a daughter of HB II; grandson of Danureja I.

---

### 20 August 1799 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- **Burial of Danureja I at Imagiri** at the feet of Sultan Hamengkubuwana I.

---

### 21 August 1799 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- Affairs of Danureja I officially entrusted to Mērtanēgara.

---

### 9 September 1799 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- In receipt of letter from Batavia authorising the official appointment of **Danureja II (DR II)**:
  - **Raden Tumenggung Natayuda:** Re-appointed as **1st Kliwon** of Danureja II.
  - **Raden Aria Mandura** (eldest son of DR I): Appointed as **2nd Kliwon** of Danureja II.
  - **Raden Tumenggung Danukusuma** (2nd son of DR I): Appointed as assistant of Danureja II.

---

### 20 September 1799 (Resident of Surakarta):

- Danureja II departs for Semarang, accompanied by an escort/following of three Tumenggungs:
  - Kyai Tumenggung Sasradiningrat / Sendunēgara.
  - Mas Tumenggung Sumadipura (?Japan).
  - Raden Tumenggung Purwadipura.
- Traveling to deliver official letters:
  1. To Batavia.
  2. To the Governor of Java’s Northeast Coast.
  - Purpose: Confirmation of the new appointment of Patih Danureja II.
