### Surakarta Tollgate Lease Contracts (November 1808 - Part 1)

* Reference: **N.V. and B. No. 1 p. 6 (J.A. van Braam - Nov. 1808)**
* Document: Official 3-year lease contracts (*Tollgate Contracts 1809–1811*) signed by Resident J.A. van Braam for Surakarta regional tollgates.

#### Lease Terms & Tax Farm Awards:
1. **Amber Tollgates:** Leased to Chinese **Oei Kentien** for **5,400 Sp. Reals per annum** (3-year contract 1809–1811; paid half-yearly at *Mulud* and *Puasa*).
   * 864 Reals paid immediately upon signing (11% tax for Patih; 5% tax for Resident's *Edel Heer Seld*).
   * **STRICT EXEMPTIONS:** **No toll taxes to be levied on goods belonging to the Sultan or Sunan, or on the movement of royal horses.**
   * No new toll posts or toll rate increases permitted on the *Ringstoo* road network (though physical tollgates may be relocated).
2. **Djamben Tollgate & Rangkah:** Leased to Chinese **Oei Kiam** for **1,200 Sp. Reals p.a.** (11% tax = 198 d.).
3. **Banyumas Tollgates:** Leased to Chinese **Oei Kiangsing** for **10,000 Reals p.a.**
4. **Gunung Tawang Tollgate:** Leased to **Oei Kansing** for **420 Reals p.a.**
5. **Pakis Tollgate:** Leased to **Oim Hok Sing** for **7,200 Reals p.a.**
