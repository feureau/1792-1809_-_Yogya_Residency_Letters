### Speculatieve Memorie: Strandgeld History & Royal Revenue Comparison (1808)

* **Military Policy:** Once sufficient European forces are assembled to overawe the courts, aggressive territorial annexations should be attempted. Non-royal Bupatis to be retained in office. Termination of the 10,000 Np.D. annual *Strandgeld* lease recommended; lands around Boyolali, Klaten, and Salatiga to be acquired.

#### History of Strandgeld Coastal Lease Payments:
* Originally instituted as indemnity for loss of tollgates, Shahbandars, rice production, and teak timber:
  * **Nov. 1746 Decision (PB II / PB III):** 9,000 Rijksdaalders for PB III, 1,000 for Ratu Amangkurat, 2,000 for the two Patihs, 1,000 for Nayakas, 2,000 for the Crown Prince.
  * Bupatis required to pay homage at court during *Mulud* and *Puasa* (fell into disuse).
  * Shahbandar compensation later fixed at 5,000 R.d. (Contract of 18 May 1746).
* **1755 Division:** Fixed at 10,000 Np.P. For Yogyakarta and 10,000 Np.P. For Surakarta. HB I waives claims to Madura pasisir in 1755 Giyanti Treaty.

#### Royal Revenue Comparison (1808):
* **Pakubuwana IV Annual Income:** **44,016 Np.D.** (plus 10,000–12,000 Np.D. in *Pundhutan Dalem / Pangiring* homage money).
* **Hamengkubuwana II Annual Income:** **60,000 Np.D.** (plus ceremonial presents and tributes).
