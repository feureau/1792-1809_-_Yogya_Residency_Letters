### Tax Levies, Reksanegara Duties, and Mangkunagaran Estate Seizure (1797–1799)

* Reference: **P.J. Br. no. 48 (1797–1799)**

#### Reksanegara Tax Schedule to HB II (Value: 1,230 Reals):
* **Patholen Textiles:** 10 pieces *Cindhe* Jelamprang (@ 25 Reals), Kanigara (@ 15 Reals), Kanonga.
* **Gold & Silk Thread:** 10 lbs gold thread (12 strands @ 24 Reals/lb); spun silk (20 *kaces*: 5 green, 2 red @ 8 Reals/kace).
* **Teal Klamas Sugar:** 430 Reals per 1/2 year.
* **Patoronpo Tax:** 10 Reals per 1,000 Reals of tax (56 for purchase receipt, 28 *Penglamur*). Exemption for tobacco/presents brought directly by peasants to their lord.

#### Contract & Mangkunagaran Estate Seizure:
* **Contract (31 Aug. 1797):** Barend Jan van Nieuwkerken & R. Adip. Mangkupraja authorized by Van Reede / PB IV.
* **Seizure of MN I's Estate:** Upon Van Reede's death, estate taken over by A.H. Wiese and P. Walbeek. Van Reede had destroyed all documents concerning Prangwedana's claims on MN I's personal estate. Prangwedana allowed to inherit 1/2 year after Van Reede's death only through intervention of Nicolaas Engelhard.
* **Deathbed Seizure:** On MN I's deathbed, Van Reede brought bodyguards and scribe Michaelis to the *Dalem*. Prangwedana remained at the residence to prevent seizure of inheritance and protect against rumored assassination plot by Pangeran Mangkubumi (did not accompany MN I's body to Matesih/Imagiri). Prangwedana assisted in transferring estate chests to Van Reede's house under pretext of safeguarding them from PB IV.
