### Van Alphen - Engelhard 239: Surakarta Inter-Factional Intrigues & Regent Backgrounds

* Reference: **Van Alphen - Engelhard No. 239 (Aanw. 1900)**
* **Intrigues Surrounding Pangeran Mangkubumi:**
  * R.T. Cakra-winata very close to Pangeran Mangkubumi.
  * Patih Mangkupraja and Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III drawn into high-stakes court intrigues between PB IV and Pangeran Mangkubumi.
  * Mangkupraja's close relationship with Raden Rongga III excites PB IV's suspicion, ultimately leading to Mangkupraja's downfall and murder.
* **Banyumas Exile & Murder of Mangkupraja:** R.T. Yudanegara (Banyumas Patih of PB IV until the 1790 Pakepung affair) murders exiled Patih Mangkupraja in Banyumas.
* **Bagelèn Regent:** R.T. Arung Binang (Bupati of Bagelèn) described as impoverished (*armzalig*).
* **Mangkupraja's Son:** R.T. Sasrawijaya is son of Patih Mangkupraja by an unofficial wife from the Batavian *Bovenlanden* (born during Mangkupraja's Batavia exile).
* **Mangkunagaran Regents:**
  * Eastern Mangkunagaran provinces governed by **Pangeran Purbanegara** (eldest son of R.M. Said / Mangkunagara I; uncle of Prangwedana). Praised as an honest, highly capable Regent.
