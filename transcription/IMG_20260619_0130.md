### Architectural Layout of the Yogyakarta Kraton & Rural Mobilizations (August 1808)

* **Architectural Layout of the Kraton:**
  * *Prabayeksa:* Contains 3 separate residences for the *Ratus*; R.A. Nitis' house at Gintang; small pendhapa to S. of Prabayeksa (Bangsal Mas).
  * **Bangsal Rukma:** The primary open audience hall (*pendhapa*) where HB II receives official visitors.
  * *Gates & Plazas:* 1st Gate (*Nrimenganti*) with 2 open pendhapas; 2nd Gate (*Mandungan*) with *Witara Mandungan* in the central court.
  * *High Ground (*Lamadhuwur*):* *Witana Lamadhuwur* pavilion; lower level leads to the **Pagilaran** pillars.
  * *Bangsal Penganwit:* Sultan's elevated throne seat; flanked by open *Pengapit* pavilions and the twin *Waringin kurung* banyan trees.
  * *Incarceration & Outer Gates:* *Gladhag* (holding area for prisoners); *Gadhing* (rear gate); *Gamping* (West rear gate); *Kadipaten* (N.E. compound for Crown Prince).
* **Prajurit Mobilization & Kirigan Contingents (22–29 Aug. 1808):**
  * HB II issues calls for additional *kirigan* (corvée) troops from rural desas.
  * Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III orders Madiun subjects to assemble arms and horses prior to his June Semarang trip, providing an armed escort of Madiun *kirigan* troops.
