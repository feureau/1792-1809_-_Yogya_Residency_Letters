### Official Flags of Yogyakarta Regiments & 1809 Events Overview

* Reference: **Waterloo to Engelhard (22 March 1808 / Pj. Br. 20.45)**

#### Official Regimental Flag Designs of Sultan's Army (*Flags of Sultan's Troops*):
* **Mantri Jero & Katanggang:** Black and white circular discs.
* **Trunjaya Nyutra & Jagakarya:** Red circular discs.
* **Daeng Secadipura:** Yellow and red circular discs.
* **Patang Puluh:** Red and white circular discs.
* **Wirabraja:** Dark circular discs.
* **Mandung & Mangundara:** Dark blue circular discs.
* **Januhastra / Jayakata:** Dark blue circular discs.
* **Sarazata:** White and black circular discs.
* **Nameng Yuda:** Red and white circular discs.
* **Patra Indo:** Light blue circular discs.
* **Yuda Ningsala:** Black circular discs.
* **Blambangan Sepuh / Pranatubahan / Blambangan:** Blue circular discs.
* **Nuryagama:** Yellow with black edges.
* **Bugis:** Black with white edges.
* **Prajurit Lipit & Sesela:** Black and white with white monogram.

#### Events in Yogyakarta (January – March 1809):
* **17–26 Jan. 1809:** Inspection journey to Yogyakarta by Lt.-Col. Ballard (Army Commander), Capt. Rhuws (Artillery), and Lt. Nollat.
* **27 Jan. 1809:** *Garebeg Besar* celebrated.
* **15 Mar. 1809:** Lt.-Col. De Chanteauvieux (garrison commander) departs Yogyakarta for Semarang.
