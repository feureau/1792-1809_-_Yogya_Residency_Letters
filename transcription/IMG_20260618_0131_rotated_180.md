### Mataram Reunification Prophecy & Intermarriage Risks

* **Crown Prince Alignment:** Crown Prince steps aside from court friction.
* **Alliance with Surakarta:** Van Overstraten emphasizes importance of maintaining friendship with Susuhunan for peace in Java, but notes more is to be feared from Surakarta than Yogyakarta.
* **Prophecy of Mataram Reunification (1790 Reference):**
  * *"The best one can say with certainty is that this court down to this day does not forego the hope, that the Empire of Mataram will be once again reunited and that the Emperor will triumph over Java."*
* **Dynastic Intermarriage Risks:**
  * Intermarriage between Regents' families under the V.O.C. and Surakarta princely lines creates severe conflicts of interest:
  * *"If hostilities break out between Company and Princes, loyalties would be dangerously divided."*
* **Maltreatment of Pasisir Regents' Daughters:**
  * Pasisir *Bupatis* dislike sending daughters to marry into Surakarta court.
  * Example: Two daughters of Pati Regent **R.T. Aria Magatwari** married to Surakarta nobles (Pangeran Buminata line) were done to death at Surakarta court.
