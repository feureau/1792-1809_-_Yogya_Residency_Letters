Br. 45: W. van Ysseldijk, "Eerbiedige Bericht aangaande de landen van zijn Hoogheid den Sultan te Djocjocarta", 1793 / Letter to Van Overstraten, 15 Jan. 1793)*<br><br>#### Overview of Plantations & Cacahs:<br><br>- On pepper plantations: **Loano**, **Gèntang**, **Pacitan**.<br>- **700 cacahs / 168 jungs sawah**.<br>- **+ 100 cacahs Indigo** = Idea of setting up Indigo Factory.<br>- Owners / farmers to be released from corvée services to Sultan.<br>- After Van Overstraten's visit to Yogya (Apr. 1792), Van IJsseldijk visits pepper estates.<br>  *(Cross-ref: BL Add MS 14397 f. 4r on pepper contingent from Loano / Pacitan; f. 50r–51v pepper contingents Gedhong Tèngen)*.<br><br>---<br><br>### Local Conditions & Soil Suitability:<br><br>- **Loano + Adjacent Lands** (= Winong / Malarang) = 350 cacahs (2 hours' journey to W. of Yogya) + **Gèntang** = 150 cacahs.<br>- Pepper not suited for cultivation in Gèntang: during W. monsoon Praga River floods its banks, submerging gardens.<br>- **Loano**: Suited to pepper cultivation: red / moist soil; small quantities grown there for years.<br>- **Malarang**: Similarly favourable, but too dry in E. monsoon.<br>- *Recommendation:* Instead of Gèntang, pepper should be planted in **Nelo Tiyang** / **Pekalongan**.<br><br>---<br><br>### Social Conditions & Administrative Tensions:<br><br>- Complaints of *wong cilik* regarding oppression and extortion by local chiefs.<br>- Inhabitants would plant more pepper if they could escape oppression.<br>- *Demangs* of Loano are unforthcoming, and *lurah*s fear to provide information.<br>- Under the new system, *demangs* are cut out of local administration of forced deliveries.<br>- Shortage of working buffaloes.<br>- **Tumenggung Mangundipura** (*Tum. Loano*): Entangled in family relationships with *demangs*; reports that *demangs* demand 6 jungs of land each. |
