### Coastal Defense Plots, Tegal Exile, and Cotton Trade (1801)

* **Rangga Jajar Lineage:** Descendants of Pangeran Rangga Jajar in Malang / Kadilangu area:
  * *Sons:* Pangeran Rangga, Raden Purbakusuma, Raden Anrangkusuma, Raden Sasradipura.
  * *Grandsons:* Raden Sasrawijaya, Raden Sumadirana.
  * Assembling 1,000 men in Solo village of Kayu to launch a land attack on V.O.C. posts if the British launch a naval attack.
  * Van Overstraten intends to arrest the Radens (targeting Demak Bupati and Surakarta Patih/Nayakas), but succeeds in persuading them to come voluntarily to Solo.
* **Exile of Bupati of Tegal (p. 160):** Raden Haji, Bupati of Tegal, flees to Bali and is subsequently exiled to the Cape of Good Hope (*Pès*). Owes 2,000 Np.D. to Gusti Ngenang of Karangasem; wives remain in Bali.
* **Cotton & Textile Trade (p. 196–197):**
  * Poor Javanese cotton harvest unable to compete with imported thread from Coromandel and Makassar.
  * Capitan Cina in Yogya produces 350 corges of slave cloth annually for Batavia (@ 16.32 Np.D. p.a.). In 1788, order split 1/2 to Surakarta due to excessive volume for Yogya alone.
