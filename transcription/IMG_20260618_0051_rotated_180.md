### Mangkudiningrat, Ratu Bendara, and Mangkunagaran Succession (1795–1796)

* **Pangeran Mangkudiningrat:** In disfavour with HB II; lives in very reduced circumstances (in a sober fashion), but conducts himself in a very quiet manner. Pangeran Ngabehi hopes to see Mangkudiningrat appointed over the head of the Crown Prince.
* **Demang:** Married to a daughter of DRI (Danureja I). Resident Van den Burgh reported very bad reports about him as early as 1780, but he made no move during the 1792 succession.
* **Ratu Bendara:** Highly respected by other members of the Royal Family.
* **G.G. Greetings Protocol:** Refusal of HB I to pay respects to an incoming Governor-General in Semarang. Resolved by Article 24 of 1792 contract, permitting felicitations in Semarang instead of Batavia (PB IV agreed to similar terms in 1788).
* **Death of Mangkunagara I (28 Dec. 1795):** Mangkunagara I dies aged 71.
* **Mangkunagaran Succession:** Pangeran Prangwedana allowed to succeed to 4,000 cacahs, but **not** permitted to inherit MN I's title of *Pangeran Adipati*. Rank fixed immediately after the Surakarta Crown Prince.
* **PB IV Opposition:** Much difficulty in winning PB IV's consent to Prangwedana's succession in 1795. On 25 Jan. 1796, PB IV yields, permitting succession to 4,000 cacahs under the name **Pangeran Aria Prabu Prangwedana**. V.O.C. requires Prangwedana as a balance to the Sunan.
