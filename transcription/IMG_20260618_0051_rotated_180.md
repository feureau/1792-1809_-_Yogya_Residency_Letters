### [Transcription]

#### 1. Factions within the Yogyakarta Royal Family

- **Pangeran Mangkudiningrat:**  
  In disfavour with HB II; lives in very reduced circumstances (*in a sober fashion*), but conducts himself quietly.  

- **Pangeran Ngabèhi & Pangeran Demang:**  
  Pangeran Ngabèhi hoped to see his son **Pangeran Demang** appointed over the head of the Crown Prince.  
  Demang is married to a daughter of Danureja I. Governor Van den Burgh had reported very negative things about him as early as 1780. However, Demang made no move during the 1792 succession.  

- **Ratu Bèndara:**  
  Very widely respected by other members of the royal family.

#### 2. Protocol: Felicitations to Incoming Governor-General

- Historical refusal of HB I to pay respects in person to an incoming Governor-General in Semarang.  
- Resolved by **Article 24 of the Treaty of 2 April 1792**, permitting felicitations to be delivered in Semarang rather than traveling all the way to Batavia. (In 1788 PB IV had also agreed to a similar article).

#### 3. Succession of Mangkunegara II (Pangeran Prangwedana)

- **28 Dec. 1795:**  
  **Mangkunegara I dies** aged 71 years old.  

- **Pangeran Prangwedana:**  
  Allowed to succeed to the **4,000 cacahs**, but *not* permitted to inherit MN I's personal title of *Pangeran Adipati*.  
  To enjoy court precedence immediately after the Sala Crown Prince.  

- *[Red ink note:]* **Trouble to get agreement of PB IV to successions:**  
  Great difficulty in winning over PB IV in 1795 to consent to Prangwedana's inheritance.  

- **25 Jan. 1796:**  
  PB IV formally allows him to succeed to the 4,000 cacahs with the title **Pangeran Aria Prabu Prangwedana**.  

- Strategic imperative: Need of the V.O.C. to use PWD as a counterweight / balance against the Sunan.
