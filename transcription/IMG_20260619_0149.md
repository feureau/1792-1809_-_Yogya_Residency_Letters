### Klaten Security Accord Articles 8–12 & Gladhag Agreement (16 October 1808)

* Reference: **Klaten Accord (16 Oct. 1808)**
* **Arts. 8–12 Legal & Security Protocols:**
  * **Art. 8 (Inter-Court Accusations):** Accusations between Surakarta and Yogyakarta subjects require formal proof; if unproven, the accused must purge himself by oath. Credible joint cases investigated under interstate procedure.
  * **Art. 9 (Conspiracy Penalties):** Officials conspiring with rebels subject to death or banishment; families (*wives/children*) confined to Kraton custody.
  * **Art. 10–11 (*Kamisepuh* Oaths):** Senior *Kamisepuhs* (eldest Regents) bound by oath to act jointly with village *Mantris* and *Tampings*. *Mantris/Tampings* required to report *Kamisepuh* intrigues directly to Patih Danureja II.
  * **Art. 12 (Road Guarding):** Principal regional roads guarded; *Lurahs* required to supply guard posts.
* **Gladhag Accord:** **Parallel agreement regulating porter/transport guilds (*Gladhag*) signed on 16 October 1808.**
