## Diplomatic Transcription

8. Subjects Solo accuse Yogya and vice-versa of Felony / Robbery  
   But no proof = no case allowed  
   but <strike>the</strike> accused must purge Himself By Taking an oath.  
   But if accused By Solo / Yogya affair to Be considered credible as a case.  

9. If Officials conspire + Rebels they are / to Be punished.  
   + Death or Banished // & wives / children to Kraton
10. Kamisêpuhs eldest of Regents to Be bound by an oath to act jointly + Mantri Tampings.
11. Mantri / Tampings must Tell DR II If Kamisêpuhs Intrigue + Robbers.
12. Guard on principle Roads in area.  
    & govnt Lands to be provided By Lurahs.
