### Breakdown of Producer Margins & Transport Costs in the Rice Trade

#### Example Profit Calculation for a Rice Trader (*Bakul*):
* **Purchase:** *Bakul* buys 240 catties of white rice @ 60 dubbeltjes at Payaman market.
* **Transport:** Hire of 1 horse to transport load to Yogya = 16 dubbeltjes.
* **Market Tax:** Tax per horse pikul at market = 2.8 dubbeltjes.
* **Tollgate Fees (10 Tolls):** Magelang, Muntilan, Salam, Kalitoko, Tempel, Sleman (2 duits / 0.5 dubbeltje each) = 9.8 dubbeltjes.
* **Yogya Bazaar Sales Fee:** 3 dubbeltjes.
* **Total Overhead:** 15.6 dubbeltjes (transport cost almost matches net profit).
* **Net Margin:** Selling at usual Yogya market price (96 3/5 dubbeltjes), the *bakul* nets only 2 4/5 dubbeltjes per 240 catties (scarcely 4 dubbeltjes profit after 11 hours of labor traversing impassable roads and risking highway robbery).

#### Geographic Price Disparities:
* Rice in Yogyakarta is significantly more expensive than in Surakarta or North Coast ports due to rougher inland terrain (rice in Solo costs 1/2 the Yogya price).
* Regions like Bagelèn, Banyumas, Rema, and Ledok yield vast rice surpluses, but transport difficulties restrict distribution.
