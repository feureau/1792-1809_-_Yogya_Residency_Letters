### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.86: Salt Import Monopoly & Population Statistics (1806)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 86 (28 Feb. 1806 - Waterloo to Engelhard)**
* **Salt Import Trade:**
  * North Coast salt consumed exclusively in Eastern Mancanagara, Solo, Mataram, Pajang as far as Boyolali, Kedu, Bagelèn, Banyumas, Ledok, and Rema.
  * Duty-free salt import from North Coast would enrich only a small group of traders without benefiting the general populace.
* **Demographics & Population Growth:**
  * High infant mortality for children under age 12 (*see Kedu Register*).
  * Population growth rate: 1 in every 23 people over 5 years (4% per 5 years = 1% p.a.).
  * **Family (Heart) Count by Territory:**
    * Surakarta (*Sunan*): 87,000 families
    * Yogyakarta (*Sultan*): 90,000 families
    * Mangkunagaran (*MN*): 4,000 families
    * *Total:* 181,000 families.
  * *Population Estimate (@ 5 per family):* 905,000 people in Vorstenlanden.
  * *Historical Growth:*
    * 1760–1765: 950,250 total.
    * 1801: 1,393,472 (a 50% increase of 488,472 people in 45 years).
    * 1800–1805: Increase of 63,857 people (1 in 23).
  * *Village Expansion (1785–1805):* Substantial population growth within villages alongside opening of new villages in waste lands without damaging older settlements.
