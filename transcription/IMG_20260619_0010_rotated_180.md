### Wat – Eng. | 28 Feb. 1806.

**Question of Free Import of Salt from N. Coast:**  

* N. Coast salt only consumed in E. MN [Eastern Mancanagara] / Solo / Mataram / Pajang as far as Boyolali
* Rest Këdhu / Bagelèn / Banyumas / Lêdhok / Rêma get salt from S. Coast.
* If salt from N. Coast Free import = great profit would be made by a Handful of Traders / Rest would not Benefit.

---
