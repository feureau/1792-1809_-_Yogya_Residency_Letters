### [Transcription]

H 698 b **-1809-** AVJ 1809 (Yogya)  
[Letters from Batavia & to Batavia — but to Daendels / or to Sec Gen H. Veekens NOT specified Because bundle badly bound.]

*4 Jan.*  
*-> Batavia (?Wiese)*  
Treaty of 10 July 1796 made with knowledge of HB II: and Gezaghebber van Overstraten.  
By van Ysseldijck, & DR I  
concerning salutes to be given to Sultan / CP / Ratus / & other nobles on passing residency.  
ENTITLED to salutes : but NOT on EXTRA-ORDINARY OCCASIONS:  
But only NOT in evening

*13 Jan.*  
*-> Batavia*  
Necessary to build a new powder magazine:  
would be built at Expense of HB II  
but would have to await for MULUD (April 1809) when following old usage the MN Regents would arrive in Yogya.

*21 Jan.*  
*From Bat. to Wiese*  
Daendels complaints about the attitude of DR II concerning arrest of some "Jagers" (mounted Dutch soldiers)  
"You must tell this Patih, who has conducted himself improperly via HB II that 2nd time he has conducted himself so (? on orders of HB II) 3rd x a punishment would not fail to be given."  
In order to keep Peace in Java:  
Corps of Prangwēdana — 3000 men to be stationed at Klaten  

[Red ink: *1809 Stationing of PWP's Troops at Klaten.*]

*22 Jan. 1809*  
*-> Bat*  
Feest of Besar: CP / & all nobles to come to Residency after CEREMONIES on Pasêrbaan — to Drink Healths -> salutes with small shot (*klein geschut*)

*17 April*  
*-> Bat*  
Demand (July 1808) of Daendels -> HB II to fell wood in Sultan's domains in Oosthoek for stakes at FORT LODEWIJCK (Surabaya).  
Demand completed at MENARI stakes.
