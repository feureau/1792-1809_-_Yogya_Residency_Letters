### AVJ Letters from Batavia: Salute Protocols, Jager Arrests, & Klaten Garrison (1809)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1809 (H 698b)**
* **4 January 1809 (Salute Protocols):** Reaffirms 10 July 1796 treaty (*made with HB II, Governor Van Overstraten, Van IJsseldijk, and Danureja I*) regarding salutes for the Sultan, Crown Prince, and Ratus passing the Residency: salutes reserved strictly for official evening occasions.
* **13 January 1809 (Powder Magazine Construction):** New powder magazine to be constructed in fort at HB II's expense, awaiting arrival of Mangkunagaran Regents in April (*Mulud*).
* **21 January 1809 (Daendels Warning on Jager Arrests):**
  * **Daendels issues a severe warning to Patih Danureja II over improper arrest of mounted Dutch soldiers (*Jagers*):**
    > *"You must tell this Patih, who has conducted himself improperly toward HB II, that the 2nd time he has conducted himself so ... the 3rd time a punishment will not fail to be given."*
  * **Stationing of Prangwedana Legion at Klaten:** To preserve peace in Java, **Prangwedana's 3,000-man legion ordered to garrison Klaten Fort**.
* **17 April 1809 (Fort Lodewijk Stakes Delivered):** Requisition of Menari timber stakes from Sultan's domains in the *Oosthoek* for Fort Lodewijk (Surabaya) completed.
