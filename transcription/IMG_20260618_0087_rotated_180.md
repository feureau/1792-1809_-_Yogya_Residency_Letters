### Van Reede Memorie van Overgave 1801 (Cancellation of Nederburgh Visit & Court Solidarity)

* Reference: **N.O.K. 47 MvO Van Reede 3**
* **Refusal of Protocol:** HB II refuses to grant Commissioner-General Nederburgh greater honors than a Governor of Java's North-East Coast, offering only to meet him at Valley of Jean and send the Crown Prince to Kalavan. Points out no contract clause obliges such a reception.
* **National Dignity:** HB II declares that if Nederburgh visits Solo without visiting Yogya, he will treat it as an honor rather than an insult, stating God chose him *"to uphold the honors of the Javanese Nation"*.
* **Rice-Ball Covenant (*Sumpah Kepel*):** In a private council with his royal brothers, HB II forms a united front against Dutch demands, **personally rolling rice balls with his own hands which all his brothers eat as a binding oath**.
* **Patih Reconciliation:** Danureja I reconciled at this conference (pre-August 1799).
* **Finances & Mobilization:** Court Treasurer Raden Panji Padma Wijaya ordered to raise funds; royal lifewatch takes new oath of loyalty and funds allocated for armor for the *Prajurit* guards.
* **Outcome:** Nederburgh cancels visit to both courts; Patihs dispatched to Semarang instead.
* **Translation Note:** Words *"Leen"* (fief) and *"Leenheer"* (overlord) absent in Javanese contract translations (translator Martinghi wrote excellent Javanese; terminology altered in Yogya copy).
* **Garrisons:** Van Overstraten instructs Van IJsseldijk (upon 1798 Batavia return) to reinforce Solo and Yogya garrisons.
