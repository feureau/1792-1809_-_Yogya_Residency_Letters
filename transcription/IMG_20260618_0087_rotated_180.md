### Nederburgh's Visit Cancelled & HB II's Defiance:

- HB II persists in his outright refusal to accord Commissioner-General Sebastiaan Cornelis Nederburgh any greater ceremonial respect than that accorded to an ordinary Governor of Java's Northeast Coast.
  - Although HB II conceded he would meet him at the valley of Téam [?] and send the Crown Prince to Kalasan.
  - HB II argued there was no stipulation in the treaties and contracts with Yogyakarta compelling him to receive such a visit.
- **National / Divine mission:** HB II declared that if Nederburgh visited Surakarta without visiting Yogyakarta, he would treat it as an intolerable insult, proclaiming:
  - **\*** *Says that God had chosen him "to uphold the Honour of the Javanese Nation".* **\*** [red margin bar and underline].

---

### The Sultan's Blood-Brotherhood Pact & War Preparations:

- In a secret conference with his royal brothers, HB II secures their absolute loyalty to maintain a united front:
  - In a solemn traditional ritual, HB II **makes rice balls with his own hand**, which all the brothers eat together in covenant.
  - Danureja I also assisted at this conference (dating it prior to August 1799, c. 1796–1799).
- **Raden Panji Padnawijaya:** Royal Treasurer of HB II; directed to produce funds.
- Royal bodyguards (*Lifewatch*) swear a new oath of fealty to HB II.
- Treasury funds prepared to purchase arms and ammunition for the Sultan's *prajurit* regiments.

---

### Diplomatic Outcome & Feudal Vocabulary Dispute:

- Nederburgh is forced to postpone his visit to both Yogyakarta and Surakarta.
- Instead, the Patih is sent to Semarang.
- **Linguistic dispute over Sovereignty:** Van Reede observes that the feudal Dutch terms *"Leen"* (fief) and *"Leenheer"* (feudal overlord) do not occur in the official Javanese translations of the contracts.
  - Nicolaas Hartingh spoke and wrote excellent Javanese and would never have let such words slip by unintentionally; questions whether the text was modified in the Yogyakarta Kraton copy.
- In 1798, Governor Van Overstraten advised Resident Van IJsseldijk upon his return from Batavia to obtain military reinforcements for the garrisons in Surakarta and Yogyakarta.
