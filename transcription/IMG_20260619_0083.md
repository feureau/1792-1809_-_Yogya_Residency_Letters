### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.41: Embassy Tributes and Gold Regalia Presented to Daendels (Sept. 1808)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.41 (September 1808)**
* **Yogyakarta Embassy Departure (19 Sept. 1808):**
  * Patih Danureja II leads the Yogyakarta delegation to Semarang, accompanied by Kliwons R.T. Natadiningrat, Kyai T. Nindunagara, Mas T. Numanagara (Regent of Jipang/Kapadangan), and Secretary Ng. Prawirananstra.
* **Lavish Gold Tributes Presented by HB II to Daendels:**
  * 3 pairs krises with gold sheaths named *Naganizeka* (solid gold fittings).
  * 3 pairs gold-mounted pikes.
  * 1 solid gold betel box (*Sirih Box*), 2 small gold cartons, 1 gold *Pinang* box, 2 gold betel-leaf boxes, 1 gold nutcracker, 1 gold tray, 1 gold *Bakkor Daltok* box, 1 gold spittoon, 2 gold-mounted glasses, 2 gold-mounted pipes, 6 gold spoons, 6 gold forks.
  * 1/2 pikul prime birds' nests and 17 fine horses.
* **Surakarta Tributes (18 Sept. 1808):**
  * Raden Adipati Danuningrat, R.T. Sasrawijaya, Arung Binang, and Yudanegara (Banyumas) present 6 small horses, 2 large horses, 6 gold *Naganari* krises, and 16 gold pikes.
