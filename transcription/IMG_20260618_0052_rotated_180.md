| **24 March 1792** | ### [Header]<br><br>3. MvO Van Overstraten<br><br>### [Transcription]<br><br>#### 1. Mangkunegaran Affairs & Succession<br><br>- Prangwedana has shown signs of complete loyalty to the V.O.C.  <br>- After the death of Mangkunegara I in 1795, the V.O.C. no longer pays the **4,000 Sp.D.** yearly subsidy for the upkeep of Mangkunegaran troops.  <br>- Equal division of the inheritance of MN I [Mangkunagara I] (no mention of Van Reede's appropriations); divided according to the advice of Muslim priests (*ulama*).  <br>- Strategic consideration: Need for the V.O.C. to designate a successor for Prangwedana in case of his sudden demise.<br><br>#### 2. Inter-Court Relations: Failed Patih Protocol (1790–1792)<br><br>- 1790 exchange of visits between the Grand Viziers (Patih) of Sala and Yogya to each other's kratons to iron out outstanding difficulties.  <br>- *[Red marginal note:]* **1790 mutual compliments do not go through:**  <br>  Mutual compliments could not be formally exchanged between the Rulers via the Patih because of the protracted illness of HB I [Sultan Hamengkubuwana I] and the marriage celebrations in Sala (PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] marrying Ratu Kencana).<br><br>- HB I died (24 March 1792) before relations could be formally normalized through the Patih visits.<br><br>#### 3. The Fate of Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat<br><br>- Out of the 4,000 Sp.D. yearly stipend granted to MN I in 1790, **1,000 Sp.D.** was set aside in 1793 for **Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat**, who had recently returned from exile in Ceylon.  <br>  - Intended to keep him away from PB IV by residing at Semarang.  <br>- However, PB IV fell out with Kusumadiningrat while in Semarang and requested the V.O.C. to exile him from Java entirely.  <br>- Sent to Batavia in August 1795; looked after there by the Commissioner for Native Affairs (*Commissaris voor Inlandsche Zaken*).  <br>- *[Red ink annotation:]* **PB IV falls out + MN Prince Kusumadiningrat.** |
