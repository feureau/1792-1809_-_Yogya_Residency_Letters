### Van Overstraten Memorie van Overgave 1796 (Prangwedana & Kusumadiningrat)

* Reference: **3. MvO Van Overstraten**
* **Pangeran Prangwedana:** Demonstrates complete loyalty to V.O.C. Following death of MN I in 1795, V.O.C. ceases 4,000 guilders (*Np.D.*) annual troop maintenance subsidy. Equal division of MN I's personal inheritance arranged through religious scholars (*priests*). V.O.C. advised to designate a successor in event of Prangwedana's sudden death.
* **Inter-Court Relations (1790):** Exchange of Patih visits between Solo and Yogya to resolve outstanding disputes. Exchange of mutual compliments between rulers via Patihs failed due to HB I's illness and PB IV's marriage to Ratu Kencana. HB I dies before full diplomatic relations restored.
* **Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat:** Out of 4,000 guilders formerly paid to MN I, 1,000 guilders set aside in 1793 for Kusumadiningrat upon his return from exile in Ceylon (to separate him from PB IV and retain him at Semarang). PB IV later falls out with Kusumadiningrat at Semarang and requests V.O.C. exile him from Java; transferred to Batavia in August 1795 under care of Commissioner for Native Affairs.
