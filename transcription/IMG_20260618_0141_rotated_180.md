## Archival & Historical Notes

- **The Banyumas Chinese Conspiracy (12 November 1803):**
  - Following the intelligence reports noted on folio 0133 regarding the secret blood-oath conspiracy among Chinese secret society members (*triad* / Tiandihui elements) and fugitives from Cirebon and Tegal at Sokaraja/Banyumas, Resident Waterloo treats the situation as a security emergency of prime importance.
  - Through Patih Danureja II, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II is officially requested to mobilize armed patrols across the southwestern districts—specifically targeting Rêma, Lèdok, Karang Dhuwur, Bagêlèn, and Kêdhu—to locate and arrest all Chinese suspects.
  - Two European officers are dispatched to Tengaran near Sokaraja to oversee operations and coordinate with local regents.
