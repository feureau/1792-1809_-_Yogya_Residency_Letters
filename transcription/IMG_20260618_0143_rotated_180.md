### Archive Entries: July – August 1803 (Waterloo Correspondence & Kraton Fortification)

* Reference: **A.N. Yogya Letters No. 49 (July – August 1803)**
* **26 July 1803 (Waterloo -> Engelhard):** Official gifts presented to HB II upon taking office (July 1803): carriage, elephant, and a Persian horse. Van den Berg adds a Bimanese horse prior to departing for Semarang. Javanese prohibited from entering Sultan's stables; Patih prohibited from owning Bimanese horses.
* **3 August 1803 (Waterloo -> Engelhard):** HB II appoints Sasrakusuma as 2nd Bupati in Grobogan area, subordinate to Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III.
* **7 August 1803 (Waterloo -> Engelhard):** Residency lacks a Javanese translator. Patih Danureja II confers with Waterloo on state business.
* **17 August 1803 (Waterloo -> Engelhard):**
  * HB II attends court tournament on the *Pasirbaan*. Complaints regarding vulgarity at Javanese feast day celebrations.
  * Van den Berg noted as 50x busier than Waterloo. Kraton fortification (Matbon) incomplete; Van den Berg inspects progress from Solo.
  * Land negotiations between Engelhard, Crown Prince, and HB II resolved amicably. Carpenters arrive to work on garrison buildings.
