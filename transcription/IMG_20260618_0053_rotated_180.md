### [Transcription]

#### 1. Succession Safeguards & Guardianship Agreements (1792)

- HB I had wanted Van Overstraten to be present in Yogyakarta at the hour of his death, but was reluctant to summon him prematurely in vain.  
- PB IV stated in a sealed letter that in the event of his own demise, the **guardianship of his infant son** [Crown Prince] should be entrusted to Van Overstraten or another high V.O.C. official, together with the Patih and the Western Inner Bupati (*Bupati Nayaka / Gedhong Kiwa*).  
  - Formally agreed to by Van Overstraten in a letter dated **12 June 1792**.  
- Prangwedana and the House of Mangkunegaran pose no danger to the Court of Sala, as they are completely loyal to the Dutch.

#### 2. The Grand Viziers of Surakarta & Yogyakarta

- **Surakarta Patih Change (1796):**  
  - **Raden Adipati Jayaningrat** dismissed because of corruption and harsh government over the common people.  
  - Succeeded in 1796 by R.T. Japrawijaya [Nastrawijaya] under the title **Raden Adipati Mangkuprojo**.  
  - *Assessment of Mangkuprojo:* Appears to be a skilful, intelligent Javanese; seems genuinely attached to the Dutch.  
- **Yogyakarta Patih (Danureja I):**  
  - Well-disposed towards the V.O.C., but already **83 years old** and physically worn out.

#### 3. Court Rivalry in Yogyakarta: Sumadiningrat vs. Natayuda

- **Raden Tumenggung Natayuda** has fallen into deep disfavour; now reduced to serving merely as an adjoint Kliwon.  
- **Hatred of R.T. Sumadiningrat for Natayuda:**  
  Sumadiningrat actively works through his wife, **Ratu Bèndara**, to orchestrate the downfall of Natayuda.  

- Van Overstraten admits he dare not intervene in this domestic court feud, given the critical geopolitical situation of the V.O.C.
