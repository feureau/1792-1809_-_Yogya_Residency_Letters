### Court Succession Wills & Ministerial Changes (1792–1796)

* **Hamengkubuwana I Deathbed Request:** HB I requested Van Overstraten's presence in Yogya at his death, but was reluctant to summon him prematurely.
* **Pakubuwana IV Guardianship Will:** PB IV specifies in a sealed letter (12 June 1792) that upon his death, guardianship of his son shall be entrusted to Van Overstraten or another V.O.C. official, alongside the Patih and Inner Regents.
* **Prangwedana House:** Pangeran Prangwedana and his household pose no danger to Surakarta court due to total loyalty to Dutch.
* **Surakarta Patih Dismissal (1796):** Raden Adipati Jayaningrat dismissed for corruption and harsh governance over the common people. Succeeded by R.T. Sasrawijaya (**Raden Adipati Mangkupraja**), described as an intelligent and skillful Javanese attached to the Dutch.
* **Yogyakarta Patih & Natayuda:**
  * **Danureja I:** Loyal to V.O.C., but 83 years old and frail.
  * **Natayuda:** Falls into disfavor at Yogya court; demoted to Adjoint Kliwon. Mangkudiningrat's hostility toward Natayuda worked through his wife (Ratu Bendara) to ensure Natayuda's downfall. Van Overstraten avoids intervening due to delicate geopolitical circumstances.
