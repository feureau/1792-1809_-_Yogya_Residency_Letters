| **12 June 1792** | ### [Transcription]<br><br>#### 1. Succession Safeguards & Guardianship Agreements (1792)<br><br>- HB I [Sultan Hamengkubuwana I] had wanted Van Overstraten to be present in Yogyakarta at the hour of his death, but was reluctant to summon him prematurely in vain.  <br>- PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] stated in a sealed letter that in the event of his own demise, the **guardianship of his infant son** [Crown Prince] should be entrusted to Van Overstraten or another high V.O.C. official, together with the Patih and the Western Inner Bupati (*Bupati Nayaka / Gedhong Kiwa*).  <br>  - Formally agreed to by Van Overstraten in a letter dated **12 June 1792**.  <br>- Prangwedana and the House of Mangkunegaran pose no danger to the Court of Sala, as they are completely loyal to the Dutch.<br><br>#### 2. The Grand Viziers of Surakarta & Yogyakarta<br><br>- **Surakarta Patih Change (1796):**  <br>  - **Raden Adipati Jayaningrat** dismissed because of corruption and harsh government over the common people.  <br>  - Succeeded in 1796 by R.T. Japrawijaya [Nastrawijaya] under the title **Raden Adipati Mangkuprojo**.  <br>  - *Assessment of Mangkuprojo:* Appears to be a skilful, intelligent Javanese; seems genuinely attached to the Dutch.  <br>- **Yogyakarta Patih (Danureja I):**  <br>  - Well-disposed towards the V.O.C., but already **83 years old** and physically worn out.<br><br>#### 3. Court Rivalry in Yogyakarta: Sumadiningrat vs. Natayuda<br><br>- **Raden Tumenggung Natayuda** has fallen into deep disfavour; now reduced to serving merely as an adjoint Kliwon.  <br>- **Hatred of R.T. Sumadiningrat for Natayuda:**  <br>  Sumadiningrat actively works through his wife, **Ratu Bèndara**, to orchestrate the downfall of Natayuda.<br><br>- Van Overstraten admits he dare not intervene in this domestic court feud, given the critical geopolitical situation of the V.O.C. |
