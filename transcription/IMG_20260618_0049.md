### Main Text & Margin Notes

| Margin Notes | Main Text |
| :--- | :--- |
| **13 October 1796** | ### [Transcription]<br><br>#### 1. Geopolitical Threat: The English & The Prophecy<br><br>- If the English reach Java, they would be in a position to **make the prophecy come true** [regarding the Amangkurat restoration].  <br>- Present parlous condition of the V.O.C. requires **doubling of prudence** with regard to the courts.  <br>- Guard especially against any contacts between the English and the courts.<br><br>#### 2. Character of Pakubuwana IV & Royal Family Dynamics<br><br>- Van Overstraten doubts PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] is merely credulous / superstitious (*bijgeloovig*), as Resident Van Greven made him out to be during the 1790 Pakepung crisis:  <br>  > He is *"young, shrewd, and full of fire"*.<br><br>- **Pangeran Aria Kusumadiningrat:** a very bad influence on PB IV.  <br>- PB IV married his 3rd wife (daughter of Bupati of Pamekasan / Ratu Kencana) in 1791.  <br>  - **Crown Prince [later PB V]:** Born c. 1788; appointed Crown Prince in August 1792 by Van Overstraten.  <br>  - Doted on by PB IV, but little done about his education (*zeer verwaarloosd*); character of CP [Crown Prince / later HB III] might be spoiled by excessive doting.  <br>- **Two children of 3rd wife (Ratu Kencana):**  <br>  - Princess: **Ratu Pembayun** (born 1795).  <br>  - Prince: **Pangeran Purbaya** (born 1796).  <br>- **Marriage link:** Sister of Ratu [Kencana] was given to **Pangeran Mangkubumi**, but had since died (pre-1796).  <br>- **Pangeran Buminata:** married daughter of the Bupati of Pati.  <br>  - *Mangkubumi:* more fiery and energetic.  <br>  - *Buminata:* gentle and subtle.  <br>  - V.O.C. must maintain the friendship of these two princes, especially as PB IV is very fond of them. |
