**Date Range:** 25 December 1805 – 28 February 1806  
**Primary Subjects:**  
- Governor Nicolaas Engelhard's socio-economic and agrarian inquiry on Yogyakarta (25 Dec 1805)  
- Proposal for toll-free export of rice to Semarang and the North Coast  
- 8-point questionnaire: sawah expansion, district production, second harvest (palawija), draught buffaloes, seed padi (*bibit*), tax oppression, introduction of new ploughs, and 5-year demographic/village (*desa*) growth  
- Resident Waterloo's comprehensive 68-page memorandum on Central Javanese agriculture (28 Feb 1806)  
- Limits of agrarian expansion: immediate environs of Yogyakarta saturated with sawahs; indigenous irrigation systems fed by Mount Merapi  
- Envoys dispatched to Eastern Mancanagara (E. MN) to survey agrarian conditions  
- Regional survey: Mataram province (shared between Sunan and Sultan), high fertility, optimal water management, and intensive rice cultivation

### [25 December 1805: Engelhard's Agrarian Questionnaire]

*[Left margin:]*  
Eng. to Wat.  
25 Dec. 1805.  
*[Red double vertical line]*

HB II should be prevailed upon to allow Rice to be exported to N. Coast without Tolls.  
great expansion of Rice production in Yogya in recent years.

(1) Asks How many Rice fields laid out in Recent years.  
(2) Production of Each District.  
(3) ? If 2nd Harvest generally planted.  
(4) Use of Buffaloes in agriculture.  
(5) Sale of Bibit (Seed padi) to Farmer.  
vi. If Farmer not Discouraged by Terms / Tax into not cultivating land.  
vii. ? if new plough should be introduced.  
x. If pop. increases in last 5 years. If Desas increase in last 5 years.

### [28 February 1806: Waterloo's 68-Page Reply]

*[Left margin:]*  
68 page Reply of Wat.  
Wat. – Eng.  
28 Feb. 1806.  
*[Annotated:]* [Similar letter in Djokjo Br. no. 37]

Asks HB II for Free Export of Rice to Semarang + N. Coast from Yogya + encourages opening up of new sawahs.

HB II active in encouraging Rice production in Yogya.  
Not possible to open up any new Ricefields in immediate vicinity of Yogya.  
everything has been done which could be done.

Special messengers sent to E. MN [Eastern Mancanagara] by Wat to enquire into state of affairs there.

Concerning Export of Rice to N. Coast:  
(1) Situation in HB II's Lands = Mataram / Pajang / Kèdhu / Bagelen etc Regarding Commerce to Pasisir.

**Mataram:**  
Shared Sunan / Sultan.  
Very fertile Farmers.  
well irrigated from Merapi / Jav. lay out simple but effective irrigation system.  
*[Red asterisk and double vertical red line]*  
almost completely opened up to sawahs.  
mostly Rice produced.
