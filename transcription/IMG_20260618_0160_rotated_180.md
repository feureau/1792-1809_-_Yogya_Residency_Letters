### Archive Entries: May – July 1804 (Rice Price Surge & Klaten Fort Agreement)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1804**
* **5 May 1804 (Historic Rice Price Surge):**
  * Rice prices reach an all-time high of **3.46 silver Rixdollars per 100 catties (1 pikul)**.
  * Coconut oil (*Klapper olie*) surges to 6–7 stuivers per bottle. Price drops slightly; white rice falls 30 stuivers per pikul, while red rice remains high (coconut oil in Kedu at 10 stuivers/bottle).
* **16 June 1804 (Klaten Fort Construction):** Materials collected to begin construction of **Klaten Fort (Marborg)**. HB II distributes material quotas evenly among all Yogya Regents.
* **21 June 1804 (Kedu Coffee Assembly):** Plan with R.T. Natayuda to set up a coffee assembly point at Bedara; Natayuda reports planting 150,000 coffee trees in Kedu.
* **18 July 1804 (Klaten Fort Conference):**
  * HB II dispatches Danureja II, R. PWD III, R.T. Sumadiningrat, Natayuda, and Kyai Wiraguna (Patih of Kadipaten) to Klaten.
  * **Klaten Fort Agreement signed on Saturday, 21 July 1804.** Waterloo confers with Solo Patih and Resident.
* **28 July 1804:** Waterloo inspects Klaten site with 1st Resident of Solo (Lt.-Col. Von Woltzogen). Fort site selected between two rivers with abundant water supply.
