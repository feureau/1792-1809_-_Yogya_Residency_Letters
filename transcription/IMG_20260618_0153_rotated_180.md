### Archive Entries: November 1803 (Peranakan Kampong Plan & Sultan's Movements)

* Reference: **A.N. Yogya No. 49 (30 Nov. 1803)**
* **Security & Peranakan Demographics:** Security in Java perceived in crisis due to constant external rumors. 25,000 Chinese *peranakans* estimated in Central Java. Van Overstraten proposes establishing dedicated *peranakan* kampongs (e.g. West side of Surabaya / Brantas River).
* **Ujung Border Incident:** Yogya Demang's soldiers (*Tambour Nitipranira*) robbed by Chinese at Ujung. Crown Prince prepares 2 companies of *Prajurits* to march to Ujung; Waterloo intervenes and instructs CP's Patih **Wiraguna** to restrain the Crown Prince.
* **Solo-Yogya Tension:** Van den Berg orders Chinese in Solo to return the stolen drum immediately, narrowly averting open conflict between Solo and Yogya.
* **Sultan HB II's Movements:**
  * *"Sultan is adopting his old, former behavior and Day & Night makes the rounds of his places (Pesanggrahans), He resides preferably in the Limestone hills"* (*Zijn Hoogheid de Sultan begint thans zijn oude gang weder te gaan, en zwerft nachten en dag op zijn plaatsen rond, hij logeert thans bij preferentie op de kalkberg*).
