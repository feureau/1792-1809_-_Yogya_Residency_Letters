### Archive Entries: April – May 1807 (Pacitan Robbery Investigation & Klaten Fort Completion)

* **20 April 1807 (Pacitan Robbery Investigation):**
  * Joint commission investigates the robbery of Cadet Bombardier Du Bois in Pacitan (Yogya village of Jangang returned).
  * Surakarta and Yogyakarta send envoys: HB II's *Mantris* Ng. Praja Leksana and Wiraraga, *Jaksas* Wangsatamura and Citra Dirana, and Infantry Corporal P. Gulo.
  * Levied 472.9 Ronde Reals from *Mancapati/Mancalimas* (4–5 neighboring villages) as guarantee money for the robbery.
* **14 May 1807 (Mancanagara Regent Appointments):**
  * Following death of Rajegwesi Bupati R.T. Sasradiningrat, Grobogan Regent R.T. Sasradiwiria transferred to Rajegwesi.
  * R. Sasrataruna appointed Regent of Grobogan under the title **R.T. Sasranegara**.
* **16 May 1807 (Completion of Klaten Fort):**
  * **Klaten Fort completed post-21 April 1807.**
  * Outside the fort, necessary auxiliary structures constructed, including a *pesanggrahan* (resting house) for the Governor of Java's N.E. Coast during official court journeys.
