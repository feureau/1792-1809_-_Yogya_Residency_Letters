### [Transcription]

*11 April 1809*  
*-> Bat.*  
HB II makes a visit (*een uitstapje*) to his Son-in-Law Natadiningrat.  
Goes in full state accompanied by all the Dragoons:  
arrival of Rangga PWD III & lesser MN heads in Yogya + WORK FORCE.

*2 Feb. 1809*  
*from Bat.*  
Decision to abolish Prefectweg Rèmbang:  
to join: Regency of Tuban / Prefectweg Gresik -> Pref. Japara Reg. Juana.  
Kabupaten Lasêm / Rembang / Cengkalsewu / Gronggang / under Direct ADMIN of College of Forests.

*11 Oct. 1809*  
*from Bat*  

[Red double margin line:]

Daendels gives orders to BURN HB II's Desas in Tegal border who had a hand in Robbery in Regency of BREBES.  

[Red ink: *Kēdhawong*]

Daendels feels exceedingly provoked by CONSTANT marauding in woods of Keduin. (Kadoew) where incident much ARSON.

*1 Aug. 1809*  
*from Bat*  
Payment of Strandgeld to Sala / Yogya ->  
Sp. D. 10,000 = 12,500 V.I.N.G. money / Rond Realen.

*14 Nov. 1809*  
*from Bat / to*  
After Festival of Puasa: HB II takes advantage of PRESENCE of many Bupatis present & work forces / retinue  

[Red ink: *pojok balowêrti extended*]

[Red double line:]

all pressed ganged into Projecting the 4 points of the Kraton's Bastions 15-18'.  
DR II says that HB II does it to copy Dutch fortress  
also Wiese learns that HB I had put up the whole wall of the kraton in less than 3 MONTHS.

*20 Nov. 1809*  
*to Bat.*  
<u>Death of Ratu Madretna wife of PWD III on 16 Nov.</u>
