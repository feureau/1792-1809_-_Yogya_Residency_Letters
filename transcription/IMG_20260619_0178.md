### AVJ Letters: Arson Orders at Tegal Border, Pojok Balowerti Bastions, & Death of Ratu Maduretna (1809)

* **11 April 1809:** HB II travels in state under Dragoon escort to visit his son-in-law R.T. Natadiningrat. Raden Rongga III and Mangkunagaran heads arrive in Yogyakarta with court labor forces.
* **11 October 1809 (Daendels Arson Order on Tegal Border):**
  * **Daendels orders Dutch troops to BURN Yogyakarta villages along the Tegal border (Kedhawong)** involved in Brebes robberies.
  * Expresses intense provocation over persistent illegal logging and arson in Kedu teak forests.
* **14 November 1809 (Construction of Projecting Pojok Balowerti Bastions):**
  * **HB II leverages the presence of rural Bupatis and corvée workforces post-*Puasa* to rebuild the Kraton walls, constructing 4 projecting corner bastions (*Pojok Balowerti*) extended 15–18 feet.**
  * Patih Danureja II notes HB II designed the bastions to copy Dutch fortresses; Minister Wiese learns HB II completed the entire outer wall in under 3 months.
* **20 November 1809:** **Death of Ratu Maduretna (wife of Raden Rongga Prawiradirja III) on 16 November 1809.**
