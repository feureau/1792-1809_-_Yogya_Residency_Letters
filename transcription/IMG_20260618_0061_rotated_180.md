#### Madura Contingents & Revenues (Continuation from folio 0060)

- **Each year:**  
  - **4,000 Sp. Realen** from *Shahbandar* (harbour master / customs) rights.  
  - **60 koyangs** green beans (*kacang hijau*).  
  - **12,000 cans** of coconut oil at 6 stuivers per can.  
  - **30 pikuls** cotton thread (in 1789 produced 8 pikuls extra due to a successful harvest).
