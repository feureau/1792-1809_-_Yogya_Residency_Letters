### Archive Entries: August 1808 (HB II Defense Preparations & Ceremonial Reception for Daendels)

* Reference: **Pj. Br. 23 (15–26 Aug. 1808)**
* **15 August 1808 (Daendels Direct Intervention Notice):**
  * HB II receives notice that Daendels will arrive in person with 3,000 troops to settle all outstanding court disputes, citing HB II's unstable sentiments (*"geen vastigheid in mijn sentimenten"*).
  * HB II cancels troop dispatches to Bagelèn/Kedu (originally intended to monitor Prangwedana's legion near Cilacap) after Engelhard assures him Prangwedana acts under strict government orders.
* **Reception Arrangements for Daendels:**
  * Temporary barracks and provisions arranged for Daendels' 3,000 troops.
  * **Royal Reception Line:** HB II awaits Daendels at Demangan; Crown Prince awaits at Papringan; Mangkukusuma awaits at Kalasan; Patih Danureja II and Outer *Nayakas* accompany Engelhard to Klaten.
* **18 August 1808 (New Ceremonial Delivery):**
  * Engelhard delivers Daendels' new ceremonial rules to HB II in the Kraton.
  * Engelhard's royal umbrella (*payung*) carried inside the Kraton by an old *Nyai* attendant; HB II receives Engelhard in an exceptionally polite and friendly manner.
