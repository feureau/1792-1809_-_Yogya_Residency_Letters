### AvJ Archive Entries: November – December 1804 (Demographic Growth & Agricultural Exhaustion)

* **20 November 1804:** Communication received that Sunan permits Surakarta Mangkunagaran Regents to return home. Regent of Japaraga (R.T. Sasrajaya) raised to R.T. Sasrawijaya.
* **26 November 1804:** Envoys of Patih Danuningrat arrive in Yogya led by Mas Ng. Darukrama.
* **29 December 1804 (Inquiry into High Rice Prices):** Governor of Java's N.E. Coast inquires why rice has become so scarce and expensive.
  * **1. Exhaustion of Soil:** Continuous year-round rice planting (2–3 harvests annually) exhausts fields. Yields per *Mangmat* over a 25–30 year period fell from 3 sacks to 2 sacks.
  * **2. Increased Transport Costs:** Horse hire between Yogya and Semarang increased from 1/2 Ducatoon to at least 1 Ducatoon per horse (2 pikuls capacity), raising price by 1/2 Sp. D. per sack.
  * **3. Population Doubling:** Population of Java doubled over 50 years.
    * In 1755, HB I had 5–6 children (2 surviving: P. Ngabehi and HB II).
    * By 1792, HB I's descendants numbered 200 children/grandchildren. Natakusuma born post-1755. Pangeran Mangkudiningrat (born c. 1775) has 28–29 children.
