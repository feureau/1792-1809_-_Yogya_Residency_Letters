### [29 December 1804: Analysis of Java's Rice Scarcity & Demographic Expansion]

* **Date & Source:** `29 Dec. / Res Yogya` *(29 Dec. underlined in red ink)*
* **Governor's Inquiry:**
  * `Letter of Gov. of Java 23 Dec 1804:`
  * `? Why is Rice so scarce + Expensive?`

#### 1. Exhaustion of Rice Fields

* `* EXHAUSTION OF FIELDS:`
  * `That the unceasing Planting of Rice:`
  * `over a number of years — exhausting the fields:`
  * `usually 2 sometimes 3 harvests off the fields in one year —`
  * `Production over 25-30 year period fixed`
  * `say at 3 sacks (Hangmaten) door malkander`
  * `later only 2 (Hangmaten)`

#### 2. Increase in Transport Costs

* `* (2) INCREASE IN TRANSPORT COSTS:`
  * `Earlier 1/2 Ducatoon would hire a Horse from Yogya -> Semarang`
  * `now at least 1 Ducatoon.`
  * `Each Horse 2 Pikuls Rice = .: price increased by 1/2 Sp. D per sack.`

#### 3. Demographic Doubling & Royal Proliferation

* `* (3) much greater POPULATION of Java:`
  * `Pop Had Doubled itself in 50 years: (throughout Java)`
  * `1755: HB I has 5-6 children = 2 still alive (Ngabêhi / HB II)`
  * `1792: HB I has 200 grandchildren   .: NK [Natakusuma] born after 1755`
* **Circled Note at Bottom:**
  * `P. Mangkudiningrat not yet 30 years old Has (born c. 1775)`
  * `(son of Ratu Anom / later Ratu Mas) (has 28-29 children)`
