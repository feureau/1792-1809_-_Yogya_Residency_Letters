| **1793** | ### 1793 Loano / Pacitan Report 2 (Van IJsseldijk)<br><br>*(Dj. Br. 45: W. van Ysseldijk, 'Eerbiedig Bericht')*<br><br>#### Pepper Acreage & Yield Targets:<br><br>- **700 cacahs of pepper lands** — to be increased by **100 cacahs** by new agreement.<br>- + Labourers from Winong (where no pepper planted) to be used on estates.<br>- V.O.C. could now begin to expect yearly **5,000 pikuls of pepper** promised by HB I [Sultan Hamengkubuwana I].<br><br>---<br><br>#### Indigo Factory Negotiations (Plaosan & Merapi):<br><br>- *Cf. Original Act of Verbintenis of Danureja I (DRI).*<br>- **Plaosan & Adjacent lands / Desas**:<br>  - Promise of HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]: V.O.C. to be allowed 100 cacahs for setting up Indigo Factory near Merapi.<br>  - But Indigo requires huge investment of labour (especially gathering wood to boil/process indigo); doubtful if 100 cacahs sufficient.<br>  - Very difficult to get *bupatis* to surrender people: all hands needed to meet *pajak* and corvée services of HB II.<br>  - Proposed site near Merapi turned down due to lack of labour and timber.<br>- **Winong Alternative**:<br>  - Area of Winong far more suitable: mountain streams and woods.<br>  - Crop rotation: Rice in wet season / Indigo in dry season.<br>  - HB II prepared to surrender people on 100 cacahs in Winong, with permission to tend ricefields during the wet season.<br><br>---<br><br>#### Production in Pacitan & Labor Organization:<br><br>- 700 cacahs (168 jungs) in Loano / Winong etc.<br>- In Pacitan: not counting 200 cacahs in Pacitan (*+ 200 from Nelo*).<br>  - Very good appearance; densely bushed.<br>  - Indigo / pepper could be produced together: Pepper planted in wet monsoon; during harvest fewer hands needed and could work indigo.<br>- Outside Pacitan: 87 desas (119 jungs) suitable for pepper; **2,245 people** could be employed on pepper estates.<br>- 2 *Demangs* increased to 4 by Sultan to organize labour force.<br>- Need to distribute more buffaloes to inhabitants of Loano / Malarang:<br>  - HB II suggests ½ provided by Resident, ½ by Patih, so that Sultanate retains a semblance of control over the area.<br><br>#### Land of Pacitan:<br><br>- **Extremely suited for cultivation of pepper.** Currently growing wild — similar to plots in **Banten**. |
