### Rajawinangun Review (Cont.), Dutch Linen Weaving, & Crown Prince's Use of Malay (June – July 1808)

* **Rajawinangun Review Protocols:**
  * Princes pay homage at the *Pagelaran* at Rajawinangun. Regiments exercise for 2 hours before returning to Yogya at 4:00 p.m.
  * *Crown Prince Popularity:* Crown Prince praised for dignified, friendly demeanor (*"om zijn goede hoedanigheden, gevoegd bij een deftig en vriendelijk voorkomen van groot en klein bemind word"*).
  * **Mandatory Use of Malay Language:** Crown Prince calls out loudly to his followers to speak **only Malay** with Dutch visitors, declaring Malay to be the proper language of friendship between the Dutch and Javanese courts.
* **Prangwedana Troop Exercises (28 June 1808):** Servatius reports 5,050 Prangwedana troops participate in exercises (2,337 armed with muskets, though unskilled in marksmanship).
* **Weaving Uniform Cloth for Dutch Troops (July 1808):**
  * Col. Gordon commissioned to procure Javanese woven cloth patterns in Surakarta for military uniforms.
  * T. Winter identifies skilled weavers in Karang Bolong and Tangang area to build simple looms for Semarang.
* **Cavalry Logistics (11–15 July 1808):** Lt. Petele attempts to assemble 98 pack-runners for Yogyakarta cavalry. Two subjects (Ningadirana and Totruna) arrested for arson in Yogyakarta.
