### 15 / 28 January 1800 (Resident of Surakarta):

- Departure of Surakarta Mangkunegaran regents to their districts.

---

### 28 October 1800 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- Resident keeps a vigilant eye out for roaming Muslim "priests" / religious leaders (*Paapen* / *santris*) in the countryside.

---

### 21 January 1801 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- HB II grants formal permission for the Yogyakarta Mangkunegaran Regents to return home.

---

### 26 June 1801 (Resident of Surakarta):

- Marriage of the sister of PB IV to the Regent of Pamekasan (**Raden Tumenggung Cakradiningrat / Prawirasêbênch**).

---

### 1 October 1801 (Resident of Surakarta):

- Arrival of envoys / messengers from Danureja II to Surakarta Patih Raden Adipati Mangkupraja:
  - Head Jaksa: **Nitipraja**.
  - Ngabèhis: **Kêrtadimeja**, **Karsawikrama**, **Patradiwiria**, **Ungawêcana**, **Mērtadiwiria**, **Citrayuda**.
  - Attended by Lurahs and retainers (*usatawas*) of the Kepatihan.

---

### 21 November 1801 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- On the occasion of a rare good disposition of HB II following the royal tournament on the *Pasir Baan*:
  - Resident speaks with HB II inside the Kraton.
  - Inquiry into the administration of PWD III in Madiun; agreed that a trustworthy person should be appointed.
  - **Kyai Adipati Purwadiningrat:** Head Regent of Magetan.

---

### The Rift between Pangeran Natakusuma and HB II:

[Red marginal bars:]

- Talk of a deepening split between **Pangeran Natakusuma** and **HB II**.
- However, Natakusuma still occasionally appears at Court on ceremonial occasions, such as bringing forward official letters.
- **Character assessment:** Natakusuma is a very clever, shrewd prince who **would initiate nothing against the peace of the kingdom** so long as he is not pushed too far or provoked beyond endurance by HB II.
