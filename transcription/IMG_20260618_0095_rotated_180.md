### AvJ Archive Entries: 1800–1801 (Court Envoys, Gambling Watch, and Natakusuma Rift)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1800–1**
* **15 / 28 January 1800:** Surakarta Mangkunagaran Regents depart. Dutch keep watch for *Presti* (*Paapen* / religious agitators) in the countryside.
* **21 January 1801 (Res. Yogya):** HB II grants permission for Yogyakarta Mangkunagaran Regents to return home.
* **26 June 1801 (Res. Solo):** Sister of PB IV marries Regent of Pamekasan (Prawiraserdikah – R.T. Cakraadiningrat).
* **1 October 1801 (Res. Solo):** Envoys of Patih Danureja II (headed by Mangkupraja and Head Jaksa Nitipreja) arrive with Ngabehis (Kertadimeja, Kartaunikrama, Paradiwiria, Unganwicana, Mertadiwiria, Citrayuda) regarding Kadipaten *Ventaras* land dispute.
* **21 November 1801 (Res. Yogya):** Following tournament on the *Pasirbaan*, Resident confers with HB II in Kraton:
  * Inquiry regarding Adipati PWD III in Madiun; trustworthy candidate to be appointed.
  * Kyai Adipati Purwadiningrat appointed Head Regent of Hajifan.
  * **Split between Natakusuma and HB II:** Natakusuma appears at court only to deliver official correspondence. Shrewd prince who avoids provoking HB II unnecessarily.
