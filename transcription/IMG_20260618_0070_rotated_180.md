## Content

### AvJ Archive Entries: February – October 1796 (Yogya Administrative Reshuffle & Troop Quality)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1796**
* **15 February 1796 (Res. Yogya):** **HB II dismisses R.T. Natayuda as Kliwon** (reassigned as Tumenggung Miji).
  * **R.T. Danukusuma** replaces R.T. Natayuda.
  * Danukusuma's position taken by **R.T. Mertanegara** (later Danureja II) as *Dalem Regent*.
  * Mertanegara's position taken by **R.T. Sumadiningrat**.
* **4 March 1796:** Pangeran Adinegara takes over post. Death of HB II's sister, Raden Ayu Jasrakusuma (3 March 1796).
* **12 July 1796:** News received of dismissal of Surakarta Patih Jayaningrat and appointment of Raden Adipati Mangkupraja.
* **25 July 1796:** **Installation of Surakarta Crown Prince (later Pakubuwana V):** R.M. Gusti Timur (illegitimate son of PB IV) raised to Pangeran Panular / Crown Prince.
* **4 October 1796 (Res. Yogya):** Van IJsseldijk reports that in event of British landing, HB II will send auxiliary troops to Batavia under revised 1796 pay terms. Each Regent required to furnish troops based on sawah appanages.
* **Critique of Mataram Auxiliary Troops:**
  * Regents avoid sending family or trusted followers; instead impress common peasants (*Negorij folk*) equipped with pikes, unserviceable rifles, and poor horses: *"Sum total of a Mataram soldier and his equipment."*
