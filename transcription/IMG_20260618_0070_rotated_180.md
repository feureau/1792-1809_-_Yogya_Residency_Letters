### 15 February 1796 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- Past Monday, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II dismisses **Raden Tumenggung Natayuda** as *kliwon*; placed as Tumenggung Mijil.
- **Raden Tumenggung Danukusuma** takes the place of R.T. Natayuda [as First Kliwon].
- The place of Danukusuma is taken by **Raden Tumenggung Mērtanēgara** (later Danureja II).
- The place of Mērtanēgara as *Dalem* Regent is taken by **Raden Tumenggung Sumadiningrat**.

---

### 4 March 1796 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- Pangeran Adinegara takes place of [Dalem Regent / previous post].
- **3 March 1796:** Death of the sister of HB II, **Raden Ayu Sasrakusuma**.

---

### 12 July 1796 (Resident of Yogyakarta -> Surakarta):

- News of the dismissal of Sala Patih [Jayaningrat]; appointment of **Raden Adipati Mangkupraja**.

---

### 25 July 1796 (Resident of Surakarta):

- **APPOINTMENT of PANGERAN ADIPATI ANOM (future PB V)** [Red circled; red marginal annotation: *Sala Crown Prince*].
- R.M. [...] illegitimate brother of PB IV becomes **Pangeran Panular**.

### 4 October 1796 (Resident of Yogyakarta - Van IJsseldijk):

[Red marginal annotation: **?? Yogya Troops to Batavia.**]

- Van IJsseldijk is convinced that if the English make a landing, HB II would send mercenaries / reinforcement troops to Batavia.
- But HB II would **NOT** do it under the same conditions as in 1793.
- New regulations regarding payments to troops and leaders of troops.
- Each Regent is expected to produce troops according to their *numbang* / number of household heads / receptacles.
- *Observations on recruitment (red margin bar):*
  - The Regents never willingly employ members of their own families or accredited followers.
  - They usually round up common *negorij* folk (? Yogya).
  - *"To a pike, an unserviceable rifle, a miserable horse, & a yet more miserable rider — is the sum total of a Mataram soldier and his equipage."* [Underlined in red]
