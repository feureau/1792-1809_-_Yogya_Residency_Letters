### [Transcription]

**3 Jan [1794]**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede (Solo):**  
Latest exchanges of land between Solo / Yogya in area of Penging noted in letter of Jayaningrat $\rightarrow$ Danureja II.

---

**19 Jan [1794]**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:**  
HB II reports marriage in his family:  
*[Red ink annotation:]* ? R. Ayu Sepuh = d. of Sasradiningrat.

---

**20 Jan [1794]**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:**  
Dispute of HB II + Governt [Government] over Bangil - Japan / Prabalingga cleared up.

---

**6 Feb 1794**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede:**  
Messengers of Jayaningrat:  

- Raden Ng. Riksa-jaya  
- Ng. Kērtatija  
To settle dispute in Prabalingga Kidul.  
HB II told.

---

**22 Feb 1794**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:**  
Arrival in Batavia of Nederburgh / Frijkenius as Commissioners-General.  
Appointment of Alting as Governor-General [W.N. - waarnemend].  
Letter of greeting to new G.G. written by HB II.

---

**18 Mar [1794]**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:**  
Concerning indigo — Hopes to deliver indigo in August 1794.

---

**18 June 1794**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Reede (Solo):**  
News of death of P.A. Mangkudiningrat from Solo.  

+ Appointment of son R.M. Prawirawata commanding Jartarra [Dhartatraya / Tartar?] Regiment + same name.  
Incident at Solo désa of Banjakkan [Bancakan / Blakan] = *so vast bigwiry*.
