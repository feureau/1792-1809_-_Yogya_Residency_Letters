**Daendels to Engelhard, 16 May 1808:**  

* Wants immediate report from Eng about situation in Yogya Residency Office (Financial Report).

**Eng to Daendels, 8 Jun. 1808:**  

* Report on situation at Yogya Fort.
* HB II's MN subjects supposed to work on Fort for 90 days in a row after Garebeg Mulud. But HB II burdens them with many other Building projects so that they never achieve v. much.
* Fort at Klathèn labouring under same difficulties.
* Roads. Fort at Bayalali: ½ Responsibility of Reg. of Semarang, ½ Responsibility of HB II.

<span style="color:red">Bps Kêdhu etc. Reside in Yogya.</span>  

* Bps of Kêdhu / Bagelèn / Mataram / Pajang / Sukawati now have Residences in MN Regencies = all Reside in Yogya.
* If Border Dispute = Commission sent out from courts to Inquire by Patih + accompanied by Europeans.
* 1st Inner Regent Purwadipura. all communications to HB II have to pass through him.
* Enough Suitable ground in Mataram for Coffee production. See Memorie of Waterloo.
* Roads to Semarang / Solo from Yogya in a v. poor state. almost no Bridges over Rivers.
* <span style="color:red">\*</span> *Wang Kêrigaji* = Raised from local population as contribution for Road Building = But HB II hands over v. little of it to DR II for this. Most kept for HB II's Treasury. Despite stipulation in Treaty of 1792 that HB II must keep up Roads.
