### [Transcription]

**13 Oct 1794**  
**Van IJsseldijk $\rightarrow$ Van Overstraten:**  
Agreement of HB II to Pepper Plantations in lands (? Loano) ceded to V.O.C.  
Plantations should be brought as soon as possible to necessary broadness.  
Javanese workers there discharged from all forms of *Herrendienst* (labour services).  
Van IJsseldijk: would see that they would remain free of any oppression from their own heads or European overseer.  
See that no displacement of people from Sultan's lands to V.O.C. lands.  

**Refusal of HB II to allow measurement of land:** fears annexation by V.O.C.  
Jurisdiction of land should be settled by Kraton $\therefore$ under HB II's law.  

HB II says cannot persuade Bupatis to PLANT pepper — because already have to cede LAND for estate; fear that inhabitants would be brought into poverty:  
> *(te veel bezwaard, tot misnoegdheid, ja zelve tot armoede)*  

Great prejudice against this ESTATE in LOANO on part of HB II.  
Promises of considerable sums of money for Kraton for pepper // Fear that other lands would follow.  
**PLAN TO EXTEND activities of INDIGO FACTORY.**
