### Archive Entries: October 1794 (Loano Pepper Agreement & Land Jurisdiction)

* Reference: **Port 3 No. 1. NvB 1794 (Yogya)**
* **13 October 1794 (Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten):**
  * Agreement by HB II regarding pepper plantations in Loano lands ceded to V.O.C. Plantations to be expanded as quickly as possible.
  * Javanese laborers in Loano exempted from all *herrendienst* (feudal labor services) to ensure they remain free from oppression by local chiefs or European overseers.
  * Guarantee that no forced displacement of population from Sultan's domain to V.O.C. lands will occur.
  * **Jurisdiction & Survey Refusal:** HB II refuses to allow land measurement near V.O.C. annexed areas; insists land jurisdiction must remain under Kraton and HB II's laws.
  * **Resistance to Pepper:** HB II states he cannot compel *Bupatis* to plant pepper due to existing agricultural burdens, fearing inhabitants would be driven into poverty (*te veel bijwaard, niet misnoegdheid, ja zelve tot armoede*).
  * Strong prejudice noted on part of HB II against the Loano estate.
  * Substantial monetary payments promised; plans made to extend Indigo Factory operations.
