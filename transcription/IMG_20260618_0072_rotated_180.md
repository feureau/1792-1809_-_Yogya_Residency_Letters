## Content

### AvJ Archive Entries: January – April 1797 (Dismissals, Promotions, & Nayaka Reshuffle)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1797**
* **26 January 1797 (Res. Yogya):**
  * **HB II dismisses Hajifan Regent R.T. Yudakusuma** from his post and service. Plan to appoint an Under-Regent rejected; ricefields distributed among other Regents. Hajifan district placed under direct administration of DRI (Danureja I).
  * **R.T. Natayuda** granted 100 cacahs by HB II (now total 500 cacahs); returning to royal favor (relative of Ratu Kedhaton).
* **2 February 1797 (Res. Solo):**
  * Susuhunan Pakubuwana IV appoints father-in-law R.T. Sasrawinala as **Kyai Adipati Purwadiningrat** (ranked directly under DRI).
  * Mangkunagaran Regent R.T. Sasradiningrat placed as Kliwon replacing R. PWD III.
* **19 March 1797:** Death of Raden Ayu Ngabehi (aunt of HB II) on 18 March.
* **3 April 1797 (Major Nayaka Reshuffle):**
  * **Dismissal of 2 Bupati Dalem (Chiefs of Bandhars):** Kyai Tumenggung Mangundipura & Pangeran Dipakusuma.
  * **Appointment of 2 Outer Regents (*Bupati Jaba*):** R.T. Danukusuma & R.T. Mertanegara (Kliwon to Patih DRI).
  * **1st Bupati Dalem:** R.T. Sumadiningrat (son-in-law of HB II) granted +1,000 cacahs.
  * **2nd Bupati Dalem:** R. Jayingrani (**R.T. Purwadipura**, brother of Ratu Kencana Wulan).
  * 1,000 cacahs confiscated from Pangeran Dipakusuma's son Purwadipura. Danukusuma appointed Outer Regent (+100 cacahs and *Bawat* state umbrella).
  * Former *Bupati Dalem* R.T. Mangundipura demoted to lowest *Dalem* Regent rank (loses 500 cacahs).
