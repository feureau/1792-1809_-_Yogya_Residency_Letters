### 26 January 1797 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- HB II dismisses the Regent of Madiun/Magetan, **Raden Tumenggung Yudakusuma**, from part of his service.
- The plan to appoint an under-regent does not go through, nor does the plan to distribute his rice fields to other Regents.
- The area is now administered directly by Danureja I (DR I).
- **Raden Tumenggung Natayuda** is given 100 *cacahs* by HB II; he seems to be winning back the favour/faith of HB II; now holds 500 *cacahs*.

---

### 2 February 1797 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- HB II appoints his father-in-law, **Raden Tumenggung Sasrawinata** (father of Ratu Kêdhaton), as **Kyai Adipati Purwadiningrat**.
  - Holds rank directly under Patih Danureja I.
  - Given the administration of Magetan directly under HB II’s orders.
- MN [Mangkunegaran] Regent **Raden Tumenggung Sasradiningrat** placed as *kliwon* in place of Raden Prangwedana III.

---

### 19 March 1797 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

- **18 March 1797:** Death of the aunt of HB II, **Raden Ayu Ngabèhi**.

---

### 3 April 1797 (Resident of Yogyakarta):

[Red diagonal heading: **Changes amongst Nayakas.**]

- **Dismissal of two Bupati Dalèm:**
  1. *Kyai Tumenggung Mangundipura* [Red note: *Chief of Bandhars (tollgate keepers)*].
  2. *Pangeran Dipakusuma*.
- **Two Bupati Jaba / Kliwons of Patih DR I:**
  - Raden Tumenggung Danukusuma.
  - Raden Tumenggung Mērtanēgara.
- **New Appointments:**
  - **1st Bupati Dalèm:** Raden Tumenggung Sumadiningrat (son-in-law of HB II).
  - **2nd Bupati Dalèm:** Raden Jayèngsari (*Raden Tumenggung Purwadipura*, brother of Ratu Kencana Wulan).
  - Assigned 1,000 *cacahs*.
- **Consequences of demotions:**
  - 1,000 *cacahs* confiscated from Pangeran Dipakusuma’s son [Purwadipura]; Dipakusuma is left with only 100 *cacahs* and his *bawat* (state umbrella).
  - Dalem Bupati Raden Tumenggung Mangundipura is demoted to lowest Dalem Bupati, losing 500 *cacahs*.
