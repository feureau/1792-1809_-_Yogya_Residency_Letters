### Tollgate Regulations (Typewritten Transcript Page 2 & Weight Conversions)

```text
Chin. or Jav. from Kebumen to Pekalongan or from Kebumen to Semarang via Kedhu carrying kleedjes pay 3 1/4 stuivers more than old tax of 40 ? stuivers.
From Yogya to Sala carrying rice 1 stuiver per horse.
From Pepedan (onderd. Beji, Klaten) carrying kapas 1 st. per horse.
From Yogya to Solo carrying kleedjes either on horseback or per pikul same tax (corge = 1,200 avoirdupois lbs).
Toll of gedhong tembakau from Yogya to Solo for each corge 3 ronde reals thus 6 ronde reals for Solo / Yogya now 3 1/4 for Yogya.
Tax of gedhong of Sibebeck (? Karangkobar / Banjaranegara) and Batur 5 ronde reals per corge passing through Yogya/Solo now 6 ronde reals.

How much did horses carry ? See Louw on horse traffic.
```

* Red ink conversion notes:
  * **1 horse pikul = 180 lbs.**
  * **1 man pikul = 125 lbs.**
  * *Ref:* See Dj. Br. 23 (Van IJsseldijk -> Daendels, 31 Aug. 1808).
