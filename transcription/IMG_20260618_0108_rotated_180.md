### Tollgate Tariffs (cont. - carbon copy)

* Chin. or Jav. from Kĕbumen to Pĕkalongan or from Kĕbumen to Sĕmarang via Kĕdhu carrying kleedjes pay 31¼ stuivers more than old tax of 40 ? stuivers.
* From Yogya to Sala carrying rice 1 stuiver per horse.
* From Pepedan (onderd. Beji, Klaten) carrying kapas 1 st. per horse.
* From Yogya to Solo carrying kleedjes either on horseback or per pikul same tax.
* Toll of gĕdhong têmbakau from Yogya to Solo for each corge 3 ronde real thus 6 ronde reals for Solo/ Yogya now 31¼ for Yogya.
* Tax of gĕdhong of Sibĕbĕk (? Karangkobar / Banjarnĕgara) and Batur 5 ronde reals per corge passing through Yogya/Solo now 6 ronde reals.
* How much did horses carry ? See Louw on horse traffic.
