### AvJ Archive Entries: October – November 1803 (Death of Ratu Ageng & Panolan Reassignment)

* Reference: **Archief van Jogja 1803**
* **18 October 1803:** **DEATH OF RATU AGENG TEGALREJA** (mother of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and great-grandmother of Prince Dipanagara).
* **31 October 1803:** **Death of Regent of Panolan, R.T. Narawijaya.** Succeeded by his son, Raden Ngabehi Natadiwiria.
* **7 November 1803:** Return of 3 Mangkunagaran Regents (including R.T. Prawirasentika to Jipang/Banwerna) hastened due to border disturbances in Madiun. (Re-alignment of Panolan and Jipang regencies following death of Mangkunagaran Bupati Wedana in Yogya).
