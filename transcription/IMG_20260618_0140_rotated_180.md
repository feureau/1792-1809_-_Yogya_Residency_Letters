## Archival & Historical Notes

- **Death of Ratu Agêng Tegalreja (18 October 1803):** Official archival registration of the passing of Ratu Agêng (consort of HB I, mother of HB II, and Dipanegara's guardian at Tegalreja).
- **Panolan Regency Succession (31 October 1803):** Following the demise of the elderly Regent of Panolan, Raden Tumenggung Natawijaya, his son Raden Ngabèhi Natadiwiria is appointed in his place.
- **Mancanagara Regents' Movement & Administration (7 November 1803):**
  - Return of three Eastern Mancanagara (*Mancanagara Wétan*) bupatis to their respective territories:
    - Raden Tumenggung Prawirasêntika returning to Jipang / Bauwêrno, urgently dispatched due to border unrest and robber incursions reported within 5 days' march.
    - Raden Tumenggung Natawijaya returning to Jipang / Panolan.
    - Mas Tumenggung Suranêgara returning to Jipang / Kapadang.
  - Carey cross-references this with the structural list of Mancanagara *Bupati Wedana* administered between Yogyakarta and Surakarta from Jipang to Madiun, and Panaraga to Kediri (*Notulen van de Algemeene en Directie-Vergaderingen van het Bataviaasch Genootschap van Kunsten en Wetenschappen*, p. LXXV).
