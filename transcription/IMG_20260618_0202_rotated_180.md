**Folio / Reference:** H 698 b, AvJ 1805 (Yogya) (Continued)  
**Date Range:** 25 April 1805 – 16 November 1805  
**Primary Subjects:**  
- Violence in East Java: Mas Tumenggung Aria Wiriadiningrat (2nd Surakarta Bupati Wedana in Panaraga) reports wounding of local officials in the Malang area of Gondhanglegi  
- Departure of HB II's Mecca pilgrims: priests depart Yogyakarta for Semarang escorted by royal royal courier (*gandhèk*) Jiwaraga (13 May 1805)  
- Kedu coffee cultivation crisis: new Kedu Regent R.T. Natadiningrat repudiates coffee agreements made by his predecessor R.T. Natayuda; Waterloo's persuasion fails (Nov 1805)  
- Demobilization of troops: 104 prajurits from Mergasari and Sesela under Ngabehi Rajamenggala sent home  
- Gagatan mantris: Raden Demang Prawiradigdaya, Raden Demang Sekti, Raden Demang Taruna  
- Anti-insurgency alert in Northern enclaves: *kamisepuh* (village elders) of Grobogan and Wirasari ordered to watch for any *"tapa"* (ascetic / religious mystic) entering Grobogan, Wirasari, Sesela, or Gagatan to preach revolt / take up arms

### [9 July 1805 & 16 November 1805]

*[Left margin:]*  
9 July 1805  
Res Yogya

CONCERNING COFFEE: No prospect that would be able to produce any coffee this Year:  
Because newly appointed Regent in Kèdhu R.T. Natadiningrat NOT prepared to ENTER into agreement previously arranged + R.T. Natayuda over coffee.

*Later 16 Nov 1805:*  
all my attempts to persuade the new Kèdhu Regent Natadiningrat have been fruitless: He is very much afraid to enter / carry out the old agreement into *[in red ink:]* **Sala**.
