### Archive Entries: August – December 1807 (Cilacap Defense & Widespread Highway Robberies)

* Reference: **Pj. Brieven No. 38 (Naar Soerakarta 1803–1808)**
* **25 August 1807:** Marriage of PB IV's daughter R. Ayu Natariah to R.M. Aria Natakusuma (son of P. Mangkubumi).
* **28 August 1807:** Death of Bok Sasradiningrat (sister of HB II and wife of Sasradiningrat) after long illness.
* **27 October 1807:** 7 followers and a Yogyakarta *oppasser* dispatched to Cilacap with firearms to protect local residents against pirate raids.
* **10 December 1807 (Surge in Highway Banditry):**
  * Massive increase in highway robberies; **heads of districts (*Demangs*) actively placing themselves at the head of robber gangs**.
  * **3 December 1807 (Robber Dens near Kartasura & Semarang):**
    * Saminang (near Kartasura/Asem; split Yogya/Solo): Haven for major robber gangs.
    * Plawarangan, Janti, Brensang (Solo; belonging to P. Adinegara): Demang Potrayuda and inhabitants operating as bandits.
    * Joak (near Klica; Solo): Lurah Angawidjaya.
    * Sangir (near Kartasura; Prangwedana domain): Son of Pinang Pincasewiria leading robber bands.
    * Duki, Combang, Lampir (Yogya; near Asem): Demang Coplagi, Reknadirdja, and Kertadiwangsa.
    * Gagah Nipat & Gabang Bangak (Yogya; near Banyudana): Demang Kertalaksana.
    * Jan Angerim (Yogya; Merbabu foothills): Densely populated village where all residents, **including the local priest**, engage in highway robbery.
    * Pesanggrahan Demangs: Ngedo, Konco Ninian, Trunadilaga (under P. Prangwedana).
