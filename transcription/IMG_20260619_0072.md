### AN Djokjo Brieven 20.41: Daendels Fortifications in Semarang & Court Envoys (1808)

* Reference: **AN 'Djokjo Brieven' 20.41 (May – Aug. 1808)**
* **Surakarta Embassy to Semarang (27 May 1808):** PB IV notifies Daendels that Pangeran Mangkubumi and Pangeran Aria Cakrakusuma are dispatched to Semarang to present the Sunan's official compliments to "Grandfather" Daendels (departing 28 May under J.A. van Braam's guidance).
* **Daendels' Defense Directives to HB II (23 May 1808):**
  * HB II receives Daendels' missive (dated 12 May 1808) announcing his arrival in Semarang and the replacement of Governor Nicolaas Engelhard.
  * Reassures HB II of King Louis' friendship toward Javanese subjects; pledges to prevent British landings.
  * **Semarang Troop Concentration:** Daendels concentrates 3,000–4,000 troops at Semarang to repel British landings. Requests HB II send royal envoys to Semarang to witness defense preparations.
* **Appointment of Rear-Admiral Buyskes:** Rear-Admiral A.A. Buyskes appointed Lieutenant Governor-General in Batavia as designated successor to Daendels.
* **Yogyakarta Troop Exercises:** HB II promises to exercise Yogyakarta troops so they can march to Daendels' assistance with all speed (Yogyakarta embassy departs 22 Aug. 1808).
