## Diplomatic Transcription

<span style="color:red">**— Sept. 26. 1808 —**</span> &emsp;&emsp; **Agreement Yogya — Solo** &emsp;&emsp; **Eur F. 148 / 18**  
<span style="color:red">**Klatèn Agreement**</span> &emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp; **Sept. 26. 1808 (Klatèn)** &emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp; 'Memoirs of Java at the Time of the capture.'  
**Sept. 26 1808 / 6 Ruwah Dal 1735:** &emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp; collected by Capt. Robinson.

**Signed:** &emsp; **J.A. van Braam**  
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp; **P. Engelhard.** on orders of Daendels  
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp; **Danuningrat (Solo)** &emsp; **DR II (Yogya)**

<strike>Murders</strike> Robberies at Tollgates / Borders Between villages. Solo / Yogya \* border Belonging to Princes / Govmt. murders etc. areas + govmt.

1. All Bandhars to Receive a letter + seal of the minister  
   20 of Desas around Tollgates, to guard the Tollgate. To provide men  
   To Be watchful for property of the Princes.  
   If Tollgate Robbed / Burnt inhabitants to answer for the damage.  

2. If Desa attacked By Robbers others <ins>Desas</ins> lying nearby to assist Desa attacked.  
   Must follow Robbers as long as tracks visible.  
   To apprehend Robbers + goods.  
   give notice Regents / DR II  

3. If Bêkêl cannot find Robbers.  
   to Be stretched out in the sun on Paseban.  
   From 7.00 am - 11.00 am for a week.  

4. If Bêkêl allows Robber to escape into <strike>his</strike> a House — allowed 40 days to apprehend them = <ins>meanwhile</ins> Family Taken From Him
5. If mantri / Tampings suspect evil persons abiding in the Desa — not allowed to let Them Remain.
6. If Travellers to stay in a village overnight — to Report First to Pak Lurah.
7. If Regents Do not obey — Ornaments Taken From them suspended from Offices.
