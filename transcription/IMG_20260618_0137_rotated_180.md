### Archive Entries: 1803–1805 (Bandit Expeditions, Waste Land Surveys, & Pacitan Incursion)

* Reference: **P.J. Brieven no. 38 (1803–1808)**
* **11 March 1803:** Van den Berg permits Engelhard to cut teak in Sultan's forests under strict conditions. Anti-bandit expedition returns from Mangkunagaran carrying severed ears and prisoners.
* **7 March 1803 (Danureja II Teak Request):** DR II requests permission to cut teak in Jipang for Pasisir areas between Rembang and Nidayu; timber to be harvested strictly by HB II's Pasisir subjects without Chinese or Pasisir involvement without V.O.C. approval.
* **31 January 1804 (Waste Land Survey Dispute):** Waterloo queries surveying unallocated waste lands of Sunan/Sultan. HB II quashes survey plans (reiterating his 1792 stance) fearing 1773 census base will be used by V.O.C. to claim new lands. Extensive new sawahs opened in Grobogan and Joana.
* **12 February 1804:** Boundary disputes over villages between PB IV and HB II (Nilang, Pakidulan, Pasumpakkan, Pelanggu, Gentan).
* **5 March / 1 April 1804:** 80–90 Mandarese pirates from Buleleng (Bali) operate off Prigi (North Coast). Assassination of King of Banten reported.
* **19 April 1805 (Balinese/Bugis Landing at Pacitan):**
  * **32 pirate ships anchor at Negarawedi (Pacitan / Surakarta area); 400 armed men come ashore.**
  * R.T. Purwadipura and 15 *Mantris* from *Gedhong Tengen* dispatched to Pacitan to investigate.
  * Local villagers barter ginger, *boreh*, and benzoin for guns; chickens sold for 1/2 Rupiah, rice traded for ginger.
