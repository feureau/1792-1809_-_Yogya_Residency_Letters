## Content

### Archive Entries: March 1791 – March 1792 (Death of Sultan Hamengkubuwana I)

* Reference: **NvB. 1792 (Yogya)**
* **19 March 1791 (Yogya):** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Reede (Surakarta): Messengers of Patih Jayaningrat (Solo) sent to resolve attack on Solo village of Brangkal.
* **27 March 1791:** Letter to DRI regarding murder at Torowolong; Chinese have no knowledge of stolen goods.
* **12 April 1791:** Uluan persuaded to hand over 4,000 reals for plundering in Solo (refers to 1790 affair; see Ricklefs).
* **24 March 1792 (11:30 p.m.):** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten: **DEATH OF SULTAN HAMENGKUBUWANA I AT 11:00 P.M.**
  * Resident goes immediately to Kraton where he remains until burial. All peaceful except for expected consternation on such a weighty event.
* **24 March 1792 (Ibid.):** -> Van Reede (Solo): Note on illness of HB I starting Jan. 1792 (plans laid after June 1790; see Ricklefs p. 353).
* **25 March 1792 (7:00 a.m.):** Van IJsseldijk -> Van Overstraten:
  * References secret letter sent by Van Overstraten on 1 Dec 1791 regarding coronation project of HB II (fixed 9 May 1785).
  * Resident convenes Kraton gathering to open and read secret instructions.
  * Attendees: Crown Prince (HB II), DRI (Danureja I), Pangeran Mangkubumi (HB III), R.T. Natayuda, R.T. Danukusuma (Outer Regents), R.T. Mangundipura, Pangeran Dipakusuma (Inner Regents).
