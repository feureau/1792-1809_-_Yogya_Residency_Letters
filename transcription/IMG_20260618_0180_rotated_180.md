## Complete Diplomatic Transcription

*[Continuation of 23 Aug 1806 Klaten Fort report:]*  
*[Red double vertical line]*  
Wiraguna in charge of Yogya workforce but absent

Yogya [workforce] 3 months behind Sala workers.  
HB II needs to give necessary orders to spur on workers.

### [22 December 1808 / 4 Dulkangidah 1735]

*[Left margin:]*  
G.W. Wiese to HB II,  
4 Dulkangidah 1735  
22 Dec. 1808

Question of HB II providing a sum of money to govrmt.  
*[Underlined with red pen, and annotated above in red ink:]* **Daendels**  
"as sign of attachment to Dutch govrmt."  
Wiese no doubt that HB II would pay such a sum.

### [24 January 1809]

*[Left margin:]*  
Wiese to DR II,  
24 Jan. 1809

All supplies of gunpowder for salutes at feasts etc to be stopped.  
Limited to resupply of 500 lbs p.a.

*[Note below:]*  
[note: MN [Mancanagara] Regents given orders to hold themselves in readiness to march at first opportunity]

---

### [31 January 1809]

*[Left margin:]*  
Id. to Id.  
31 Jan. 1809  
*[In red ink in margin, with double vertical red line:]* **Mil. Exercises HB II**

HB II gives orders to hold Military Exercises for Yogya inhabitants.  
Daendels had already warned HB II in 1808 that such military manoeuvres would be interpreted as a Declaration of war.  
asks DR II to prevail on HB II to halt military exercises.

*[Red underline:]*  
Refusal of Wiese to appear in Kraton unless stool provided for him.

---

### [3 February 1809]

*[Left margin:]*  
Wiese to DR II,  
3 Feb. 1809

Orders of HB II to halt military manoeuvres.  
ask for 500 lbs of gunpowder to be paid at f.p. 200 Ronde Real.

---

### [4 February 1809]

*[Left margin:]*  
Id. to Id.

### [21 January 1809]

*[Left margin:]*  
Daendels to Wiese  
21 Jan. 1809

Annoyance of Daendels about DR II:  
(1) arrest of 4 Dutch *jagers*  
(2) sending letter to Sala (30 Dec. 1808) without permission of Resident.

If DR II makes 3rd mistake would not escape necessary punishment, + Prang Wedono's corps would be sent to Klaten to garrison the fort.
