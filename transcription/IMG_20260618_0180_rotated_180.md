### Archive Entries: 1806–1809 (Military Exercises, Daendels Clashes, & Danureja II Warning)

* **9 September 1806:** Yogya workforce 66 days behind at Klaten fort; workers gambling in huts. Capitan Cina of Japan Jan Junko involved.
* **3–11 September 1806:** Bandit attack in Numbing/Pajangan; 1 Chinese killed and goods plundered by Babawangsing / Raden Rongga III.
* **22 December 1808 (G.W. Wiese to HB II):** Wiese requests HB II supply a monetary contribution to the government *"as a sign of attachment to Daendels / Dutch government"*.
* **24 January 1809 (Gunpowder Cut):** All gunpowder supplies for ceremonial salutes stopped; annual allowance capped at 500 lbs p.a. Mangkunagaran Regents instructed to hold troops in readiness.
* **31 January 1809 (HB II Military Exercises & Daendels Warning):**
  * **HB II orders large-scale military maneuvers for Yogya inhabitants.**
  * Marshal Daendels had warned HB II in 1808 that military maneuvers would be interpreted as a **declaration of war**.
  * Wiese requests Danureja II persuade HB II to halt military exercises. Wiese refuses to enter Kraton unless provided a stool.
* **3 February 1809:** HB II halts military maneuvers; requests 500 lbs of gunpowder @ 200 Ronde Reals. New powder magazine constructed in fort.
* **21 January 1809 (Daendels' Ultimatums against Danureja II):**
  * **Daendels' Annoyance with Danureja II:** (1) Arrest of 4 Dutch *Jagers*; (2) Direct letter sent to Solo on 30 Dec. 1808 without Resident's permission.
  * **Warning:** Daendels warns that if DR II commits a 3rd offense, he will not escape punishment, and the **Prangwedana Corps will be dispatched to garrison Klaten Fort**.
* **1 March 1809 (Chanteauvieux Inspection):** Lt.-Col. de Chanteauvieux reports 2,000 cavalry of HB II, Crown Prince, and other princes performing Saturday exercises on the Alun-alun. Promises maneuvers will be organized satisfactorily during Daendels' upcoming visit to Yogya.
