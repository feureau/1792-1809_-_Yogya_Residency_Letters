About the Collection & Historical Background

This digital edition compiles the archival transcripts and research notes produced by Prof. Peter Carey documenting the Vorstenlanden (the Principalities of Yogyakarta and Surakarta) during the second reign of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (reigned 1792–1810, 1811–1812, 1826–1828).

The corpus captures critical correspondence between the Yogyakarta Court (Kraton) and successive Dutch East India Company (VOC) and Franco-Dutch colonial officials (Residents Matthijs Waterloo, F.J. Rothenbühler, J.G. van den Berg, Governor Nicolaus Engelhard, and Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels) leading up to the British intervention under Thomas Stamford Raffles.

  • Primary Archival Repositories: Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (ANRI, Jakarta), Nationaal Archief (The Hague), Leiden University Library, KITLV Collections, and the British Library (Mackenzie & Raffles Collections).
  • Scope: 413 digitized manuscript scans and synchronized markdown transcriptions.
  • Transcription & Editorial Model: Diplomatic transcription preserving original orthography, archaic 18th/19th-century Dutch and Javanese phrasing, marginalia, and archival cross-references.
