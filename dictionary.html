Javanese Royalty and Princely Families Dipanagara, Prince (Pangéran Dipanagara) Central figure of the Java War (1825–1830) and primary subject of the book. Childhood names: Bendara Radèn Mas Mustahar, Radèn Antawirya. Religious/wandering names: Sèh Ngabdurahim, Ngabdulkamit. Assumed the title of the Javanese messianic *Ratu Adil* ('Just King') and styled himself Sultan Ngabdulkamid Èrucakra Sayidin Panatagama Kalifat Rasulullah, Kabiril Mukminin, and Kalipah Rasulullah Jawa in his exile letters.
